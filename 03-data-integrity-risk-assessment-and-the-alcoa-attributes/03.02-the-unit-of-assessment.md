## 1. What this chapter settles

Phase 02 fixed the unit of **determination**: a record type, `ADR-0013`, argued at `02.04 §2`. That unit
answered a scope question — is this class of record a Part 11 record — and it answered it once for the
class, because the answer does not vary with where the class is held. A batch production and control
record is required by `21 CFR 211.188` whether it sits on the manufacturing execution system or on the
archive, and no fact about either system changes that.

**Integrity is not like that.** Whether a record is attributable depends on how accounts are administered
on the thing holding it. Whether an original or a true copy exists depends on what that thing does when a
record is copied, exported or superseded. Whether an entry was contemporaneously recorded depends on when
the thing captures it. **The same record type is not at the same risk on every system that holds it**, and
a unit that cannot express that difference cannot express most of what this phase is for.

This chapter fixes the unit of **assessment** as the **record-type/system pair**, records the decision at
`ADR-0016` / `DEC-303`, derives the population by joining Phase 02's two registers, and describes the
shape of that population before anything has been assessed.

**It states no assessment result.** The determinations are `03.06-the-assessment.md`'s; the ratings are
`03.10-the-scored-risks.md`'s.

## 2. The argument: the pair is the unit, and neither half is

**This section owns this argument for the repository.**

> **The unit of assessment is one record type on one system that holds it. Never the system alone, and
> never the record type alone.**

The reason is that the two halves carry different information and the assessment needs both at once.

**The record type carries what the record is for.** It carries the predicate rule that makes it a required
record, the decision it influences, and what is lost if it is wrong. Those facts are properties of the
class and travel with it everywhere. They are what fixes **consequence**, and consequence is therefore
constant across every system holding a given type — a point `03.05 §6` makes operative and `ADR-0021`
records.

**The system carries how the record is actually held.** It carries who administers access, what the
capture mechanism is, whether the format permits interaction, what happens on export, and whether anybody
has ever looked. Those facts are properties of the instance's environment and vary from one system to the
next. They are what moves **likelihood**.

**Split them and the assessment loses one or the other.** Assess by system and every record on the system
takes the system's answer, including records whose consequence is nothing like each other's. Assess by
record type and every instance takes the type's answer, including instances held somewhere with a
completely different administration arrangement. **The pair is the smallest object about which both a
consequence and a likelihood can be stated without one of them being borrowed from something else.**

This is `ADR-0013` doing its work rather than being restated. The record type remains the unit of
determination; nothing in this phase re-determines anything. The pair is what the record type becomes when
it is asked a question about a place.

## 3. Why not the system alone

Assessing system by system is faster, it produces a shorter register, and it is how a great deal of this
work is done. Phase 02 has already published what is wrong with it as a scope method at `02.04 §3`, and
the failure repeats here in a form that is harder to see.

A system-level integrity assessment produces one answer per system. That answer has to cover every record
type the system holds, and so it becomes the answer for the worst of them or the answer for the most
common of them, depending on who wrote it. Neither is a fact about anything.

The estate makes this concrete without needing a result. The densest system in the population holds seven
of the thirty-four in-scope record types, and **thirty of the sixty-one hold exactly one each** —
`03.06 §2` gives the cuts. **A single integrity statement about that densest system is a statement about
seven different records resting on five different predicate-rule provisions, whose consequences if they
are wrong run from a periodic evaluation to the release decision itself.** Compressing them into one row
does not simplify the assessment. **It discards the thing the
assessment was for.**

There is a second failure and it runs the other way. A system-level unit invites the question *is this
system risky*, and that question has an answer everybody already believes before the assessment starts.
The chromatography data systems will be said to be risky because of what the 483 raised; the learning
management system will be said not to be. **A unit that lets the assessment be pre-empted by reputation is
a unit that will produce the answer the organisation walked in with**, and the register will record the
reputation rather than the record.

## 4. Why not the record type alone

The mirror error is more respectable and it is the one this programme had to argue itself out of, because
the record type is already the repository's unit of determination and reusing it would have cost nothing.

Thirty-four determinations rather than one hundred and twenty-one is a real saving. It would also have
been wrong, and Phase 02's own register shows why: **thirty-one of the thirty-four in-scope record types
are held on more than one system.** A single integrity statement about such a record type is a statement
about instances sitting in different places under different arrangements, and the assessment would have
had to choose. It would have chosen the worst instance, in which case the register understates the
condition of every other instance and the remediation is aimed at the wrong systems; or it would have
chosen a representative instance, in which case the register is a sample presented as a population.

**The record type is where the requirement lives. The system is where the record lives. An assessment of
reliability has to be made where the record is.**

## 5. The join, and the population it produces

The population was derived, not designed. It is the join of two published registers, and neither was
altered to produce it.

| Input | Source | What was taken |
|---|---|---|
| **The thirty-four in-scope record types** | `../02-the-system-inventory-and-part-11-applicability/logs/record-type-register.md` | Every record type determined a Part 11 record at `02.05`, with its predicate-rule anchor |
| **The sixty-one systems that hold at least one of them** | `../02-the-system-inventory-and-part-11-applicability/logs/systems-register.md` | Every system whose Part 11 position was derived at `02.08`, with the record types named on its entry |
| **The join** | The `Record types held` column of the systems register, read against the `Systems` column of the record-type register | **One pair for each record type on each system that holds it** |

**The join produces one hundred and twenty-one pairs.** Each carries five attribute determinations, so the
instrument runs **one hundred and twenty-one times five, which is six hundred and five determinations**.
Those two figures are the phase's arithmetic spine and they are asserted nowhere else in this chapter than
here.

Four rules govern the join and each of them is a refusal.

**No pair is created that the registers do not support.** If a record type is not named on a system's
entry, there is no pair. The join is mechanical and it is re-runnable; a reader with the two registers can
reproduce the population and get the same number.

**No pair is dropped because it looks uninteresting.** Thirty of the sixty-one systems carry exactly one
pair each, and several of those single pairs sit on systems nobody would have nominated for an
assessment. They are in the population because the register puts them there.

**The join does not re-open the determination.** Whether a record type is a Part 11 record was settled at
`GOV-09` on 2026-07-24 and changes only through `ADR-0015`'s trigger and change control. **An assessment
that would prefer a different scope does not get one.** That constraint is Phase 02's, imposed at
`02.13 §6`, and this phase keeps it.

**The join does not create a record type or a system.** No identifier is minted here. `RT-nn` and
`SYS-nnn` mean in this phase exactly what they meant in Phase 02, and a pair is written as the two
identifiers together — the record type first, because the record type is what is being assessed and the
system is where.

## 6. What a pair carries

Each of the one hundred and twenty-one pairs carries the same fields on the same form, and the form is at
`templates/`. The fields are listed here because the shape of the record is the method; the contents are
the register's.

| Field | What it holds | Why it is on the form |
|---|---|---|
| **Pair identity** | The record type identifier and the system identifier | The unit. Both halves, always |
| **Predicate-rule anchor** | The provision that makes the record a required record | Carried through from Phase 02. **A determination without it is a preference** |
| **Owner** | The named individual who owns the record type's determination and the named individual who owns the system | `ADR-0008`. Two names, because the two halves have different owners and the assessment needs both |
| **Five attribute determinations** | One of **Met**, **Not met**, **Not determinable**, for each of FDA's five attributes | `03.03` states the attributes; `03.05 §3` states the three determinations and what each means |
| **The basis of each determination** | What was examined, and by whom | A determination whose basis is not recorded cannot be reviewed, and cannot be re-run when the facts change |
| **Consequence, with its basis** | One to five, with the reason, drawn from what the record decides | `03.05 §5`. **Recorded separately from likelihood, always** |
| **Likelihood, with its basis** | One to five, with the reason, and it is the likelihood of the **undetected** alteration | `03.05 §6`, `ADR-0021` |
| **The register the pair goes to** | The scored register or the unscored one | `ADR-0018`. **A pair goes to one or the other and never to both** |

**Consequence and likelihood are recorded on separate lines with separate bases, and the product is
written out rather than being the only thing written down.** A register that carries a product and not its
two components cannot be argued with, cannot be corrected in one dimension, and cannot show which half
moved when a rating changes. `03.05 §5` owns that rule.

## 7. What the shape of the population tells you about the unit

The join produced **one hundred and twenty-one pairs** and therefore **six hundred and five attribute
determinations**. Those two figures are this chapter's, and everything else about the population's shape —
the spread of record types across systems, the density of pairs on individual systems, the distribution by
owner and by location — **is published once, at `03.06-the-assessment.md §2`, and is cited from here rather
than restated.** It is counted from the register at `logs/attribute-determination-register.md` and from
nothing else.

Three properties of that shape are worth naming here, because each is an argument for the unit rather than
a finding about the estate.

**The distribution is uneven, and a system-shaped method would have hidden the unevenness.** Most of the
systems in the population carry a single pair and a small number carry five or more. A register with one
row per system would have spent the same space on each of sixty-one rows; the pair count says where the
population actually is, and `03.06 §2` gives the cuts.

**The two chromatography data systems hold the same five record types as each other** — one at Ridgemont
and one at Cary. That is the clearest illustration in the estate of why the pair is the unit: **five
identical record types, two different places, and nothing in the method that forces the two places to
receive the same answer.** Whether they did receive the same answer is `03.06`'s and `03.07`'s to report,
and this chapter does not.

**The two most widely held record types sit on ten systems each.** A single record-type-level statement
about either would have been a statement about ten different environments, and those two record types
alone generate twenty of the one hundred and twenty-one pairs. **That is the record-type-only alternative
at `§4` failing on the estate's own numbers rather than in principle.**

## 8. What the pair cannot see

The unit is a record type on a system. **It is therefore an information-technology-shaped unit, and the
best available regulator-authored method source says an assessment should not be shaped that way.**
PIC/S PI 041-1, at `§5.5.4`, directs that risk assessments focus on a business process, evaluate data flows
and the methods of generating and processing data, and *"not just consider information technology (IT)
system functionality or complexity"*.

**Thirty-one of the thirty-four in-scope record types are held on more than one system, and the pair
assesses the record on each system and assesses the transfer between them nowhere.**

This chapter states that limitation and hands it on rather than resolving it, because it is a criticism of
the unit and not a defect in the join. `03.11-the-flow-the-unit-does-not-see.md` owns the argument, states
what the proxy buys and what it costs, and raises `IS-18`. **A phase that does not name the hole in its own
instrument has published an instrument nobody can calibrate.**

## 9. What this chapter does not do

- **It states no determination for any pair**, and no count of Met, Not met or Not determinable. `03.06`.
- **It states no rating.** `03.10`.
- **It does not describe the instrument or the scale.** `03.05`.
- **It does not name the five attributes' definitions.** `03.03`, in FDA's words.
- **It does not re-open the Part 11 applicability determination**, and nothing in the join may be read as
  altering it.
- **It states no validation status for any system and no control position for any paragraph of
  `21 CFR 11.10`.** Phase 04 and Phase 05.
- **It reaches no legal conclusion** and forecasts no inspection outcome.
