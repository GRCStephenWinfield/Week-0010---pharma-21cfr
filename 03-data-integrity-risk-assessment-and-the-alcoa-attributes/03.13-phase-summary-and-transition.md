## 3. The seven architecture decisions and what each leaves behind

| ADR | Decision | `DEC` | Consequence the programme now lives with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`ADR-0016`** | **The unit of assessment is the record-type/system pair**, because the same record type is not at the same risk on every system that holds it | `DEC-303`, 2026-08-12, Priyanka Venkataraman | 121 assessments rather than 61, 605 determinations, and a register that has to be read across rather than sorted |
| **`ADR-0019`** | **ALCOA is not a control set, and the mapping Helix uses is FDA's own footnote 5 to the predicate rules** | `DEC-305`, 2026-08-19, Priyanka Venkataraman | **Two assessments of the same records rather than one**, and an obligation to keep saying which one is being done — which is what stops Phase 05 being skipped |
| **`ADR-0017`** | **The five attributes are assessed separately and are never averaged, weighted or traded off** | `DEC-307`, 2026-08-26, Dr Sunita Raghunathan | No single number per pair, and an audience that will ask for one anyway — `PR-21` |
| **`ADR-0018`** | **Not determinable is a determination in its own right, and a pair carrying one is not scored** | `DEC-310`, 2026-09-04, Terrence Abbatiello | **47 pairs with no rating, a second register, and the phase's central argument** — `IS-16` |
| **`ADR-0020`** | **The scale is Helix's own, is kept small, and is published**, because under `21 CFR 211.22(d)` a written procedure is one Helix will be held to | `DEC-313`, 2026-09-11, Terrence Abbatiello | A written procedure now enforceable against Helix — `IS-19` — and a coarse instrument that will sometimes put two visibly different pairs in one band |
| **`ADR-0021`** | **Likelihood is of the undetected alteration, and a rating moves on likelihood unless the consequence changed** | `DEC-316`, 2026-09-22, Joachim Reuter | Every likelihood basis has to name the act that would do the noticing, and 54 of the 74 sit at likelihood 2 because a comparison exists |
| **`ADR-0022`** | **The assessment is re-run on a trigger and not on a calendar** | `DEC-319`, 2026-09-29, Gideon Halvorsen | A trigger set operated by named individuals in the absence of any procedure requiring it — `AS-25`, `IS-19` |

**Three of the seven are decisions to accept a cost rather than avoid one** — `ADR-0017`, `ADR-0018` and
`ADR-0020`. The first refused a number that would have been easier to present, the second refused
forty-seven of them, and the third published a scale knowing that publishing it makes it enforceable. The
full records, with the alternatives argued fairly, are at `adr/README.md`.

**`IS-11` closed and it is worth being exact about what closed.** Phase 02's question was whether anybody
had assessed whether the data in the 34 in-scope record types is any good. **Somebody has**, across the
whole population, with a named determiner and the evidence examined on every determination. **A
determination of Not determinable is a result of assessment and not an absence of one** — which is the
proposition `DEC-317` turns on, and **`IS-16` opens in `IS-11`'s place** to carry the 47 forward.

**`AS-17` closed because it failed.** Vendor documentation for the seven newly in-scope record types
describes what the products do and does not describe what those record types do at Helix. **An assumption
that fails is closed and recorded as failed** rather than widened until it holds — the precedent is
`AS-03`'s split verdict at the `MS-06` gate.

**Three entries are deliberately unresolved and stay that way** — `IS-01`, `IS-04` and `IS-07` — together
with `PR-10`, which still has no agreed response and whose cell still says so. **`IS-01` stays open and
stays unweighted, and nothing in Phase 03 bears on any of its four candidate explanations** — `03.12 §5`.

**No programme risk carries a likelihood, an impact or a score, and none ever will.** Phase 03 is the first
phase with a scale, and the first thing it did with the scale was refuse to point it at the RAID log.

## 6. What Phase 04 gets, and what it must not do

Phase 04's slug is `04-the-validation-framework-and-policy-architecture`. **`MS-08`, 2026-11-30**, owned by
Colm Ó Braonáin. **Sixty-one days.**

**`MS-07` before `MS-08` is a sequencing rule the programme does not own**: the validation framework's
rigour is set against assessed risk, and **building the framework first sets the rigour by habit.**

| Phase 04 **must** | Phase 04 **must not** |
|---|---|
| Take **both** registers as an input to the validation framework's rigour. **The risk-basing of validation extent is FDA's guidance, not FDA's regulation** — December 2018 Q&A, **Q3**: *"The extent of validation studies should be commensurate with the risk posed by the automated system"*, a nonbinding *should*. `21 CFR 211.68(b)`'s *shall* graduates **input/output verification**, and is cited only for that — `03.01 §5`. **Phase 04 must also record that neither axis of this scale is the axis `21 CFR 211.68(b)` names.** The regulation grades on *"the complexity and reliability of the computer or related system"*; this assessment grades on what the record decides and on whether an alteration would be noticed. **Where Phase 04 relies on this assessment to size input/output verification, it must first state how the two axes relate to complexity and reliability, or assess those separately. That statement does not exist at this vantage.** | **Size rigour from the 74 alone.** It receives **121 pairs, not 74.** The 47 carry no number, and a framework sized against the numbers it has is a framework that ignores the pairs it cannot see — `PR-24` |
| Write the procedure that says what a determination obliges Helix to do — the thing `IS-21` records does not exist | **Treat the assessment as the procedure.** A register is a description; a description nobody is required to act on remains a description |
| Bring the assessment, the applicability determination and the scale inside the quality system, with an owner, a review trigger and a change-control step — `IS-10`, `IS-19`, `IS-21`, through `DP-11` and `DP-19` | **Re-open the assessment to get a more convenient answer.** A pair's determination changes on `ADR-0022`'s trigger and through change control, not because a framework would prefer a different input |
| Size for the seventeen systems with no validation lifecycle behind them, eleven of which carry pairs in these registers — `IS-13`, `PR-17` | **State a control position.** Which paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10` are met is Phase 05's — `IS-20`, `IS-09` |
| Carry the closure routes for the 47 to the phases that own them — Phase 05 for attribution and contemporaneity, Phase 06 for the chromatography pairs, Phase 07 for retention and the archive | **Convert an `ND` row into a `DR` row.** A pair that becomes determinable is re-assessed and enters the scored register with a new identifier and a date |
| Extend `EV`, `DEC`, `IS`, `AS`, `DP` and `PR` rather than restarting them | **Score anything in the programme registers**, or apply this scale to a delivery risk — `01.12 §4` |

**And two things Phase 04 must not read into the registers.** **A rating is not a control position and not a
validation status.** **The absence of the 35 GxP-relevant systems that hold no Part 11 record from the 121 is
not an assessment of them.** **Many of them are reached by `21 CFR 211.68`**, which applies to computers and
related systems used in the manufacture, processing, packing and holding of a drug product whatever the Part
11 answer is; **others are reached by different provisions of Part 211 according to what they do, and this
phase has not determined which provision reaches which system.** `AS-26` carries the assumption.

## 7. The question Phase 03 leaves open

**The assessment says where the data is weak. Nothing in the quality system yet says what Helix must do
about it.**

There is no procedure requiring the assessment to exist. There is no procedure requiring a determination of
*Not met* or *Not determinable* to produce an action. There is no threshold at which a rating obliges
anybody to do anything, and no owner for the consequence of a row. **Helix now knows, pair by pair and
attribute by attribute, and knowing is not a control.**

**`IS-21`** carries it, owned by Priyanka Venkataraman, raised 2026-09-30, open. **Phase 04 is where the
procedure that would close it has to be written, and `DP-19` is the only route by which it closes.**

**It is the same shape as the question Phase 02 left.** `IS-10` recorded that the applicability determination
existed and that nothing required it to be maintained. **`IS-19` and `IS-21` say the same of the scale and of
the assessment.** Three artefacts now exist that the quality system does not know about, and the absence of a
requirement is what made the absence of the first one invisible for the whole of the period `02.10` searched.
**Writing the document fixes the document. It does not fix the requirement.**

## 9. What is still not true

Stated as absences, because an absence written down is an absence somebody can act on.

- **There is no validation framework and no validation status for any system**, including the seventeen the
  walk found. Phase 04, `MS-08`.
- **No control has been implemented, no audit trail has been configured or examined, and no electronic
  signature has been deployed.** Phase 05, `MS-09`. **Which paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10` are met, for any
  system, is not stated anywhere in this phase** — `IS-20`.
- **`21 CFR 11.10` has still not been assessed for the three vendor-hosted systems** — `IS-09`.
- **No retention period is recorded against any of the 34 in-scope record types**, and that absence is part
  of why "original or a true copy" could not be determined on 41 pairs — `IS-14`, `DP-18`.
- **No procedure in Helix's quality system requires the applicability determination, the assessment or the
  scale to exist, to be maintained or to be acted on** — `IS-10`, `IS-19`, `IS-21`.
- **Forty-seven pairs cannot be scored and have not been.** They are not low risk and they are not adverse;
  **they are undetermined, and every one of them says what would determine it** — `IS-16`.
- **The transfer of a record between systems has been assessed nowhere**, for 31 of the 34 in-scope record
  types — `IS-18`.
- **No periodic review has been performed**, and no internal or third-party audit of the remediated
  programme has been performed. Phases 08 and 09.
- **There has been no further inspection and there is no inspection outcome of any kind.** The programme does
  not forecast one, at this vantage or any other.
- **The reason four internal audits raised nothing on three observations is not established**, and nothing in
  Phase 03 bears on any of the four candidate explanations — `IS-01`, `ADR-0012`, `03.12 §5`.
- **There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate and no FDA approval or validation of any
  software Helix runs.**
- **No document in this phase reaches a legal conclusion of any kind**, and none characterises the
  inspection, its observations, or any past practice at Helix as a violation of anything.
