# CHANGELOG — Phase 03: Data Integrity Risk Assessment and the ALCOA Attributes

## 1.0 — 2026-09-30

First issue of the Phase 03 record set, speaking as at the vantage of **2026-09-30**.

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| **Nothing required this assessment.** No FDA regulation and no FDA guidance requires a data integrity risk assessment. FDA invokes risk in **one general statement in the December 2018 Q&A's Introduction and three bounded invocations in its answers**, all nonbinding, none naming an artefact | `03.01`, `GOV-11` |
| **The one binding requirement the method is anchored on is `21 CFR 211.68(b)`** — *"the degree and frequency of input/output verification shall be based on the complexity and reliability of the computer or related system"* — and **it is cited for input/output verification and for nothing else** | `03.01 §5` |
| **FDA's own ALCOA mapping is footnote 5, and it maps to the CGMP predicate rules and cites 21 CFR Part 11 nowhere.** The industry crosswalk onto `21 CFR 11.10(a)` to `(k)` is an invention that FDA's own footnote refutes. **An ALCOA assessment and a Part 11 control assessment are two different assessments of the same records**, and Phase 05 does the second | `03.04`, ADR-0019, `DEC-305` |
| **FDA writes "contemporaneously recorded" and "original or a true copy".** Neither is abbreviated anywhere in this phase | `03.03` |
| **The unit of assessment is the record-type/system pair** — 121 pairs, derived by joining Phase 02's 34 in-scope record types to its 61 systems, never asserted | `03.02`, ADR-0016, `DEC-303` |
| **The five attributes are assessed separately and are never averaged, weighted or traded off.** 605 determinations: 388 Met, 96 Not met, 121 Not determinable | `03.06`, ADR-0017, `DEC-307` |
| **THE CENTRAL ARGUMENT — "Not determinable" is not a low score.** 47 of the 121 pairs carry at least one attribute Helix cannot determine, and **they are not scored at all**. An absence of evidence scores nothing; it is a gap, not a low number | `03.08`, ADR-0018, `DEC-310` |
| **The attribute that fails most is not the one anybody expected.** *Original or a true copy* is Not met on 34 pairs and Not determinable on 41 — **75 of the 121**. The expectation was *attributable*, and the expectation was wrong | `03.07`, `IS-15` |
| **The scale is Helix's own, and `21 CFR 211.22(d)` is why it is kept small** — the quality control unit's procedures shall be in writing and *"such written procedures shall be followed"*. No regulator prescribes a scale; once Helix writes one, its own procedure is enforceable against it | `03.05`, ADR-0020, `DEC-313` |
| **The phase publishes the hole in its own instrument.** PIC/S PI 041-1 §5.5.4 warns against assessing on *"information technology (IT) system functionality or complexity"*, which is the frame `21 CFR 211.68(b)` uses. **31 of the 34 record types move between systems and the transfer is assessed nowhere** — and `21 CFR 211.68(b)`'s second sentence makes checking input and output a `shall` | `03.11`, `IS-18` |
| **Correction made at source:** the printout-route and PIC/S quotations were truncated; every quotation in this phase is now exact or explicitly elided | `03.11`, `03.03` |
| **Correction made at source:** footnote 5's twelve Part 211 citations resolve to **ten distinct sections**, of which nine appear in Phase 02's register. **`21 CFR 211.122` appears in none of it** — carried on `AS-16` | `03.04 §8` |
| **Correction made at source:** `DP-09` is closed on its first limb only; its second — the 35 systems reached through `21 CFR 211.68` — **was not discharged and is recorded as unmet rather than dropped** | RAID log |
