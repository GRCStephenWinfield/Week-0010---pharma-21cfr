## 0. What is in this index and what is not

**Every item below exists at the vantage.** Nothing in this index is a plan, and no item is dated after
2026-09-30.

**This is the first evidence index in this repository that contains a score**, and **the group that says
most about the phase is one that contains no score: the 47 completed Not-determinable records at `EV-316`**,
each recording what could not be determined and what would be needed to determine it.

**What is not here.** No validation plan, protocol, summary report or validation status — Phase 04. **No
control test result, no access review, no audit trail export and no audit trail configuration record** —
Phase 05; where an audit trail could not be inspected, the evidence of that is the determination record and
not an export. **No retention schedule.** **No periodic review, no internal audit of the remediated
programme, no third-party audit report, and no inspection record after 2026-02-13.**

**Nothing in this index is a certification, an approval or an attestation of compliance by any agency.**
**There is no Part 11 certification**, and no artefact here could be one.

**On the numbering.** One `EV` block per phase, `EV-3nn` for Phase 03, matching document IDs
`HLX-DI-2026-3NN`. **No identifier already issued changes meaning:** `EV-226` is the same artefact it was,
and `EV-316` will mean the same artefact in Phase 09 as it does here.

**Retention.** Each item is retained for at least the life of the determination, decision or register it
supports. For the two registers at `EV-318` and `EV-320` that is for as long as the assessment is in force
and for the retention period of the records it describes — which **is not stated, because no retention
period exists for any of the 34 record types** (`IS-14`, `DP-18`).

## 2. What the index shows about the phase

| Class | Items | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| The method, fixed before the assessment | `EV-302`, `EV-303`, `EV-306`, `EV-308` to `EV-313` | Nine items, and their dates matter: **not one of them post-dates the first completed determination record.** An instrument settled after the results are in is not an instrument |
| The population | `EV-304` | One item. **The population is derived and not enumerated**, so the working paper is the whole of the evidence for it |
| **The determinations** | **`EV-314`, `EV-315`** | **Two items and 605 determinations.** `EV-315` is the one an auditor would ask for: it says what was examined for each *Met*, and a *Met* nobody can trace to an examination is an opinion |
| **The refusal** | **`EV-316`, `EV-322`** | **Two items, and they are the phase.** Forty-seven records of an assessment that could not reach an answer, and the acceptance of them as a population rather than as a score |
| The ratings | `EV-317`, `EV-320`, `EV-323` | Three items. Every scored pair carries two bases and they are recorded separately |
| The findings | `EV-324` to `EV-328` | Five items — the attribute that fails most, the expectation that was wrong, the seven, the vendor documentation that did not answer, and the transfers nobody assessed |
| Governance and registers | `EV-301`, `EV-305`, `EV-307`, `EV-318`, `EV-319`, `EV-321`, `EV-329` to `EV-336` | Fourteen items. **9 + 1 + 2 + 2 + 3 + 5 + 14 = 36**, and every item is counted once |

**`EV-315`, `EV-316` and `EV-325` are the three an auditor would ask for first**, and all three are records
of something inconvenient. The first says what a *Met* rests on. The second is forty-seven records of an
assessment that could not reach an answer, each with the evidence that would let it. **The third is a record
that the phase expected a different result and was wrong**, kept because an expectation that is quietly
dropped once it fails leaves a phase looking as though it never had one.

**`EV-331` is indexed with no entries in it.** A trigger record opened and empty is evidence that the
mechanism exists; it would be evidence of nothing if it were indexed only once something had fired.

## 3. Rules the index keeps

| Rule | Statement |
|---|---|
| **It records what exists** | An artefact that has not been produced has no `EV` number. **No item is dated after the vantage** |
| **One artefact, one identifier** | An artefact is indexed once. Later phases cite the same number rather than re-issuing it |
| **Named owners** | Every item has a named individual owner — `ADR-0008` |
| **No result in a template** | The four templates in `../templates/` are empty forms and carry no `EV` number. `EV-308`, `EV-311` and `EV-312` are the **approved instruments**; `EV-314`, `EV-316`, `EV-317` and `EV-331` are **completed or opened instances**, which is a different thing |
| **No validation status** | No item states, implies or evidences the validation state of any system |
| **No control position** | No item states whether any paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` is met for any system — `IS-20` |
| **No forecast** | No item supports a statement about what a future inspection or audit will find |
| **No legal conclusion** | No item adjudicates anything or characterises any practice as a violation of anything |
| **Continuity** | Phase 04 opens at `EV-4nn`. **No identifier is reused and none already issued is renumbered** |
