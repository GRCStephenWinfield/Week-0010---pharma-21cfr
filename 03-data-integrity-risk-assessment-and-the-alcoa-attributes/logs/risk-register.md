## 0. Two tables that never merge

**`DR` and `ND` are two registers published in one file, and they are not two halves of a scale.**

`DR-01` to `DR-74` carry a rating. `ND-01` to `ND-47` carry no rating, no likelihood, no consequence and no
product, **because a pair Helix cannot assess is not a pair Helix has assessed favourably.** An absence of
evidence scores nothing. **The two tables do not merge, they are not summed, and neither identifier converts
into the other** — `ADR-0018`, `DEC-310`, argued at `../03.08-what-cannot-be-determined.md`.

| Population | Count | Table | Carries a rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pairs fully determinable on all five attributes | **74** | §2 | **Yes** |
| Pairs carrying at least one Not determinable attribute | **47** | §4 | **No** |
| **Total** | **121** | | **74 + 47 = 121** |

**Nothing in the programme registers is scored, and nothing here is a programme register.** `PR-01` to
`PR-26` in `raid-log.md` carry no likelihood, no impact and no score and never will —
`01.12 §4`. **A scheduling concern does not belong on the same scale as a threat to a batch record**, and
this file and that one are kept apart for that reason.

## 1. The scale, cited and not restated

`../03.05-the-method-and-the-scale.md` owns the scale and this register applies it. In one table, so that a
row can be read without leaving the file:

| Element | Position |
|---|---|
| Rating | **Likelihood × consequence**, each 1 to 5 |
| Bands | **High ≥ 15 · Moderate 8 to 12 · Low ≤ 6** |
| **Likelihood** | **Of the *undetected* alteration, not of the alteration.** An attribute recorded Not met is a fact, not a likelihood — `ADR-0021` |
| **Likelihood 1** | **Reserved and unused.** No row below carries it |
| Movement | **A rating moves on likelihood unless the consequence changed** — `ADR-0021`, `DEC-316` |
| **Both bases recorded** | **Separately, on every row.** A single sentence covering both is not a basis for either |
| The impossible products | **7, 11, 13, 14, 17, 18, 19, 21, 22, 23 and 24 are not products of two integers between 1 and 5, and no row carries one** |

**Consequence is a property of the record type and likelihood is a property of the pair.** What is lost if
the record is wrong travels with the class; whether an alteration would be noticed depends on the system
holding it. That is why the same record type carries the same consequence on most of its rows and different
likelihoods, and why the exceptions are exceptions of role rather than of preference — where an instance is
a secondary or derived holding of a record type, its consequence is recorded lower, and **the band the
basis's closing clause names is what records the difference**.

**Seven of the 74 take their likelihood from a comparison with an instance that is itself in the unscored
register.** The comparison is an act somebody performs and its existence is a fact, but **the trueness of
the instance compared against is undetermined**, and those seven ratings would move if that changed. The
rows are `DR-01`, `DR-02`, `DR-62`, `DR-64`, `DR-65`, `DR-71` and `DR-72`. **No rating and no product is
altered by recording this** — it is disclosure, and `ADR-0022`'s `R2` is the route by which any of the
seven would be re-run.

**`RT-40` is named *Annual product review record* because that is what Helix's quality system calls it.
`21 CFR 211.180(e)` does not use that phrase**; it requires records to be maintained so the data in them can
be used for *"evaluating, at least annually, the quality standards of each drug product."* **The record type
name is Helix's; the obligation is the regulation's, and the two are not the same thing.**

turned into a rating, because the rating would have to be taken across five attributes and one of them is
not there.**

## 6. What a row in this file does not authorise

| A rating is not | Because |
|---|---|
| **A finding** | A finding is made by somebody with the authority to make one, against a standard, in a process that produces findings. This is Helix's own assessment of its own position |
| **A deficiency** | A deficiency is a shortfall against a stated requirement. This register states no requirement and tests nothing against one |
| **A violation** | **No document in this repository reaches a legal conclusion of any kind** |
| **A control position** | **Nothing here says whether any control works** — `IS-20`. Which paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10` are met, for any system, is Phase 05's |
| **A validation status** | Phase 04, `MS-08`. **`IS-13`'s seventeen systems appear in these tables and their validation state is not stated on any row** |
| **An ordering** | **A band is a product of two axes and nothing more.** No row goes anywhere before another because of its band, and **no High row is placed in front of Phase 04 first** — `../03.05-the-method-and-the-scale.md §7` |
| **A priority order for remediation** | **The assessment says where the data is weak. Nothing in Helix's quality system yet says what any band obliges anybody to do** — `IS-21`, and Phase 04 owns it |

**A Low rating is the one most likely to be misread.** It says that on this pair, at this vantage, on this
scale, an undetected alteration is met by something that would show it and the consequence if it were not is
bounded. **It does not say the pair is compliant, controlled, validated or safe**, and `PR-20` carries the
risk that somebody will read it that way.
