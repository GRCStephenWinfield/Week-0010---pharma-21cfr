## 3. What the committee checked before approving

**Approval was conditioned on four checks, each performed against the reconciliation working paper at
`EV-319` and each recorded as passed.**

| Check | Position |
|---|---|
| **The population** | 121 pairs, derived from Phase 02's two registers by join and not typed in. **Reconciled to the record-type register and the systems register** |
| **The determinations** | **121 × 5 = 605.** Met **388** · Not met **96** · Not determinable **121**. **388 + 96 + 121 = 605** |
| **The division** | **74 fully determinable and scored · 47 carrying at least one Not determinable attribute and not scored. 74 + 47 = 121.** No pair appears in both registers and no pair appears in neither |
| **The scale, as applied** | Every scored row carries a likelihood and a consequence with **separate** bases; **no row carries likelihood 1**; **no row carries an impossible product** |

**A fifth check was performed and is recorded because it is the one an auditor would ask about.** **Every
determination recorded *Met* names the evidence examined** — `EV-315`. A *Met* that cannot be traced to an
examination is an opinion in a register that looks like a determination, and the committee satisfied itself
that none is.

## 5. `IS-11` closes, and what closed

**`IS-11`**, raised 2026-07-31 at the Phase 02 vantage: *the determination exists and it says 34 record types
across 61 systems are in scope. Nobody has assessed whether the data in any of them is any good.*

| Element | Position |
|---|---|
| Closure condition, as written when the issue was raised | An assessment of the integrity of the records in the in-scope population exists |
| What exists | **121 pairs assessed on five attributes each, with a named determiner, a date, a quality unit reviewer and the evidence examined on every determination** |
| Closure decision | `DEC-317`, 2026-09-24, Priyanka Venkataraman |
| Ratified | 2026-09-29 at `GOV-15` |
| **Closure evidence named** | `EV-318` · `EV-320` · this approval |
| **Replaced by** | **`IS-16`**, opened at `GOV-14` |

**The committee recorded the proposition the closure turns on, in terms, because the opposite reading is
available and is wrong.**

> **A determination of Not determinable is a result of assessment and not an absence of one.** To record
> that an attribute cannot be determined, somebody has to establish what evidence would settle it, look for
> that evidence where it would be, and record that it is not there. **A pair nobody looked at and a pair
> somebody looked at and could not resolve are different objects, and only the second has a closure
> condition.**

**And the committee recorded what does not close with it.** The 47 remain undetermined; `IS-16` carries
them. **Closing `IS-11` records that the assessment was performed. It records nothing about whether Helix's
records can be relied on.**

## 6. What this approval does not do

- **It does not approve a validation status for any system** — Phase 04, `MS-08`.
- **It does not approve a control position.** Which paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10` are met, for any system, is
  not stated in either register — **`IS-20`**, Phase 05.
- **It does not approve an audit trail configuration, a review frequency or a retention period.**
- **It does not convert any rating into a finding, a deficiency, a deviation or a violation.** **A rating is
  Helix's own assessment of its own position at one vantage.**
- **It does not merge the two registers, and it forbids doing so.** Neither identifier converts into the
  other.
- **It draws no conclusion from the `OBS-2` or `OBS-4` links**, both of which are recorded in the phase and
  neither of which may be reasoned from before Phase 06 and Phase 08 — `GOV-10 §6`.
- **It reaches no legal conclusion and forecasts no inspection outcome.**
