## 1. One idea

**Phase 03 produced numbers, and this is the first phase in this repository that did.** A number is read
differently from a sentence. It is extracted, ranked, put in a slide and carried into rooms where the
document that produced it is not present.

**This chapter states what the numbers do not mean**, in the same file as the numbers, at the same version,
and as a numbered chapter rather than as a caveat at the foot of a register. **A caveat in a footnote is a
caveat nobody reads.**

It is owned by internal audit, which designed none of the assessment, applied none of the instrument and
took none of the ratings — `ADR-0004`. **That is the point of who owns it.**

## 2. A rating is not a finding, and the vocabulary stays separate

**`ADR-0006` established that this repository keeps one set of books**, and that `OBS-n`, `DEV-nnn` and
*finding* remain three separate words for three separate things. **`DR-nn` is now a fourth**, and it is not
any of the other three.

| A rating is not | Because |
|---|---|
| **A finding** | A finding is made by somebody with the authority to make one, against a standard, in a process that produces findings. **A `DR` row is Helix's own assessment of its own position at one vantage** |
| **A deficiency** | A deficiency is a shortfall against a stated requirement. This assessment states no requirement and tests nothing against one |
| **A violation** | **No document in this repository reaches a legal conclusion of any kind** |
| **An inspectional observation** | The 483 carries seven observations in its own numbering and this programme never renumbers, regroups or extends them — `ADR-0006`. **No `DR` identifier is an observation** |
| **A deviation** | A deviation is raised in Helix's quality system, has an owner, an investigation and a closure. **Nothing in this phase raises one** |
| **A CAPA, an action or a commitment** | Nothing in either register obliges anybody to do anything, and **that is itself the phase's open question** — `IS-21` |

**And a determination of *Not met* is not a finding either.** It records that an attribute does not hold. It
does not record that a record is wrong, that a control failed, or that anybody did anything improper.
**An open gap is Helix's own assessment of its own position. It is not a violation, not a breach, not a
non-compliance, not a finding and not an inspectional observation.**

## 4. Two things the phase must never be read as saying

**First: ALCOA does not map onto `21 CFR 11.10`.**

**FDA's own footnote 5 maps the five attributes to the CGMP predicate rules and cites 21 CFR Part 11
nowhere.** The industry crosswalk that pairs each attribute with a paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10(a)` to `(k)`
is an invention that FDA's own footnote refutes — `../03.04-footnote-five.md`, `ADR-0019`, `DEC-305`.

**Where an attribute and a Part 11 paragraph both bear on the same record, they are two assessments of the
same record.** This phase performed the first. **Phase 05 performs the second, and saying so is what stops
Phase 05 being skipped by somebody who reads this register and concludes the work is done.**

**Second: nothing here is a Part 11 certification.**

**There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate, and no FDA-approved, FDA-validated or
FDA-certified software.** FDA does not certify electronic systems and does not pre-evaluate them for Part 11
compliance. A product has features that support compliance; compliance is a property of Helix's validated
system in its intended use. **No row in either register is, resembles or could become one**, and `PR-07`
carries the standing risk that a vendor document is read as though it were.

## 5. `IS-01` stays open, and stays unweighted

**Nothing in Phase 03 bears on `IS-01`.**

`IS-01` records that four internal audits between 2023-06 and 2025-09 covered all three systems between them,
each falling inside the scope of at least two, and that none raised a finding on the subject matter of
`OBS-2`, `OBS-4` or `OBS-6`. **Why is not established.** `01.09 §6` recorded four candidate explanations and
chose none of them; `ADR-0007` published the history in full; `ADR-0012` and `02.11` established the premise
of one candidate and refused to promote it.

**Phase 03 has produced nothing that discriminates between the four.** It assessed records, not audit
programmes. It examined no audit scope statement, no checklist, no test design and no working paper, and it
reconstructed nobody's technical access at any historical date. **The four candidates stand exactly where
Phase 02 left them, in the order `01.09 §6` set out, with no ranking, no probability, no confidence level,
no leading candidate and no ordering.** `EV-333` is the record of that position at the `MS-07` gate.

**The temptation available in this phase is specific and is named so that it can be refused.** This phase
found that "original or a true copy" fails or cannot be determined on 75 of 121 pairs, that three of the
record types on which it cannot be determined are chromatography data system record types, and that the two
pairs on which nothing at all could be determined are on the two chromatography data systems. **A sentence
joining any of that to why four internal audits raised nothing is one line long, costs nothing to write, and
would be a conclusion this phase has no evidence for.**

> **Establishing a candidate explanation's premise does not promote the candidate to the answer** —
> `ADR-0012`. **Nor does producing a finding in the same subject area.** A finding about records is not a
> finding about audit programmes, and the step between them is the step nobody has taken.

**What would be needed before `IS-01` can be answered**, unchanged from `GOV-10 §4`: the determination, which
exists; an assessment of what the audit trails on the three systems record, which is **Phase 05** with the
chromatography data system in **Phase 06**; and an examination of the four audit programmes against both,
which is **Phase 08**. **Two of the three still do not exist at this vantage.**

**`IS-01` therefore stays open and stays unweighted** — in this chapter, in the registers, in the decision
log, in any diagram and in any later phase, until the evidence that would discriminate between the four
exists. **No document in this phase may be cited in support of any of the four, and if one is, it is wrong
and this sentence is why.**

## 6. What the registers do not cover at all

**Two absences are absences of scope rather than of conclusion, and both are published rather than left to
be discovered.**

**The transfer of a record between systems is assessed nowhere.** **31 of the 34 in-scope record types are
held on more than one system.** The pair assesses the record on each system and assesses the movement
between them on none of them — **`IS-18`**, and `../03.11-the-flow-the-unit-does-not-see.md` owns the
argument, including the tension with PIC/S PI 041-1 **§5.5.4**: *"Risk assessments should focus on a
business process (e.g. production, QC), evaluate data flows and the methods of generating and processing
data, and not just consider information technology (IT) system functionality or complexity."* That is good
practice rather than a requirement, from a body that does not regulate Helix — and **its final clause names
the frame `21 CFR 211.68(b)` uses and this phase is anchored on**, which is why the tension is stated as a
choice rather than smoothed away. `../03.11-the-flow-the-unit-does-not-see.md §2` carries that argument and
`§6` carries the `shall` the gap also sits on.

**The 35 GxP-relevant systems that hold no Part 11 record are not in this assessment.** They are outside
the 121 because the unit is a record-type/system pair and they hold no in-scope record type. **Many of them
are reached by `21 CFR 211.68`**, which applies to computers and related systems used in the manufacture,
processing, packing and holding of a drug product whatever the Part 11 answer is; **others are reached by
different provisions of Part 211 according to what they do, and this phase has not determined which
provision reaches which system.** `AS-26` carries the assumption. **"Not in the assessment" does not mean
"assessed and satisfactory"**, and `AS-26` also carries the assumption that no later phase reads it that way.

## 7. What a reader may legitimately take from the phase

Stated positively, once, so that the chapter is not only a list of refusals.

- **That an assessment was performed across the whole population**, pair by pair and attribute by attribute,
  with a named determiner, a date, a quality unit reviewer and the evidence examined on every determination
  — `EV-314`, `EV-315`.
- **That 47 pairs could not be assessed on at least one attribute, that this was recorded rather than
  scored, and that every one of them carries what would be needed to determine it** — `EV-316`.
- **That the method, the instrument and the scale were fixed and approved before any determination was
  made**, and that their dates prove it — `EV-303`, `EV-308`, `EV-311`, `EV-312`, `EV-313`.
- **That the phase's own expectation was recorded before the work and was wrong**, and that the record of
  both halves was kept — `EV-325`.

**None of those is a statement about whether Helix's records can be relied on.** They are statements about
whether Helix's assessment can be.

## 8. What the refusals cost, and why they were paid

**Refusals are cheap to write and expensive to keep**, so the cost is recorded here rather than left
implicit.

**The register cannot be ranked end to end.** Forty-seven pairs carry no rating and cannot be ordered
against the 74. Anybody wanting a single ranked list of the estate's data integrity position will not get
one from this phase, and the reason is that no such list exists to be given.

**The phase produces no priority order and no plan.** It says where the data is weak and stops. Somebody
will reasonably ask what Helix is going to do, and the honest answer at this vantage is that nothing in
Helix's quality system yet requires it to do anything — **`IS-21`**, which is Phase 04's and is the largest
thing this phase hands on.

**Every later phase inherits the vocabulary discipline.** `OBS-n`, `DEV-nnn`, *finding*, `DR-nn` and `ND-nn`
are five separate things, and keeping them separate is work that has to be done again in every document
that cites any of them. **A programme that lets them blur has started keeping two sets of books.**

**And internal audit owns this chapter while owning `IS-01`**, which means the function that declines to
answer a question about its own predecessors' work also states the limits of the phase that might have been
read as answering it. **That is uncomfortable by design.** `ADR-0004` keeps the party that designs
remediation, the party that executes it and the party that audits it apart, and it is why this chapter is
not owned by the people who produced the numbers.

## 9. The standing framing this phase keeps

- **Part 11 applies to records, not to systems**, and no document here says "a Part 11 system" without
  saying which records make it one — `ADR-0001`.
- **Enforcement discretion is FDA's stated intention. It is not a legal exemption and not a safe harbour.**
  `21 CFR 211.68` requires appropriate controls over Helix's computerised systems whatever FDA does about
  Part 11 — `ADR-0003`.
- **FDA's Computer Software Assurance guidance is device guidance and does not apply to Helix.** Adopted
  voluntarily and labelled as adopted at every citation — `ADR-0005`.
- **GAMP 5 is ISPE good-practice guidance, never a requirement**, and no software-category count appears
  anywhere in this repository.
- **FDA uses plain ALCOA. The four additional attributes are other bodies' formulations, set out with
  definitions by MHRA (March 2018, §6.1), PIC/S PI 041-1 (2021) and WHO TRS 1033 Annex 4 (2021)**; MHRA
  itself refers to the shorter acronym, states there is no difference in expectations regardless of which is
  used, and defines all four. **No originator is named for either set.**
- **MHRA's and PIC/S's risk formulations are adopted voluntarily and labelled as adopted at every citation.**
  Neither body regulates Helix — `DEC-309`, `PR-09`.
- **A Form FDA 483 is not a final agency determination**, a Warning Letter is informal and advisory, and only
  a court order binds. **No Warning Letter, Untitled Letter or judicial action of any kind exists.**
- **This programme does not forecast the outcome of any future FDA inspection**, and no document in this
  phase contains such a forecast — not as an objective, not as a success criterion, not as a milestone and
  not in a steering committee paper.
- **All content about Helix is fictional and illustrative.**
