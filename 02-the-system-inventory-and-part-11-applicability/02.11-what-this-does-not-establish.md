## 1. One idea

Phase 02 has established a fact that one of Phase 01's four candidate explanations depended on.

**It has not established the explanation.**

That is the whole of this chapter. Everything below is the argument for it, the consequence of it, and the
mechanism that stops it being quietly abandoned later.

## 2. The position as Phase 01 left it

`01.09-the-four-audits-that-found-nothing.md` published the internal audit history in full and recorded
**four candidate explanations** for why four internal audits between 2023-06 and 2025-09, which between
them covered the chromatography data system, the packaging line systems and the electronic batch record,
raised nothing on the subject matter of `OBS-2`, `OBS-4` or `OBS-6`. It set them out in no order of
likelihood and **chose none of them**. `IS-01` carries the question and is open by design.

The four, named only — the arguments belong to `01.09` and are not restated here:

| # | Candidate, in a phrase |
|---|---|
| One | An audit programme scoped to procedures rather than to records |
| Two | A checklist derived from a prior inspection |
| Three | Auditors without the technical access to read an audit trail |
| **Four** | **A company that had never written down which records were Part 11 records** |

**`01.09 §7` gave a reason for refusing to choose, and the second of its three reasons is the one Phase 02
has now moved.** It was that the determination which would have anchored the audits did not exist, so no
candidate could be tested against anything. Phase 02 has produced the determination and, at
`02.10-nobody-ever-wrote-it-down.md`, has established by search that none existed before it.

**Candidate four's premise is now a fact rather than an assumption.** That is a real change and it is the
only one.

## 3. Why a true premise does not make a true explanation

Candidate four has two parts and they are not the same claim.

> **Premise.** Nobody had written down which records were Part 11 records.
>
> **Explanation.** *That absence is why* the four audits found what they found.

**Phase 02 has established the premise. It has established nothing about the explanation.**

The gap between them is not a technicality. An explanation asserts that one thing accounts for another,
and accounting for something requires more than being true at the same time as it. To move from premise to
explanation, somebody would have to establish at least three further things, and Phase 02 has established
none of them:

**First, that the absence operated on these audits.** The determination's absence is a fact about the
company across the whole period. It was equally absent during audits that raised fourteen findings between
them, and equally absent in areas the audits did examine closely. A condition that holds everywhere cannot
by itself explain a difference between one place and another. Establishing that it operated **here**
requires evidence about how these audit programmes were built, and that evidence is in working papers this
phase has not examined for that purpose.

**Second, that a determination would have changed what the audits did.** This is a counterfactual, and it
is the load-bearing one. It asserts that had a determination existed, the audit scopes, checklists and
tests would have been different in a way that would have reached the subject matter of the three
observations. Nobody has tested it. It is plausible; plausible is not established. An audit programme can
be pointed at a determination and still test the presence of a signature rather than what the signer
examined.

**Third, that the other three candidates do not account for the same thing at least as well.** Two
explanations that both fit the evidence do not become one explanation because one of them acquired a
verified premise. **Candidate four's premise is now a fact rather than an assumption. The candidate itself
is exactly where it was on 2026-05-29**, because nothing that would discriminate between the four has been
produced, and the comparison that would select any of them has not been performed.

**The temptation is specific and worth naming.** Phase 02 spent two months establishing something, and it
happens to be the thing candidate four rests on. The natural next sentence — *and so that is why* — costs
nothing to write, closes an uncomfortable question, and would give the remediation a shape decided by the
phase that happened to run first. `01.09 §7` called that choosing for comfort. Producing evidence for a
candidate's premise makes the comfort more available, not less.

## 4. The other three are untested, not eliminated

Nothing in Phase 02 bears on candidates one, two or three. **They have not been weakened, and they have
not been examined.** The distinction between *untested* and *eliminated* is the distinction between a
question that is still open and a question somebody has closed without saying so.

| Candidate | Position after Phase 02 | What would test it |
|---|---|---|
| One — scoped to procedures rather than to records | **Untested.** Phase 02 examined no audit scope statement, checklist or test design | Reading the four audit programmes against the procedure set in force at each date, and asking whether an audit conforming to those procedures could have reached the subject matter of the three observations |
| Two — a checklist derived from a prior inspection | **Untested.** Phase 02 traced no checklist to its source | Tracing each checklist line to its origin, and comparing the coverage it produced with the coverage the current estate would require |
| Three — auditors without the technical access to read an audit trail | **Untested.** Phase 02 gathered access administration evidence for three vendor-hosted systems for a different purpose entirely — `02.07-open-and-closed.md` — and gathered none for the audit function at any historical date | Reconstructing what read access the audit function held on each system at each audit date, from access administration records, and establishing what an audit trail on those systems would have shown |
| **Four — no written determination** | **Premise established. Candidate untested** | The three things at §3, and the comparison against the other three |

**They are also not mutually exclusive**, as `01.09 §6` recorded. All four can be true at once, in
different proportions for different audits. **Phase 02 has no basis for stating a proportion and states
none.**

## 5. `IS-01` stays open, and stays unweighted

**Open** is straightforward: the question has not been answered and the issue is not closed.

**Unweighted** is the harder commitment and it is stated because it is the one that erodes. It means that
`IS-01` carries **no ranking, no probability, no confidence level, no "leading candidate", no "most
likely", and no ordering of the four**, in this chapter, in the register, in any diagram, in any decision
record or in any later phase before the evidence exists. It also means that no other document may cite
Phase 02 as support for one of the four — and if one does, it is wrong and this sentence is why.

**The prohibition is not stylistic.** A weighted candidate becomes a working assumption within about a
quarter. A working assumption becomes the premise of the remediation design, and the remediation design
becomes the thing the third-party audit tests against. By that point nobody re-opens the question, because
the answer is load-bearing. **The cheapest moment to refuse to weight it is now.**

**`IS-01` also carries no score of any kind**, in common with every entry in every register in this
repository. That rule is `01.12 §4`'s and it holds here for the ordinary reason: nothing in this phase is
scored, and the data integrity risk assessment is Phase 03's.

## 6. What would settle it, and who could do it

The programme has committed to answering the question rather than to leaving it. Three things have to
exist before it can be answered, and two of them do not exist yet.

| # | What is needed | Exists? | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A written determination of which records are Part 11 records, so that an audit programme can be assessed against a scope that can be stated | **Yes, from 2026-07-24** | `02.05-the-fifty-eight-record-types.md`, `GOV-09` |
| 2 | An assessment of what the audit trails on the three systems actually record — whether they record what `21 CFR 11.10(e)` describes — so that "what there was to find" is a known quantity rather than an assumption | **No** | **Phase 05**, with the chromatography data system in **Phase 06** |
| 3 | An examination of the four audit programmes, scopes, checklists, test designs and access position against 1 and 2 | **No** | **Phase 08** |

**Who could do it.** The examination at row 3 is internal audit's own history, and the function that owns
`IS-01` is the function whose work is in question. That is not a reason to move it — internal audit
reports to the Board's audit committee and not to Quality or to IT, and it published the history in the
first place at `ADR-0007`. It is a reason to say plainly how the independence is held:

- **Internal audit owns `IS-01`** and reports on it to the Board audit committee, as it has since
  2026-05-19 at `GOV-05`.
- **The independent third-party auditor, Vashti Okonjo & Partners, may examine the same question**, and
  is engaged on terms that exclude design, drafting and implementation of anything at Helix — `ADR-0004`,
  `EV-119`, `EV-120`. It has designed nothing it would be assessing.
- **The advisory that designs remediation may not audit it** — `ADR-0004` again. Thorne Delacroix
  Consulting, LLC is outside this question entirely.

**Phase 08 is where `IS-01` is returned to**, when the periodic review, the internal audit of the
remediated programme and the 483 response commitments come together and rows 2 and 3 exist. That was the
commitment at `01.09 §7` and Phase 02 has not varied it. **`IS-01` stays open across six phases, with an
owner, a date and a reason.**

## 7. `ADR-0012`

**`DEC-217`, 2026-07-30, Gideon Halvorsen.** Recorded at
`governance/GOV-10-steering-committee-minute-2026-07-30.md`.

**Decision.** Establishing that a candidate explanation's premise is true does not promote the candidate to
the answer. Where a phase produces evidence bearing on one of a set of recorded candidate explanations, it
records what it has established, records what it has not, and leaves the set unweighted until the evidence
that would discriminate between the candidates exists.

**Refused:** recording candidate four as the explanation, or as the likely explanation, or as the
programme's working hypothesis. **Reason:** Phase 02 established a premise and no more, and a repository
that promotes on a premise has stopped distinguishing evidence from convenience.

**Refused equally:** removing the four candidates from the record on the ground that the question cannot
yet be answered. **Reason:** the candidates are the shape of the question. Deleting them would make
`IS-01` an issue with no content, and an issue with no content quietly closes.

**Cost accepted.** The repository carries an unresolved question about its own assurance function across
six phases, and every phase in between has to decline to answer it again. That is repetitive, it reads as
indecision to anybody who has not read this chapter, and it is the price of an answer that will survive
being tested.

## 8. What this chapter does not say

- **It does not say candidate four is wrong.** It says it is unproven. Those are different, and the
  chapter would be just as wrong to reject the candidate as to select it.
- **It does not say the audits were inadequate**, and it does not say they were adequate. `01.09` said
  what they covered and what they raised, and this chapter adds nothing to that.
- **It names no individual** and characterises no Helix employee, no auditor and no FDA investigator. No
  statement here is about anybody's work.
- **It reaches no legal conclusion**, does not characterise the inspection or its observations as a
  violation of anything, and takes `OBS-2`, `OBS-4` and `OBS-6` as recorded rather than adjudicated. A
  Form FDA 483 is not a final agency determination.
- **It does not renumber or restate the observations.** `OBS-n` is the 483's own numbering — `ADR-0006`.
- **It scores nothing and weights nothing.**
- **It forecasts nothing** about Phase 08's conclusion, about any future audit or about any future
  inspection.
