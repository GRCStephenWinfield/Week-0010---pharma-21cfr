# ADR-0012 — Establishing a candidate explanation's premise does not promote the candidate

## Context

`01.09` published Helix's internal audit history in full and recorded that **four internal audits between
2023-06 and 2025-09 covered all three systems between them — each system falling inside the scope of at
least two — and none raised a finding on the subject matter of `OBS-2`, `OBS-4` or `OBS-6`.** It set out
**four candidate explanations** and chose none of them. `IS-01` carries the question, and Phase 08 is
where it is returned to.

**The fourth candidate was that Helix had never written down which of its records were Part 11 records.**

**Phase 02 has established that premise as fact.** There was no Part 11 applicability determination
anywhere in Helix's quality system — not in a validation plan, not in the system inventory, not in a
policy, not in an SOP — and no procedure required one to exist.
`../02.10-nobody-ever-wrote-it-down.md` sets out where the programme looked and what it found, and
`IS-02` closes there.

**That creates a pressure, and the pressure is the reason this record exists.** A programme that has just
proved the premise of one of four candidate explanations is a programme with an available answer, a
plausible narrative and an audience that would accept it. Nothing in the phase's own findings resists
that. The resistance has to be a decision.

## Decision

**Establishing that a determination did not exist is not the same as establishing that its absence is why
four internal audits found what they found. Phase 02 records the premise as fact and does not promote the
candidate to the answer.**

Four rules follow, and they bind every document in this phase and every later phase until `IS-01` is
answered on evidence.

**`IS-01` stays open and stays unweighted.** No document ranks the four candidates, assigns any of them a
likelihood, describes one as the most probable, or writes the word *root cause* about any of them.

**The other three candidates are untested, not eliminated.** An audit programme scoped to procedures
rather than to records; a checklist derived from a prior inspection; auditors without the technical access
to read an audit trail. Phase 02 tested none of them and disproved none of them.

**The candidates are not mutually exclusive**, as `01.09 §6` records, and establishing one premise does
not reduce the standing of the rest.

**No corrective or preventive action in this programme is justified on the fourth candidate.** Work done
because the determination did not exist is justified by the fact that it did not exist — an obligation
under `21 CFR 211.68` and a scope question under Part 11 — and never by a claim about why the audits found
what they found.

**`../02.11-what-this-does-not-establish.md` owns the argument.** This record fixes the decision, and no
other chapter in the phase argues it.

## Alternatives considered and refused

**State that the missing determination is why the audits raised nothing.** Refused. It does not follow. An
audit can fail to raise a finding for reasons that have nothing to do with the availability of a record
classification, and the absence of the determination is consistent with the audits having raised nothing
**and** with a dozen other things being true at the same time. **Consistency is not causation**, and the
premise being true tells you nothing about the strength of the link between it and the outcome.

**State it as the *most likely* explanation.** Refused, and refused specifically because it is the version
that would have passed unnoticed. A likelihood assigned without evidence is a guess wearing the grammar of
an analysis. **No entry in this phase carries a likelihood, an impact or a score** — the data integrity
risk assessment is Phase 03's and this is not one of its inputs.

**Close `IS-01` on the strength of Phase 02's finding.** Refused. `IS-01` asks why four audits raised
nothing on the subject matter of three inspectional observations. Phase 02 has not examined an audit
trail, has not assessed a single system against `21 CFR 11.10`, and cannot yet say what there was to find.
Closing the issue would close the question, and the question is the most valuable thing Phase 01 produced.

**Eliminate the other three candidates because the fourth is now supported.** Refused. Nothing in Phase 02
tested them. The third — auditors without technical access — is testable and was not tested here; the
working papers record no request for system read access, and whether such access was available is not
established from them.

**Say nothing, and let the reader draw the inference.** Refused, and it is the most tempting of the five.
Publishing the premise beside the candidate and leaving the space between them empty is an argument made
by layout. If the programme is not going to promote the candidate, it says so in words.

## Consequences

**Accepted — the phase ends with its most quotable conclusion unavailable.** "We now know why the audits
missed it" is the sentence this phase will not write.

**Accepted — `IS-01` stays open across six phases**, with an owner, a date and a reason, exactly as
`01.09 §7` committed. It is revisited in **Phase 08**, when the periodic review, the internal audit of the
remediated programme and the 483 response commitments come together and the evidence to answer it exists.

**Accepted — the discipline has to be actively held.** Every later phase that touches the audit history
inherits this decision, and the temptation returns each time the premise is restated.

**Accepted — what would settle it is written down.** `../02.11-what-this-does-not-establish.md` names the
evidence that would allow the question to be answered and who could produce it, so that the refusal is a
deferral to evidence rather than a permanent silence.

**Rejected as a consequence — no adverse characterisation of anybody.** This record says nothing about
any auditor, any audit, or any individual's work. `ADR-0007` was taken in Phase 01 on the express
condition that publishing the audit history would not become a judgement about the people who performed
it, and that condition holds here.

## Status

**Accepted.** Taken on 2026-07-30 and minuted the same day at `GOV-10`, the Data Integrity Steering
Committee sitting that recorded the closure of `IS-02` and the continuation of `IS-01`.

**`IS-01` is open, unweighted, and owned by the Head of Internal Audit.** This record states no cause,
ranks no candidate, and reaches no legal conclusion of any kind. **A Form FDA 483 is not a legal finding**,
and nothing here characterises the Ridgemont inspection as a violation of anything.
