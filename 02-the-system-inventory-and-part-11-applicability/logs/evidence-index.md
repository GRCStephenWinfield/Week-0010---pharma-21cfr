## 0. What is in this index and what is not

**Every item below exists at the vantage.** The index records what an inventory-and-determination phase
produces: walk records, reconciliation working papers, determination forms, test sheets, registers,
governance records and minutes.

**It contains no risk assessment, no ALCOA assessment and no score of any kind**, because none exists —
Phase 03, `MS-07`. **No validation plan, protocol, summary report or validation status.** **No
configuration record, audit trail export, access review or control test result**, because Phase 02
configured, examined and tested nothing. **No periodic review, no internal audit of the remediated
programme, no third-party audit report, and no inspection record after 2026-02-13.**

**Nothing in this index is a certification, an approval or an attestation of compliance by any agency.**
**There is no Part 11 certification**, and no artefact here could be one.

**On the numbering, and on a statement in Phase 01 that this supersedes.** Phase 01's index ran `EV-101`
to `EV-128` and its maintenance table stated that the series *continues into Phase 02 at `EV-129`*. **That
statement is superseded, and as published it is wrong.** The series is re-aligned so that an item's number
carries its phase block — `EV-2nn` for Phase 02, matching document IDs `HLX-DI-2026-2NN`. `EV-129` to
`EV-200` are permanently unused and are never issued. The change was taken at `DEC-201` and is recorded
here rather than left to be discovered. **No identifier already issued changes meaning:** `EV-122` is the
same artefact it was, and **`EV-215` means the same artefact in Phase 08 as it does here.**

**Retention.** Each item is retained for at least the life of the obligation, determination or commitment
it supports. For the determination at `EV-227` and the two registers at `EV-220` and `EV-221`, that is for
as long as the determination is in force and for the retention period of the records it describes.

## 2. What the index shows about the phase

| Class | Items | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| The walk | `EV-202` to `EV-207`, `EV-224`, `EV-225` | Eight items. **The walk is the only part of this phase that could not have been done from a desk**, and it is the part that produced the corrections |
| The determination | `EV-208`, `EV-210` to `EV-213`, `EV-220`, `EV-222`, `EV-227` | Eight items, and `EV-222` is the one that matters: **fifty-eight forms, each with a determiner and a date.** A determination without a determiner is an opinion |
| **The paper-printout test** | **`EV-214`, `EV-215`, `EV-223`** | **Three items, and they are the evidence that a test was applied rather than a result announced.** Eleven sheets, four capability statements |
| Open and closed | `EV-217`, `EV-218` | Two items. The access administration evidence is what makes `21 CFR 11.3(b)(4)` answerable |
| **The search** | **`EV-226`** | **One item, and it is a record of finding nothing.** A negative finding with no search record behind it is an assertion; this is why `IS-02` could close |
| Governance and registers | `EV-201`, `EV-209`, `EV-216`, `EV-219`, `EV-221`, `EV-228` to `EV-232` | Ten items. **8 + 8 + 3 + 2 + 1 + 10 = 32** |

**`EV-215` and `EV-226` are the two an auditor would ask for first**, and both are records of tests whose
outcome was inconvenient. The first found seven record types in scope that Helix had treated as out of it.
The second found nothing at all, ten times over, and that is what it was for.

**`EV-228` is indexed with no entries in it.** A trigger record opened and empty is evidence that the
mechanism exists; it would be evidence of nothing if it were indexed only once something had fired.

## 3. Rules the index keeps

| Rule | Statement |
|---|---|
| **It records what exists** | An artefact that has not been produced has no `EV` number. The index is not a plan |
| **One artefact, one identifier** | An artefact is indexed once. Later phases cite the same number rather than re-issuing it |
| **Named owners** | Every item has a named individual owner — `ADR-0008` |
| **No result in a template** | The four templates in `templates/` are empty forms and carry no `EV` number, because a blank form is not evidence. `EV-222` and `EV-228` are **completed** or **opened** instances, which is a different thing |
| **No score** | No item records a risk, a likelihood, an impact, a rating or a criticality |
| **No validation status** | No item states, implies or evidences the validation state of any system |
| **No forecast** | No item supports a statement about what a future inspection or audit will find |
| **No legal conclusion** | No item adjudicates anything or characterises any practice as a violation of anything |
| **Continuity** | Later phases open at `EV-3nn`, `EV-4nn` and so on, one block per phase. **No identifier is reused and none already issued is renumbered** |
