## 1. The enumeration and the split

**Fifty-eight record types were enumerated across the estate. Thirty-four are Part 11 records. Twenty-four
are not. 34 + 24 = 58.**

The enumeration was **driven from the GxP-relevant systems and extended to the record types held elsewhere,
so that every exclusion is published with its reason** — `ADR-0014`. **Forty-one of the fifty-eight are
held on the ninety-six; seventeen are held only on systems determined not to be GxP-relevant.** The
seventeen are enumerated rather than passed over, because a record type nobody listed is a record type
nobody can be shown to have assessed, and a negative determination has to be testable by somebody who did
not make it.

This is the answer to the first half of the question Phase 01 left open, and it is the first time any such
number has existed at Helix. `02.04-the-record-type-as-the-unit-of-determination.md` fixes what a record
type is and why it is the unit; this chapter publishes the list.

Every row below carries the identifier, what the record is, the predicate rule that requires it or the
fact that none does, and the determination. The in-scope groups at §3 to §7 also name the systems that
hold each record type; §8's rows are all out of scope and carry the reason in place of the systems column.
The systems column gives the principal holders in the register at `logs/systems-register.md`; it is not
always exhaustive, and the register is the record.

**Nothing in this chapter is an assessment of any record.** Whether the data in any of these fifty-eight
types is complete, consistent or accurate is not asked here and is not answered anywhere in Phase 02. That
is Phase 03's, at `MS-07`. **Applicability is not integrity.**

## 2. How to read the determination column

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Part 11 record** | Required by a predicate rule, maintained electronically in place of paper or in addition to it and relied on, and therefore within the reach of `21 CFR Part 11` under the narrow interpretation of the August 2003 Scope and Application guidance |
| **Not a Part 11 record — no predicate rule** | No provision of `21 CFR Part 210` or `21 CFR Part 211` requires the record to be maintained or submitted. FDA's guidance is explicit that records not required to be retained under predicate rules, though maintained electronically, are not Part 11 records |
| **Not a Part 11 record — printout route** | The paper record meets **all** the applicable predicate-rule requirements, is **the record relied on**, and the electronic record is **not also relied on**. The three conditions, two stated and one derived, all hold — FDA states the first two expressly and Helix derives the third from the narrow interpretation's second category. `02.06-the-paper-that-was-not-the-record.md` sets out the test, says which condition is whose, and shows why it holds for these four and fails for seven others |

**A negative determination removes nothing but Part 11.** `21 CFR 211.68(b)` requires appropriate controls
over Helix's computerised systems whatever the Part 11 answer is, and no row below is a statement that a
record is unimportant, unregulated or unvalidated.

## 5. Environmental monitoring, utilities and facilities — `RT-25` to `RT-31`

**Four are Part 11 records; three are not. Two of the four are among the seven.**

| ID | What the record is | Predicate rule | Systems that hold it | Determination |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `RT-25` | Environmental monitoring viable-count result record | `21 CFR 211.100(b)` and `21 CFR 211.188(b)(5)`, read with `21 CFR 211.113(a)` | `SYS-037`, `SYS-039`, `SYS-053` | **Part 11 record** |
| `RT-26` | Environmental monitoring viable-count trend record | `21 CFR 211.100(b)` and `21 CFR 211.188(b)(5)`, read with `21 CFR 211.113(a)` | `SYS-037`, `SYS-039`, `SYS-053` | **Part 11 record — moved into scope, `02.06`** |
| `RT-27` | Differential pressure, temperature and humidity trend record for manufacturing, warehouse and controlled-storage areas | `21 CFR 211.100(b)` and `21 CFR 211.188(b)(5)`, read with `21 CFR 211.46(b)` | `SYS-034`, `SYS-035`, `SYS-045`, `SYS-051` to `SYS-053`, `SYS-055`, `SYS-058` | **Part 11 record — moved into scope, `02.06`** |
| `RT-28` | Purified water quality monitoring and release record | `21 CFR 211.84(a)` — water is a component | `SYS-038`, `SYS-054` | **Part 11 record** |
| `RT-29` | Portable temperature calibrator certificate printout | `21 CFR 211.68(a)`, read with `21 CFR 211.160(b)(4)` | `SYS-068` | **Not a Part 11 record — printout route** |
| `RT-30` | Building access badge event record | **None** | Site security systems in the non-GxP class | **Not a Part 11 record — no predicate rule** |
| `RT-31` | Facilities energy consumption report | **None** | Site services systems in the non-GxP class | **Not a Part 11 record — no predicate rule** |

**On `RT-25`, `RT-26` and `RT-27`, and on which rule does which work.** `21 CFR 211.113(a)` requires
**written procedures** designed to prevent objectionable microorganisms in drug products not required to be
sterile, and `21 CFR 211.46(b)` is an equipment-and-facility requirement for ventilation and, where
appropriate, control of air pressure, humidity and temperature. **Neither specifies record content, and
neither is read here as though it did.** They are the substantive conditions these records evidence. The
record obligation runs through `21 CFR 211.100(b)`, which requires production and control activities to be
documented at the time of performance, read with `21 CFR 211.188(b)(5)`, under which the batch record
documents in-process and laboratory control results. **A rule that requires no record cannot be failed on
the content of one**, and `02.06 §6.5` and `§6.6` turn on exactly that.

**On `RT-28`.** Purified water is a component under `21 CFR 211.84(a)`. **Because purified water is
supplied continuously and cannot be withheld pending lot release, assurance is provided by a validated
system with continuous monitoring and periodic testing rather than by lot-by-lot release**, and this record
type is that monitoring and release record.

**On `RT-30`.** Badge events are a security record. No provision of `21 CFR Part 211` requires them to be
maintained. Where a Part 11 record type requires access to be limited to authorised individuals under
`21 CFR 11.10(d)`, the evidence for that control is the access administration record, which is a different
object and is Phase 05's.

## 9. `ADR-0014` — why the twenty-four are published with their reasons

**`DEC-208`, 2026-06-30, Priyanka Venkataraman. `GOV-08`.**

The twenty-four could have been left out of the published enumeration. A determination that lists only
what is in scope is shorter, is easier to approve, and reads as though it were tidier.

It was refused for one reason. **An unexplained exclusion is indistinguishable from an oversight.** A
reader — an auditor, an inspector, a colleague three years from now — who finds a record type absent from
a scope list cannot tell whether it was assessed and excluded or never assessed at all. The two look
identical from outside, and only one of them is a determination. Publishing the exclusion with its reason
is the only thing that tells them apart.

**The cost was recorded and accepted.** Twenty-four rows that assert a negative are twenty-four rows an
auditor may test, and each of them is a place where Helix can be shown to have got it wrong. That is the
point. A negative that nobody can test is not a determination; it is a silence.

**Four of the twenty-four are the harder kind.** `RT-09`, `RT-22`, `RT-23` and `RT-29` are out of scope
because the paper-printout route survives for them on the three conditions, two stated and one derived, and that survival rests on facts about instruments
rather than on the absence of a predicate rule. Those facts can change. `02.06 §7` states them, `AS-14`
records the assumption, `PR-16` records what happens if it stops being true, and `ADR-0015`'s review
trigger is what is supposed to catch it.

## 10. The seven, named here and argued in `02.06`

Helix had treated **eleven** record types as outside Part 11 on the ground that they were printed and the
paper was the record. Applying **the printout route's three conditions — two FDA states expressly and a
third Helix derives from the narrow interpretation's second category** — **seven fail the first**
and are Part 11 records; **four survive** and are genuinely out of scope. **7 + 4 = 11**, and the seven
sit inside the thirty-four.

| ID | Record type | Why it was re-opened |
|---|---|---|
| `RT-13` | Chromatographic integration and reprocessing record | Chromatography data system |
| `RT-14` | Chromatographic detector spectral and peak-purity record | Chromatography data system |
| `RT-15` | Chromatographic calibration curve and result recalculation record | Chromatography data system |
| `RT-16` | Dissolution profile data set | Dissolution workstations |
| `RT-26` | Environmental monitoring viable-count trend record | Environmental monitoring |
| `RT-27` | Differential pressure, temperature and humidity trend record | Environmental monitoring and building management |
| `RT-33` | Warehouse temperature excursion search result | Warehouse monitoring |

**The argument is not made here.** `02.06-the-paper-that-was-not-the-record.md` states the three
conditions, tests each one, and says why these seven fail and the other four hold. This chapter records
the outcome and the identifiers. `IS-08` carries the finding.

## 12. What this chapter does not state

- **It states no risk, criticality, priority or score** for any record type. Phase 03, `MS-07`.
- **It states no ALCOA assessment.** Whether any record is attributable, legible, contemporaneously
  recorded, original or a true copy, or accurate is not asked here.
- **It states no validation status** for any system in the systems column, and none is implied by a
  positive or a negative determination.
- **It states no control.** Which paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10` are met, partly met or not met for any
  record type is Phase 05's, and nothing here previews it.
- **It states no retention period.** `21 CFR 11.10(e)` expresses audit trail retention relative to the
  retention period of the subject record, and that comparison still cannot be made — `IS-05`, `IS-14`.
- **It reaches no legal conclusion** and characterises no past practice as a violation of anything.
