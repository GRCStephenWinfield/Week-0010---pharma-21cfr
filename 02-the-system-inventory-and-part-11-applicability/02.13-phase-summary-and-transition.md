## 6. What Phase 03 must do, and what it must not

Phase 03's slug is `03-data-integrity-risk-assessment-and-the-alcoa-attributes`. `MS-07`, 2026-09-30.

| Phase 03 **must** | Phase 03 **must not** |
|---|---|
| Take the determination as its assessment population — 34 record types across 61 systems, plus the GxP-relevant systems the assessment reaches through them | Re-open the determination. A record type's status changes on `ADR-0015`'s trigger and through change control, not because an assessment would prefer a different scope |
| Assess the integrity of the records: whether they are complete, consistent and accurate, and whether they are attributable, legible, contemporaneously recorded, original or a true copy, and accurate | Describe the plain five-letter acronym as anything other than FDA's, or attribute the four additional attributes to FDA. **They are not FDA's.** FDA uses the plain five, and writes them *"attributable, legible, contemporaneously recorded, original or a true copy, and accurate"* — not "contemporaneous", and not "original" alone. **MHRA's March 2018 guidance states that it *"refers to the acronym ALCOA rather than 'ALCOA +'"* and that *"there is no difference in expectations regardless of which acronym is used"*** — it defines all four of the additional attributes rather than rejecting them, and this portfolio does not say it rejects them. **This portfolio names no originator for either set**, because it has not established one from a primary source. Phase 03 states which body it is quoting, every time |
| Score what it assesses, on a scale it defines and publishes, and keep that scale away from the programme registers | Apply its scale to `PR-11` to `PR-18` or to any programme entry — `01.12 §7`. A scheduling concern does not belong on the same scale as a threat to a batch record |
| State plainly which of the 35 GxP-relevant systems that hold no Part 11 record its assessment nonetheless reaches, and why | Treat "not a Part 11 system" as "not assessed". `21 CFR 211.68` reaches all 96 |
| Use the seven newly in-scope record types as part of the population, and say where they came from | Draw any conclusion about `OBS-2` from the fact that three of the seven are chromatography data system record types. **Phase 06 and Phase 08** |
| Extend `EV`, `DEC`, `IS`, `AS`, `DP` and `PR` rather than restarting them | Publish anything as a Phase 02 outcome. Phase 02 assessed nothing |

## 7. The question Phase 02 leaves open

**The determination exists. It says 34 record types across 61 systems are in scope. Nobody has assessed
whether the data in any of them is any good.**

That is not a criticism of the phase; it is the boundary of what a scope determination can be. Applying
`21 CFR Part 11` to a record type establishes that FDA's criteria for treating an electronic record as
trustworthy and reliable are engaged. It says nothing whatever about whether **this** record, in **this**
system, at Helix, is complete, consistent or accurate. **Applicability is not integrity.** A company can
know exactly which of its records are Part 11 records and still not know whether any of them can be
relied on.

**`IS-11`** carries it out of the phase, owned by Priyanka Venkataraman, raised 2026-07-31, and open.
Phase 03 is where the difference gets measured, and `MS-07` on 2026-09-30 is when.

There is a second thing the phase leaves open and it is smaller and more corrosive. **`IS-10`**: the
determination now exists and nothing in Helix's quality system yet requires it to be maintained. The
absence of a requirement is what made the absence of the determination invisible for the whole of the
period `02.10` searched. Writing the determination fixed the document. It did not fix the requirement, and
Phase 04 is where the procedure that would has to be written.

## 9. What is still not true

Stated as absences, because an absence written down is an absence somebody can act on.

- **There is no data integrity risk assessment and no ALCOA assessment.** Nothing in this phase is scored.
  Phase 03, `MS-07`.
- **Nobody has assessed whether the data in any of the 34 record types is complete, consistent or
  accurate.** `IS-11`.
- **There is no validation framework and no validation status for any system**, including the seventeen
  the walk found. Phase 04, `MS-08`.
- **No control has been implemented, no audit trail has been configured, no audit trail has been examined,
  and no electronic signature has been deployed.** Phase 05, `MS-09`.
- **`21 CFR 11.10` has not been assessed for the three vendor-hosted systems**, and the additional
  measures recorded against `21 CFR 11.30` remain in place — `IS-09`, `02.07`.
- **No procedure in Helix's quality system requires the determination to exist or to be maintained.**
  `IS-10`.
- **No periodic review has been performed**, and no internal or third-party audit of the remediated
  programme has been performed. Phases 08 and 09.
- **There has been no further inspection and there is no inspection outcome of any kind.** The programme
  does not forecast one, at this vantage or any other.
- **The reason four internal audits raised nothing on three observations is not established.** Phase 02
  established a premise of one candidate explanation and did not select it — `ADR-0012`, `02.11`.
- **There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate and no FDA approval or validation of any
  software Helix runs.** None is available to be obtained, and nothing in the systems register is one.
- **No document in this phase reaches a legal conclusion of any kind**, and none characterises the
  inspection, its observations, or any past practice at Helix as a violation of anything.
