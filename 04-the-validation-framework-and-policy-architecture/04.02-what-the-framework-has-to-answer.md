## 1. What this chapter settles

A framework designed from first principles is a framework designed for a company that does not exist. This
one was designed against a specific set of inputs, all of them published, none of them produced by this
phase, and every one of them re-checkable by a reader who opens the register it came from.

**This chapter sets out what Phase 04 was handed, what each input can and cannot be used for, and the
questions the framework therefore has to answer.** It is the bridge between the negative at
`04.01-nothing-requires-a-framework.md` — nothing required this — and the instrument at
`04.05-the-framework.md`.

**Nothing in this chapter is a result of Phase 04.** Every figure below is Phase 02's or Phase 03's, cited
and not re-derived, and where a figure belongs to a later chapter of this phase it is cited to that chapter
rather than anticipated here.

**Seventy-four and forty-seven make one hundred and twenty-one, and the two registers never merge.** That
is `ADR-0018`'s rule and this phase does not touch it: no `ND` row is converted into a `DR` row here, no
pair is re-scored, and nothing in the framework's design is permitted to require a more convenient input
than the one that exists.

## 3. What the two registers may be used for, and what they may not

**They set what evidence the framework requires about the record.** That is the first of the two
graduations at `04.04-two-graduations.md §3`, and it is the whole of what the registers do in this phase.

**They do not set the degree or frequency of input/output verification.** That is graduated by
`21 CFR 211.68(b)` on the complexity and reliability of the computer or related system, and neither axis of
Phase 03's scale is either of those things. The reason this phase has a second graduation at all is that
`DP-15` would otherwise be discharged by a citation that had stopped being true.

**They do not order anything.** A band confers no ordering. **Nothing in Helix's quality system yet says
what any band obliges anybody to do** — that is `IS-21`, and it is the thing this phase exists to close.
**No pair comes to Phase 04 first because it is rated High**, and no chapter of this phase presents a
sequence derived from a band.

**And a framework may not be sized against the seventy-four alone.** Phase 03 recorded that risk at `PR-24`
and stated it in terms: **it receives one hundred and twenty-one pairs, not seventy-four**, and a framework
sized against the numbers it has is a framework that ignores the pairs it cannot see. The way this phase
answers that is `04.04 §7`, and it is not a workaround: it is a recorded consequence, published system by
system, that one of two inputs is missing.

## 4. The sixty-one, and the split that matters to a framework

The framework's unit is **the system**, because a validation plan is written for a system and a validation
package is held against one. Phase 03's unit was **the record-type/system pair**, because the same record
type is not at the same risk on every system that holds it — `ADR-0016`. **Those two units do not line up,
and the framework has to say what happens where they do not.**

Rolling the pairs up onto the systems that hold them produces one division and it is the one that governs
the rest of the phase.

| | Systems | What the framework knows about them |
|---|---|---|
| **Carrying only scored pairs** | **36** | Both graduations exist. The rigour rules at `04.05 §6` operate on two inputs, and where the two point in different directions **the framework requires the higher** — `04.04 §6` |
| **Carrying at least one unscored pair** | **25** | **No established data integrity position.** Rigour is set by complexity and reliability alone until the position is determined — `IS-27`, argued at `04.04 §7` |
| **Total** | **61** | **36 + 25 = 61** |

**A system carrying one unscored pair is in the second group even if every other pair on it is scored**, and
that is deliberate rather than conservative. A data integrity position for a system that is derived from the
pairs Helix could see, on a system where there are pairs Helix could not, is a position built out of the
part of the picture that happened to be visible. **`ADR-0018` refused that inference at the pair level in
Phase 03. Reintroducing it at the system level in Phase 04 would be the same error with an extra step.**

## 5. The two gaps Phase 03 published against its own method

Both were found by Phase 03, about Phase 03, and published in Phase 03. A programme that only records
defects once somebody else finds them has learned nothing from having been audited, and this is the second
consecutive phase to act on that.

### 5.1 `DP-15` — the graduation clause grades on something the scale does not

`21 CFR 211.68(b)` requires the degree and frequency of input/output verification to be based on *"the
complexity and reliability of the computer or related system."* **Phase 03's two axes are what the record
decides and whether an alteration would be noticed. Neither is complexity and neither is reliability.**

The dependency's own instruction is exact: **state how the two axes relate to complexity and reliability,
or assess those separately, before relying on the assessment to size input/output verification.**

**Helix assessed them separately.** `04.04-two-graduations.md` is the answer, `ADR-0025` is the decision,
and **`DP-15` closes** — recorded with its evidence at `04.13-phase-summary-and-transition.md`. This chapter
records only that the framework had to answer it and where the answer is.

### 5.2 `IS-18` — the unit sees the endpoints and not the journey

Thirty-one of the thirty-four in-scope record types are held on more than one system. Phase 03's assessment
looked at the record on each of those systems and at the movement between them nowhere. **The gap sits on a
`shall`:** the sentence immediately before the graduation clause provides that *"Input to and output from
the computer or related system of formulas or other records or data shall be checked for accuracy."*

**`IS-18` does not close in Phase 04 and this phase does not claim that it does.** What Phase 04 does is
narrower and it is worth being precise about, because the temptation to describe it as a closure is real.
The framework graduates the degree and frequency of input/output verification, on the axis
`21 CFR 211.68(b)` names, and requires a validation plan to state what verification a system's inputs and
outputs receive. **Graduating a verification is not the same act as assessing the movement being verified**,
and a framework that required the first and reported the second would be claiming a substitute assessment
Phase 03 expressly declined to claim.

## 6. The questions the framework has to answer

Seven, and the framework is the answer to all seven together rather than to any one of them.

**What evidence does a system have to produce, and how is that decided?** Not *how much validation*, which
is a question with no unit, but which artefacts, for which of `21 CFR 11.10`'s eleven paragraphs, at what
depth. `04.05 §4` and `§6`.

**What sets the degree and frequency of input/output verification?** The regulation answers it; the
framework has to carry the answer without borrowing from the other graduation. `04.04 §3`.

**How are `21 CFR 11.10(a)`'s four clauses specified and evidenced?** Separately, with the fourth stated
explicitly rather than inherited from the first three — `ADR-0024`, argued at `04.03`.

**What happens to a system with no established data integrity position?** Its rigour is set by complexity
and reliability alone, the absence is recorded on its row rather than defaulted, and the rigour is
re-derived when the position arrives. `04.04 §7`, `IS-27`.

**Where does an obligation that the framework generates actually go?** Into a named procedure that requires
the act, or it is recorded as landing nowhere — `ADR-0027`. **An obligation with no home is published, not
absorbed.** `04.06-the-obligations.md` and `04.08-where-the-obligations-land.md` own the results.

**What about a procedure that exists and does not require the act?** It is a distinct class of gap from a
procedure that does not exist, because only the second is visible to the question *is there a procedure?* —
`ADR-0028`, and `04.08-where-the-obligations-land.md` owns it.

**And what does the framework refuse to say?** Whether any paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` is met for any
system. **The framework states what evidence is required and states no control position** — `ADR-0029`,
`04.05 §10`.

## 7. What the framework was not handed, and must not assume

- **No validation status for any system.** Phase 03 stated none and none exists at this vantage. **A rating
  is not a validation status.**
- **No control position.** `IS-09` remains open — `21 CFR 11.10` has never been assessed for the three
  vendor-hosted systems — and `IS-20` was raised expressly so that no later document could read a rating as
  a control position.
- **No retention period for any of the thirty-four in-scope record types** — `IS-14`, `DP-14`, `DP-18`, open
  since 2026-07-24. Part of why *original or a true copy* could not be determined on forty-one pairs is that
  there is no rule for a copy to be tested against, and **this phase sets no retention period**. Phase 07.
- **No audit trail configuration and no review frequency.** Phase 05.
- **No assessment of the thirty-five GxP-relevant systems that hold no Part 11 record.** **Many are reached
  by `21 CFR 211.68`, others by different provisions of Part 211 according to what they do, and no phase has
  yet determined which reaches which** — `AS-26`, and `DP-09`'s second limb, recorded as unmet rather than
  dropped.
- **No answer to `IS-01`.** The reason four internal audits raised nothing on the subject matter of three
  observations is not established, **`IS-01` stays open and unweighted**, and nothing in this phase bears on
  any of its four candidate explanations — `ADR-0012`.
- **And no procedure requiring any of the artefacts already published to exist, be maintained or be acted
  on** — `IS-10`, `IS-19`, `IS-21`. That absence is the subject of this phase rather than a caveat on it.

## 8. What this chapter does not establish

- **It states no result of Phase 04** — no obligation count, no procedure count, no package count, no
  complexity-and-reliability distribution, and no rigour outcome for any named system. Those are
  `04.06-the-obligations.md` to `04.09-the-policy-architecture.md` and `logs/system-rigour-register.md`.
- **It does not describe the framework.** `04.05-the-framework.md`.
- **It does not argue the two graduations.** `04.04-two-graduations.md`.
- **It re-scores nothing and re-opens nothing.** The thirty-four, the sixty-one, the seventy-four and the
  forty-seven are as published.
- **It states no validation outcome and no control position**, sets no retention period, configures no audit
  trail and deploys no signature.
- **It reaches no legal conclusion** and forecasts no inspection outcome.
