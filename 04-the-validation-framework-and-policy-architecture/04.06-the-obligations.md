## 1. What this chapter settles

Phase 03 produced two registers and no instructions. It said, pair by pair and attribute by attribute, what
Helix's records are like, and it closed with the observation that nothing in the quality system said what
Helix was to do about any of it — `IS-21`. **This chapter is the conversion.** It takes the two registers and
the method's own recorded gaps and turns them into thirty-eight acts, each of which is a thing a named
individual must do.

The register is at `logs/obligation-register.md` and it is authoritative. This chapter argues the shape of
it, and the shape has one feature worth the whole chapter.

**Nineteen of the thirty-eight obligations arise from the forty-seven pairs Helix could not assess, and only
fourteen from the seventy-four it could.**

That ratio looks wrong on first reading. It is not wrong, and the rest of this chapter is why.

## 2. What the framework asks of a determination, and why that produces an act

The framework at `04.05-the-framework.md §5` requires evidence about a record before it will accept that a
system holding that record has been validated in respect of it. **It does not ask whether a pair rated High
or Low.** It asks a narrower and more awkward question: what does Helix hold that would let somebody outside
Helix satisfy themselves that the record is what it purports to be?

For a pair on which every attribute was determined, the answer is mostly available. Somebody looked, somebody
recorded what they looked at — `EV-315` — and the gaps are specific: this attribute does not hold, on this
pair, for this reason. **The act the framework requires is bounded by the finding.** Where *attributable* was
recorded Not met on the five laboratory instrument data systems, the act is to record which named individual
held the credential in use for each acquisition — `OB-05`. It is one act, it has an owner, and its completion
is recognisable.

For a pair on which an attribute could not be determined at all, the answer is not available and the act is
different in kind. **The framework cannot require evidence about a record whose condition nobody can
establish; what it can require is the work that would make the condition establishable.** That is a
prior act, it is usually larger, and it is frequently an act nobody has ever performed at Helix — because if
anybody had, the attribute would have been determinable.

**Determining an attribute is work, and the framework has to require that work before anything can be
scored.**

## 3. The three sources, and the count from each

| Source | Obligations | The form the obligation takes |
|---|---|---|
| **The 74 scored pairs** | **14** | An act closing a specific determination of *Not met*, or the evidence set a High-rated pair calls for |
| **The 47 unscored pairs** | **19** | The act that would make a determination possible — the prior work |
| **The method's own recorded gaps** | **5** | `DP-15`, `IS-18`, and the fourth clause of `21 CFR 11.10(a)` |
| **Total** | **38** | **14 + 19 + 5 = 38** |

The 33 that trace to a pair trace to a named `DR` or `ND` identifier, and the register carries the
identifiers rather than a description of them. **A row that cannot be traced back to a row in Phase 03's
registers or to a recorded method gap is not an obligation of this phase**, and there is no such row.

**Each row carries two provisions and they answer two different questions.** The first is the **predicate
rule the obligation serves** — the provision of Part 211 that requires the record the act is performed on,
carried from Phase 02's determination and not re-derived. The second is the **paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10`
for which the obligation produces evidence in the system validation plan** — where the product of the act
lands in the instrument `04.05-the-framework.md §4` requires, at element 7.

**The second column is a fact about Helix's own framework and it is not a crosswalk.** It does not say that
an attribute of FDA's five corresponds to a paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10`; **no such correspondence is
asserted anywhere in this repository and `ADR-0019` forbids one** — FDA's only published mapping of the five
attributes is footnote 5, which cites the predicate rules and cites 21 CFR Part 11 nowhere,
`04.03-the-fourth-clause.md §7` and `04.00-README.md §10`. Where the act produces evidence for no paragraph,
the cell reads *None* and the predicate rule carries the requirement alone.

## 4. Why nineteen against fourteen is the right way round

Take the two populations at their face. Seventy-four pairs were assessed on all five attributes and
ninety-six determinations of *Not met* were recorded across the whole population of 121. Forty-seven pairs
could not be assessed on at least one attribute, and 121 determinations of *Not determinable* sit on them.
**On any reading that treats a rating as a measure of exposure, the 74 are the population with the findings
and the 47 are the population with nothing in it.**

That reading is exactly backwards, and the arithmetic of the obligations is what shows it.

**A determination of *Not met* is a closed question with a bad answer.** Helix knows what the position is,
knows why, and knows what would change it. The work is bounded. Fourteen obligations discharge every one of
the **sixty-three** *Not met* determinations that sit on scored pairs — **the remaining thirty-three sit on
unscored pairs and are reached by the nineteen** — because many of those determinations are
the same defect repeated across instances of one record type, and one act closes the set. `OB-01` alone
reaches every scored row carrying *original or a true copy* Not met, because the missing thing is a single
statement per record type of which instance Helix relies on.

**A determination of *Not determinable* is an open question with no answer at all.** Nothing can be
consolidated, because there is nothing yet to consolidate: each undetermined attribute on each pair needs
somebody to go and establish a fact that does not currently exist anywhere. Nineteen obligations were the
fewest that could be written across the forty-seven without a row becoming so broad that its completion
would be unrecognisable. **Two of the nineteen — `OB-18` and `OB-25` — are obligations to determine
something on which nothing at all could be determined**, and an obligation to determine is not an obligation
to conclude.

The ratio, then, is not a statement that the 47 are worse than the 74. **It is a statement that not knowing
costs more work than knowing badly**, which is a proposition every practitioner would accept in the abstract
and almost no register makes visible.

## 5. Phase 03's refusal to score is what makes this visible

This is the part of the argument that has to be drawn explicitly, because it is the part that would have
disappeared without a trace.

`../03-data-integrity-risk-assessment-and-the-alcoa-attributes/03.08-what-cannot-be-determined.md` records
the two easy answers Phase 03 was offered and refused. The first was to score an undeterminable attribute at
the bottom of the scale, which is the usual practice and is defended as conservative. The second was to score
it at the top, which is what a cautious reviewer proposes instead. Both were refused at `ADR-0018`, and the
chapter states the reason for refusing the first in terms, at `03.08 §2`:

> *"**Scoring an undeterminable attribute low is not cautious. It is an inversion.** Nothing is known;
> therefore nothing adverse is known; therefore the pair looks unremarkable and nobody returns to it."*

The further limb — that scoring it low closes the gap, because a scored pair looks assessed — is
`DEC-310`'s, at `../03-data-integrity-risk-assessment-and-the-alcoa-attributes/logs/decision-log.md`, and is
cited to the decision rather than to the chapter.

**Follow what would have happened in this phase if that refusal had not been made.**

Forty-seven pairs would have arrived here carrying a Low rating. The framework sizes the evidence it requires
against the record's position, and a Low position calls for the least. Those forty-seven pairs would have
generated the smallest evidence requirement in the estate, and — this is the load-bearing consequence —
**they would have generated no obligations at all.** An obligation is written from a determination that
something is not as it should be. A pair whose five attributes have been scored, and which sits at the bottom
of the scale, produces nothing to write an obligation from. The nineteen obligations that dominate this
register exist because forty-seven pairs arrived with no rating and an explicit statement, on every row, of
what would be needed to determine them.

**A programme that had scored the forty-seven low would have generated no obligations from them, would have
sized the least rigour in the estate against them, and would have had nothing in its register to say so.**

**The twenty-five systems those pairs sit on** are, in several cases, the systems this phase ends up sizing
most carefully — the two chromatography data systems, the archives and the monitoring estate. On the scored-low
counterfactual they would have been the systems the framework asked least of, and the register would have
looked complete while it did it.

## 6. What the fourteen do, in one pass

The fourteen from the scored pairs are not the interesting half of the register but they are the half that
can be finished, and they are set out here so that the chapter is not only an argument about the other
nineteen.

| Group | Obligations | What the act is |
|---|---|---|
| **Which instance is the original** | `OB-01` | One statement per record type, plus the method by which a copy is shown to be a true copy. It reaches every scored row carrying *original or a true copy* Not met, which is the single largest group in the scored register |
| **When the entry was captured** | `OB-02`, `OB-03`, `OB-14` | Record the capture time against the time of the act, on the cleaning and use log, the line clearance record and the reserve sample inspection |
| **Who the entry is attributable to** | `OB-04`, `OB-05`, `OB-06`, `OB-07`, `OB-08`, `OB-09`, `OB-10` | Seven acts across the document, laboratory, stability, water, warehouse, calibration and environmental estates. Attribution is the second-largest group in the scored register and the one most widely distributed |
| **Whether the record can be read** | `OB-11` | Demonstrate that the record can be rendered from the store that holds it in complete and readable form, with the context needed to understand it |
| **Whether the record matches what it records** | `OB-12` | Compare the record as held against the event, or against an independent instance |
| **The evidence set for the nine High** | `OB-13` | Specify, in the system validation plan for each system carrying one of Phase 03's nine High-rated pairs, the evidence required for each of `21 CFR 11.10`'s eleven paragraphs `(a)` to `(k)` |

**`OB-13` is the only one of the fourteen that arises from a rating rather than from a determination**, and
it is worth being exact about what it does and does not do. It does not put those systems first. **A band is
a product of two axes and nothing more; it is not a finding, not a deficiency and not an ordering, and no
pair goes to any phase first because it is rated High.** What `OB-13` does is require the evidence set to be
written out in full rather than assumed, on the systems where the consequence of assuming it is largest.

## 7. What the nineteen do, and the shape they take

The nineteen are grouped by the act rather than by the system, because the same missing fact recurs across
systems that have nothing else in common. **Three acts account for most of the population.**

**Say which instance is the original.** `OB-17`, `OB-28`, `OB-32` and `OB-33` between them reach the
chromatography estate, the warehouse monitoring estate, the batch record archive and the analytical archives.
The undetermined attribute in every case is *original or a true copy*, which Phase 03 found to be both the
most-failed and the most-undeterminable of the five — 34 recorded Not met and 41 Not determinable across the
121 pairs, and `IS-15` carries it. **The act is the same act every time and nobody has ever performed it.**

**Say when the entry was captured, against when the thing happened.** `OB-16`, `OB-19`, `OB-22`, `OB-24`,
`OB-26` and `OB-29` reach the cleaning skids, the chromatography detectors, the dissolution workstations, the
whole differential-pressure and trend estate, the microbiology systems and the excursion searches.

**Say who held the credential.** `OB-15`, `OB-23`, `OB-27`, `OB-30` and `OB-31` reach the checkweigher
network, the particle size analyser, the vendor-hosted calibration system, the serialisation repository and
the reserve sample store.

Two more sit outside the three groups. **`OB-18`** requires the five attributes to be determined for the
chromatographic integration and reprocessing record on `SYS-023` and `SYS-024` — `ND-05` and `ND-10`, the two
pairs on which nothing whatever could be determined. **`OB-25`** requires a demonstration that a record held
in the laboratory data archive can be rendered at all.

**Twenty-four of the forty-seven arise from Phase 02's seven newly in-scope record types**, which were
treated as outside Part 11 for the whole of the period in which they were Part 11 records, so nothing was
ever retained about which instance is the original, when capture happens relative to the act, or what the
record is to be compared against — `IS-17`, and
`../03-data-integrity-risk-assessment-and-the-alcoa-attributes/03.09-the-seven-assessed-for-the-first-time.md`
owns them. **That population is where the prior work is concentrated, and it is concentrated there for a
reason that is a fact about scope and not about anybody's practice.**

## 8. The five from the method's own gaps

**Two of Phase 03's most uncomfortable entries were criticisms it made of itself, and both produce work
here.**

**`DP-15`** recorded that `21 CFR 211.68(b)` graduates on *"the complexity and reliability of the computer or
related system"* and that neither axis of Phase 03's scale is complexity and neither is reliability. The
answer is `ADR-0025` and it belongs to `04.04-two-graduations.md §3`; the obligations it produces are
**`OB-34`**, to assess every system on complexity and reliability separately and record the determination
with its basis, and **`OB-35`**, to set the degree and frequency of input/output verification from that band
and from nothing else.

**`IS-18`** recorded that the unit of assessment is IT-shaped and that the transfer of a record between
systems is assessed nowhere, for the 31 of 34 in-scope record types held on more than one system. Its owner
is Joachim Reuter and the register's own wording is that **"The gap is not only a departure from good
practice; it sits on a `shall`."** The `shall` is the second sentence of `21 CFR 211.68(b)`: *"Input to and output from the
computer or related system of formulas or other records or data shall be checked for accuracy."* The
obligation is **`OB-36`**, and its owner is the owner of the issue.

**The fourth clause of `21 CFR 11.10(a)` produces the last two.** `04.03-the-fourth-clause.md §2` owns the
argument and it is cited, not restated. **`OB-37`** requires every system validation plan to specify the
evidence demonstrating the ability to discern invalid or altered records, separately from accuracy,
reliability and consistent intended performance. **`OB-38`** requires the evidence set for all eleven
paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10`, `(a)` to `(k)`, to be recorded, together with a record of where that evidence
does not yet exist.

**`OB-38` is the row that makes `IS-28` unavoidable**, because the moment the evidence set is written down it
becomes possible to observe that none of it has been produced.

## 9. What this chapter does not do

- **It does not state a control position.** No obligation says whether any paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` is met
  for any system, and none may be read as saying so — `ADR-0029`, `IS-20`, and Phase 05 owns it.
- **It does not state a validation outcome.** **No package has been executed, no protocol has been run, and no
  test result exists.** **A requirement is not a control, and an obligation is not evidence.**
- **It does not re-score anything.** Phase 03's registers are an input to this phase. No rating, product,
  likelihood, consequence or determination moves because an obligation was written from it, and none can.
- **It does not order the thirty-eight.** No obligation is first because the pair behind it rates High.
- **It does not say the 47 are worse than the 74**, or better, or comparable. **They are not on the scale.**
  What it says is that they cost more work, which is a different claim about a different thing.
- **It does not resolve `IS-01`.** Nothing in this chapter bears on any of the four candidate explanations
  recorded at `01.09`, and **`IS-01` stays open and stays unweighted.**
- **It reaches no legal conclusion and forecasts no inspection outcome.**
