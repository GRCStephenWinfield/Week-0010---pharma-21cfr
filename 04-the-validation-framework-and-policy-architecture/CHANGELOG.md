# CHANGELOG — Phase 04: The Validation Framework and Policy Architecture

## 1.0 — 2026-11-30

First issue of the Phase 04 record set, speaking as at the vantage of **2026-11-30**.

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| **Nothing requires a validation framework.** No regulation and no FDA guidance requires a framework, a validation master plan or a validation policy. What is required is written procedures — `21 CFR 211.100(a)`, `21 CFR 211.22(d)` and `21 CFR 211.68(a)`'s written program. **The framework is Helix's instrument and its own first section says so** | `04.01`, ADR-0023, `DEC-403` |
| **`21 CFR 11.10(a)` has four clauses in nineteen words, and the fourth is a different property from the first three** — *"the ability to discern invalid or altered records"*. **It is the property whose absence made *original or a true copy* undeterminable on 41 of Phase 03's pairs**, and the two findings are the same finding seen from two ends | `04.03`, ADR-0024, `DEC-405` |
| **Not one of the 44 validation packages Helix holds contains a requirement stated in the terms of the fourth clause, with a test directed at it and a recorded result.** Some contain audit trail functional testing that could be attributed to it; none attributes it. **17 of the 61 systems hold no package at all** | `04.07`, `IS-22`, `IS-23` |
| **THE ANSWER TO `DP-15` — two graduations, never merged and never averaged.** Data integrity risk sets the depth at which each of `21 CFR 11.10`'s eleven paragraphs is evidenced and removes none of them; system complexity and reliability sets input/output verification under `21 CFR 211.68(b)`. **Where they disagree the higher governs** | `04.04`, ADR-0025, `DEC-408` |
| **And the second use of the complexity-and-reliability band is labelled as Helix's own.** `21 CFR 211.68(b)` authorises the graduation of input/output verification and nothing else under that provision; **Helix's further use of the same band as an input to evidence depth is a method choice, not made on that regulation's authority**, and on the 25 systems with no established data integrity position it is the only input | `04.04 §2`, `IS-27` |
| **The pairs Helix could not assess generate more obligations than the pairs it could — 19 against 14.** Determining an attribute is work, and the framework has to require that work before anything can be scored. **Phase 03's refusal to score is what makes this visible** | `04.06`, `03.08` |
| **A procedure that exists and does not require the act is worse than one that does not exist**, because an auditor who asks *"is there a procedure?"* gets a yes. **12 obligations land in exactly that; 5 land nowhere; 21 land in a procedure that requires the act** | `04.08`, ADR-0028, `DEC-417` |
| **The framework states what evidence is required and states no control position.** Whether any paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` is met, for any system, is Phase 05's | `04.05`, ADR-0029, `DEC-420` |
| **CSA and GAMP are adopted voluntarily and labelled as voluntary at every citation.** CSA is device guidance under `21 CFR Part 820` as amended by the Quality Management System Regulation and does not apply to Helix; **it grades software features, functions and operations rather than systems** | ADR-0026, `DEC-411` |
| **Correction made at source:** `21 CFR 211.22(d)` reaches the quality control unit's own procedures. **Four of the nine SOPs are owned outside it**, and `21 CFR 211.100(b)` and `21 CFR 211.160(a)` are what make those enforceable — which provision bites depends on which procedure it is | `04.11 §4` |
| **Correction made at source:** the obligation register no longer maps an ALCOA attribute onto a paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10`. **The Part 11 column records where evidence lands in the validation plan**, which is a framework fact and not a crosswalk — ADR-0019 | obligation register |
| **Correction made at source:** `IS-10` and `IS-19` close on their own stated conditions, not on `IS-28`'s. **Whether the procedures have ever been applied is `IS-28`'s question** | RAID log |
| The question left open: **the framework says what evidence every system must produce. Nothing has produced any of it yet, and the first paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` a system will be measured against is the one nobody has ever evidenced** | `04.13`, `IS-28`, `DEC-422` |
