## 4. What closes

**`IS-21` closes.** Its text was that the assessment says where the data is weak and nothing in the quality
system says what Helix must do about it — no procedure requiring the assessment to exist, no procedure
requiring a determination of *Not met* or *Not determinable* to produce an action, no threshold at which a
rating obliges anybody to do anything, and no owner for the consequence of a row.

**Closed 2026-11-25, `DEC-420`, taken by Colm Ó Braonáin, ratified at `GOV-20` on 2026-11-25.** Closure
evidence: **`EV-426`** the validation policy · **`EV-427`** the framework · **`EV-428`** the nine SOPs as
approved · **`EV-413`** the obligation register · **`GOV-19`** the approval.

**It is worth being exact about what closed.** The quality system now says what Helix must do: thirty-eight
acts, each with a named individual owner, each traceable to a row in Phase 03's registers or to a recorded
method gap, each landing in a named document or recorded as landing nowhere. **What has not closed, and what
`IS-21` never asked, is whether any of it has been done.** `IS-28` opens in its place.

**`DP-15` closes.** Its text was that neither axis of Phase 03's scale is the axis `21 CFR 211.68(b)` names,
and that Phase 04 must state how the two axes relate to complexity and reliability, or assess those
separately, before relying on the assessment to size input/output verification.

**Closed 2026-10-27, `DEC-408`, taken by Joachim Reuter, ratified at `GOV-18`.** Closure evidence:
**`EV-408`** the two-graduation determination · **`EV-411`** the 61 complexity-and-reliability determination
records · **`EV-412`** the system rigour register · **`ADR-0025`**.

**Helix did not reconcile the two axes. It kept two.** Data integrity risk sets **the depth at which** each
of `21 CFR 11.10`'s eleven paragraphs is evidenced in respect of the record, and **removes no paragraph**;
system complexity and reliability sets the degree and frequency of input/output verification, directly under
`21 CFR 211.68(b)`, and nothing else **under that provision**. **Helix additionally uses the same band, as
its own method choice and not under `21 CFR 211.68(b)`, as one of two inputs to evidence depth**, and on the
25 systems with no established data integrity position it is the only input — `IS-27`. **The regulation
authorises the first use. The second is Helix's, and it is labelled as Helix's wherever it operates.**
**Neither is derived from the other, neither is averaged with the other, and where they disagree the higher
governs.**
`04.04-two-graduations.md §3` owns the argument, and `§6` owns what happens where they disagree.

**`IS-10` and `IS-19` close, and each closes against the condition it set for itself.**

**Closed 2026-11-20, `DEC-418`, ratified at `GOV-19`.** Closure evidence for both: **`EV-428`** the nine SOPs
as approved. `IS-10` asked for a procedure naming the owner, the trigger and the change-control step by which
the applicability determination is maintained, and **`SOP-DI-05` is that procedure**. `IS-19` asked that
something require the scale to be applied, and **`SOP-DI-01` requires the assessment's output as the input to
rigour**.

**Whether either has ever fired or been applied is `IS-28`'s, and it was never these two entries'
condition.** Holding them open against that condition would have held them open against `IS-28`'s, which is
the substitution `04.09-the-policy-architecture.md §6` refuses; it would also have been asymmetric with
`IS-21`, which closed against the same SOP set on the same date.

**Nothing else closes.** `IS-06` is untouched and this chapter does not answer it, including by implication.
`IS-01` stays open and stays unweighted. `IS-04` and `IS-07` remain deliberately unresolved and this phase
resolved neither, including by implication.

## 7. The question Phase 04 leaves open

**The framework says what evidence every system must produce. Nothing has produced any of it yet, and the
first paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` a system will be measured against is the one nobody has ever evidenced.**

Take the three facts in order. The framework requires a system validation plan for each of the 61 systems and
**not one has been written**. Every plan must specify the evidence for each of the eleven paragraphs of
`21 CFR 11.10`, and the first of those is `(a)`, whose fourth clause requires the ability to discern invalid
or altered records. **Helix holds forty-four validation packages and not one of them contains a requirement
stated in the terms of the fourth clause, with a test directed at it and a recorded result.** Some contain
audit trail functional testing that could be attributed to the fourth clause; **none attributes it**, and an
evidence set in which no item is attributed to the clause cannot be reported as addressing it — `ADR-0024`.
**Whether such an item, once attributed, satisfies the clause is Phase 05's.**

So the first evidence any Helix system will be asked for, under an instrument Helix wrote for itself, is
evidence of a property no package in the estate has ever been asked to demonstrate in the clause's own
terms, on systems that in seventeen cases hold no package at all. **The framework did not create that position. It made it legible**, which is
the most a framework can do and is less than a reader wanting progress will want it to be.

**`IS-28`** carries it, owned by Colm Ó Braonáin, raised 2026-11-30, open. **Phase 05 owns the first half of
the answer**, because the control position is where the evidence question first becomes concrete, and the
plans that would produce the evidence open after this vantage on `DP-05`.

**It is the same shape as the question every previous phase left, and the pattern is now four deep.** `IS-02`
recorded that no written determination existed of which records were Part 11 records. `IS-10` recorded that
the determination existed and nothing required it to be maintained. `IS-21` recorded that the assessment
existed and nothing said what to do about it. **`IS-28` records that the requirement now exists and nothing
has been done under it.** Each phase closed the previous question by writing the document, and each
discovered that the document was upstream of the thing anybody actually wanted. **Writing the requirement
fixes the requirement. It does not fix the evidence.**
