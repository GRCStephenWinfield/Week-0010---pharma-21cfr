## 1. One idea

**Phase 03 produced numbers. Phase 04 produces requirements, and a requirement is read as though it were a
control.**

That is the specific hazard of this phase and it is different from the last one's. A number gets extracted
and put in a slide. **A requirement gets read as a description of the world.** A document saying that every
system validation plan must specify the evidence for each of `21 CFR 11.10`'s eleven paragraphs is one
careless sentence away from being reported as a statement that the evidence exists, and the sentence is
careless in a direction nobody notices, because it reports progress.

This chapter states what the requirements do not mean, in the same phase as the requirements, at the same
version, as a numbered chapter rather than as a caveat at the foot of a register.

It is owned by internal audit, which designed none of the framework, wrote none of the nine procedures, read
none of the forty-four packages and made none of the sixty-one determinations — `ADR-0004`. **That is the
point of who owns it.**

## 2. A requirement is not a control, and an obligation is not evidence

**`ADR-0006` established that this repository keeps one set of books**, and `03.12 §2` added `DR-nn` to
`OBS-n`, `DEV-nnn` and *finding* as a fourth separate word for a separate thing. **`OB-nn` is now a fifth**,
and it is not any of the other four.

| An `OB` row is not | Because |
|---|---|
| **A control** | A control operates. **An obligation requires an act, and not one of the thirty-eight has been performed under the framework at this vantage** |
| **Evidence** | Evidence is a thing somebody can hold and inspect. An obligation is a sentence saying that such a thing must come to exist |
| **A control position** | **No row says whether any paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` is met for any system** — `ADR-0029`, and `IS-20` carries the same refusal forward from Phase 03 |
| **A validation status** | **No system is validated by this phase.** A rigour band says what evidence the framework requires; it says nothing about whether the evidence exists |
| **A finding, a deficiency or a violation** | **An open gap is Helix's own assessment of its own position. It is not a violation, not a breach, not a non-compliance, not a finding and not an inspectional observation** |
| **A deviation or a CAPA** | Nothing in this phase raises one. **No CAPA arising from this programme has been closed** |
| **A regulatory obligation** | `O1` to `O12` are the programme's regulatory obligations, each with an exact citation. **`OB-01` to `OB-38` are acts Helix requires of itself and the two series never merge** |

**And a rigour band is not a rating of a system.** It is the higher of two graduations, one of which grades
the system and one of which grades the record, kept in two columns that are never combined — `ADR-0025`.
**A system in the High band is not a system with a problem, and a system in the Low band is not a system that
is fine.**

## 4. What has not changed, and could not have

**Phase 03's registers are an input to this phase and are never re-scored.**

**121 pairs · 74 scored `DR-01` to `DR-74` · 47 unscored `ND-01` to `ND-47` · High 9, Moderate 31, Low 34.**
Not one rating has moved. Not one product, likelihood or consequence has moved. Not one determination of Met,
Not met or Not determinable has moved. **No `ND` identifier has been converted into a `DR` identifier**, and
none can be: a pair that becomes determinable is re-assessed on `ADR-0022`'s trigger and enters the scored
register as a new entry with a new identifier and a date.

Two temptations were available in this phase and both were refused, so both are named.

**The first was to score the 47 in order to size rigour against them.** The framework needs a position on
every system and twenty-five systems have none. It would have taken one sentence to assign the unscored pairs
a provisional band for framework purposes, and the sentence would have been defensible in a meeting.
**`ADR-0018` would have been reversed by a phase that was not entitled to reverse it**, and the twenty-five
would have acquired a position derived from nothing. What was done instead is `IS-27`: their rigour is set by
complexity and reliability alone, and the register says so on every one of the twenty-five rows.

**The second was to merge the two graduations into one number.** Every reader who has met a risk-based
validation framework expects a single band per system, and Helix's two columns disagree on seventeen of the
thirty-six systems where they can be compared. A mean would have removed the disagreement and produced the
number everybody expects. **`ADR-0025` refuses it — `04.04-two-graduations.md §5` — and the two columns do not even share a vocabulary, so
that the merge is not available to somebody working quickly** — `logs/system-rigour-register.md`.

## 5. `IS-01` stays open, and stays unweighted

**Nothing in Phase 04 bears on `IS-01`.**

`IS-01` records that four internal audits between 2023-06 and 2025-09 covered all three systems between them,
each falling inside the scope of at least two, and that none raised a finding on the subject matter of
`OBS-2`, `OBS-4` or `OBS-6`. **Why is not established.** `01.09 §6` recorded four candidate explanations and
chose none; `ADR-0007` published the history in full; `ADR-0012` and `02.11` established the premise of one
candidate and refused to promote it; `03.12 §5` confirmed that nothing in Phase 03 discriminated between
them.

**The temptation available in this phase is specific, it is sharper than Phase 03's, and it is named here so
that it can be refused.**

`04.08-where-the-obligations-land.md §5` establishes that a procedure that exists and does not require the
act is invisible to the question *is there a procedure?*, and it uses `IA-2025-01/F3` to describe how that
mechanism operated once at Helix: an audit found the register, tested it against the procedure that governed
it, raised what the procedure allowed to be raised, and closed it. **That is a description of a mechanism. It
is one sentence away from being one of `01.09`'s four candidate explanations, promoted.**

The mechanism this phase describes is the premise of one of the four candidate explanations at `01.09 §6`.
**The four are set out there, in the order that chapter set, and this chapter reproduces none of them,
because reproducing one and not the others is a weighting.**

**This phase has produced a general argument that the mechanism exists. It has produced nothing whatever
about whether the mechanism operated on those four audits.** It examined no audit scope statement, no
checklist, no test design and no working paper; it traced no checklist to its source; it reconstructed
nobody's technical access at any historical date. **Establishing that a mechanism is real does not establish
that it was the mechanism**, which is `ADR-0012`'s discipline — *establishing a candidate explanation's
premise does not promote the candidate to the answer* — applied to a third object.

**The other three candidates are untested, not eliminated**, exactly as `02.11` left them. `IS-01` therefore
stays open and stays unweighted: **no ranking, no probability, no confidence level, no leading candidate and
no ordering of the four**, in this chapter, in the registers, in any diagram, in any governance record or in
any later phase. **No document in this phase may be cited in support of any of the four, and if one is, it is
wrong and this sentence is why.** `EV-437` records the position at the `MS-08` gate.

What is still needed is unchanged: an assessment of what the audit trails on the three systems record —
**Phase 05**, with the chromatography data system in **Phase 06** — and an examination of the four audit
programmes against it, **Phase 08**. **Two of the three still do not exist.**

## 6. Two things the phase must never be read as saying

**First: the framework is not required and does not say it is.**

No regulation and no FDA guidance requires a validation framework, a validation master plan or a validation
policy. What is required is written procedures — `21 CFR 211.100(a)`, `21 CFR 211.22(d)`'s *"such written
procedures shall be followed"*, and `21 CFR 211.68(a)`'s written program designed to assure proper
performance. **`ADR-0023` requires the framework document to say so in its own first section**, and
`04.01-nothing-requires-a-framework.md §2` owns the argument.

**The risk-basing of validation extent is guidance and not regulation.** December 2018 Q&A, **Q3**: *"The
extent of validation studies should be commensurate with the risk posed by the automated system."* A
nonbinding *should*. **`21 CFR 211.68(b)`'s `shall` graduates input/output verification and nothing else
under that provision**, and a regulation cited beyond its own subject matter stops being a citation.
**Helix's further use of the same complexity-and-reliability band, as one of two inputs to evidence depth,
is Helix's own method choice and is not made under `21 CFR 211.68(b)`** — `ADR-0025`, and it is labelled as
Helix's wherever it operates.

**CSA and GAMP are adopted voluntarily and are labelled at every citation** — `ADR-0026`, `ADR-0005`. FDA's
Computer Software Assurance guidance is device guidance under 21 CFR Part 820, as amended by the Quality
Management System Regulation, issued by CDRH and CBER, and **it does not apply to Helix**. GAMP 5 Second
Edition is ISPE good-practice guidance and is **never a requirement**; **no software-category count appears
anywhere in this repository.** **Enforcement discretion is FDA's stated intention. It is not a legal
exemption and not a safe harbour, and it confers no rights.**

**Second: nothing here is a Part 11 certification.**

**There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate, and no FDA-approved, FDA-validated or
FDA-certified software.** FDA does not certify electronic systems and does not pre-evaluate them for Part 11
compliance. A product has features that support compliance; compliance is a property of Helix's validated
system in its intended use. **A framework document is not one, a rigour band is not one, and an approved SOP
is not one**, and `PR-07` carries the standing risk that a vendor document is read as though it were.

| Artefact | What it does not authorise |

| **The obligation register** | A statement that any of the thirty-eight has been performed. **Not one has.** Discharge requires the evidence named on the obligation record to exist |
| **The system rigour register** | A statement that any system is validated, controlled or compliant; an ordering of the 61; or the combination of its two grading columns into one |
| **The nine SOPs** | A statement that any of them is being followed. **A procedure approved is not a procedure followed**, and `21 CFR 211.22(d)` makes that the expensive distinction — `04.11-what-a-framework-cannot-fix.md` |
| **The package coverage read** | A statement that any package is inadequate, invalid or withdrawn. **No package is superseded by this phase**, and a package that does not address a clause is not a package that fails it |
| **The complexity-and-reliability determinations** | A statement about the condition of any system. They record configuration, interfaces and failure history. **Under `21 CFR 211.68(b)` they size input/output verification and nothing else**; Helix additionally uses the same band, **as its own method choice and not under that provision**, as one of two inputs to evidence depth, and as the only input on the 25 with no established data integrity position — `ADR-0025`, `IS-27` |
| **The four templates** | Anything at all. **They are empty forms and no template contains a result** |

## 8. What a reader may legitimately take from the phase

Stated positively, once, so that the chapter is not only a list of refusals.

- **That thirty-eight acts now exist, each with a named individual owner and each traceable backwards to a
  row in Phase 03's registers or to a recorded method gap** — `logs/obligation-register.md`.
- **That the seventeen obligations with no home are published rather than absorbed**, and that the twelve and
  the five are kept as two classes because only one of them is visible to the question an auditor asks —
  `ADR-0027`, `ADR-0028`.
- **That the fourth clause of `21 CFR 11.10(a)` is specified explicitly rather than assumed to follow from
  the first three**, on a phase that read forty-four packages and found none addressing it — `ADR-0024`,
  `IS-22`.
- **That two graduations exist and are not merged**, and that where they disagree the higher governs rather
  than a mean — `ADR-0025`.
- **That the twenty-five systems with no established data integrity position are recorded as having none**,
  rather than being given one for the framework's convenience — `IS-27`.

**None of those is a statement about whether Helix's systems can be relied on.** They are statements about
whether Helix's framework can be.

## 9. The standing framing this phase keeps

- **Part 11 applies to records, not to systems**, and no document here says "a Part 11 system" without saying
  which records make it one — `ADR-0001`.
- **A closed system is defined by who controls access** — `21 CFR 11.3(b)(4)`. **Hosting is not the test**, and
  all 61 are closed — `ADR-0011`.
- **`21 CFR 11.3(b)` contains nine definitions and Part 11 contains ten sections.** Citations are exact and
  can be looked up. **Part 11 requires no reason-for-change field, no read-event logging, no cryptographic
  hashing under `21 CFR 11.70`, no multi-factor authentication under `21 CFR 11.200` and no password interval
  under `21 CFR 11.300(b)`.**
- **FDA uses plain ALCOA. The four additional attributes are other bodies' formulations**, and MHRA itself
  refers to the shorter acronym, states there is no difference in expectations regardless of which is used,
  and defines all four. **No originator is named for either set. ALCOA does not map onto `21 CFR 11.10`** —
  FDA's own footnote 5 maps the attributes to the CGMP predicate rules and cites Part 11 nowhere,
  `ADR-0019`.
- **NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 is a control catalogue Helix chose, not an obligation it is under. No FIPS 199
  categorization, no baseline, no ATO and no authorizing official** — Helix is not a federal system.
- **A Form FDA 483 is not a final agency determination**, a Warning Letter is informal and advisory, and only
  a court order binds. **No Warning Letter, Untitled Letter or judicial action of any kind exists**, and **no
  document in this repository reaches a legal conclusion of any kind.**
- **This programme does not forecast the outcome of any future FDA inspection**, and no document in this
  phase contains such a forecast — not as an objective, not as a success criterion, not as a milestone and
  not in a steering committee paper. **The same refusal applies to the third-party audit and to the internal
  audit of the remediated programme. Neither has occurred and neither's result is anticipated anywhere.**
- **No entry in the four programme registers carries a likelihood, an impact or a score. None ever will.**
- **All content about Helix is fictional and illustrative.**
