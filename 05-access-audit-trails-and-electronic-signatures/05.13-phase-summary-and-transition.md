## 4. What closes

**`IS-28` closes.**

Its text was that the framework says what evidence every system must produce, that nothing had produced any
of it, and that **the first paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` a system would be measured against was the one
nobody had ever evidenced.**

**Closed 2027-01-28, `DEC-518`, taken by Dr Marguerite Oyelaran, ratified at `GOV-25` on 2027-01-28.**
Closure evidence: **`EV-522`** the control position register · **`EV-523`** the 671 control position records
· **`EV-524`** the 34 control gap records · **`EV-503`** the assessment method as authorised · **`GOV-24`**
the approval.

**It is worth being exact about what closed.** `IS-28` asked a question in two halves and Phase 04 said so:
*Phase 05 owns the first half of the answer.* The first half is whether anything has been measured, and the
answer is now yes — **six hundred and seventy-one positions across all eleven paragraphs and all sixty-one
systems, including `21 CFR 11.10(a)` and including the three vendor-hosted systems for which
`21 CFR 11.10` had never been assessed at all.** Evidence of a kind now exists where none existed, and the
paragraph nobody had evidenced has been assessed on every system in scope.

**What has not closed, and what `IS-28` never asked, is whether the evidence the framework requires has been
produced.** It has not. **Not one of the 38 obligations is discharged, not one system validation plan is
written, and no validation package has been executed.** `IS-34` carries that, and it is a different entry
because it is a different question: `IS-28` asked whether anything had been measured, and `IS-34` records
that measuring is not validating.

**`IS-09` does not close, and the reason is recorded rather than assumed.** `21 CFR 11.10` has now been
assessed for `SYS-040`, `SYS-041` and `SYS-056`, which is what the entry's first limb wanted. **Its second
limb is untouched**: Phase 02 recorded that the encryption and digital-signature measures on those three
were not wrong, were not removed, and that **whether they perform as intended has not been assessed** —
and this phase has not assessed it. **`21 CFR 11.30`'s additional measures are additive to the
`21 CFR 11.10` controls it requires be included as appropriate, and never a substitute for them**, and an
entry closed on one limb is an entry closed.

**Nothing else closes.** `IS-01` stays open and stays unweighted. `IS-04`, `IS-06` and `IS-07` remain
deliberately unresolved and this phase resolved none of them, including by implication. `DP-01` and `DP-12`
are consumed at this vantage and are carried open on what they still owe.

## 7. The question Phase 05 leaves open

**The position is known for every paragraph on every system, and on 218 of them what is present is required
by nothing. A position is not a control that will still be there next year.**

Take the three facts in order. **Six hundred and seventy-one positions are determined**, which is more than
this programme has ever known about its own controls and is the first thing any of the four previous phases
was working towards. **Two hundred and eighteen of the five hundred and twenty met positions are met by a
configuration that no sentence in the quality system requires**, so that on sixty of the sixty-one systems
at least one paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` is answered by something that could be withdrawn tomorrow without
a deviation, because no procedure would be breached. **And a determination is dated.** Every cell in the
register speaks as at 2027-01-29 and as at no other day.

**Put those together and the register describes a state rather than a mechanism.** It says what is true on
one Friday. It does not say what keeps any of it true, and on two hundred and eighteen positions the honest
answer is that nothing does. **A periodic review comparing the estate against this register in twelve
months would find differences and would have no way to say whether any of them was a change**, because a
change is a departure from a required state and most of these states are not required.

**That is a different question from every one this programme has closed so far, and the pattern is now five
deep.** `IS-02` recorded that no written determination existed of which records were Part 11 records.
`IS-10` recorded that the determination existed and nothing required it to be maintained. `IS-21` recorded
that the assessment existed and nothing said what to do about it. `IS-28` recorded that the requirement
existed and nothing had been done under it. **`IS-35` records that the position now exists and nothing holds
it in place.** Each phase closed the previous question by producing the artefact, and each discovered that
the artefact was upstream of the thing anybody wanted. **Producing the position fixes the position. It does
not fix the durability of anything the position describes.**

**`IS-35`** carries it, owned by Joachim Reuter, raised 2027-01-29, open. **Phase 06 and Phase 07 own the
next part of the answer** — Phase 06 on the estate this phase's `(e)` position is weakest on, and Phase 07
on the lifecycle, the backup and the archive that decide whether a record protected today is protected in a
year. **Phase 08 owns the mechanism**, because periodic review is where a state either becomes a control or
stays a configuration.

## 9. What is still not true

Stated as absences, because an absence written down is an absence somebody can act on.

- **No system has been validated.** **No validation package has been executed, no protocol has been run,
  no test result exists and no system validation plan has been written** — `IS-34`, and the framework
  requires one per system.
- **Not one of the 38 obligations has been discharged**, and **not one of the nine SOPs has been applied to
  anything.**
- **Not one of the 34 control gaps is closed**, and no remediation plan, remediation cost, sequence or
  priority exists for any of them. **A control gap is not a deviation, and no deviation and no CAPA arises
  from this phase.**
- **Two hundred and eighteen met positions rest on nothing that requires them**, and nobody has yet decided
  which of them Helix intends to require of itself — `IS-29`.
- **Nothing has been assessed about whether the `21 CFR 11.30` additional measures on the three
  vendor-hosted systems perform as intended** — `IS-09`, second limb.
- **No retention period is recorded against any of the 34 in-scope record types** — `IS-14`, `DP-18`, Phase
  07 — so `21 CFR 11.10(e)`'s retention clause has been assessed against a period that does not exist,
  **except where the trail is overwritten while the record it describes is still live on the system and no
  period that could be set would save it.** That is `CG-17`, and **it is the only footing on which the
  clause is recorded as failed.**
- **Twenty-five systems still have no established data integrity position**, and the forty-seven unscored
  pairs behind them remain undetermined. **They are not low risk and they are not adverse; they are
  undetermined** — `IS-16`, `IS-27`.
- **The transfer of a record between systems has been assessed nowhere**, for 31 of the 34 in-scope record
  types — `IS-18`.
- **No periodic review has been performed**, and no internal or third-party audit of the remediated
  programme has been performed. **Vashti Okonjo & Partners has audited nothing.** Phases 08 and 09.
- **There has been no further inspection and there is no inspection outcome of any kind.** The programme
  does not forecast one, at this vantage or any other.
- **The reason four internal audits raised nothing on three observations is not established**, and nothing
  in Phase 05 bears on any of the four candidate explanations — `IS-01`, `ADR-0012`, `05.12 §5`.
- **There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate and no FDA approval or validation of any
  software Helix runs.** **There is no FIPS 199 categorization, no baseline and no authorization.**
- **No document in this phase reaches a legal conclusion of any kind**, and none characterises the
