## 1. What this chapter settles

`05.01-what-a-control-position-is.md` said what a determination is. **This chapter says what it is made
about**, which is a question the programme has now had to answer three times, in three phases, about three
different objects.

Phase 02 asked what the unit of a Part 11 applicability determination is and answered **the record type** —
`ADR-0013`, argued at `02.04 §2`. Phase 03 asked what the unit of a data integrity risk assessment is and
answered **the record type on the system that holds it**, because the same record type is not at the same
risk on every system that holds it — `ADR-0016`. **Phase 05 asks what the unit of a control position is and
answers: a paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` on a system.**

**Eleven paragraphs across sixty-one systems is 671 control positions.** That is the grid, it is the
population this phase assessed, and `ADR-0030` fixes it.

**This chapter states no result.** How the six hundred and seventy-one fall across the three determinations
is `05.08-the-position.md`'s and `logs/control-position-register.md`'s.

## 2. The unit, and the arithmetic

**`21 CFR 11.10` has eleven paragraphs, `(a)` to `(k)`**, and they are the controls for closed systems.
`01.03 §5` owns the enumeration and `04.05 §5` owns what evidence the framework requires for each of them.
Part 11 contains ten sections in three subparts; **there is no `21 CFR 11.60`, no `21 CFR 11.400` and no
Subpart D**, and the gaps in the numbering are original to the rule.

**Sixty-one systems hold at least one Part 11 record** — Phase 02's determination at `02.08`, carried
forward unchanged and never re-derived. **All sixty-one are closed systems**, on the test the regulation
sets at `21 CFR 11.3(b)(4)`, which is who controls access — `ADR-0011`, `02.07`.

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| Paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10` | **11** — `(a)` to `(k)` |
| Systems holding at least one Part 11 record | **61** — Phase 02's, unchanged |
| **Control positions** | **11 × 61 = 671** |

**Every cell is assessed and none is skipped.** There is no cell marked *not applicable*, because every one
of the eleven paragraphs applies to every closed system a firm uses to create, modify, maintain or transmit
Part 11 records, and all sixty-one are such systems. **The framework already refused to let a graduation
remove a paragraph** — `04.05 §6`, rule 7: the graduations move depth, not coverage — and this phase
inherits that refusal at the level of the assessment rather than the requirement.

**One paragraph carries a condition in its own words, and the rule above has to be read with it.**
`21 CFR 11.10(j)` requires written policies that hold individuals accountable and responsible *"for actions
initiated under their electronic signatures"*, and **nineteen of the sixty-one execute electronic
signatures** — `05.06 §4`, cited and not re-derived. The cell is still assessed on all sixty-one, and what
it records on the other forty-two is stated here rather than left to be inferred:

> **`21 CFR 11.10(j)` is conditioned on electronic signatures by its own words.** On the 42 systems that
> execute none, the determination records **the existence and adherence of the written accountability
> policy in the estate that would govern a signature on that system**, not a signature control operating
> on it. **The cell is a real determination about a real policy; it is not a claim that a signature
> control is in place where no signature is executed**, and a reader must not take it as one.

**`21 CFR 11.30` is not engaged for any of the sixty-one at this vantage**, because none of them is an open
system. Where it is engaged, its additional measures **include those identified in `21 CFR 11.10`, as
appropriate, *and* additional measures** — the additional measures are additive and never a substitute, and
`02.07 §7` records what reading it the other way cost Helix on three systems.

## 3. Why the system alone is indefensible

The obvious unit is the system, because that is how an estate is organised, how it is budgeted, how it is
owned and how anybody speaks about it. **It is indefensible for one reason, and the reason is not subtle: a
system meets some paragraphs and not others.**

Take any system in the register and ask the eleven questions separately. Whether access is limited to
authorised individuals is a question about accounts and permissions. Whether the audit trail retains what
`(e)` describes is a question about a configuration and a retention arrangement. Whether the persons who
use the system have the education, training and experience `(i)` calls for is a question about a training
record, and the record may not even live on that system. Whether the documentation controls at `(k)` are in
place is a question about a document library. **These are four different questions about four different
objects, and no property of "the system" answers any two of them.**

**So a determination at the system level has to do one of three things, and each of them is worse than the
last.**

It can report the worst paragraph, in which case a system with ten paragraphs present and one absent is
indistinguishable from a system with none present, **and the ten are invisible to everybody including the
people who would have to rebuild them.**

It can report the best paragraph, which nobody would propose out loud and which is what a report headed
*Part 11 compliant* does in practice.

Or it can report some mixture — a proportion, a majority, an average, a maturity level. **That is the one
that gets built**, because it produces a number per system and a number per system is what an audience
asks for. **A number that mixes eleven determinations about four different objects is a number that
describes none of them**, and it is the same defect `ADR-0017` refused when it forbade averaging FDA's five
attributes and `ADR-0025` refused when it kept two graduations in two columns. **The argument is not new
here; the object is.**

**And there is a second failure, which is about what happens next.** A system-level position cannot be
closed, because there is nothing to close. A paragraph-level position names a control, a cause and a route.
**A control gap is a paragraph and a set of systems sharing one cause** — `logs/control-gap-register.md`
carries them — and a gap that cannot be stated at that resolution cannot be given a closure condition
either.

## 4. Why the paragraph alone is indefensible

The other coarse unit is the paragraph, and it fails by the mirror argument: **a paragraph is met on some
systems and not on others.**

`21 CFR 11.10(e)` is a single sentence of regulation and it reaches an estate of sixty-one systems that
were bought at different times, from different suppliers, under different expectations, and configured by
different people. Some of them retain an audit trail that does everything the paragraph describes. Some
retain one that does part of it. **A statement of the form *Helix meets `21 CFR 11.10(e)`* is true of some
systems and false of others**, and a statement that is both is not a determination.

**There is a specific way this failure shows up in practice and Helix has already seen it.** A firm that
reports at paragraph level reports on its best-understood systems and generalises. The systems nobody has
looked at are absorbed into a position that was formed by looking at the ones somebody had. **Phase 02
found the same mechanism operating on the inventory** — the register described the estate the register knew
about, and the physical walk found seventeen systems that were not in it — `ADR-0009`, `02.02 §3`. **A
paragraph-level control position is that error committed deliberately.**

**And a paragraph-level position destroys the one thing this phase can hand forward.** Phase 06 needs to
know which paragraphs are absent on the chromatography data systems, not which paragraphs are absent
somewhere. Phase 07 needs the same for the archives. **A position stated per paragraph tells the next phase
that something is wrong and not where.**

## 5. What a cell carries

The unit is a cell; the register is sixty-one rows and eleven determination columns. **What each cell
carries is fixed, and it is the minimum that makes the determination checkable by somebody who did not make
it.**

| Field | What it holds | Why it is there |
|---|---|---|
| **The system** | Phase 02's `SYS` identifier, name and owner, **carried without drift** | `SYS-041` means the same system in Phase 09 as it does in Phase 02, and a register that renames a system has broken the join |
| **The paragraph** | One of `(a)` to `(k)`, cited as `21 CFR 11.10(e)` and never as "Part 11.10" | **A citation that cannot be looked up is not a citation** |
| **The determination** | One of the three named values, and nothing else | `05.03-the-three-determinations.md`, `ADR-0031` |
| **The basis** | What was looked at, and by whom | **A determination whose basis is not recorded is an opinion with a register entry** |
| **The requiring sentence, where there is one** | The procedure and the sentence in it that obliges somebody to keep the control | It is what separates the first determination from the second — `05.03 §4` |
| **The evidence reference** | The `EV` identifier of the artefact | **An artefact that has not been produced has no `EV` number, and the index is not a plan** |
| **The gap reference, where the value is *Not met*** | The `CG` identifier the position groups into | A gap is a paragraph and a set of systems sharing **one** cause |

**The cell carries no rating, no band, no likelihood, no consequence and no priority.** Phase 03's `DR` and
`ND` registers and Phase 04's rigour register are inputs to the work and are not columns in this one —
`02.09 §3` fixed the discipline of saying what a register deliberately does not carry, and this register
keeps it.

## 6. What the grid is not

**It is not a scoring instrument, and nothing in this phase is scored.** The three determinations are three
named values. They are never converted into numbers, never averaged, never weighted, never summed across a
row to produce a figure for a system or down a column to produce one for a paragraph, and never expressed
as a proportion of anything. **A row of eleven named values is the position of that system, and it is
already the shortest true statement available.**

**It is not a maturity model.** Nothing in regulation or guidance requires a data integrity maturity model,
score, rating or scale, and Helix publishes none. **The grid records what is there. It does not place Helix
on a journey.**

**It is not a crosswalk from ALCOA.** FDA's own footnote 5 maps the five attributes to the CGMP predicate
rules and **cites 21 CFR Part 11 nowhere**; the industry crosswalk mapping attributes one-to-one onto
`21 CFR 11.10(a)` to `(k)` is an invention and FDA's footnote refutes it — `ADR-0019`, `03.04`. **No column
in this register is an ALCOA attribute and no attribute determination has been read into any cell.**

**It is not a validation status.** Eleven determinations about a system are eleven facts about present
controls. **A control assessment is not validation** — `05.01 §4`, `ADR-0034`.

**And it is not a compliance statement.** There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate and no
FDA-approved or FDA-validated software. **Compliance is a property of Helix's validated system in its
intended use, under its procedures, operated by its trained people**, and no register produces it.

## 7. `ADR-0030` — the unit is a paragraph on a system

**`ADR-0030`, taken at `DEC-503` on 2026-12-10 by Colm Ó Braonáin, fixes the unit of a control position as
a paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` on a system, and requires that every one of the six hundred and seventy-one
cells carry a determination.**

Three rules follow.

**No determination is stated at a coarser unit.** No document in this phase says that a system is compliant,
that a system meets Part 11, or that Helix meets a paragraph. **Where a sentence of that shape is wanted, it
is written as a citation to the register instead.**

**No cell is left blank and no cell is marked not applicable.** Every one of the eleven paragraphs applies
to every one of the sixty-one closed systems. **An unassessed cell would be a silence dressed as a
determination**, and *"a negative that nobody can test is not a determination; it is a silence."*

**And the grid is not collapsed for reporting.** A summary may cite the register; it may not compute a
figure from it. **The moment a row of eleven values becomes one value, the information that made the unit
defensible has been thrown away by the reporting layer rather than by the method** — which is the failure
mode `ADR-0025` guarded against when it refused to merge two graduations into the single number every
audience asks for.

## 8. What this chapter does not establish

- **It states no control position.** No determination for any paragraph on any system appears here —
  `05.08-the-position.md` owns them and `logs/control-position-register.md` carries them.
- **It states no validation outcome**, and **a control assessment is not validation** — `ADR-0034`.
- **It re-opens no applicability determination.** The sixty-one are Phase 02's and the population is
  unchanged; `AS-28` carried the assumption that no `ADR-0015` or `ADR-0022` trigger would fire between the
  Phase 04 vantage and this one.
- **It re-scores nothing and re-bands nothing.** Phase 03's registers and Phase 04's rigour register are
  inputs and are never edited by this phase.
- **It maps no ALCOA attribute onto any paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10`** — `ADR-0019`.
- **It raises no CAPA and no deviation. A control gap is not a deviation.**
- **It reaches no legal conclusion**, characterises nothing as a violation of anything, and forecasts no
  inspection outcome.
- **Nothing here bears on `IS-01`**, which stays open and stays unweighted.
