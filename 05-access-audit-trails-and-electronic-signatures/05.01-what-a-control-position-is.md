## 1. What this chapter settles

Phase 04 refused to state a control position and said so in a decision record. `ADR-0029` reads that **the
framework states what evidence is required and states no control position**, and that whether any paragraph
of `21 CFR 11.10` is met, for any system, is this phase's. **This chapter says what the thing Phase 04
refused to state actually is**, before any of it is stated.

That ordering is deliberate. A phase that publishes six hundred and seventy-one determinations and then
explains what a determination is has already been read. **The definition has to arrive before the result,
because the result is what will be quoted.**

The chapter does four things. It says what a control position is. It says what it is not, in six specific
directions, each of which somebody will otherwise assume. **It says why a control assessment is not
validation, in both directions.** And it says what Phase 04 is still owed, which is all of it.

**It states no result.** How the six hundred and seventy-one positions fall is `05.08-the-position.md`'s,
and this chapter cites it rather than reporting it.

## 2. What a control position is

**A control position is a determination, made at a stated vantage, about one paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` as
it applies to one system, recorded with its basis and the evidence it rests on.**

Five properties, and each of them is load-bearing.

**It has a unit and the unit is a cell.** Not a system, not a paragraph, but the intersection of the two —
`05.02-the-grid.md` owns that argument and `ADR-0030` records it.

**It has a value from a fixed set of three.** *Met and required* · *Met but not required* · *Not met*.
Nothing else may be entered, and in particular no fourth value meaning *partly*, *in progress* or *being
addressed* — `05.03-the-three-determinations.md`, `ADR-0031`.

**It has a date, and the date is not decorative.** A control position speaks as at 2027-01-29 and about
nothing else. It is not a statement about what was true when the system was installed, and it is not a
statement about what will be true next quarter. **A position with no date is a claim with no expiry, and a
claim with no expiry is one nobody will ever think to re-test.**

**It has a basis.** The register records what was looked at — a configuration read, a demonstration, an
account listing, a document — and who looked. **A determination whose basis is not recorded is an opinion
with a register entry.**

**And it has a named individual owner**, because every control, procedure, record and obligation in this
programme has one and no control is owned by a team — `ADR-0008`.

## 3. What a control position is not

Six things, and each is a reading somebody will make of the register if it is not refused here.

**It is not a CAPA.** A corrective and preventive action is a record under Helix's quality system, raised
under Helix's own procedure, with its own identifier. **No `CG` identifier is a `CAPA` identifier**, no
determination in this phase opens one, and nothing in this phase authorises anybody to close one.

**It is not a deviation.** A deviation is Helix's own quality record, raised where something departed from
a requirement that was in force. **A control gap is Helix's own assessment of its own position at a
vantage.** Where a programme entry and a quality record concern the same subject matter, both identifiers
are kept and the link is recorded — `01.12 §6` owns the rule and this phase applies it without variation.
**A control gap is not a deviation**, and `05.12-what-this-does-not-establish.md` says so in the phase's
own words.

**It is not a finding.** A finding is what internal audit or a third-party auditor raises, and it is theirs.
Nothing in this register has been audited by anybody. **Gideon Halvorsen's function has raised nothing on
the strength of it, and internal audit does not implement remediation** — `01.07 §6`.

**It is not an inspectional observation.** `OBS-1` to `OBS-7` are the Form FDA 483's own numbering and are
never renumbered, regrouped or given identifiers of the programme's own — `ADR-0006`. **A programme that
gives an inspector's observation its own identifier has started keeping two sets of books**, and the
converse holds equally: a programme that dresses its own assessment in the vocabulary of an observation has
started keeping the same two sets from the other end.

**It is not a legal conclusion.** *Not met* is Helix's assessment that a control described in the text of a
paragraph is not present on a system at a vantage. **It is not a statement that Helix has violated
`21 CFR 11.10`, that any record is unreliable, or that any product is affected.** No document in this
repository reaches a legal conclusion of any kind, and none forecasts what any inspection will find.

**And it is not a rating, a score, a tier or a band.** Nothing in this phase is scored. The three
determinations are three named values and they are never converted to numbers, never averaged, never summed
across a row or a column into a single figure for a system or for the estate, and never expressed as a
proportion. `05.02-the-grid.md §6` owns that prohibition, and it is the same discipline `ADR-0017` applied
to FDA's five attributes and `04.05 §6` applied to evidence depth.

## 4. A control assessment is not validation

**This is the sentence the chapter exists for, and it is the sentence most likely to be lost between this
phase's publication and its first quotation.**

The two questions are different questions about different objects, and they are asked with different
instruments for different purposes.

| | **Validation** | **A control position** |
|---|---|---|
| **The question** | Does the system do what it is specified to do, and does it keep doing it? | Is the control described in this paragraph present on this system now? |
| **What it is about** | **Specified behaviour over time** | **A state of affairs at a vantage** |
| **The instrument** | An approved plan, a protocol executed against a written acceptance basis set before execution, a recorded result, a review by somebody who did not produce it | A read of the configuration, the account arrangement, the manifestation or the document, recorded with its basis |
| **What it produces** | Evidence that a specification was met | A determination that a control is or is not there |
| **What it can survive** | It is defeated by a change, and re-evidenced after one | It is defeated by a change, and it does not re-run itself |
| **Where the requirement comes from** | `21 CFR 11.10(a)` and, for Helix and whatever FDA does about Part 11, **`21 CFR 211.68`** | The eleven paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10`, and the framework's evidence set at `04.05 §5` |

**Neither substitutes for the other, and the substitution fails in both directions.**

**A control position does not become validation evidence by being favourable.** *Met and required* on
`21 CFR 11.10(e)` records that the system produces an audit trail of the kind the paragraph describes and
that a procedure requires it. **It does not record that anybody specified what the audit trail must capture,
executed a test against that specification, recorded the result, or had the result reviewed by a second
person.** A validation package is a chain of those acts and a control position is none of them. **An
observation that a thing is present is not a demonstration that it was specified, tested and found to
perform.**

**And validation evidence does not become a control position by existing.** A package that proves a system
computes accurately, behaves reliably and performs consistently has evidenced three of the four clauses of
`21 CFR 11.10(a)` — `04.03-the-fourth-clause.md` owns that argument and this chapter does not restate it.
**It has said nothing about who can get into the system, what the audit trail retains, or what appears on
an exported form.** Those are `(d)`, `(e)` and `21 CFR 11.50`, and a package addressed to `(a)` addresses
none of them.

**There is a narrower point inside this one and it is the one that will be argued with.** An artefact
produced for one purpose may sometimes be attributed to the other, and Phase 04 permitted exactly that:
audit trail functional testing produced under `21 CFR 11.10(e)` **may be attributed to `(a)`'s fourth clause
and evidenced there**, while an assertion that `(e)` is met **may not discharge `(a)` at all** —
`ADR-0024`, `04.03 §6`. **The same asymmetry governs here.** A determination in this phase's register may
be cited in a future validation plan as a fact about the system's state on 2027-01-29. **It may not be
cited as the evidence the plan was required to produce**, because it was not produced against a
specification, was not executed against an acceptance basis set first, and was not reviewed as evidence.

## 5. Why this matters more in this phase than in any before it

Phases 01 to 04 could not be misread this way because they produced nothing that looked like an answer.
A scope determination, a risk assessment and a framework are all visibly upstream of the question *is Helix
all right?* **A control position is the first artefact in this programme that looks like an answer to it**,
and an artefact that looks like an answer will be used as one.

**Three specific misreadings are foreseeable and each is refused here.**

**That a system with all eleven paragraphs Met is validated.** It is not. It has eleven determinations about
present controls and no validation package produced under the framework, because none exists.

**That the control position discharges the obligations Phase 04 raised.** It does not. **Thirty-eight
obligations exist and not one of them has been performed** — `04.13 §1`, cited and not re-derived — and a
determination that a control is present is not the performance of an act that was required.

**And that a paragraph recorded Met needs no further evidence.** It needs exactly the evidence the framework
requires for it, at the depth the two graduations set — `04.05 §5` and `§6`. **The determination tells the
plan where to start. It does not tell the plan it may stop.**

## 6. What Phase 04 is still owed

**Phase 04's framework requires evidence for all eleven paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10`, `(a)` to `(k)`, for
every system in scope. None of that evidence has been produced.**

The position at this vantage, stated as absences because an absence written down is an absence somebody can
act on:

- **No system-level validation plan has been written.** The framework requires one per system and none
  exists — `IS-28`, and `04.13 §7` states the question in the form the phase left it.
- **No validation package has been executed, no protocol has been run and no test result exists.**
- **Not one of the thirty-eight obligations has been performed**, and **not one of the nine SOPs approved at
  `GOV-19` has been applied to anything.**
- **`21 CFR 11.10` had never been assessed for the three vendor-hosted systems** before this phase —
  `IS-09`, `DP-12`. What this phase did about that is `05.08-the-position.md`'s to report.

**`IS-34` carries the consequence:** a control position is not validation evidence, and the evidence Phase
04 requires is owed in full. **The register makes the position legible. It does not make the evidence
exist.** That is the same limit `04.11 §2` recorded against the framework — an instrument that requires
evidence produces none — and it is recorded again here because this phase's output is more easily mistaken
for the thing than Phase 04's was.

## 7. `ADR-0034` — a control assessment is not validation

**`ADR-0034`, taken at `DEC-505` on 2026-12-17 by Colm Ó Braonáin, records that a control assessment is not
validation, that neither substitutes for the other, and that the validation evidence Phase 04 requires is
owed in full and is not reduced by anything in this phase.**

The decision has three limbs.

**No determination in this phase's register may be presented as validation evidence**, in a validation plan,
a package, a summary report, a governance record or a management report. It may be cited as a fact about
the system's state at this vantage, with its date attached.

**No validation package may be presented as a control position.** What a package addresses is a readable
fact about a document; whether a control is present on a system is not — the distinction `ADR-0029` drew
from the other side of the same line.

**And the evidence requirement is stated as undischarged wherever the control position is reported.** The
phase summary carries it, the governance record carries it, and this chapter carries it. **The requirement
is not reduced by a favourable determination, and it is not increased by an unfavourable one.**

## 8. What this chapter does not establish

- **It states no control position.** How the six hundred and seventy-one determinations fall is
  `05.08-the-position.md`'s, and nothing here reports a count of anything.
- **It states no validation outcome.** No system is validated in this phase, **no package has been executed,
  no protocol run and no test result exists.**
- **It raises no CAPA and no deviation, and it authorises neither.** **A control gap is not a deviation.**
- **It draws no conclusion from `OBS-2` or `OBS-4`.** The links are recorded at `05.05-audit-trails.md` and
  `05.04-access.md` respectively, and their disposition is Phase 06's and Phase 08's.
- **It sets no audit trail review frequency, no retention period and no access design.** Those are
  `05.05-audit-trails.md`'s subject matter, Phase 07's and `05.04-access.md`'s respectively, and none of
  them is set as a requirement here.
- **It re-scores nothing and re-bands nothing.** Phase 03's registers and Phase 04's rigour register are
  inputs to this phase and are never edited by it.
- **It reaches no legal conclusion**, characterises nothing as a violation of anything, assesses no
  individual's work, and forecasts no inspection outcome.
- **Nothing here bears on `IS-01`**, which stays open and stays unweighted.
