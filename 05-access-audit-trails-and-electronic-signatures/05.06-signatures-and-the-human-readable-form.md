## 1. What this chapter settles

Nineteen of the sixty-one systems execute electronic signatures. `21 CFR 11.50` requires three things of a
signed electronic record, and requires them in any human readable form of that record. **This chapter
reports where the three things are and where they are not**, and the answer differs depending on which
human readable form you look at.

**The meaning of the signature appears on screen on all nineteen and is absent from the exported or printed
form on twelve of them.** That is the finding, and the rest of the chapter is the regulation's text, the
population, the method and the reason nobody at Helix had found it before.

The chapter states no control position for any paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` — those are in
`logs/control-position-register.md` and are read at `05.08-the-position.md`. **`21 CFR 11.50` is a
different section with a different population**, and it is not a column in that register.

## 2. `21 CFR 11.50`, and the words it uses

**Signature manifestations.** A signed electronic record shall contain information associated with the
signing that clearly indicates three things:

| # | Element |
|---|---|
| 1 | **The printed name of the signer** |
| 2 | **The date and time when the signature was executed** |
| 3 | **The meaning associated with the signature** — such as review, approval, responsibility or authorship |

And then the paragraph this chapter turns on. **`21 CFR 11.50(b)`, entire:**

> *"The items identified in paragraphs (a)(1), (a)(2), and (a)(3) of this section shall be subject to the
> same controls as for electronic records and shall be included as part of any human readable form of the
> electronic record (such as electronic display or printout)."*

**`(b)` is quoted verbatim and entire. The three elements above are `(a)`'s and are stated in the
repository's own words**, because no verbatim quotation of the whole of `21 CFR 11.50` appears in the source
phases and one is not manufactured here. The distinction is recorded because a quotation that has been
tidied is not a quotation, and truncating one and closing it with a full stop has been this engagement's
most persistent defect.

**`21 CFR 11.50(b)` has two conjoined limbs and this phase assessed one of them.** The items shall be
subject to the same controls as for electronic records, **and** shall be included as part of any human
readable form. Everything below is about the second limb. **The *same controls* limb was not assessed and
is owed** — `IS-30` carries it, and §6 is why it is material here rather than incidental: on twelve systems
the meaning is held in the signature workflow rather than on the record, and whether it is under the same
controls as the record is a question nobody has asked.

**Three properties of the second limb do the work.**

**It says *shall be included as part of*, not *shall be available on request*.** The elements are part of
the human readable form. A system that can produce the meaning of a signature from a second screen, a
separate report or an administrator's query has not included it as part of the form; it has made it
obtainable, which is a different thing and is what most systems in this estate do.

**It says *any* human readable form.** Not the form the vendor demonstrated, not the form the reviewer
normally uses, and not the form the firm happens to check. Any of them.

**Its parenthesis is illustrative and not exhaustive.** *"(such as electronic display or printout)"* names
two forms as examples. The electronic display is one. The printout is another. **A PDF export is a human
readable form of the electronic record whether or not anybody prints it.**

**And there the extension stops being obvious, so the chapter stops asserting that it is.**
`21 CFR 11.50(b)` requires the three items in any human readable form **of the electronic record**. Where an
artefact the system produces is a rendering of the signed record — the display, the printout, a PDF of that
record — the three items must be in it. **Where an artefact is a derived report about several records,
whether it is a human readable form of any one of them is a question this phase decides system by system
and records on the test record.** **Helix adopts the broader reading; it is an interpretation, no FDA
guidance addresses it, and this repository says so rather than presenting it as settled.**

## 3. "Any human readable form" reaches the export and the printout

**A human readable form of an electronic record is any rendering of that record a human being can read.**
The screen is one. The printout is named in the regulation's own parenthesis. **The export is a rendering a
human being reads, and nothing in the section distinguishes it from the two the parenthesis names.**

`ADR-0032`, `DEC-511`, taken by Priyanka Venkataraman on 2027-01-13: **`21 CFR 11.50` is tested against
every human readable form of the record, not against the screen.** The decision records what was refused —
testing the display alone, on the ground that the display is the form a reviewer uses — and the reason it
was refused: **the regulation's own parenthesis names the printout, so a test confined to the display is
already narrower than the text of the section it is testing against.**

**What follows is a method rule and it is not a strict one.** For each of the nineteen systems, every human
readable form the system can produce was enumerated first, and each was tested for all three elements
separately. The enumeration is `EV-514`; the test records are `EV-516`, one per form per system, on the
instrument at `templates/signature-manifestation-test-record-template.md`. **A form that was not enumerated
could not be tested, and `AS-36` records that the enumeration is the whole of what each product can
produce.**

**The other forty-two systems execute no electronic signature and are outside this chapter entirely.** A
system that captures no signature cannot fail a signature manifestation requirement, and recording an
absence as a pass would be the same error `02.05 §9` names: *a negative that nobody can test is not a
determination; it is a silence.*

**Nothing in this chapter is a statement about `21 CFR 11.100` or `21 CFR 11.300`.** Whether each signature
is unique to one individual, whether identity was verified before the signature was sanctioned, and whether
the certification to FDA has been made, are separate requirements of separate sections.

## 5. The screen

**On the electronic display, all three elements appear on all nineteen.**

Every one of the nineteen shows the printed name of the signer, the date and time the signature was
executed, and the meaning of the signature, in the record view a reviewer opens. **That is a real result
and it is stated first because a chapter that led with the failure would misdescribe the estate.** Helix's
systems were configured by people who had read `21 CFR 11.50` and who had implemented what it requires in
the place they were looking at.

**It is also the whole of what Helix had ever checked**, and that is §7's subject.

## 6. The exported or printed form — the twelve

**On the exported or printed form, the meaning of the signature is absent on twelve of the nineteen.**

| Cut | Systems |
|---|---|
| **The meaning is absent from the exported or printed form** | **12** — `SYS-001`, `SYS-019`, `SYS-023`, `SYS-024`, `SYS-027`, `SYS-028`, `SYS-037`, `SYS-039`, `SYS-040`, `SYS-042`, `SYS-044`, `SYS-050` |
| The meaning is present in every form the system produces | **7** — `SYS-025`, `SYS-026`, `SYS-041`, `SYS-043`, `SYS-047`, `SYS-048`, `SYS-049` |
| **Check** | **12 + 7 = 19** |

**The printed name and the date and time survive the export on all nineteen. It is the meaning that
drops.** That is worth stating precisely, because it is the pattern rather than an accident of any one
product: an export routine is written to carry the fields a record has, and *who signed* and *when* are
fields on the record. **The meaning of a signature is not a field on the record. It is a property of the
act**, held in the signature workflow — this signing is an approval, that one is a verification — and an
export written against the record's own fields does not reach into the workflow to fetch it.

**On several of the twelve the meaning is not lost so much as flattened.** The exported form shows the
signer and the timestamp against a step, and a reader can often infer from the position of the signature
what it was for. **Inference is not indication.** `21 CFR 11.50` requires the record to clearly indicate the
meaning; a form from which the meaning can be reconstructed by somebody who already knows how the workflow
is built is not a form that indicates it.

**And on others — `SYS-019`, `SYS-028`, `SYS-037` and `SYS-039` — the export carries the signer and the
time and nothing that names the act at all.** There is no position to infer from.

**On `SYS-001`, `SYS-023` and `SYS-024` the export is the form anybody outside the operating function ever
sees.** `SYS-001`'s batch record pack, `SYS-023`'s and `SYS-024`'s reported chromatographic result: in each
case the reviewer at the next desk works from the produced form and not from the screen.
**Where the export is the form the record travels in, an element absent from the export is an element
absent from the record as anybody downstream receives it.**

**The `OBS-2` link is recorded and no conclusion is drawn from it.** `SYS-023` and `SYS-024` are the
chromatography data systems, and `OBS-2` concerns audit trail review on that estate. This chapter records
that the two systems are among the twelve and stops there; **the chromatography-specific work is Phase 06's
and this document draws no conclusion of any kind from the link.**

## 7. Why nobody had found it

**Helix had only ever checked the screen.**

That is the most useful sentence in this phase for a working reader, and it is worth setting out how it
came to be true, because it was not carelessness and nobody at Helix decided to check one form and not the
others.

**The vendor demonstration is a screen demonstration.** When a product is selected, the capability that is
shown is the record view: here is the signature block, here is the name, here is the timestamp, here is the
meaning. It is a fair demonstration of a real capability and it is the form the product's own documentation
illustrates.

**The qualification test is written from the demonstration.** A test script asking the tester to execute a
signature and confirm that the name, the date and time and the meaning appear is a correct test of the
regulation's requirement — against the display. It is written by somebody looking at the display, because
that is where the signature was just executed.

**The reviewer's habit reinforces both.** Inside the operating function the record view is the working
surface. The export is produced for somebody else — for a pack, for an archive, for a request — and the
person who produces it is not usually the person who would notice what is missing from it.

**And no procedure required the other forms to be looked at.** The computerised system validation procedure
requires a validation package; it does not require `21 CFR 11.50` to be tested against each human readable
form the system can produce. **That absence is the same class of absence Phase 04 published as its twelve:
a procedure that exists, covers the subject matter and does not require the act** —
`../04-the-validation-framework-and-policy-architecture/04.08-where-the-obligations-land.md §5`, `IS-24`,
and this chapter does not re-argue it. **An auditor who asked *is `21 CFR 11.50` tested?* got a yes on all
nineteen, and the yes was accurate.**

**What changed is not the estate. It is the question.** `ADR-0032` requires the test to enumerate the forms
before it tests any of them, and the enumeration is what turns *does the signature manifest correctly* into
*in which forms does it manifest correctly*. **The twelve were visible the moment the second question was
asked, and were invisible to the first.**

## 8. What this finding is not

- **It is not a control position under `21 CFR 11.10`.** `21 CFR 11.50` is in Subpart B and is a separate
  section with a separate population; **no cell in `logs/control-position-register.md` moves because of
  anything in this chapter.**
- **It is not a validation outcome.** **No validation package has been executed, no protocol has been run
  and no test result under Phase 04's framework exists.** The manifestation tests at `EV-516` are control
  assessment records taken under `GOV-21`'s method, and **assessing that a control is present is not
  validating it** — `ADR-0034`, `IS-34`.
- **It is not a deviation and it is not a CAPA.** Nothing here breaches a procedure, because no procedure
  required the export to be tested. **A control gap is not a deviation**, and
  `05.12-what-this-does-not-establish.md` says so.
- **It is not a criticism of any vendor, any tester or any reviewer.** A product that manifests a signature
  correctly on screen has the feature the regulation describes; **compliance is a property of Helix's
  validated system in its intended use and is not a property a supplier can sell.** There is no Part 11
  certification and no FDA-approved or FDA-validated software.
- **It reaches no legal conclusion and forecasts no inspection outcome.** It does not say what any
  inspector would ask or find. **An open gap is Helix's own assessment of its own position. It is not a
  violation, not a breach, not a non-compliance, not a finding and not an inspectional observation.**
- **It says nothing about whether any signature is genuine, or about whether any record has been altered.**
  `21 CFR 11.70` requires signatures to be linked to their records so that they cannot be *"excised,
  copied, or otherwise transferred to falsify an electronic record by ordinary means"* — **which imposes no
  hashing requirement and no digital signature requirement** — and that is a different question this
  chapter does not answer.

## 9. What would close it

**Stated as the condition, not as a plan.** No date, no cost, no sequence.

| For | The condition |
|---|---|
| The twelve | **The meaning of the signature included as part of every human readable form of the record the system produces**, evidenced on a signature manifestation test record for each form |
| All nineteen | **A procedure requiring `21 CFR 11.50` to be tested against every enumerated human readable form**, so that the test that found this is required rather than remembered |
| The estate | **The enumeration of forms kept current**, because a product update that adds an export adds a form, and a form nobody has enumerated is a form nobody tests |

**The second row is the one that matters more than the first.** Correcting twelve exports closes twelve
exports. **Requiring the test is what stops the thirteenth**, and it is the difference between a control
that is present and a control that is required to remain so — which is `05.09-the-controls-nothing-requires.md`'s
argument, arriving here from a different direction.

**`IS-30` carries the twelve**, owned by Priyanka Venkataraman, raised 2027-01-13, open. Nothing is closed
by this chapter.
