## 1. One idea

**Phase 04 produced requirements. Phase 05 produces positions, and a position is read as a verdict.**

That is the specific hazard of this phase and it is sharper than the last one's. A requirement gets read as
a description of the world. **A position gets read as a judgement about a system** — and this phase has
determined, for the first time in the programme, that a paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` is not met on a named
system. **One careless sentence turns that into a statement that the system is non-compliant, which it is
not, and which nothing in this phase says.**

This chapter states what the positions do not mean, in the same phase as the positions, at the same
version, as a numbered chapter rather than as a caveat at the foot of a register.

It is owned by internal audit, which assessed no system, filled in no cell, wrote no gap record and took no
determination in this phase — `ADR-0004`. **That is the point of who owns it.**

## 2. A control gap is not a deviation

**This is the sentence the chapter exists for and it is not a technicality.**

A deviation is a departure from a requirement. **On the great majority of the hundred and fifty-one Not met
positions, there was no requirement to depart from**, because Phase 05's own second determination records
that Helix's quality system does not require most of the controls it has — two hundred and eighteen of them
— and it does not require most of the ones it lacks either. **Nothing has been breached. A control gap
records the absence of a control against the text of a regulation, not against a sentence in Helix's own
quality system.**

**The distinction is not a comfort and it should not be read as one.** It is the opposite: a deviation has
a procedure behind it, an owner, an investigation route and a closure condition. **A control gap has none of
those by default**, which is precisely why `logs/control-gap-register.md` gives each of the thirty-four a
named individual owner and a stated closure condition. **A gap that is not a deviation is a gap the quality
system has no machinery for**, and inventing machinery is not this phase's to do.

`ADR-0006` established that this repository keeps one set of books. **`CG-nn` is now the sixth separate
word for a separate thing**, after `OBS-n`, `DEV-nnn`, *finding*, `DR-nn` and `OB-nn`, and it is not any of
the other five.

| A `CG` row is not | Because |
|---|---|
| **A deviation** | **No procedure has been breached.** A deviation departs from a requirement; on most of these rows there was no requirement |
| **A CAPA** | **No CAPA arises from this phase and none has been raised.** A closure condition is not a corrective action |
| **A finding, a deficiency or a violation** | **An open gap is Helix's own assessment of its own position. It is not a violation, not a breach, not a non-compliance, not a finding and not an inspectional observation** |
| **An inspectional observation** | `OBS-1` to `OBS-7` are FDA's, in the 483's own numbering — `ADR-0006`. **The two series never merge and a `CG` identifier is never attached to an observation** |
| **A remediation plan** | The register states a condition. **It states no date, no cost, no sequence and no priority**, and no remediation plan or cost exists anywhere in this phase |
| **Evidence of anything** | A gap is the record of an absence. **It is not a test result and it is not a validation artefact** |

**And a Met but not required position is not a gap at all.** Nothing is absent from it except a sentence.
`05.09-the-controls-nothing-requires.md` owns that and it matters here because the two hundred and eighteen
are the population most likely to be reported as a problem to be fixed. **They are a population to be
decided about.**

## 3. A control position is not validation evidence

**Phase 04's framework requires evidence for all eleven paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10` for every one of the
sixty-one systems. None of that evidence has been produced. This phase produces none of it.**

`ADR-0034`, `DEC-505`, taken by Colm Ó Braonáin on 2026-12-17: **a control assessment is not validation, and
neither substitutes for the other.**

| A control position is not | Because |
|---|---|
| **A validation package, a protocol or a test result** | **No package has been executed and no protocol has been run in this phase.** A control assessment observes a system's present configuration and behaviour; a validation package demonstrates, against a specification, that a system performs as intended |
| **Evidence for `21 CFR 11.10(a)`** | `(a)` requires *"Validation of systems to ensure accuracy, reliability, consistent intended performance, and the ability to discern invalid or altered records."* **A determination that `(a)` is met is a statement about whether validation is in place. It is not validation** |
| **A discharge of any of the 38 obligations** | **Not one of `OB-01` to `OB-38` is discharged at this vantage.** Discharge requires the evidence named on the obligation record to exist |
| **A system validation plan** | **The framework requires one per system and not one has been written**, at this vantage as at the last |

**The temptation this phase carried was to let the register stand in for the evidence**, and it was
available in a specific form: thirty-seven systems carry a Met and required position on `(a)`, and a
sentence reporting that validation is in place on thirty-seven systems is one short step from a sentence
reporting that thirty-seven systems are validated. **They are different claims.** The first says a package
exists and a procedure requires it. The second says something about the system, and nothing in this phase
supports it.

**`IS-34` carries it**, owned by Colm Ó Braonáin, raised 2026-12-17, open. **The validation evidence Phase
04 requires is still owed in full.**

## 5. `IS-01` stays open, and stays unweighted

**Nothing in Phase 05 bears on `IS-01`.**

`IS-01` records that four internal audits between 2023-06 and 2025-09 covered all three systems between
them, each falling inside the scope of at least two, and that none raised a finding on the subject matter
of `OBS-2`, `OBS-4` or `OBS-6`. **Why is not established.** `01.09 §6` recorded four candidate explanations
and chose none; `ADR-0007` published the history in full; `ADR-0012` and `02.11` established the premise of
one candidate and refused to promote it; `03.12 §5` and `04.12 §5` each confirmed that nothing in their
phase discriminated between them.

**The temptation available in this phase is the sharpest the programme has had, and it is named here so
that it can be refused.**

`04.12 §5` recorded that Phase 04 produced a general argument that a procedure which does not require an act
is invisible to an ordinary enquiry, and refused to convert it into a cause. **Phase 05 has now produced
something more concrete than an argument: an audit trail position for every one of the sixty-one systems,
including the systems `OBS-2`, `OBS-4` and `OBS-6` concern.** `04.12 §5` said in terms what was still
needed — *an assessment of what the audit trails on the three systems record*, with the chromatography data
system in Phase 06, *and an examination of the four audit programmes against it*, in Phase 08 — and
recorded that two of the three did not exist. **One of the two now exists.**

**That is exactly why this section is longer than it was last time.** A phase that has produced the first
half of what an unresolved question was said to need is a phase under pressure to supply the second half,
and the second half is somebody else's.

**What this phase has produced is a position at 2027-01-29.** It has examined no audit scope statement, no
checklist, no test design and no working paper. It has traced no checklist to its source. It has
reconstructed nobody's technical access at any historical date, and **it has reconstructed no system's
configuration at any date before this vantage** — which is the specific limitation that matters, because a
control position taken today says nothing whatever about what a system's audit trail did or did not record
in 2023, 2024 or 2025. **A determination made in January 2027 is not evidence about a system in June 2023**,
and treating it as such would be `ADR-0012`'s error committed with better data.

**The four candidate explanations stand exactly where Phase 02, Phase 03 and Phase 04 left them.** **No
ranking, no probability, no confidence level, no leading candidate and no ordering of the four**, in this
chapter, in the registers, in any diagram, in any governance record or in any later phase. **The four are
set out at `01.09 §6`, in the order that chapter set, and this chapter reproduces none of them, because
reproducing one and not the others is a weighting.** **No document in this phase may be cited in support of
any of the four, and if one is, it is wrong and this sentence is why.** `EV-539` records the position at the
`MS-09` gate.

**What is still needed, restated.** The chromatography data system in **Phase 06**, and an examination of
the four audit programmes against what has been assessed, in **Phase 08**. **One of the three still does not
exist.**

## 6. Two things the phase must never be read as saying

**First: nothing here is a Part 11 certification, and nothing here says any system is compliant.**

**There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate, and no FDA-approved, FDA-validated or
FDA-certified software.** FDA does not certify electronic systems and does not pre-evaluate them for Part 11
compliance. A product has **features that support** compliance; **compliance is a property of Helix's
validated system in its intended use, under its procedures, operated by its trained people.** A control
position register is not a certificate, a row of eleven determinations is not a compliance statement, and
**five systems carrying no Not met position are not five compliant systems.** `PR-07` carries the standing
risk that a vendor document is read as though it were a certification, and `PR-42` now carries the risk
that this register is.

**Second: enforcement discretion is not an exemption, and the eighty-nine are not mitigated.**

**Enforcement discretion is FDA's stated intention about what it will enforce. It is not a legal exemption
and not a safe harbour, and it confers no rights.** It reaches `21 CFR 11.10(a)`, `(b)`, `(c)` and `(e)`,
and `(k)` in part — the guidance names `(k)(2)`, the second of `(k)`'s two subparagraphs, and says nothing
about `(k)(1)` — together with the corresponding requirements in `21 CFR 11.30`. **`21 CFR
211.68` and `21 CFR 211.100(b)` survive it entirely.** Eighty-nine of the hundred and fifty-one Not met
positions fall inside the four paragraphs it reaches in full and sixty-two fall outside; **the position is
stated identically inside and outside it**, and `05.11-discretion-changes-nothing.md` owns the argument.
**A programme that reads enforcement discretion as permission has read the wrong document.**

**And the standing refusals that go with it.** **FDA's Computer Software Assurance guidance is device
guidance under 21 CFR Part 820, as amended by the Quality Management System Regulation, and does not apply
to Helix**; it is adopted voluntarily and labelled at every citation — `ADR-0005`, `ADR-0026`. **GAMP 5 is
ISPE good-practice guidance and is never a requirement, and no software-category count appears anywhere in
this repository.** **NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 is a control catalogue Helix chose, not an obligation it is
under: no FIPS 199 categorization, no baseline, no ATO and no authorizing official.**

## 8. What a reader may legitimately take from the phase

Stated positively, once, so that the chapter is not only a list of refusals.

- **That a position exists at 2027-01-29 for every paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` on every one of the
  sixty-one systems**, on a method fixed and authorised before the first system was looked at — `GOV-21`.
- **That the position separates a control Helix requires of itself from a control that merely exists**, and
  that on two hundred and eighteen positions the answer is the second — `ADR-0031`, `IS-29`.
- **That the hundred and fifty-one failures are published with one cause, one paragraph and one named
  individual owner apiece** rather than as a count — `logs/control-gap-register.md`.
- **That `21 CFR 11.50` was tested against every human readable form each system produces**, which is what
  found the twelve — `ADR-0032`, `IS-30`.
- **That the eighty-nine inside enforcement discretion are published in the same words as the sixty-two
  outside it** — `ADR-0035`, `IS-33`.

**None of those is a statement about whether any Helix record can be relied on.** They are statements about
what Helix now knows about its own controls, on one day.

## 9. The standing framing this phase keeps

- **Part 11 applies to records, not to systems**, and no document here says "a Part 11 system" without
  saying which records make it one — `ADR-0001`.
- **A closed system is defined by who controls access** — `21 CFR 11.3(b)(4)`. **Hosting is not the test**,
  and all 61 are closed — `ADR-0011`. **A shared account does not make a system open**; what it defeats is
  attribution.
- **`21 CFR 11.3(b)` contains nine definitions and Part 11 contains ten sections.** There is no §11.60, no
  §11.400 and no Subpart D. Citations are exact and can be looked up.
- **Part 11 requires no reason-for-change field, no read-event logging, no cryptographic hashing under
  `21 CFR 11.70`, no multi-factor authentication under `21 CFR 11.200` and no password interval under
  `21 CFR 11.300(b)`.** Where Helix implements any of them, it is Helix's decision and this repository says
  so.
- **`21 CFR 11.30`'s additional measures are additive to the `21 CFR 11.10` controls it requires be
  included as appropriate, and never a substitute for them.**
- **FDA uses plain ALCOA**, and its own footnote 5 maps the attributes to the CGMP predicate rules and
  cites Part 11 nowhere. **The four additional attributes are MHRA's; MHRA itself refers to the shorter
  acronym, states there is no difference in expectations regardless of which is used, and defines all four.
  It does not reject them.** No originator is named for either set. **ALCOA does not map onto
  `21 CFR 11.10`.**
- **A Form FDA 483 is not a final agency determination**, a Warning Letter is informal and advisory, and
  only a court order binds. **No Warning Letter, Untitled Letter or judicial action of any kind exists**,
  and **no document in this repository reaches a legal conclusion of any kind.**
- **This programme does not forecast the outcome of any future FDA inspection**, and no document in this
  phase contains such a forecast. **The same refusal applies to the third-party audit and to the internal
  audit of the remediated programme. Neither has occurred and neither's result is anticipated anywhere.**
- **No entry in the four programme registers carries a likelihood, an impact or a score. None ever will.**
- **No document characterises any Helix employee, any auditor or any FDA investigator, and no document
  assesses any individual's work.**
- **All content about Helix is fictional and illustrative.**
