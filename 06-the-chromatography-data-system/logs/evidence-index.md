## 0. What an earlier phase said about this phase, and what is superseded

**Two statements published in Phase 03 and two published in Phase 05 need correcting or narrowing here, one
standing formulation carried since Phase 04 is widened, and
they are recorded rather than smoothed.**

**First.**
`../../03-data-integrity-risk-assessment-and-the-alcoa-attributes/03.08-what-cannot-be-determined.md §6`
hands **Phase 06** *"The chromatography data system pairs, including the two on which nothing at all could
be determined"*, and
`../../03-data-integrity-risk-assessment-and-the-alcoa-attributes/03.09-the-seven-assessed-for-the-first-time.md §9`
hands it *"The nine chromatography pairs, including the two on which nothing could be determined."*
**As published, both read as though Phase 06 would determine them. It has not, and to that extent both
statements are superseded.** Phase 06 examined `SYS-023` and `SYS-024` at depth and **converted no `ND`
into anything.** The ten pairs `ND-04` to `ND-13` stay unscored, `DP-16` is discharged only on the audit
trail behaviour limb, and `DP-17` and `DP-20` remain the closure routes. **Phase 03 is not edited**; the
originals stand with this correction recorded against them, on the discipline Phase 02's evidence index
`§0` set for `EV-129` and Phase 05's repeated for `HLX-DI-2026-5NN`. **No identifier already issued
changes meaning.**

**Second.**
`../../05-access-audit-trails-and-electronic-signatures/05.13-phase-summary-and-transition.md §6` directs
Phase 06 to *"Take `IS-05` and `IS-06` forward if the evidence permits."* **The evidence did not permit,
and the reason is recorded rather than left as a silence.** `IS-05` asks how long audit trail data is
retained relative to the retention period of the underlying record; **no retention period exists for any
of the five record types** — `IS-14` — so the relation has no second term, and the question is handed to
Phase 07 as part of `IS-42`. `IS-06` asks whether the audit trail forms part of the original record
reviewed under `21 CFR 211.194(a)(8)`; **this phase established what the audit trail on each instance
records and what it does not, and it did not say what a reviewer must do with one.** Both stay open,
neither is answered, and **neither is answered by implication.**

**Third.**
`../../05-access-audit-trails-and-electronic-signatures/05.13-phase-summary-and-transition.md §6` directs
Phase 06 to *"Draw its own conclusions about `OBS-2`, which are Phase 06's to draw."* **As published that
reads as a direction to conclude, and Phase 06 has not concluded; to that extent the statement is
narrowed.** What Phase 06 draws is a determination of what `OBS-2`'s subject matter consists of, per
instance, against the acts capable of changing a reportable result. **It draws no disposition, in either
direction** — `ADR-0041`, `DEC-613`, `../06.11-obs-2-stands.md` — and a disposition requires a periodic
review method, an internal audit position and a stated Helix response, none of which exists at this
vantage. **Phase 05 is not edited**; the original stands with this narrowing recorded against it. The
boundary was fixed at `DEC-602` on 2027-02-03, before any examination opened, and `EV-602` retains the
position paper.

**Fourth, and it is a widening rather than a correction.** Phases 04 and 05 carried the standing sentence
in the form *"no promotion of `IS-01`'s **fourth** candidate explanation by statement, by ordering or by
tone."* **Phase 06 carries it as *no promotion of **any of the four***, and the change is deliberate.**
The narrower form names one of the four in the act of refusing to promote it, and a phase whose technical
finding sits close to a different candidate cannot rely on a protection written around one. **The wider
form is strictly stronger and nothing is lost by it.** Phases 04 and 05 are **not edited**; the sentence
they published stands, and from this phase forward the wider form is the one carried — the discipline
Phase 02's evidence index `§0` set for `EV-129`. **`ADR-0012` is unchanged and no candidate is named
anywhere in this phase, by number or by content.**

**Fifth, and it is an addition rather than a correction.** **`EV-543` to `EV-600` are permanently unused
and are never issued**, on the same rule that left `EV-129`–`EV-200`, `EV-233`–`EV-300`,
`EV-337`–`EV-400` and `EV-441`–`EV-500` unused. **Phase 05 correctly recorded that Phase 06 would open at
`EV-601` and at document IDs `HLX-DI-2027-6NN`; both statements hold and neither is superseded.**

**Every item below exists at the vantage.** Nothing in this index is a plan, and no item is dated after
2027-02-26.

**The ordering discipline, which matters more in this phase than in any before it.** **The method items
pre-date the result items they govern, and the counting items post-date the records they count.** The
enumeration of acts at `EV-607` is dated 2027-02-09 and the two configuration reads at `EV-608` and
`EV-609` are dated 2027-02-10 — **because a cut drawn after the configuration is known is a cut drawn
around the configuration.** The population at `EV-614` and the reprocessing identification at `EV-615` are
dated 2027-02-16 and the thirty-eight examination records at `EV-616` are dated 2027-02-17. The register
at `EV-611` post-dates the thirty-four class records at `EV-610`; the register at `EV-617` post-dates the
records at `EV-616`; the reconciliations at `EV-613` and `EV-619` are dated **no earlier than** the items
they reconcile. **Where two items carry the same date the weaker relation is the one stated**, because a
strict *post-dates* asserted of a same-day pair is a claim the Date column does not support.

**What is not here.** **No executed validation package, no protocol, no test result and no validation
summary report produced under Phase 04's framework, and no system validation plan** — none exists for
either system. **No deviation record, no CAPA record, no remediation plan and no remediation cost** — none
arises from this phase. **No retention schedule entry, no backup specification and no archive
specification for `RT-12`, `RT-13`, `RT-14`, `RT-15` or `RT-17`, and nothing about `SYS-036`, `SYS-060` or
`SYS-061` beyond what Phase 02's register already publishes** — Phase 07. **No disposition of `OBS-2` and
no disposition of `OBS-4`** — Phase 08. **No characterisation of any reintegration.** **No periodic
review, no internal audit result, no third-party audit report and no inspection record after 2026-02-13.**

**Nothing in this index is a certification, an approval or an attestation of compliance by any agency.**
**There is no Part 11 certification**, and no artefact here could be one.

**Retention.** Each item is retained for at least the life of the determination, decision or register it
supports. For the three registers at `EV-611`, `EV-617` and `EV-621` that is for as long as the positions
they record are relied on and for the retention period of the records the two systems hold — **which is
not stated, because no retention period exists for any of the five record types** (`IS-14`, `DP-18`,
`IS-42`).

## 3. The ordering rule, stated

**Where an item counts, reconciles or concludes from other items, its date is on or after the date of the
last item it counts.** `EV-611` is dated 2027-02-12 and the class records at `EV-610` 2027-02-11;
`EV-613` post-dates `EV-611`'s inputs and shares its date, so the relation stated is **no earlier than**.
`EV-617` and `EV-616` share 2027-02-17, and **no earlier than** is the relation the Date column supports;
`EV-619` shares it too. `EV-621` is dated 2027-02-25 and the comparison records at `EV-620` 2027-02-19.

**Where an item authorises, enumerates or specifies, its date is before the date of the first item it
governs, and strictly before.** `EV-603` 2027-02-05 before `EV-608` 2027-02-10. `EV-607` 2027-02-09 before
`EV-608` and `EV-609` 2027-02-10. `EV-605` 2027-02-04 before `EV-616` 2027-02-17. `EV-614` and `EV-615`
2027-02-16 before `EV-616` 2027-02-17. **A rule settled after the results are in is not a rule**, and the
index is built so that the claim can be checked against the Date column rather than believed.
