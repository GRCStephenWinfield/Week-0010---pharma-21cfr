## 1. What this chapter settles

Every phase before this one asked its question of the whole estate. Phase 02 asked which records are Part 11
records and asked it of fifty-eight record types across the corrected inventory. Phase 03 asked five
attribute questions of every record type on every system that holds it. Phase 05 asked eleven questions of
every one of the sixty-one systems, and answered all of them. **Each of those phases produced a position
that covers the estate, and each of them produced it by asking a question that could be asked of the
estate.**

This phase asks a different kind of question, of two systems, and could not have asked it of sixty-one.

**This chapter says what that difference is, before any of the answers arrive.** It defines the depth
assessment as an instrument, distinguishes it from three things it will otherwise be mistaken for, states
why the population is two rather than sixty-one, and then makes the argument the phase most needs a reader
to carry away: **everything a depth assessment finds was, by construction, invisible to the method that
covered the estate — which means the estate-wide position understates, and Helix cannot say by how much.**

**It states no result.** What the configuration of either instance turned out to be is
`06.05-the-audit-trail-as-configured.md`'s; what the released results turned out to show is
`06.07-reintegration.md`'s; where the two instances turned out to differ is
`06.08-the-two-instances-compared.md`'s. This chapter cites those chapters and reports nothing from them.

## 2. What a depth assessment is

**A depth assessment is a determination made against the configuration of one named instance of one system,
against the acts that instance can and cannot record, and against a population of actual released results
produced on it, at a stated vantage, by named individuals, with the basis of each determination recorded.**

Six properties, and every one of them is load-bearing.

**It is made against a configuration, not against a product.** The subject is not "the chromatography data
system" as a product a vendor sells, nor the version Helix licenses, but the settings in force on one
installation in one laboratory on one date. Two installations of one product at one version are two
configurations and are assessed twice — `06.02-one-name-is-not-one-system.md` owns that argument and
`ADR-0037` records the decision.

**It is made against acts, not against features.** The question is not what the product is capable of
recording but whether the record shows what a person did to the number that leaves the laboratory.
`06.04-the-acts-that-change-a-result.md` owns that method and `ADR-0038` records it. The distinction is the
whole reason this instrument exists, because a review of a feature list can be performed from a desk and a
review of acts cannot.

**It is made against a population of actual records, not against a sample of the possible.** Phase 03 asked
whether a record type's attributes could be established. This phase asks what happened to a set of results
that were released — the one hundred and forty-two reportable results released across the two instances in
the twelve months to 2027-01-31 — and examines the records those releases rest on. **A determination about a
record type is a determination about a class. A determination about a released result is a determination
about a thing that went out of the door.**

**It is made per instance and it is never averaged.** Nothing in this phase produces a figure for "the
chromatography estate". Each instance carries its own determination, and where two determinations differ,
the difference is a finding rather than a rounding error — `ADR-0042`.

**It has a date and the date is not decorative.** The configuration position speaks as at this vantage. The
released-results population speaks about the twelve months to 2027-01-31 and about no other window. **A
depth finding is a photograph, not a guarantee**, and the same discipline `05.01 §2` applied to a control
position applies here with more force, because a configuration setting can be changed by one person in one
afternoon.

**And every determination has a named individual owner and a recorded basis.** What was read, what was
demonstrated, who read it and who watched — `ADR-0008`, and the register carries it.

## 3. The unit, and why it is not any unit this programme has used before

The programme has now used four units of determination, and they are not interchangeable. Setting them side
by side is the quickest way to see what this phase's unit adds and what it costs.

| Phase | The unit | The question it can answer | The question it cannot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 02 | **The record type** | Is this class of record a Part 11 record? | Anything about the state of any instance of it |
| Phase 03 | **The record type on the system that holds it** | Can each of the five attributes be established for this class on this system? | Whether a particular released result was altered, and whether anyone could tell |
| Phase 04 | **The system, graded twice** | What evidence does the framework require about this system, and to what depth? | Whether any of that evidence exists |
| Phase 05 | **A paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` on a system** | Is the control described in this paragraph present on this system now? | What the control, being present, actually records |
| **Phase 06** | **An act, on one instance — and a released result, on one instance** | **Does the record show what a person did to the number that left the laboratory?** | **Anything at all about any system not assessed at this depth** |

**The last column of the last row is the price.** Every earlier unit was cheap enough to apply to the whole
population, and every one of them was defined so that it could be. Phase 05's unit is a paragraph and a
system, and a paragraph of a regulation can be held against a system by one competent person reading a
configuration screen and an SOP. **This phase's unit is an act performed by a person on an instrument, and
to hold it against a system you have to know what the people in that laboratory can actually do, what the
product does when they do it, and what one hundred and forty-two released results actually record.**

**A unit that can be applied to sixty-one systems is a unit that has been designed to be applied to
sixty-one systems, and the design has consequences.** That is not a criticism of Phase 05, whose unit is
exactly right for the question Phase 05 asks. It is the observation that the questions a programme can
afford to ask of everything are a particular kind of question, and the kind is narrower than it looks from
inside.

## 4. What a depth assessment is not

Three misreadings are foreseeable, each of them by a competent reader, and each is refused here rather than
in a footnote to a register.

### 4.1 It is not validation

**A depth assessment is not validation, and neither substitutes for the other.** `ADR-0034`, taken at
`DEC-505` on 2026-12-17 by Colm Ó Braonáin, fixed that for a control position; the same rule governs here,
and it governs harder, because a depth assessment produces something that looks far more like validation
evidence than a control position does.

It looks like it for a specific reason. A depth assessment involves demonstrations. Somebody sits at the
instrument, performs an act, and looks at what the record shows. **That is procedurally indistinguishable
from a test script being executed, and it is not one.**

| | **Validation** | **A depth assessment** |
|---|---|---|
| **The question** | Does the system do what it was specified to do, and keep doing it? | What does the record show when a person does this, on this instance, now? |
| **What comes first** | A specification, and an acceptance basis written before execution | **Nothing is specified. The assessment asks what happens, and records it** |
| **What a failure means** | The system did not meet its specification | **Nothing failed.** The record shows what it shows |
| **Who reviews it** | A second person who did not produce the evidence, against the acceptance basis | The determination's approver, against the method — not against an acceptance basis, because there is none |
| **What it produces** | Evidence that a specification was met | **A determination about a state of affairs** |

**A demonstration performed without a pre-written acceptance basis is not a test, however carefully it is
performed.** It cannot be, because there was nothing to accept against. That is not a defect in the
instrument — the assessment is asking an open question and an open question has no acceptance criterion —
but it is exactly why the output may never be filed as validation evidence.

**The evidence Phase 04's framework requires is owed in full and is not reduced by one line of this phase.**
The framework requires evidence for all eleven paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10`, `(a)` to `(k)`, for every
system in scope, and none of it has been produced. `IS-34` carries that. **A depth assessment that finds a
configuration adequate has not validated anything, and a depth assessment that finds a configuration
inadequate has not failed a validation either.**

### 4.2 It is not an audit

**Nothing in this phase is an audit, and nobody in this phase audited anybody.**

An audit is what Gideon Halvorsen's function performs, or what a third party performs, and its defining
property is that the person performing it is independent of the work being examined. **The two people who
know most about what a chromatography data system records are the two people who use it and the person who
looks after it, and this assessment was performed with all three in the room.** That is the right way to
find out what a system does. It is the wrong way to produce an audit finding, and the assessment does not
claim to.

Three consequences follow and the phase holds all three.

**No finding is raised.** A finding is the auditor's, and this phase raises none. What it produces are
determinations, entered in registers that belong to the programme, and issues where a determination is a
problem now — `01.12 §6` owns the vocabulary and this phase applies it without variation.

**No individual's work is assessed.** *"No document characterises any Helix employee, any auditor or any
FDA investigator, and no document assesses any individual's work."* An analyst who reintegrated a
chromatogram in April 2026 did what the system permitted, under the procedures in force, and nothing in
this phase says anything about that person. **A cause is a property of the estate, not a judgement about
anybody.**

**And nothing here bears on the four internal audits.** `IS-01` asks why four internal audits between
2023-06 and 2025-09, each covering at least two of the three systems the electronic-records observations
concern, raised nothing on their subject matter. **A depth assessment performed in February 2027 is not
evidence about an audit performed in 2023**, and `06.12-what-this-does-not-establish.md §5` states the
position in the terms `ADR-0012` fixed.

### 4.3 It is not a disposition

**A depth assessment establishes what an observation's subject matter is. It does not close the
observation, reduce it, answer it or respond to it.**

`OBS-2` is the Form FDA 483's, in the 483's own numbering, and it is never renumbered, regrouped or given
an identifier of the programme's own — `ADR-0006`. Its subject matter is audit trail review in the QC
laboratory. **This phase is the first in the programme that can say, in technical terms, what reviewing
those audit trails would and would not have reached** — and saying so is not disposing of anything.

**Disposition requires three things that do not exist at this vantage**: a periodic review method, an
internal audit position on the remediated programme, and a stated response. `06.11-obs-2-stands.md` argues
that and `ADR-0041` records it. **An examination is not a disposition**, and a phase that produced the
technical answer and then quietly wrote the closing sentence would have disposed of an inspectional
observation in a chapter nobody approved for the purpose.

## 5. Why two of sixty-one

Two reasons, and they are different in kind. Both are stated because either one alone would be a weaker
answer than the pair.

**The first is that the inspectional observation named these systems.** `OBS-2` concerns audit trail review
in the QC laboratory on the chromatography data system. The programme has recorded that observation since
Phase 02 — at `02.06`, at `03.09`, at `05.05 §6` and elsewhere — and has concluded nothing from it in any
of them, deliberately, because until this phase nobody had established what the audit trails on those
instances record. **A programme that had spent five phases recording a link and never once opened the thing
the link points at would have been keeping a bookmark rather than doing work.**

**The second is arithmetic and it is not a comfortable one.** Depth of this kind cannot be spent on
sixty-one systems. It is not that it would be expensive; it is that the instrument does not scale, because
what makes it deep is precisely the part that cannot be industrialised — sitting with the people who
operate the instrument, establishing what their permissions actually let them do, watching the product
behave, and then reading a year of released results one at a time.

**Both reasons are needed, and stating only one would misrepresent the choice.**

Stating only the first — *we assessed what FDA looked at* — would describe a programme that goes where it
is pointed. `01.09 §1`'s sentence about the company whose own audits looked at something and passed it is
the standing warning against exactly that posture, and a programme that only examines at depth what an
inspector has already named has arranged never to find anything first.

Stating only the second — *depth is expensive, so we did two* — would leave the reader to assume the two
were chosen on some rational estate-wide criterion of risk. **They were not.** They were chosen because the
observation named them, and the fact that they would also have been defensible choices on other grounds is
not the reason they were chosen. Phase 03 rated pairs and `ADR-0021`'s discipline holds: **a band confers
no ordering, and nothing in the quality system yet says that a pair rated one way is examined before a pair
rated another.**

**So the honest statement is: the observation set the population, and the cost of the instrument means the
population could not have been extended much beyond it.** Both halves are true and neither is flattering.

## 6. What depth costs

The cost is stated as work, because work is the thing a later phase can plan and a figure is not. **No
remediation cost and no programme re-costing appears anywhere in this phase** — `06.12-what-this-does-not-establish.md`.

| What the instrument required | Why it cannot be industrialised |
|---|---|
| **The permission sets the estate actually grants on each instance**, established with the Chief Information Officer's account administration and read against what the laboratory's own people can do in practice | A permission set can be exported. **What a person can do with it cannot**, and the gap between the two is where the assessment lives |
| **The product's documented behaviour, read against each instance's configuration** — not the product's marketing material and not a vendor statement about compliance | **A vendor may say a product provides features that support compliance. Compliance is a property of Helix's validated system in its intended use**, and no supplier can sell it and FDA does not confer it |
| **The acts walked with the QC analysts who perform them**, at both laboratories, separately | The list of things a person does to a chromatogram is not written down anywhere at Helix, which is the finding underneath the method |
| **Demonstration of each act on each instance, with the resulting record read** | Two instances means every demonstration twice. **Every determination in this phase is made twice or it is not made** — `ADR-0037` |
| **One hundred and forty-two released results examined individually** across the two instances, for the twelve months to 2027-01-31 | A population this size can be read. A population an order of magnitude larger would have been sampled, and **a sample answers a different question from the one this phase asked** |
| **A comparison across the two instances, performed as a distinct act** | The comparison is not a by-product of the two determinations. `06.02 §5` owns that argument and `ADR-0042` records it |

**Multiply the middle rows by sixty-one and the instrument stops being an instrument.** That is the whole
of the cost argument, and it is why `ADR-0040` exists.

## 7. Everything this phase found was invisible to the estate-wide method

**This is the argument the chapter exists for.**

Take the three methods the programme has already run at estate scale and ask, of each, what it would have
had to be in order to find what a depth assessment finds.

**Phase 03 asked whether five attributes could be established for a record type on a system.** For the
chromatography record types on both instances it answered, largely, that they could not — the ten pairs are
unscored and carry the identifiers `ND-04` to `ND-13`, `ND-04` to `ND-08` on `SYS-023` and `ND-09` to
`ND-13` on `SYS-024`. **An unscored pair is a gap, not a low score**, and `ADR-0018`'s rule that an absence
of evidence scores nothing is the reason the register does not pretend otherwise. But notice what *Not
determinable* is: it is the method reporting that it cannot see. **It is a correct answer and it is
uninformative about everything the depth assessment went on to establish**, because the method that
produced it never opened a configuration or read a released result.

**Phase 05 asked, of `21 CFR 11.10(e)`, whether the control described in the paragraph is present on the
system.** That question has an answer and the register carries it for both instances. **But the paragraph
describes a property — *"Use of secure, computer-generated, time-stamped audit trails to independently
record the date and time of operator entries and actions that create, modify, or delete electronic
records…"* — and a system either exhibits that property or does not.** It is entirely possible for a system
to exhibit it and for the audit trail it produces to be silent about the specific act that changes the
number a batch record carries, **because the paragraph does not enumerate acts and no regulation does.**
`06.04-the-acts-that-change-a-result.md` is the whole of that argument.

**Phase 04 graded systems for complexity and reliability and set the evidence the framework requires.** It
required evidence. **An instrument that requires evidence produces none** — `04.11 §2` said so about itself
— and the requirement is undischarged.

Put together:

**The estate-wide methods asked questions that could be answered without opening the system, and they
answered them correctly. The depth assessment asked a question that could only be answered by opening it,
and what it found was, by construction, outside the reach of every method that came before.**

**The consequence is that the estate-wide position understates.** Not "may understate" and not "understates
in some cases" — understates, as a matter of the method's construction, because a method that cannot see a
class of fact will report the absence of that class of fact as a silence rather than as a finding. Phase
02's own sentence covers it exactly: **"A negative that nobody can test is not a determination; it is a
silence."**

**And Helix cannot say by how much.** There is no calibration factor. The programme cannot state that the
estate-wide method finds four-fifths of what a depth assessment would find, or half, or a tenth, because
the only two data points it has are the two instances where both methods were run, and two instances of one
product in one laboratory function are not a sample of an estate of sixty-one systems spanning
manufacturing execution, packaging, warehousing, laboratory information management and the rest.
**Extrapolating from them would be inventing a number and dressing it as an inference.**

## 8. The corollary, and it is not resolved here

**The reader is entitled to ask the next question, and it is the uncomfortable one: do the other fifty-nine
systems conceal comparable findings?**

**The truthful answer is that Helix does not know, and has not assessed them at this depth.**

That answer is unsatisfying and it is not going to be improved by rephrasing. What the chapter can do is
say precisely what is and is not known, so that the unsatisfying answer is at least a precise one.

| The question | What Helix can say at this vantage |
|---|---|
| Do the other fifty-nine hold record types where an act of this kind is possible? | **Not established.** The question has never been asked of them in these terms. Phase 02's registers record which record types each system holds; **no register records which acts each system permits**, because until this phase no method had a concept of an act |
| Would the estate-wide methods have found such an act if it existed? | **No**, for the reasons at `§7`. That is a property of the methods and it does not depend on any fact about the fifty-nine |
| Is the estate-wide position therefore wrong? | **No. It is correct about what it assessed and it understates**, which is a different thing. A position that is correct within its own frame and silent outside it is not a false position; **it is a position whose frame has to be published with it** |
| By how much does it understate? | **Nobody can state it, and this phase will not estimate it.** No entry in this programme's registers carries a likelihood, an impact or a score, and an estimate here would be a score with a paragraph wrapped round it |
| Which of the fifty-nine should be assessed next, and in what order? | **Not this phase's, and not answered by implication.** An ordering is a prioritisation, and a prioritisation is a remediation plan in a thin disguise. **This phase states no remediation plan** |

**Two of the fifty-nine are named in this phase and are named for a reason that makes the corollary sharper
rather than softer.** `RT-12` and `RT-13` — the chromatographic raw data file and acquisition metadata, and
the chromatographic integration and reprocessing record — are also held on `SYS-036`, `SYS-060` and
`SYS-061`. **The same record types, on systems that were not assessed at this depth, and two of the three
under a different named individual owner.** `06.10-what-else-holds-these-records.md` records the position
and hands it to Phase 07; **this chapter names it because a corollary illustrated by a concrete instance is
harder to file away than one stated in the abstract**, and states nothing about what those three systems
hold beyond what Phase 02's register already publishes.

**The corollary is not resolved and the phase does not pretend to resolve it.** What it does instead is
refuse two false resolutions.

**The first false resolution is silence.** A phase that published two systems' worth of depth findings and
said nothing about the other fifty-nine would have left the reader to assume either that the findings
generalise or that they do not, and both assumptions are unsupported. **A programme that has not written
down what it is not doing will be assumed to be doing all of it.**

**The second false resolution is generalisation.** A phase that published a depth finding and wrote *and
the same is likely to be true across the estate* would have converted two observations into fifty-nine
assertions in one sentence, at no cost and with no evidence. **That is the sentence `ADR-0040` exists to
forbid**, and the reason it is forbidden is not caution. It is that a generalised depth finding is
unfalsifiable: nobody can test it, nobody can close it, and it will be quoted long after anybody remembers
that it was an extrapolation from two.

## 9. Three objections, answered

Each of the three was raised while the method was being authorised, each is reasonable, and each was
refused for a reason worth writing down. `GOV-26` carries the authorisation and `ADR-0040` carries the
rule; this section carries the arguments that did not win.

### 9.1 "Take a sample of the estate at this depth instead of a census of two"

**The strongest of the three, and the one that would have produced a more generalisable result.** A
stratified sample across the sixty-one — a manufacturing execution system, a packaging line system, a
laboratory information management system, an environmental monitoring system — assessed at this depth would
have supported a statement about the estate that two instances of one product cannot support. **That is a
real advantage and the refusal is not a denial of it.**

It was refused on three grounds.

**The inspectional observation would have gone unexamined.** A sample containing one chromatography
instance would have produced a position on one of the two systems `OBS-2` concerns, and a phase that
examined half of the observation's subject matter in order to say something more general about the estate
would have subordinated the specific obligation to the general ambition. **The observation named two
laboratories and Helix had never compared them.**

**A sample of one instrument class per stratum does not license a statement about the stratum.** The
instrument's whole content is configuration, permission and act, and those are properties of an
installation. **Two installations of one product at one version differ** — `06.08-the-two-instances-compared.md`
is the demonstration and this chapter does not restate it. A method whose own output shows that two
installations of one product can diverge cannot then treat one installation as representative of a class of
different products.

**And a sample produces an estimate, which is the one thing this phase refuses to produce.** A sample with
a stated coverage is an invitation to extrapolate, and extrapolation from it would be a score in prose.
**No entry in this programme's registers carries a likelihood, an impact or a score.**

### 9.2 "Build a cheap proxy and run it across the estate"

**Proposed as the practical answer to the corollary at `§8`**: derive, from what the depth assessment
learned, a short set of questions that a system owner could answer without a walk, and put them to all
sixty-one. It would produce estate-wide coverage in a fraction of the effort.

**Refused, and it is the closest of the three calls**, because something like it is very likely to be the
right instrument at some later point in this programme.

It lost on what a proxy would have to assume. **The depth assessment's finding is that the act a person
performs and the class the product records are two different populations that have to be compared**, and a
proxy question — *does your system record changes to results?* — asks a system owner to perform that
comparison in their head, unaided, about a system nobody has walked. **A questionnaire that asks somebody
to do the hard part and report the answer has not made the hard part cheaper. It has relocated it to a
place where nobody can see whether it was done.**

There is a second reason and it is the more important one. **A proxy run across sixty-one systems would
produce sixty-one answers, and those answers would be indistinguishable in a register from two answers
produced by a depth assessment.** Within a year, the distinction would survive only in a footnote.
`ADR-0040` exists precisely to keep the depth of a determination attached to it, and authorising an
instrument whose output would sit in the same column as this phase's would have defeated the decision in
the phase that took it.

**What is not refused is the instrument's later development.** A method whose questions are derived from
this phase, whose depth is stated, and whose output is recorded in a distinct register with a distinct
identifier series is a legitimate thing for a later phase to build. **This phase does not build it, does
not design it, and does not commit any later phase to it.**

### 9.3 "Hold the finding until the estate is covered"

**Proposed on the ground that a partial position invites misreading**, which is true, and that publishing a
depth finding about two systems while saying nothing about fifty-nine leaves the reader without the frame
they need. **A finding published with an honest disclaimer is still a finding read without one.**

Refused, and the refusal is the programme's oldest reflex applied to a new object. **"A history withheld
until its cause is known is a history withheld."** The same holds for an assessment: a finding withheld
until it can be generalised is a finding withheld, and the generalisation may never arrive. **The remedy
for a partial position is to state the partiality in the same document, at the same version, as a numbered
chapter** — which is what `§7` and `§8` are and why they sit before the decision rather than after it.

There is also a practical objection to waiting that the phase records without dwelling on it. **What a
configuration records is a fact about a system as it is configured today**, and a determination held back
for a year is a determination about a state of affairs that may no longer exist when it is published.

## 10. Carrying a depth finding forward without generalising it

The rule at `ADR-0040` is easy to state and easy to breach, because the breach is usually a compression
rather than a claim. Four forms the breach takes, each named so that a later phase can recognise it in its
own draft.

| The form | What it looks like | Why it is a breach |
|---|---|---|
| **The dropped qualifier** | *"Helix's audit trails do not record changes to integration parameters."* | The subject has silently become the estate. **The determination was about two named instances** |
| **The plural of convenience** | *"the chromatography data systems"*, used as a single subject with a single position | `06.02-one-name-is-not-one-system.md` owns this one. Two instances take two determinations, and a sentence with one verb has made one |
| **The transferred inference** | *"and the archive systems hold the same records, so the same applies"* | Sharing a record type is not sharing a configuration. `SYS-036`, `SYS-060` and `SYS-061` were not assessed — `06.10-what-else-holds-these-records.md` |
| **The reassuring inversion** | *"no comparable issue has been identified elsewhere in the estate"* | True, and misleading, because nobody looked. **Unassessed is not adverse, and unassessed is not favourable** |

**The fourth is the one to watch, because it is the only one of the four that a well-intentioned reader
produces while trying to be accurate.** It is literally true that no comparable issue has been identified
elsewhere. What the sentence conceals is the reason, and the reason is that the question was not asked.
**A statement whose truth depends entirely on an absence of enquiry is a statement that has to carry the
absence of enquiry with it.**

## 11. `ADR-0040` — the depth of an assessment is stated

**`ADR-0040`, taken at `DEC-611` on 2027-02-18 by Colm Ó Braonáin, records that the depth of an assessment
is stated wherever its result is reported, and that a finding made at one depth is never generalised to
systems not assessed at that depth.**

The decision has four limbs and each of them is a rule somebody will otherwise breach in good faith.

**Every determination carries its depth.** A register row, a chapter, a governance record or a management
report that states a Phase 06 determination states, in the same place, that it was made against two
instances at a depth not available for the other fifty-nine. **The qualifier travels with the finding and
is not left behind in the chapter that introduced it.**

**No depth finding is extended, by statement or by implication, to a system not assessed at that depth.**
Not to `SYS-036`, `SYS-060` or `SYS-061`, which hold two of the same record types; not to the other
laboratory systems; not to the estate. **Sharing a record type is not sharing a configuration**, and
sharing a product would not be either.

**The understatement is published as a property of the estate-wide position rather than as a caveat on this
one.** The estate-wide position understates; the phase says so; and **the amount is stated as
unquantifiable rather than estimated, bounded, characterised or described as small.**

**And the absence of an assessment is recorded as an absence, not as a favourable position.** *"The
position is unassessed, and unassessed is not adverse."* The converse is equally binding and is the limb
most easily lost: **unassessed is not favourable either.** Fifty-nine systems on which no depth assessment
has been performed are fifty-nine systems about which this phase says nothing at all.

`IS-41` carries the consequence as an issue rather than as a reassurance, and `logs/raid-log.md` holds it.

  the comparison across instances is `06.08-the-two-instances-compared.md`'s. **No count from any of them
  appears here.**
- **It disposes of nothing, and of `OBS-2` least of all.**
  `06.11-obs-2-stands.md` states the position and `ADR-0041` records it. **`OBS-4`'s disposition is Phase
  08's and is not reached here either.**
- **It states no validation outcome.** No package has been executed, no protocol has been run, no test
  result exists, and the evidence Phase 04's framework requires is owed in full — `ADR-0034`, `IS-34`.
- **It raises no CAPA and no deviation, states no remediation plan, no remediation cost, no sequence and no
  priority**, and nothing in it authorises any of those.
- **It re-scores no Phase 03 pair and re-determines no Phase 05 cell.** The ten pairs stay unscored and the
  twenty-two control positions stand as published. **Phase 06 evidences `CG-15` and `CG-05` and changes
  neither.**
- **It states no retention, backup or archive rule**, and nothing about what `SYS-036`, `SYS-060` or
  `SYS-061` hold beyond what Phase 02's register publishes — Phase 07.
- **It sets no audit trail review frequency.** **No primary source names a stand-alone periodic audit trail
  review frequency.** FDA names the frequencies to be **inherited** — review after each significant step
  under `21 CFR 211.188(b)`, and data review before batch release under `21 CFR 211.22` — and leaves the
  firm to determine one only where CGMP specifies none for the underlying data, **which is the only place
  FDA's risk-assessment sentence operates.** **FDA's own closing requirement runs to the whole approach**:
  it *"should ensure that CGMP requirements are met, appropriate controls are implemented, and the
  reliability of the review is proven."* **This phase names none**, and `IS-04` stays deliberately
  unresolved.
- **It characterises no reintegration**, as proper, improper, justified or unjustified — `ADR-0043`.
- **It reaches no legal conclusion**, characterises nothing as a violation of anything, assesses no
  individual's work, and forecasts no inspection outcome. **A Form FDA 483 is not a final agency
  determination.**
- **Nothing here bears on `IS-01`**, which stays open and stays unweighted, and **no candidate explanation
  is promoted by statement, by ordering or by tone** — `ADR-0012`.
