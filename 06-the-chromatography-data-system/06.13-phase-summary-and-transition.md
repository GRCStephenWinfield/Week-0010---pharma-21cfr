## 1. What Phase 06 established, in order

**One. That everything needed to see two divergences had been published for months.** Four registers carry
rows for `SYS-023` and `SYS-024`, and in each of them the two systems appear as two rows with other rows
between them. `06.03-what-the-registers-already-say.md` is the first document in the programme to put the
four side by side. **Nothing in it is new; the arrangement is.**

**Two. That the audit trail on each instance had to be assessed against acts and not against the vendor's
list.** `ADR-0038`, `DEC-605`, Priyanka Venkataraman, 2027-02-10. The enumeration of acts capable of
changing a reportable result was completed and recorded at `EV-607` on 2027-02-09, **before either
configuration was read on 2027-02-10** — because a cut drawn after the configuration is known is a cut
drawn around the configuration.

**Three. The configuration itself.** The product offers **34 auditable event classes. Enabled on both 17 ·
Ridgemont only 9 · Cary only 2 · neither 6. 17 + 9 + 2 + 6 = 34. Ridgemont 26 · Cary 19.**

**Four. The cut that matters.** **Eleven of the 34 record an act capable of changing a reportable result:
both 6 · Ridgemont only 2 · Cary only 0 · neither 3. 6 + 2 + 0 + 3 = 11.** The other 23 split
**11 · 7 · 2 · 3**, and the columns reconcile **6 + 11 = 17 · 2 + 7 = 9 · 0 + 2 = 2 · 3 + 3 = 6**. **Of
the six enabled on neither, four were choices and two cannot be enabled on this product version**, and one
of the two is the class recording **a change to integration parameters applied to a single injection at
review time.**

**Five. The inversion.** **Reviewing the audit trail would not have found the act that changes the
reportable result.** `OBS-2` stands exactly as written and is not softened; **what Phase 06 adds is that
beginning to review would not, by itself, have reached this act — which makes the remediation larger, not
smaller.** `IS-36`, `ADR-0041`.

**Six. The reprocessing examination.** **142 reportable results released in the twelve months to
2027-01-31 — Ridgemont 86, Cary 56. 38 produced from a reprocessed integration — 21 and 17. 9 carrying a
reported value that differs from the value the first integration produced — 5 and 4. And on none of the 38
does the record show both values.** `21 CFR 11.10(e)` requires that record changes not obscure previously
recorded information. **No reintegration is characterised either way** — `ADR-0043`. `IS-38`.

**Seven. The two divergences, both published and neither noticed.** `21 CFR 11.10(f)` is *Met and
required* on `SYS-023` and *Met but not required* on `SYS-024`; on `RT-17`, *original or a true copy* is
*Not determinable* at Ridgemont and *Not met* at Cary. **Neither determination is wrong, and a difference
between two instances is a finding about the estate rather than about either instance** — `ADR-0042`.
`IS-37`.

**Eight. The transcription break.** On `SYS-024` the reportable result reaches the LIMS by manual
transcription; on `SYS-023` it transfers electronically. Phase 03's `IS-18` lands on
`21 CFR 211.68(b)`'s second sentence — **a *shall*** — and stays open. `IS-39`.

**Nine. The honest limit, owned rather than left for a later phase to find.** **Two of sixty-one systems
were assessed at a depth not available for the other fifty-nine, and everything this phase found was
invisible to the estate-wide method.** The estate-wide position understates, **by an amount nobody can
state.** `IS-41`, `ADR-0040`. **And `RT-12` and `RT-13` are also held on `SYS-036`, `SYS-060` and
`SYS-061` — two of them owned by Amara Sissoko rather than Dr Sunita Raghunathan, and none of the three
assessed at depth.** `IS-40`.

**Ten. Three registers, and they are the phase.** `logs/auditable-event-register.md`,
`logs/reintegration-register.md` and `logs/instance-comparison-register.md`. **Every count above is
computed in one of the three and in no other place.**

the `21 CFR 11.10(e)` position was weakest. **That part is answered.** The position on `SYS-023` and
`SYS-024` is now known as more than a determination — thirty-four event classes with their configuration on

and twenty-four points at which the two instances were read across the row.

**What has not closed, and what `IS-35` never asked, is what holds any of it in place.** `IS-29` carries
the two hundred and eighteen unrequired controls and does not close by the 218 being reduced. **`IS-42`
carries the durability of the data itself and is Phase 07's.** **The mechanism — periodic review, where a
state either becomes a control or stays a configuration — is Phase 08's.**

**The closure names its evidence.** *Believed complete* is not a closure condition — `01.12 §9`.

**And the pattern is now six deep.** `IS-02` recorded that no written determination existed. `IS-10`
recorded that the determination existed and nothing required it to be maintained. `IS-21` recorded that the
assessment existed and nothing said what to do about it. `IS-28` recorded that the requirement existed and
nothing had been done under it. `IS-35` recorded that the position existed and nothing held it in place.
**`IS-42` records that the record cannot show what happened and nobody knows where the material that could
is kept.** Each phase closed the previous question by producing the artefact and found the artefact
upstream of what anybody wanted.

## 7. The question Phase 06 leaves open

> **The record cannot show what happened, and the data it cannot show it about is retained somewhere.
> Nobody has established for how long, in what form, or whether the copy that survives is a true one.**

**`IS-42`**, raised at publication, owned by Dr Sunita Raghunathan, open.

Take the three facts in order. **The record cannot show what happened**: on thirty-eight reprocessed
results the value the first integration produced is absent from the record a reviewer opens, and on two
instances the class that would record a change to integration parameters at review time cannot be enabled
at all. **The comparison that produced the nine was therefore made against material obtained from outside
that record** — processing history, sequence-level processing records, archived first-pass result sets.
**And that material is held on systems this phase did not assess**, two of them under a different named
individual owner, one of them found by walking rather than by reading.

**So the evidence that the record is incomplete is itself held somewhere nobody has described.** That is
not a paradox and it is not an emergency; it is the next question, and it is Phase 07's. `21 CFR 11.10(e)`
requires audit trail documentation to be retained *"for a period at least as long as that required for the
subject electronic records"*, **and there is no such period.** `21 CFR 211.68(b)` requires a backup file of
data entered into the system **except where certain data — *"such as calculations performed in connection
with laboratory analysis"* — are eliminated by computerization or other automated processes, and in such
instances requires instead that a written record of the program be maintained along with appropriate
validation data.** It separately requires that hard copy or alternative systems designed to assure that
backup data are exact and complete and secure from alteration, inadvertent erasures or loss be maintained.
**Which limb reaches `RT-12`, `RT-13`, `RT-14`, `RT-15` and `RT-17` is a determination nobody at Helix has
made**, and none of these obligations has been assessed against anything on these five systems.

**Phase 06 is the shortest phase in the programme, and this is what twenty-eight days on two systems
bought: three registers, one closure, seven issues, and a question that is larger than the one the phase
opened with.**
