# CHANGELOG — Phase 06: The Chromatography Data System

## 1.0 — 2027-02-26

First issue of the Phase 06 record set, speaking as at the vantage of **2027-02-26**.

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| **Two instances, never “the CDS”.** `SYS-023` at Ridgemont and `SYS-024` at Cary are one product at one version in two laboratories, and **every determination in this phase is made twice or it is not made** | `06.02`, ADR-0037, `DEC-603` |
| **The audit trail is assessed against the acts that change a reportable result, not against the vendor's event list.** A configuration review that ticks off the vendor's classes measures the product; **Helix needs to know whether the record shows what someone did to the number that leaves the laboratory** | `06.04`, ADR-0038, `DEC-605` |
| **34 auditable event classes: enabled on both 17 · Ridgemont only 9 · Cary only 2 · neither 6. Ridgemont 26, Cary 19.** **11 of the 34 record an act capable of changing a reportable result, partitioning 6 / 2 / 0 / 3** | `06.05`, auditable event register |
| **THE FINDING THAT INVERTS THE OBSERVATION — three of the eleven result-changing classes are recorded on neither instance, and two of the three cannot be enabled at all on this product version.** One of the two records **a change to integration parameters applied to a single injection at review time**. **Reviewing the audit trail would not have found the act that changes the reportable result** — and that **does not excuse the failure to review, does not reduce `OBS-2`, and makes the remediation larger rather than smaller**, because configuration and review are now both owed | `06.06`, ADR-0041, `IS-36` |
| **FDA has published part of the benchmark, for this instrument class, by name.** The December 2018 drug CGMP data integrity Q&A states that *"the audit trail for a high performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) run should include the user name, date/time of the run, the integration parameters used, and details of a reprocessing, if any"* — **a nonbinding *should* in a guidance, partial rather than exhaustive**, and the anchor the eleven acts are consistent with and go beyond | `06.04 §7.3`, ADR-0038 |
| **142 reportable results released in the twelve months to 2027-01-31 — 86 Ridgemont, 56 Cary. 38 came from a reprocessed integration — 21 and 17. 9 of the 38 carry a value that differs from the value the first integration produced — 5 and 4. On none of the 38 does the record show both values** | `06.07`, reintegration register, `IS-38` |
| **`21 CFR 11.10(e)` requires that record changes not obscure previously recorded information**, and **a record that shows one value cannot distinguish a justified reintegration from an unjustified one** — which is exactly why **no reintegration in this phase is characterised either way** | `06.07`, ADR-0043, `DEC-617` |
| **Part 11 imposes no reason-for-change requirement.** `21 CFR 211.100(b)` and `21 CFR 211.160(a)` require deviations to be **recorded and justified**, and FDA's December 2018 Q&A says documentation should include change justification for the reprocessing — **three distinct provisions, none of them `21 CFR 11.10(e)`** | `06.07 §4` |
| **THE TWO INSTANCES DIFFER IN TWO PUBLISHED REGISTERS AND WERE COMPARED IN NEITHER.** `21 CFR 11.10(f)` is *Met and required* at Ridgemont and *Met but not required* at Cary — **at Cary the sequencing check is present and no procedure requires it, so it can be withdrawn without a deviation**. On `RT-17`, *"original or a true copy"* is Not determinable at Ridgemont and Not met at Cary. **Both were published. Nobody read across the row** | `06.08`, ADR-0042, `IS-37` |
| **The transcription break.** On `SYS-024` the reportable result reaches the LIMS **by hand**; on `SYS-023` it transfers electronically. `21 CFR 211.68(b)`'s second sentence — *"Input to and output from the computer or related system of formulas or other records or data shall be checked for accuracy."* — **is a *shall***, and the third sentence graduates **the degree and frequency of that verification and nothing else** | `06.09`, `IS-39` |
| **`RT-12` and `RT-13` are also held on `SYS-036`, `SYS-060` and `SYS-061`** — two of them owned by a different named individual — **and none of the three was assessed at depth** | `06.10`, `IS-40` |
| **THE HONEST LIMIT, owned by the phase rather than left for a later one to find.** **Two of sixty-one systems were assessed at a depth unavailable for the other fifty-nine, and everything found was invisible to the estate-wide method.** **The estate-wide position understates — and nobody can say by how much**, so no figure, proportion, range or ordering is offered | `06.01`, ADR-0040, `IS-41` |
| The question left open: **the record cannot show what happened, and the data it cannot show it about is retained somewhere. Nobody has established for how long, in what form, or whether the copy that survives is a true one** | `06.13`, `IS-42`, `DEC-618` |
