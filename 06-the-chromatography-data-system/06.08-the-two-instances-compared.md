*Two divergences, both published months before this phase opened, and neither noticed — because nobody read across the row.*

---

## 1. What this chapter reports

**Two cells in two published registers, and the fact that nothing in six phases had put them next to each
other.**

`SYS-023` and `SYS-024` are the same product at the same version, in two Helix laboratories, with the same
named individual owner, the same five Part 11 record types and the same closed determination. **They are
assessed separately and never as one object** — `ADR-0037`, argued at `06.02-one-name-is-not-one-system.md`
and cited here without rebuilding.

**On `21 CFR 11.10(f)`, Phase 05 published a different determination for each.** **On `RT-17`'s *original
or a true copy* attribute, Phase 03 published a different determination for each.** Both were published in
controlled registers, on 2027-01-29 and 2026-09-30 respectively. **Neither was noticed, because nobody read
across the row.** `logs/instance-comparison-register.md` is the register that now does, at twenty-four
points; this chapter reads the two that disagree and were already there.

**Neither determination is wrong.** That matters enough to say before either is discussed, because a
divergence between two correct cells is the finding and a divergence between a correct cell and a mistaken
one would merely be an erratum.

## 2. The first divergence — `21 CFR 11.10(f)`

**The paragraph, quoted exactly:**

> *"Use of operational system checks to enforce permitted sequencing of steps and events, as appropriate."*

**What Phase 05 published**, in
`../05-access-audit-trails-and-electronic-signatures/logs/control-position-register.md`:

| System | `(f)` |
|---|---|
| `SYS-023`, Ridgemont | **Met and required** |
| `SYS-024`, Cary | **Met but not required** |

**On both instances the sequencing check is present.** The control exists, it operates, and it enforces the
order in which steps may be taken. The determinations differ on the second question Phase 05's method asks,
which is asked only after the first is answered yes: **does a procedure in Helix's quality system require
the control to be in place?**

**At Ridgemont a named sentence in a named procedure requires it. At Cary nothing does.** That is what
*Met but not required* means, and `../05-access-audit-trails-and-electronic-signatures/05.03-the-three-determinations.md`
owns the definition.

**The consequence is Phase 05's own and is not softened here: at Cary the sequencing check can be withdrawn
without a deviation, because no procedure would be breached.** Somebody with the entitlement to change the
configuration could turn it off tomorrow. **No procedure is broken, no deviation is raised, no change
control trigger fires and no record is produced anywhere.** At Ridgemont the same act would breach a
procedure.

**Two Helix laboratories run the same product at the same version. On one of them the operational system
check is a control. On the other it is a configuration.** `IS-29` records that condition on two hundred
and eighteen positions across the estate; what makes this one different is that **the identical control on
an identical system forty miles away is required, and nobody had ever compared the two.**

## 3. The second divergence — `RT-17` and *original or a true copy*

**What Phase 03 published**, in
`../03-data-integrity-risk-assessment-and-the-alcoa-attributes/logs/attribute-determination-register.md`:

| Pair | System | Record type | *Original or a true copy* |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`ND-08`** | `SYS-023`, Ridgemont | `RT-17` System suitability test record | **Not determinable** |
| **`ND-13`** | `SYS-024`, Cary | `RT-17` System suitability test record | **Not met** |

**The other four record types carry identical determinations on both instances** — `ND-04`≡`ND-09`,
`ND-05`≡`ND-10`, `ND-06`≡`ND-11`, `ND-07`≡`ND-12`. `06.03 §4` sets all ten out.

**The two determinations are different in kind and not in degree.** **Not met records that the attribute
was assessed and was not satisfied. Not determinable records that the evidence needed to assess it was not
available** — `ADR-0018`, and **Not determinable is a determination and is not scored**. At Cary Helix
could establish that the attribute was not satisfied on `RT-17`; at Ridgemont it could establish nothing
about the same attribute on the same record type. **The Ridgemont cell is not the better of the two. It is
the one about which less is known.**

**And FDA's attribute is *"original or a true copy"*, never *original* alone.** Rendering the O as
*original* drops FDA's own qualifier, and this repository does not drop it.

**Both determinations were correctly taken on the published method.** Neither moves here. `IS-15` records
that *original or a true copy* fails or cannot be determined on seventy-five of the hundred and
twenty-one pairs, and `DP-17` — a statement, per record type, of which instance Helix relies on as the
original — is still the single largest closure route in the programme and is still not discharged.

## 4. Where the two divergences sit among the rest

**`logs/instance-comparison-register.md` compares the two instances at twenty-four points across four
registers. Twenty agree and four do not.**

| Source | Points | Agree | Disagree |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 02 — the inventory attributes | 5 | 5 | 0 |
| Phase 03 — the ten attribute determinations | 5 | 4 | **1** — `RT-17` |
| Phase 05 — the eleven control positions | 11 | 10 | **1** — `(f)` |
| Phase 06 — this phase's own comparisons | 3 | 1 | **2** — the audit trail configuration, and the transfer |
| **Total** | **24** | **20** | **4** |

**5 + 5 + 11 + 3 = 24 · 20 + 4 = 24.**

**Two of the four were published before this phase opened and two were found in it.** The two found in it
are `06.05-the-audit-trail-as-configured.md`'s — **eleven of the thirty-four classes are enabled on one
instance and not on the other, nine on Ridgemont only and two on Cary only, 9 + 2 = 11** — and
`06.09-the-transcription-break.md`'s.

**The two that were already published are the ones this chapter is about, and they are the more
uncomfortable pair, because finding them required no examination at all.** They required somebody to open
two files and look at two rows.

## 5. The finding is about the estate, not about either instance

**A determination made at one site and reported for both is not false about that site while being false
about the estate.**

That is the whole of `ADR-0042`, `DEC-615`, taken by Amara Sissoko on 2027-02-25, and it is the sentence
this chapter exists to establish.

**Take `(f)`.** *Met and required* is true of `SYS-023`. *Met but not required* is true of `SYS-024`.
Neither cell misdescribes the system it describes. **What is false is any sentence beginning "on the
chromatography data system, `21 CFR 11.10(f)` is…"** — and that sentence is the one anybody summarising
the estate will write, because the two rows carry the same system name.

**Take `RT-17`.** *Not determinable* is true at Ridgemont. *Not met* is true at Cary. **What is false is
any statement about "the system suitability test record" that carries one of the two.**

**So the defect is not in either cell. It is in the absence of a reading that puts them side by side**, and
that absence is a property of the estate's own record-keeping rather than of either laboratory.
`ADR-0042`'s consequence follows directly: **a difference between two instances is a finding about the
estate.**

**Three things follow from stating it that way.**

**It criticises nobody.** Not the determiner who took either cell, not the laboratory either cell describes,
not the individual who supplied either set of facts. **No document in this repository assesses any
individual's work**, and `PR-47` carries the risk that this chapter is read as though it did.

**It cannot be closed by changing a cell.** Making the Cary sequencing check required would remove this
divergence and would remove no others; determining `ND-08` would remove that one. **Neither would produce a
mechanism**, and the next divergence would arrive unread exactly as these two did.

**It names what would close it, which is a comparison that fires on a change.** `DP-36` — a comparison
instrument that detects a divergence between two instances of one product when it is created rather than
four registers later. **`SOP-DI-05` exists and has not fired on any change**, `DP-32` records that its
trigger set was written for a control position and not for this, and `ADR-0015`'s trigger set was written
for the applicability determination. **`IS-37` closes on the mechanism and not on either cell.**

## 6. `IS-37`

**`IS-37`, raised 2027-02-25, owned by Amara Sissoko, open:**

> **The two instances differ in two published registers and were compared in neither.**

**It does not close by either cell being changed.** It closes when a comparison exists that fires on a
change rather than on a phase — `DP-36`. **Every disagreement is traceable to the exact published file and
row** and `logs/instance-comparison-register.md` carries the traces.

## 7. What this chapter does not establish

**It re-determines nothing.** `(f)` stands as published on both rows. `ND-08` and `ND-13` stand as
published. **The ten pairs stay unscored and the twenty-two control positions stand.** A determination
moves on a trigger and through change control, not because a later phase would prefer a different one.

**It says which cell is right about nothing, because both are.** It states no preference between
Ridgemont's arrangement and Cary's, recommends neither to the other, and reaches no view on whether the
sequencing check ought to be required.

**It states no CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan and no remediation cost**, and **no retention,
backup or archive rule.**

**It draws no conclusion about `OBS-2` and none about `OBS-4`** — Phase 08. **It characterises no
reintegration** — `ADR-0043`.

**It is not an estate finding about any other pair of systems.** **Two of sixty-one were compared at this
depth** — `IS-41`, `ADR-0040`. Whether any other two instances of one product in Helix's estate carry
divergent published cells is unknown, and this chapter says neither that they do nor that they do not.

**It reaches no legal conclusion and forecasts no inspection outcome.** **Nothing here bears on `IS-01`**,
which stays open and stays unweighted.
