## 1. One idea

**Phase 05 produced positions, and a position is read as a verdict. Phase 06 produces something worse: a
story.**

That is the specific hazard of this phase and it is sharper than the last one's. A control position is a
cell in a grid, and a grid resists narrative. **This phase has an inspectional observation at one end, a
technical finding in the middle and a population of released results at the other, and the three of them
arrange themselves into a sequence with a conclusion at the end of it whether anybody writes the conclusion
or not.**

**The conclusion is not written, and this chapter is where the refusal to write it is recorded** — in the
same phase as the findings, at the same version, as a numbered chapter rather than as a caveat at the foot
of a register.

It is owned by internal audit, which walked no act, read no configuration, examined no released result and
took no determination in this phase — `ADR-0004`. **That is the point of who owns it.**

## 3. The distinctions this phase must not blur

`ADR-0006` established that this repository keeps one set of books, and five phases have added vocabulary to
it. **Phase 06 adds two more objects and neither is any of the others.**

| Object | What it is | What it is not |
|---|---|---|
| **`AE-nn`** — an auditable event class | **A class the product offers**, with its recording position on each instance and what it would have shown | **Not a control, not a gap, not a finding.** A class is not a `CG` row and does not become one |
| **`RR-nn`** — a reprocessed released result | **A row recording what the record can and cannot show about one released result** | **Not a deviation, not an out-of-specification investigation, not an event and not a characterisation.** **No row characterises the reintegration it records** |
| **A depth determination** | Helix's own assessment of two named instances at a stated vantage | **Not a validation outcome, not an audit finding, not an inspectional observation and not a legal conclusion** |
| **An instance divergence** | **A finding about the estate** — that nothing required two installations of one product to agree | **Not a deficiency at either site**, and no site is named as the correct one unless a requirement makes it so — `ADR-0042` |

**And a difference published in two registers that nobody compared is not a defect in either register.** A
register records rows; **reading across the rows is a separate act with a separate owner**, and
`06.02 §5` owns that argument. Nothing in this phase criticises Phase 03's register or Phase 05's, both of
which are correct.

## 5. `IS-01` stays open, and stays unweighted

**Nothing in Phase 06 bears on `IS-01`.**

`IS-01` records that four internal audits between 2023-06 and 2025-09 covered all three systems between
them, each falling inside the scope of at least two, and that none raised a finding on the subject matter
of `OBS-2`, `OBS-4` or `OBS-6`. **Why is not established.** `01.09 §6` recorded four candidate explanations
and chose none; `ADR-0007` published the history in full; `ADR-0012` and `02.11` established the premise of
one candidate and refused to promote it; `03.12 §5`, `04.12 §5` and `05.12 §5` each confirmed that nothing
in their phase discriminated between them.

**The pressure in this phase is the greatest the programme has faced, and it is greatest because the phase
has now supplied something that was expressly said to be missing.**

`04.12 §5` named what an answer would need: **an assessment of what the audit trails on the three systems
record** — with the chromatography data system in Phase 06 — **and an examination of the four audit
programmes against it**, in Phase 08. `05.12 §5` recorded that the estate-wide half now existed. **The
chromatography half now exists too, for two of the three systems `IS-01` concerns. The examination of the
four audit programmes, in Phase 08, does not exist.** A reader holding this phase's technical finding beside
`IS-01` will feel a sentence forming, and **the sentence is not written here, in any register, in any
diagram, in any governance record, or in any later phase that cites this one.** **The four candidates are
not reproduced here, and none is named**, because naming one and not the others is a weighting.

**Three reasons, and each is sufficient alone.**

**This phase reconstructed no configuration at any date before its own vantage.** Every determination it
holds speaks as at 2027-02-26 or about the twelve months to 2027-01-31. **A determination made in February
2027 is not evidence about a system in June 2023**, and treating it as such would be `ADR-0012`'s error
committed with better data.

**This phase examined no audit artefact.** No scope statement, no checklist, no test design, no working
paper, no sampling basis and no auditor's technical access at any historical date. **It has traced no
checklist to its source**, and it could not have: examining an audit programme is Phase 08's work and
internal audit's own function is the subject of it.

**And a technical explanation for one of three subject matters is not an explanation.** `IS-01` is about
all three of `OBS-2`, `OBS-4` and `OBS-6`, across four audits, over more than two years. **A fact about
what a chromatography audit trail records in 2027 does not reach the packaging line systems, does not reach
the electronic batch record, and does not reach the other three audits.**

**The four candidate explanations stand exactly where Phase 01 left them.** **No ranking, no probability, no
confidence level, no leading candidate and no ordering of the four**, in this chapter, in the registers, in
any diagram, in any governance record or in any later phase. **The four are set out at `01.09 §6`, in the
order that chapter set, and this chapter reproduces none of them, because reproducing one and not the
others is a weighting.** **No document in this phase may be cited in support of any of the four, and if one
is, it is wrong and this sentence is why.**

**What is still needed, restated.** The examination of the four audit programmes against what has now been
assessed, in **Phase 08**. **That still does not exist**, and the three systems the audits covered are not
all assessed at this depth in any case.

## 6. Two things the phase must never be read as saying

**First: nothing here is a Part 11 certification, and nothing here says any system is compliant.**

**There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate, and no FDA-approved, FDA-validated or
FDA-certified software.** FDA does not certify electronic systems and does not pre-evaluate them for
Part 11 compliance. A product has **features that support** compliance; **compliance is a property of
Helix's validated system in its intended use, under its procedures, operated by its trained people**, and
**outsourcing to a service provider transfers no responsibility.** An enumeration of what a product's audit
trail can and cannot record is a fact about a product and about two configurations. **It is not a
certification of either, and it is not a criticism of a vendor.**

**Second: a technical finding is not a defence, and it is not a mitigation.**

**The most quotable sentence in this phase is the one at `06.11 §4`**, and out of its chapter it will be
read as an excuse. It is not one. It is a narrow technical statement about a class of act and a
configuration, made in a phase that also records that the audit trails were not opened, that the observation
stands exactly as written, and that **configuration and review are now both owed.** **A finding that
enlarges the work is not a finding that reduces the exposure**, and any document that cites `06.11 §4`
without the four refusals at `06.11 §5` has quoted half of a sentence.

**And the standing refusals that go with it.** **FDA's Computer Software Assurance guidance is device
guidance under 21 CFR Part 820, as amended by the Quality Management System Regulation, and does not apply
to Helix**; it is adopted voluntarily and labelled at every citation — `ADR-0005`, `ADR-0026`. **GAMP 5 is
ISPE good-practice guidance and is never a requirement, and no software-category count appears anywhere in
this repository.** **NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 is a control catalogue Helix chose, not an obligation it is
under: no FIPS 199 categorization, no baseline, no ATO and no authorizing official.** **Enforcement
discretion is FDA's stated intention. It is not a legal exemption and not a safe harbour, it confers no
rights, and `21 CFR 211.68` and `21 CFR 211.100(b)` survive it entirely** — `ADR-0003`, `ADR-0035`.

## 7. What a reader may legitimately take from the phase

Stated positively, once, so that the chapter is not only a list of refusals.

- **That two named instances of one product have been assessed against the acts capable of changing a
  reportable result**, on a method authorised before the first act was walked — `GOV-26`, `ADR-0038`.
- **That every determination was made twice, per instance, or was not made** — `ADR-0037`.
- **That a population of released results was examined individually** rather than sampled, for a stated
  window, with the per-instance split published — `06.07-reintegration.md`.
- **That the two instances were compared as a distinct act**, against the registers the comparisons were
  made from — `logs/instance-comparison-register.md`, `ADR-0042`.
- **That the depth of the assessment is stated wherever its result is reported**, and that the phase
  publishes the limit of its own method rather than leaving a later phase to find it — `ADR-0040`, `IS-41`.
- **And that the phase, having produced the technical answer to a question the programme has carried since
  Phase 02, wrote no closing sentence** — `ADR-0041`.

**None of those is a statement about whether any Helix record can be relied on.** They are statements about
what Helix now knows about two of its systems, on one day.

## 8. The standing framing this phase keeps

- **Part 11 applies to records, not to systems**, and no document here says "a Part 11 system" without
  saying which records make it one — `ADR-0001`.
- **A closed system is defined by who controls access** — `21 CFR 11.3(b)(4)`. **Hosting is not the test**,
  and both `SYS-023` and `SYS-024` are closed — `ADR-0011`.
- **`21 CFR 11.3(b)` contains nine definitions and Part 11 contains ten sections.** There is no §11.60, no
  §11.400 and no Subpart D. **Part 11 defines neither "audit trail" nor "metadata" nor "data integrity" nor
  "static record" nor "dynamic record"**; FDA defines them in the December 2018 drug CGMP data integrity
  guidance. Citations are exact and can be looked up.
- **Part 11 requires no reason-for-change field, no read-event logging, no cryptographic hashing under
  `21 CFR 11.70`, no multi-factor authentication under `21 CFR 11.200` and no password interval under
  `21 CFR 11.300(b)`.** Where Helix implements any of them, it is Helix's decision and this repository says
  so — `ADR-0036`.
- **`21 CFR 11.30`'s additional measures are additive to the `21 CFR 11.10` controls it requires be included
  as appropriate, and never a substitute for them.**
- **FDA uses plain ALCOA**, and its own footnote 5 maps the attributes to the CGMP predicate rules and cites
  Part 11 nowhere — `ADR-0019`. **The four additional attributes are MHRA's; MHRA itself refers to the
  shorter acronym, states there is no difference in expectations regardless of which is used, and defines
  all four. It does not reject them.** No originator is named for either set.
- **A Form FDA 483 is not a final agency determination**, a Warning Letter is informal and advisory, and only
  a court order binds. **No Warning Letter, Untitled Letter or judicial action of any kind exists**, and **no
  document in this repository reaches a legal conclusion of any kind.**
- **This programme does not forecast the outcome of any future FDA inspection**, and no document in this
  phase contains such a forecast. **The same refusal applies to the third-party audit and to the internal
  audit of the remediated programme. Neither has occurred and neither's result is anticipated anywhere.**
- **An open gap is Helix's own assessment of its own position.** It is not a violation, not a breach, not a
  non-compliance, not a finding and not an inspectional observation.
- **No entry in the four programme registers carries a likelihood, an impact or a score. None ever will.**
- **No document characterises any Helix employee, any auditor or any FDA investigator, and no document
  assesses any individual's work.**
- **All content about Helix is fictional and illustrative.**
