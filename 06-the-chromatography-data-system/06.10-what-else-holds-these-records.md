# 06.10 — What Else Holds These Records

*Three more systems hold these records, two of them under a different named owner, and this chapter establishes nothing about any of them.*

---

## 1. What this chapter does

**It names three systems and hands them to Phase 07.** That is the whole of its business, and the
restraint is deliberate rather than modest: **Phase 06 assessed two systems at depth and it assessed these
three at no depth at all.**

Everything in `§2` is read off
`../02-the-system-inventory-and-part-11-applicability/logs/systems-register.md` and is stated exactly as
that register states it. **Nothing here is new, nothing here is a determination, and nothing here is a
statement about what any of the three does, holds, retains, protects or produces beyond what Phase 02
already published.**

## 2. The three systems, as Phase 02's register publishes them

**`RT-12` — the chromatographic raw data file and acquisition metadata — and `RT-13` — the chromatographic
integration and reprocessing record — are held on five systems, not two.**

| System | Name, as published | Site | Named individual owner | Route into the inventory | Record types held |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `SYS-023` | Chromatography data system, QC laboratory | Ridgemont | **Dr Sunita Raghunathan** | Register | `RT-12`, `RT-13`, `RT-14`, `RT-15`, `RT-17` |
| `SYS-024` | Chromatography data system, QC laboratory | Cary | **Dr Sunita Raghunathan** | Register | `RT-12`, `RT-13`, `RT-14`, `RT-15`, `RT-17` |
| **`SYS-036`** | Laboratory data archive and long-term retention store | Ridgemont data centre | **Dr Sunita Raghunathan** | Register | `RT-11`, `RT-12`, `RT-13` |
| **`SYS-060`** | Analytical data backup and restore platform, QC laboratories | Ridgemont data centre | **Amara Sissoko** | Register | `RT-12`, `RT-13` |
| **`SYS-061`** | Laboratory data archive and retention store | Cary | **Amara Sissoko** | **Walk** | `RT-11`, `RT-12`, `RT-13` |

**All five are in scope, and all five are closed systems** — the test at `21 CFR 11.3(b)(4)` is who
controls access, and Helix's own people control access to each.

**Three things in that table are worth stating in words rather than leaving in a column.**

**`SYS-060` and `SYS-061` are owned by Amara Sissoko and not by Dr Sunita Raghunathan.** The same two
record types, held under two different named individual owners. **Ownership of a system is not ownership
of the records in it** — that is the control position register's own rule — but a record type whose
holders answer to two different people is a record type whose lifecycle has a seam in it, and the seam is
where nobody has looked.

**`SYS-061` entered the inventory by walk rather than from the register.** Phase 02's corrected inventory
records it among the seventeen systems found by walking rather than by reading, and
**`SYS-023`, `SYS-024`, `SYS-036` and `SYS-060` came from the register.** `IS-13` records that seventeen
systems entered the inventory having never entered a validation lifecycle, and `DP-13` records that
confirmation and acceptance of ownership for those seventeen is still open. **A system nobody knew was
there is holding two of the five record types this phase spent four weeks examining.**

**None of the three was assessed at depth.** The audit trail configuration was not read on any of them.
No reprocessed result on any of them was examined. No comparison across the row was made between
`SYS-036` and `SYS-061`, which are the two archives, and none was made between either archive and either
chromatography data system. **The depth assessment reached two systems and stopped** —
`06.01-depth-and-what-it-costs.md`, `ADR-0040`, `IS-41`.

## 3. Why this matters to the phase that just ended

**`06.07-reintegration.md §2` records that the value the first integration produced was obtained from
outside the record the reviewer sees** — from processing history retained on the instance, from the
sequence-level processing record, or **from the archived first-pass result set held outside the working
store.** The provenance is at `EV-618`.

**Some of that material is not on `SYS-023` or `SYS-024`.** The comparison that produced the nine
therefore reached, on some rows, into an estate this phase did not assess. **`AS-45` is the assumption
that carries it**: that the values obtained from those sources are the values the first integration
actually produced, and that the sources are complete for all thirty-eight rows.

**That is the shape of the handover.** The record on the system cannot show what happened. The material
that could show it lives somewhere else. **Nobody has established, for that somewhere else, how long it
holds anything, in what form, or whether what survives is a true copy.**

## 4. What is expressly not established here

**No retention rule, no backup rule and no archive rule.** Not for `RT-12`, not for `RT-13`, not for any
of the 34 in-scope record types, and not for any of the three systems. **`IS-14` records that no retention
period exists for any of the 34**, and `21 CFR 11.10(e)` requires audit trail documentation to be retained
*"for a period at least as long as that required for the subject electronic records"* — **a period nobody
has set.** `AS-37` is the assumption Phase 05's determinations rest on in the meantime, and it is Phase
07 that changes the basis of them.

**No statement that the copies on the three systems are true copies, and none that they are not.** FDA's
attribute is *"original or a true copy"*, and `DP-17` — a statement, per record type, of which instance
Helix relies on as the original, and a demonstrated method by which a copy is shown to be a true one — is
**still the single largest closure route in the programme and is still not discharged.** `IS-15` records
that the attribute fails or cannot be determined on seventy-five of the hundred and twenty-one pairs.

**No control determination and no attribute determination for any of the three.** `SYS-036`, `SYS-060` and
`SYS-061` carry rows in Phase 05's control position register taken on the same method as every other
system, and **nothing here re-determines any of them.** Whatever Phase 03 determined about the pairs on
those three systems stands, and this chapter neither cites those determinations as adverse nor improves on
them.

**And no adverse inference from the absence of an assessment.** **Unassessed is not adverse.** That these
three were not examined at depth is a fact about the depth assessment's scope, not a statement about the
three systems, and `PR-44` carries the risk that somebody reads it as one.

**`IS-40`, raised 2027-02-24, owned by Amara Sissoko, open:**

**What Phase 07 receives, and what it must establish for itself.**

| Handed over | To | Condition on receipt |
|---|---|---|
| `SYS-036`, `SYS-060` and `SYS-061`, **named and nothing more** | Phase 07 | **In Phase 02's register, unaltered.** No depth assessment, no retention rule, no backup rule, no archive rule, no true-copy statement |
| The seam between two named individual owners on one record type | Phase 07 | Recorded. **Not characterised**, and not a criticism of either owner |
| `DP-37` — retention, backup and archive facts for the three, and which instance is relied on as the original | Phase 07, from the IT organisation and the two QC laboratories | **Open.** `DP-17` and `DP-18` are the estate-wide forms and neither has been discharged in five phases |
| **`IS-42`** — the question this phase leaves open | **Phase 07** | **Open.** *The record cannot show what happened, and the data it cannot show it about is retained somewhere. Nobody has established for how long, in what form, or whether the copy that survives is a true one* |
| `IS-12`, `IS-14`, `IS-15` | Phase 07 | Carried open, unchanged |

**Phase 07 is the data lifecycle, and this chapter is the doorway it arrives through.** What it must not do
with these three is treat their contents as adverse, treat their absence from this phase as a finding, or
read anything in Phase 06 as having established a fact about any of them.

## 6. What this chapter does not establish

**It establishes nothing about `SYS-036`, `SYS-060` or `SYS-061` beyond what Phase 02's register already
publishes.** That is the sentence the chapter was written around.

**It states no retention, backup or archive rule** — Phase 07. **It states no CAPA, no deviation, no
remediation plan and no remediation cost.** **It states no validation outcome** — the evidence Phase 04
requires is owed in full across the estate, `IS-34`. **It re-scores no Phase 03 pair, re-bands no Phase 04
system and re-determines no Phase 05 cell.**

**It draws no conclusion about `OBS-2` and none about `OBS-4`** — Phase 08. **It characterises no
reintegration** — `ADR-0043`. **It reaches no legal conclusion and forecasts no inspection outcome.**
**Nothing here bears on `IS-01`**, which stays open and stays unweighted.
