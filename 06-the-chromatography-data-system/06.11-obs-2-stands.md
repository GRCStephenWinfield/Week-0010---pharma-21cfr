## 1. The observation, as it is recorded

`OBS-2` is the second of the seven inspectional observations on the Form FDA 483 issued at the conclusion of
the Ridgemont inspection, and it is carried under the 483's own numbering — never renumbered, never
regrouped and never given an identifier of the programme's own, `ADR-0006`.

**The programme's record of it, in Helix's own words**, at `01.02 §4`:

> **Laboratory records generated on the chromatography data system were reviewed and approved without the
> associated audit trail forming part of that review. The reviewing analyst examined the result, the
> chromatogram as reported and the calculation; the record of who changed what, and when, was not opened.**

**That text is a summary written by Helix for its own use. It is not a transcription of the 483 and it is
not FDA's language.** Nothing in this phase revises it, narrows it, re-words it or reads a qualification
into it.

**The regulatory anchor is the predicate rule and not Part 11.** `21 CFR 11.10(e)` requires audit trails to
be generated, retained and available for agency review and copying, and **imposes no review obligation at
all.** The review obligation for laboratory records is `21 CFR 211.194(a)(8)`'s — *"[t]he initials or
signature of a second person showing that the original records have been reviewed for accuracy,
completeness, and compliance with established standards"* — and FDA's December 2018 drug CGMP data integrity
guidance treats the audit trail as part of the record being reviewed.

## 2. What Phase 06 has done, and what it has not

**Phase 06 has established what `OBS-2`'s subject matter is. It has disposed of nothing.**

Since Phase 02 the programme has recorded the observation and drawn nothing from it — at `02.06`, at
`03.09`, at `05.05 §6` and elsewhere — for a reason stated each time: **nobody had established what the
audit trails on those two instances actually record.** That is now established, per instance, against the
acts capable of changing a reportable result, on a method authorised before the first act was walked.
`06.04-the-acts-that-change-a-result.md` owns the method, `06.05-the-audit-trail-as-configured.md` owns the
configuration position, and `06.06-what-review-would-not-have-found.md` owns the finding.

**Establishing the subject matter of an observation is not disposing of it, and this phase does neither more
nor less than the first.**

## 3. Why disposition is Phase 08's

**A disposition requires three things, and none of them exists at this vantage.**

**A periodic review method.** There is none. The frequency at which anything is reviewed at Helix, where
CGMP specifies none for the underlying data, is `IS-04`'s and is deliberately unresolved. **No primary
source names a stand-alone periodic audit trail review frequency**; FDA names the frequencies to be
**inherited** — review after each significant step under `21 CFR 211.188(b)`, and data review before batch
release under `21 CFR 211.22` — and leaves the firm to determine one only where CGMP specifies none for the
underlying data, **which is the only place FDA's risk-assessment sentence operates.** **FDA's own closing
requirement runs to the whole approach**: it *"should ensure that CGMP requirements are met, appropriate
controls are implemented, and the reliability of the review is proven."* **This phase names none.**

**An internal audit position.** Gideon Halvorsen's function has audited nothing arising from this
programme, and internal audit does not implement remediation — `01.07 §6`. **The third-party audit by
Vashti Okonjo & Partners has not occurred**, and neither audit's result is anticipated anywhere.

**And a stated response.** Helix's response to the 483 was filed on 2026-03-06 and stated what the
organisation would do; **a response concedes nothing, contests nothing and adjudicates nothing.** What
position Helix takes on the observation once the work is done is a quality unit act, taken through the
quality system, and this programme does not take it on the quality unit's behalf.

**So the disposition is Phase 08's, and Phase 06 does not reach it by implication, by ordering or by
tone.** `ADR-0041`, taken at `DEC-613` on 2027-02-23 by Terrence Abbatiello, records exactly that.

## 4. The sentence this phase exists for

**The observation says the audit trails were not reviewed. Phase 06 finds that reviewing them, on the
configuration in place, would not by itself have reached the act that changes the reportable result.**

`06.06-what-review-would-not-have-found.md` reports the finding and `logs/auditable-event-register.md`
carries the rows it is computed from. **No figure from either appears in this chapter.**

The sentence looks like a defence and it is not one. What it says is narrow and technical: **there is a
class of act, available to people holding the permissions the estate grants, that moves the number reported
to the batch record and to the certificate of analysis, and the record a reviewer would have opened does
not contain it.** Review reaches what the record contains. **Against an act the record does not contain,
review is not a weaker control; it is not a control at all**, and a more diligent reviewer, a longer review
and a more frequent review all produce the same result.

## 5. Four things that sentence does not do

**Each of the four is a reading somebody will produce in good faith, and each is refused here rather than in
a footnote.**

**It does not excuse the failure to review.** The audit trails were not opened. That is what the
observation records and it remains exactly what it records. **A record that would have shown less than
somebody hoped is still a record that was not looked at**, and the reasons for not looking at it are not
addressed, improved or explained by anything in this phase.

**It does not reduce the observation.** `OBS-2` stands exactly as written at `§1`. It is not narrowed to the
acts the record does contain, it is not re-scoped, and no sentence in this phase may be cited as qualifying
it. **An observation is FDA's investigator's, recorded at a point in time, and this programme does not edit
it.**

**It does not convert a review failure into a product limitation.** The two coexist and neither absorbs the
other. **Helix did not review; and separately, the record on the configuration in place would not have shown
one class of act.** A phase that folded the first fact into the second would have replaced an act nobody
performed with a limitation nobody chose, which is a considerably more comfortable story and is not the
one the evidence supports. **Some of what the record does not show is a configuration choice within Helix's
control** — `06.05-the-audit-trail-as-configured.md` distinguishes the states — and a choice is not a
limitation.

**And it makes the remediation larger, not smaller.** **Configuration and review are now both owed.** Before
this phase, the work implied by `OBS-2` was: begin reviewing the audit trails. After it, the work is: begin
reviewing the audit trails, **and** establish what the record has to contain for that review to reach the
acts that move the number, **and** determine what to do where the record cannot contain it on the product
version in use. **That is more work, on more owners, over a longer period**, and it is the honest reading of
what the phase found.

## 6. What Phase 08 will need in order to dispose of it

Stated as what is needed rather than as a plan, because **this phase states no remediation plan, no
sequence, no priority, no date and no cost.**

| What Phase 08 will need | Where it stands at this vantage |
|---|---|
| **The configuration position on both instances**, per act and per instance | **Exists.** `06.05-the-audit-trail-as-configured.md` and `logs/auditable-event-register.md` |
| **The examination of the released results in the window** | **Exists.** `06.07-reintegration.md` and `logs/reintegration-register.md`, and **no reintegration is characterised** — `ADR-0043` |
| **A determination of what the laboratory record review procedure requires**, and whether the audit trail forms part of the original record reviewed under `21 CFR 211.194(a)(8)` | **Open — `IS-06`.** The procedure does not say that it does and does not say that it does not |
| **A review method, with its frequency stated on the correct basis** | **Open — `IS-04`.** Inherited where CGMP specifies a frequency for the underlying data; determined by the firm only where it does not |
| **A position on what happens where the product version cannot record the act** | **Open.** `06.05-the-audit-trail-as-configured.md` states which acts are in that state; **what to do about it is not this phase's** |
| **An internal audit position on the remediated arrangement** | **Does not exist.** Nothing has been remediated and nothing has been audited |
| **An examination of the four internal audit programmes against what has now been assessed** | **Does not exist — Phase 08.** `04.12 §5` named it and `05.12 §5` recorded that one half of what it needed had arrived. **The other half has not** |
| **A stated Helix position, taken through the quality unit** | **Does not exist**, and this programme does not take it on the quality unit's behalf — `21 CFR 211.22` |
| **Retention, and whether the data the record cannot speak about survives in a true copy** | **Open — `IS-42`, and it is Phase 07's**, the data lifecycle |

**Two of the nine exist. Seven do not.** That is the position, and it is the reason `§3` is short.

## 7. What this chapter does not establish

- **It disposes of nothing — not `OBS-2` and not `OBS-4`.** Phase 08.
- **It states no figure**, from `logs/auditable-event-register.md`, from `logs/reintegration-register.md`,
  from `logs/instance-comparison-register.md` or from anywhere else.
- **It raises no CAPA and no deviation** and states no remediation plan, cost, sequence or priority.
- **It states no validation outcome** — the evidence Phase 04 requires is owed in full, `IS-34`.
- **It sets no audit trail review frequency**, as a requirement or by implication.
- **It characterises no reintegration** — `ADR-0043`.
- **It re-scores no Phase 03 pair and re-determines no Phase 05 cell.**
- **It reaches no legal conclusion.** FDA's own statement of what a 483 is, verbatim: *"The FDA Form 483
  does not constitute a final Agency determination of whether any condition is in violation of the FD&C Act
  or any of its relevant regulations."* **No document in this repository reaches a legal conclusion of any
  kind, and none forecasts what any future inspection will find.**
- **It assesses no individual's work**, and it says nothing about any reviewing analyst, any auditor or any
  FDA investigator.
- **Nothing here bears on `IS-01`**, which stays open and stays unweighted, and no candidate explanation is
  promoted by statement, by ordering or by tone — `ADR-0012`.
