## 1. One idea

**This is the boundary chapter of the last phase, and it is the one most likely to be read on its own.**
**`09.13-close-out.md` follows it and is the last chapter in the repository.**

A close-out pack travels. It goes into a board paper, a due diligence request, a customer questionnaire and
an induction slide, **and it travels without the eight phases that make its context obvious.** The material
in this phase has the shape of an ending — a charter scored, a handover made, an issue answered, a programme
closed — **and out of context an ending reads as a resolution.**

**It is not a resolution, and several of the sentences a reader will produce from this phase are sentences
this phase does not make.** This chapter is a numbered chapter at the same version as the findings rather
than a caveat at the foot of a register, for the reason every phase since Phase 02 has published one: **a
caveat is read once and a chapter is cited.**

It is owned by internal audit, which scored no objective and drew no register in this phase.
**`09.09-independence-cannot-be-retrofitted.md` is internal audit's own work about its own position and is
the exception; nothing else in the phase is.**

## 3. The distinctions this phase must not blur

`ADR-0006` established that this repository keeps one set of books, and eight phases have added vocabulary to
it. **Phase 09 adds three objects and none of them is any of the others.**

| Object | What it is | What it is not |
|---|---|---|
| **`OS-nn`** — an objective score | **A statement that the artefact one charter measure names is, or is not, in the state that measure describes, at a stated vantage, read from the artefact and from nothing else** | **Not a verdict, not a finding, not a deviation, not an inspectional observation, not a non-compliance, not a compliance conclusion and not a statement about the objective's owner.** **There is no partial value and none may be supplied** — `ADR-0058` |
| **`DK-nn`** — a decay register row | **A statement about one artefact: what event would falsify it, how often that event occurs, who holds it after close-out, and the first action owed** | **Not a statement that the artefact is currently accurate, not a review frequency, not a cadence, not a due date and not a commitment.** **It records what would make an artefact go out of date, not whether it has** — `ADR-0062` |
| **`HO-nn`** — a handover row | **A statement about one open entry: where it has been sent, whether a named individual has said it is theirs, and what the destination is being asked to hold** | **Not a closure, not a status change, not a plan, not a date and not a statement that anybody will act.** **A function is not a destination**, and a row whose destination is a function reads No in the acceptance column — `ADR-0060` |

**And three sentences carry the same discipline, each owned by one chapter.** **A score is not a verdict** —
`09.01-how-an-objective-is-scored.md`. **An arrangement is not an assessment** —
`09.09-independence-cannot-be-retrofitted.md`. **Readiness is not compliance** —
`09.05-readiness-is-the-ability-to-answer.md`. **No qualifier attaches to any of the three**, because a
qualifier on one of these is the distinction conceded in the drafting and withdrawn in the summary.

## 6. Two things the phase must never be read as saying

**First: that a programme ending is a matter ending.**

**A programme is a funded, chartered, time-boxed piece of work, and its close-out date was fixed on
2026-03-06.** Reaching that date says nothing whatever about the conditions the programme was set up to
examine. **Nothing recorded as absent becomes present because a programme has closed**, and no obligation is
discharged by the ending of the body that was looking at it. **The obligations existed before this programme
and will outlast it**, and `21 CFR 211.22` puts the quality unit's responsibility and authority exactly where
it has always been.

**The word that will do the damage is *closed*, and it will not arrive through this phase.** It will arrive
when a fact travels out of its document into a slide, a management report, a board paper or a supplier
discussion, **and the context that made its status obvious does not travel with it.** `ADR-0035` established
the mechanism in Phase 05 for a landscape fact and `ADR-0050` in Phase 07 for a number; **the status of a
programme is coarser than either and survives no better.** That is why no register in this phase carries a
status column in which the word could be entered, why the handover form has no closure field and an express
statement in its place, and why this chapter exists at the same version as the findings.

**Second: that a complete record of a position is a good position.**

**This phase publishes an unusually complete account of what Helix does not have.** The charter is scored
against its own measures, the artefacts carry what would falsify them and who holds them, the open entries
carry their destinations or the absence of one, and the estate-wide position carries its age. **Out of
context that completeness reads as control**, and it is not control. **It is a description.**

**`09.05-readiness-is-the-ability-to-answer.md` makes the point that a complete and honest register of a
firm's own failures is a better inspection position than an incomplete register of its successes — and makes
the answering point in the same breath: saying so is not saying the position is good.** **The quality of the
record and the quality of the thing recorded are independent.** Every gap the registers describe is still a
gap; every control recorded as absent is still absent; **and an open gap is Helix's own assessment of its own
position — not a violation, not a breach, not a non-compliance, not a finding and not an inspectional
observation — which is a statement about what it is not, and not a mitigation.**

**Nor is the completeness itself assurance.** **All of it was produced by the functions that own the things
described**, on methods those functions published, and none of it has been tested by anybody without an
interest in the answer. **`IS-58` closed on exactly that**, and its answer was no.

## 7. What the reader of this repository must not do with it

**This section is addressed to the reader of the published portfolio rather than to anybody at Helix,
because at this vantage that is who is left.**

**It is an illustrative portfolio sample and every document in it says so.** **Helix Pharma, Inc. does not
exist.** The company, its sites, its estate, its systems, its people, its inspection, its Form FDA 483, its
observations, its programme, its money and every register entry in nine phases are **fictional and
illustrative**, and were written to demonstrate how work of this kind is documented. **No figure in this
repository describes a real firm**, and no name in it is a real person.

**What is real, and is the only thing that is, is the regulatory material.** The sections of `21 CFR Part 11`
and of Parts 210 and 211, the guidance documents, the EU and other non-US material where it is labelled as
such, and the statements about what a Form FDA 483 is — **those are quoted from primary sources, cited
exactly, and can be looked up.** **A citation that cannot be looked up is not a citation**, and the
repository's rule has been that a reader should **read for the arguments rather than the citations**: where a
chapter cites `21 CFR 11.10(e)` or `21 CFR 211.194(a)(8)`, **it is locating a requirement in the text, not
asserting that anybody meets it.**

**It is not advice.** **Nothing in this repository is legal advice, regulatory advice, quality advice or
professional advice of any kind**, and nothing in it should be relied on in respect of any real system,
record, product, site or firm. **It reaches no legal conclusion anywhere**, it makes no representation to any
agency, and **it forecasts no inspection outcome.** A firm with a real question about its own records has a
quality unit, a regulatory function and, if it needs one, counsel; **this is a sample of documentation and is
none of those.**

**Three specific things a reader must not do with it.**

**Do not lift a determination.** The record-level determinations, the control positions, the retention
derivations and the scores are determinations about a fictional estate, taken on stated methods against
stated facts. **A determination is only as good as the facts it was taken against**, and none of these facts
is anybody's.

**Do not lift a number.** Every figure in this repository belongs to a fictional population and means nothing
outside it. **No count here is a benchmark, a norm, a target or an industry position**, and none may be cited
as one.

**And do not read the presence of a document as evidence about anything.** **A complete-looking set of
registers is a demonstration of a documentation approach and is not a demonstration that anything works.**
The repository has spent nine phases arguing that about a fictional firm's records; **the same argument
applies to the repository itself.**

## 8. The standing framing this phase keeps

- **There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate and no FDA-approved or FDA-validated
  software.** **"Part 11 certified" is not a thing.** A product has **features that support** compliance;
  compliance is a property of a validated system in its intended use, under its procedures, operated by its
  trained people. **Outsourcing to a service provider transfers no responsibility.**
- **Part 11 applies to records, not to systems**, and no document here says "a Part 11 system" without saying
  which records make it one — `ADR-0001`, `01.03 §2`.
- **Enforcement discretion is FDA's stated intention, and the set is stated with all its limbs.** FDA has
  stated an intention to exercise it over **`21 CFR 11.10(a)`, `(b)`, `(c)` and `(e)` in full**, over
  **`21 CFR 11.10(k)` in part** — the guidance names `(k)(2)` — over **the corresponding requirements in
  `21 CFR 11.30`**, and over **all Part 11 requirements for legacy systems operational before 20 August 1997
  that meet the guidance's conditions, against which no Helix system has been assessed.** **It is not a legal
  exemption, it is not a safe harbour, and it confers no rights** — the guidance carries FDA's own disclaimer
  that *"It does not create or confer any rights for or on any person and does not operate to bind FDA or the
  public"*. **`21 CFR 211.68`, `21 CFR 211.100(b)` and `21 CFR 211.180` survive it entirely.** **A programme
  that reads enforcement discretion as permission has read the wrong document.**
- **FDA's Computer Software Assurance guidance is device guidance under `21 CFR Part 820`, as amended by the
  Quality Management System Regulation, and does not apply to Helix.** Adopted voluntarily as good practice
  and **labelled as adopted every time** — `ADR-0005`, `ADR-0026`.
- **GAMP 5 Second Edition, ISPE, July 2022, is good-practice guidance and never a requirement**, and **no
  software-category count and no enumeration of the categories appears anywhere in this repository.**
- **NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 is a control catalogue Helix chose, not an obligation it is under:** **no FIPS
  199 categorization, no baseline, no ATO and no authorizing official.**
- **FDA uses plain ALCOA, and ALCOA does not map onto `21 CFR 11.10`** — FDA's own footnote 5 maps the
  attributes to the CGMP predicate rules and cites Part 11 nowhere, `ADR-0019`. **The four additional
  attributes are MHRA's**; MHRA itself refers to the shorter acronym, states there is no difference in
  expectations regardless of which is used, and defines all four — **and it does not reject them.** **No
  originator is named for either set.**
- **Part 11 requires no reason-for-change field, no read-event logging, no cryptographic hashing under
  `21 CFR 11.70`, no multi-factor authentication under `21 CFR 11.200` and no password interval under
  `21 CFR 11.300(b)`** — and **no review frequency of any kind.** `21 CFR 11.10(e)` requires audit trails to
  be generated, not to obscure previously recorded information, to be retained and to be available for agency
  review and copying, **and imposes no internal review obligation at all.** Where Helix implements any of
  them, it is Helix's decision and this repository says so — `ADR-0036`.
- **`21 CFR 11.30`'s additional measures are additive to the `21 CFR 11.10` controls it requires be included
  as appropriate, and never a substitute for them.** **That *as appropriate* is `21 CFR 11.30`'s own and
  reaches open systems only; on a closed system the eleven paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10` are owed in full, and
  all sixty-one systems in scope are closed** — `ADR-0011`.
- **A closed system is defined by who controls access** — `21 CFR 11.3(b)(4)`. **Hosting is not the test.**
- **`21 CFR 11.3(b)` contains nine definitions and Part 11 contains ten sections.** There is no §11.60, no
  §11.400 and no Subpart D. **Part 11 defines neither "audit trail" nor "metadata" nor "data integrity" nor
  "static record" nor "dynamic record" nor "archive"** — FDA defines the first five in the December 2018 drug
  CGMP data integrity guidance. Citations in this repository are exact and can be looked up.
- **`21 CFR 211.68` requires appropriate controls over Helix's computerised systems, at `(b)` the checking of
  input and output for accuracy with the degree and frequency of that verification based on the complexity
  and reliability of the system, and at `(a)` a written program designed to assure their proper
  performance.** **It imposes no general computerised-system validation requirement**, and the word
  *validation* appears in the section once, inside `(b)`'s backup carve-out.
- **`21 CFR Part 11` sets no retention period of its own**, and **`21 CFR 211.180(e)` is a product review and
  is never the basis for a periodic review of a computerised system** — `ADR-0053`.
- **Nothing in `21 CFR Part 11` or Parts 210 and 211 requires a periodic review of a computerised system**,
  and **Parts 210 and 211 contain no self-inspection, internal audit or quality audit provision.**
- **EU GMP Annex 11 is EU-derived, only its January 2011 text is in force, and no Annex 11 obligation binds
  Helix's US-facing route.** **FDA does not enforce EudraLex; an FDA inspection is not an Annex 11
  inspection and an Annex 11 expectation is not an FDA requirement.** **The Qualified Person is not a US
  regulatory role.**
- **A Form FDA 483 is not a final agency determination** — *"The FDA Form 483 does not constitute a final
  Agency determination of whether any condition is in violation of the FD&C Act or any of its relevant
  regulations."* **A Warning Letter is informal and advisory and only a court order binds.** **No Warning
  Letter, Untitled Letter or judicial action of any kind exists**, and **no document in this repository
  reaches a legal conclusion of any kind.**
- **This programme does not forecast the outcome of any future FDA inspection**, and the same refusal applies
  to the internal audit of the remediated programme and to the third-party audit by Vashti Okonjo &
  Partners. **Neither has occurred and neither's result is anticipated anywhere.**
- **An open gap is Helix's own assessment of its own position.** It is not a violation, not a breach, not a
  non-compliance, not a finding and not an inspectional observation.
- **Applicability is not integrity, a rating is not a validation status, a requirement is not a control, a
  control position is not validation evidence, a depth finding is not an estate finding, a derived period is
  not a decision Helix has taken, a disposition is not a closure, an arrangement is not a review, a grant
  decided is not access performed, a score is not a verdict, an arrangement is not an assessment, and
  readiness is not compliance.**
- **No entry in the four programme registers carries a likelihood, an impact or a score. None ever will.**
- **No document characterises any Helix employee, any auditor or any FDA investigator, and no document
  assesses any individual's work.**
- **No figure anywhere in this repository is a fine, a penalty, a sanction or a forfeiture**, and none of
  those exists. **Programme cost, professional fees, licence costs and consultancy fees are stated and are
  not penalties.**
- **All content about Helix is fictional and illustrative.**
