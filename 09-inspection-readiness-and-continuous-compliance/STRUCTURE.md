## What this phase deliberately does not establish

**Helix is not described as ready and is not described as compliant**, and neither phrase appears in this phase except to forbid it. **No observation is closed or described as closed. No representation is made to FDA.** **No compliance conclusion and no legal conclusion.** **No forecast of what any future inspection will find.** **No CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan and no remediation cost.** **No result of any periodic review and none of any independent assessment** — the arrangement exists and the assessment does not. **No access is described as performed**; the read-access grant is recorded as decided and dated. **Nothing is re-determined, re-scored, re-banded or re-derived** — the Phase 03, 04, 05, 06 and 07 positions stand exactly as published, and the control position handed over is ninety-one days old and is said to be. **No retention period is set for any of the fourteen record types nothing sets one for.** **No entry is closed for the sake of closing it at close-out.** **No candidate explanation of `IS-01` is named** — it closed at Phase 08 and the refusal is permanent.
