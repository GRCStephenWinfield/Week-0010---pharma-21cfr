# 09.09 — Independence Cannot Be Retrofitted

*The question was whether Helix can make this programme independent retrospectively, and the answer is no. All content is fictional and illustrative.*

---

## 1. One idea

**Helix cannot make this programme independent retrospectively, and no act available to Helix at this
vantage or at any later one would change that.**

**`IS-58` closes here, on that answer.** It was raised on 2027-04-09 by the Head of Internal Audit and closed
on 2027-04-29 by the same individual, at `DEC-916`, minuted at `GOV-44`. **The closing note and its named
evidence are at `logs/raid-log.md §1.8` and are not restated here.**

**What Phase 09 establishes instead is the arrangement under which the *next* assessment is independent.**
Read access granted to the audit function, and the periodic review owned by a function that owns no system.
**Both limbs are arrangements**, and this chapter is careful about the difference between an arrangement and
the thing it is an arrangement for. **`IS-63` carries the assessment**, which nobody has scoped, commissioned
or scheduled.

**This chapter states no count and no register figure.** The published position on read access is the audit
condition register's and does not move here; **the entries this chapter names stand exactly as
`logs/raid-log.md` carries them**, and `09.07-the-handover.md` records where each of them goes.

## 1.1 Nothing required the independence either

**Parts 210 and 211 contain no provision requiring a self-inspection, an internal audit or a quality
audit programme** — the negative claim `08.11 §1.1` establishes against the full `21 CFR 211.1` to
`21 CFR 211.208` section list.

**`21 CFR 211.22(a)` provides that there shall be a quality control unit with the responsibility and
authority to approve or reject *"…all components, drug product containers, closures, in-process materials,
packaging material, labeling, and drug products, and the authority to review production records to assure
that no errors have occurred or, if errors have occurred, that they have been fully investigated"*, and
that the unit is responsible for approving or rejecting drug products manufactured, processed, packed or
held under contract by another company.** **That records-review limb is the nearest thing in Parts 210 and
211 to an internal examination duty, and it is not an audit function: it is exercised before batch
disposition, by the unit that must itself approve the batch, over the records that unit approves.** **It
establishes no function independent of the work it examines, and Parts 210 and 211 establish none anywhere
else.**

**EU GMP has a self-inspection chapter — EudraLex Volume 4, Part I, Chapter 9, *Self Inspection* — and it
is EU-derived, it is not FDA's, and no obligation in it binds Helix's US-facing route.** **US drug CGMP has
no counterpart.** **Helix's internal audit function exists because its Board's audit committee established
it**, and the four audits are measured against Helix's own audit programme and against nothing else.

**So the arrangement this chapter records is adopted voluntarily, exactly as the periodic review was at
`08.08`, and it is labelled so wherever it is cited.** **That makes it easier to withdraw and not harder**
— which is a consequence `ADR-0061` records rather than one it solves.

## 2. What `IS-58` asked

**It asked a structural question about who made the determinations in this repository, and it did not ask
whether they were right.**

The programme's determinations were made by the functions that own the things determined. The record-level
determination was made by the Data Integrity Lead and approved through Quality. The complexity-and-reliability
band was determined by the validation function. The estate-wide control position was determined by the
function that operates the systems. The backup and restore position was taken from enquiries answered by the
people who operate the arrangements. **And the one function that could have tested any of it independently
has never held the access it would need to look** — `IS-55`, `AC-05`, `DP-46`.

**That arrangement was not concealed and it was not accidental.** Phase 01 named it in the first month:
**"[a] data integrity function inside the organisation that operates the systems is assessing its own work."**
`ADR-0002` put the Data Integrity Lead outside the line that builds and administers the systems for exactly
that reason — *"…the function that judges whether a system's validated state holds should not sit in the line
that builds and administers it"* — and `ADR-0004` fixed the three-way separation of advisory, execution and
audit, on the proposition that **an assurance opinion from the party that designed the thing being assured is
not an opinion, it is a restatement.**

**What the programme could do about it, it did, and it is worth being precise about what that was.** Every
determination was made on a method fixed and published before the work it governs, with the sequence on the
record so that the claim can be checked. Every determination names a determiner and a date. Every register
carries its assumptions as stated entries rather than burying them. **None of that is independence.** It is
transparency about the absence of independence, which is a different property and a lesser one.

**`IS-58` asked whether the lesser property could be converted into the greater one after the fact.**

## 3. The answer is no, and what it does and does not mean

**A determination is an act performed by somebody at a time, and no later act reaches backwards into it.**

**That is the whole of the argument.** Independence is a property of the relation between the party making a
judgement and the thing judged, **and that relation is fixed at the moment the judgement is made.** A grant
of access in April 2027 does not alter who determined a record type in July 2026, on what information, under
what incentives, or with what capacity to be contradicted. **A later assessment would be an assessment of the
artefacts, and not of the making of them.**

**Nothing available to Helix changes it.** A review of the determinations by another party would produce a
second opinion about the same artefacts, taken later, by somebody with different access — **a useful thing,
and not a retrospective independence.** A restatement of the determinations by an independent party would be
a new set of determinations, not the old ones made differently. **And a declaration that the determinations
should be treated as independent would be a declaration, which is the one thing this repository has never
accepted as a substitute for a fact.**

**Four things the answer does not mean, and each needs saying because each is the reading a hurried reader
will produce.**

**It does not mean the determinations are wrong.** `IS-58` is a statement about how the determinations were
produced, not about their content. **Every one of them stands exactly as published**, and nothing in this
phase re-determines, re-scores, re-bands or re-derives any of them — `ADR-0049`. A determination made by the
owner of the thing determined may be entirely correct; **what is missing is the assurance that somebody
without an interest in the answer has tested it**, and missing assurance is not evidence of error.

**It does not impugn anybody.** `IS-58` names no individual and is not an allegation of bias. **No document
in this repository characterises any Helix employee, any auditor or any FDA investigator, and none assesses
any individual's work.** The finding is about a structure, and the structure was Helix's before this
programme existed.

**It does not mean the programme should have been run differently, in any way that was available.** The
programme was funded to determine what Helix had never determined. **The people who could determine it were
the people who held the information**, and no alternative was available in which the determinations were
made by somebody who did not own the systems, because nobody who did not own the systems could see them.
That is not a defence; **it is the same fact stated from the other side, and it is the reason `IS-55` is
about access rather than about willingness.**

**And it does not mean nothing is available now.** What is available now is an arrangement for the future,
which is `§4` and `§5`. **What is not available now is a different past.**

**One further thing this closure does not touch.** **`IS-58` is not `IS-01` in another form.** `IS-01` closed
at Phase 08 on what the programme can establish and expressly not on why; its refusal is permanent; it was
expressly not handed to this phase; **and no candidate explanation of it is named here, reachable through
anything here, or supported by any document in this repository.** The two entries share a subject-matter
neighbourhood and are different questions, **and the join is forbidden in terms rather than left to a
reader's discretion.**

## 4. The arrangement, and its access limb

**The arrangement has two limbs and both of them are arrangements.**

**The first limb is read access for the audit function.** It is what `AC-05` turns on, what `DP-46` asks for,
and what `IS-55` records the absence of. Without it the function that reports to the Board's audit committee
— and to no part of Quality or IT — cannot examine a configuration, a control position or an account list
except by asking the people who own them, **which is the request producing the answer, in the shape
`ADR-0004` exists to prevent.**

**The second limb is that the periodic review is owned by a function that owns no system.** The review
established at Phase 08 covers the estate; the ownership question is who takes a view of whether it has been
performed and what it produced. **A review of a system owned by the function that operates the system is the
same structure `IS-58` is about, reproduced in the successor arrangement**, and the arrangement puts it
elsewhere.

**Neither limb is retrospective and neither is claimed to be.** They govern what happens after the vantage.
**Every determination in this repository was made before them.**

**And the arrangement was approved by the individual who raised `IS-58`, reporting to the body he reports to
rather than to the functions the arrangement concerns.** `GOV-44` records the approval and records that what
was approved is an arrangement — **which is a sentence a governance record does not usually have to contain,
and does here because the alternative reading is the one everybody would prefer.**

## 5. The grant is recorded as decided and dated, never as performed

**`ADR-0061`, taken by the Chief Information Officer on 2027-04-26 at `DEC-911` — the individual who owns the
estate and access administration, and the only individual in the cast able to cause the access to exist.**

> **An access grant is recorded as decided and dated, never as performed, until an artefact evidences the
> performance.**

**The distinction is not a technicality and the repository has spent nine phases refusing to lose it.** **A
decision to grant access is an act of governance. An account somebody can log into is an act of
administration.** The first is evidenced by a decision record carrying a date, a decider and the systems it
names. The second is evidenced by a record of the access as administered — the entitlement, the individual it
attaches to, and the date it took effect — **held where access administration records are held.** `EV-925` is
the first. **No artefact in this repository is the second.**

**The shape is one this programme has met and refused repeatedly.** An arrangement to review a system is not
a review of it. A frequency adopted is not a review performed. A configuration is evidence of a setting and
not of an operation. A completion criterion written now is not a commitment filed. **A grant decided is not
access performed** — and `PR-33` carries the general form, a requirement read as a description of the world,
inverted again at this vantage.

**The consequences are recorded rather than softened.** **`IS-55` stays open and says so. `DP-46` stays open
and says so. `AC-05` is not described as capable of being met.** The published position on requested and
granted read access is the audit condition register's, it does not move here, and **nothing in this chapter
adjusts, supersedes or reinterprets it.**

**This is the sentence the last phase of the programme would most like to write differently**, and it is
worth being explicit about why it does not. **A programme that spent more than a year establishing that
Helix's records must evidence acts rather than intentions cannot record its own intention as an act in its
final phase.** `OBS-4` is about attribution — the ability to say which individual did what. `OBS-6` is about a
change that happened and did not appear in the record the reviewer saw. **Recording a decision as a
capability would concede every argument the programme made about somebody else's records.**

**And there is a practical reason that matters more than the principled one.** There is no later phase to
correct the record. **A statement that access was granted, published on the last day of a programme with
nobody left to test it, is the most durable inaccuracy this repository could produce** — whereas a statement
that a grant was decided on a date, and that no artefact evidences access on any system, **leaves a question
somebody can answer.** `ADR-0061` argues the alternative at length, including its strongest form, and
`PR-68` carries the risk that the decision is reported as access anyway.

## 6. An arrangement is not an assessment

**Nobody has scheduled one.**

**`IS-63` carries it and is open on the day it was raised.** No date, no scope, no commissioning decision and
no party — **and it does not close by the arrangement being approved, which is what created it.** `DP-50` is
the route and it has no date. **`09.07-the-handover.md` records where `IS-63` goes**, and the register there
is authoritative.

**`CAL-09` and `CAL-10` are not it.** They are the programme's own assurance activities — an internal audit
of the remediated programme and an independent third-party audit — **and both sit inside the programme that
is closing.** Neither is an assessment under the arrangement this phase establishes, and **neither's result
is reported or anticipated anywhere in this repository.**

**The instrument the next assessment would use is published as an empty form.**
`templates/independent-assessment-scope-record-template.md` names the scope, the objects of examination, the
access such an assessment would require, and — completed before the scope is drawn — **who may not perform
it: anybody who owns a system in scope, anybody who made a determination in scope, anybody who designed,
drafted, specified or implemented an artefact in scope, and the advisory firm engaged to design and draft.**
**The programme that produced the artefacts is excluded too, and its having ended does not make it
eligible.**

**The form is an empty form and its emptiness is the finding.** **An arrangement under which an assessment
could be independent, with an instrument ready and nobody commissioned to use it, is an arrangement and
nothing more** — and `PR-69` carries the risk that it is reported as more.

**One thing follows that is uncomfortable and is not softened.** The arrangement's access limb depends on
access nobody has evidenced, and its assessment limb depends on a commissioning decision nobody has taken.
**Neither dependency is inside the closing programme's gift**, and the programme records both rather than
resolving either.

## 7. What this chapter does not establish

- **No independent assessment, and no result of one.** **None has been scoped, commissioned, performed or
  scheduled** — `IS-63`. **An arrangement is not an assessment.**
- **No access described as performed.** The grant is decided and dated — `ADR-0061` — and **no artefact in
  this repository evidences access on any system.**
- **No change to `IS-55`, `DP-46` or `AC-05`**, each of which stays open and says so, and **no adjustment to
  any published position on requested or granted access.**
- **No count or register figure of any kind.**
- **No retrospective independence.** **The closure of `IS-58` does not make any determination in this
  repository independent**, and no reader may take it as having done so. **What is closed is the question
  whether Helix can retrofit independence.**
- **No re-determination of any Phase 05 control position, no re-scoring of any Phase 03 pair, no re-banding
  of any Phase 04 obligation and no re-derivation of any Phase 07 period** — `ADR-0049`.
- **No result of any periodic review** — `IS-57` — and **no anticipation of the result of the internal audit
  of the remediated programme or of the third-party audit by Vashti Okonjo & Partners. Neither has
  occurred.**
- **No allegation against anybody.** **`IS-58` names no individual, is not an allegation of bias, and no
  document in this repository assesses any individual's work.**
- **No compliance conclusion, no legal conclusion and no readiness position** — `ADR-0059`.
- **No closure of any inspectional observation and no representation to FDA** — `ADR-0051`, `ADR-0064`.
- **No forecast** of what any inspection, audit or assessment would find.
- **No candidate explanation of `IS-01`**, which closed at Phase 08 and stays closed. **`IS-58` is not
  `IS-01` in another form, and closing it does not touch `IS-01`.**
