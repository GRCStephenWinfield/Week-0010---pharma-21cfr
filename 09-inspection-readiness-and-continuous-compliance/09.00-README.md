## 1. What this phase is

**This is the last phase. The programme ends at this vantage and there is no tenth.**

Phase 01 published the inspection, the seven observations, the four internal audits and a charter with eight
objectives, each with a named individual owner and a measure readable from evidence. Phase 02 determined,
record type by record type, which records are Part 11 records. Phase 03 assessed the ALCOA attributes across
the pairs the determination produced. Phase 04 built the validation framework and banded every system on
complexity and reliability. Phase 05 took a control position on every paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` on every
system in scope. Phase 06 characterised the chromatography estate at depth. Phase 07 derived a retention
position and read backup and restore across the estate. Phase 08 stated the present position of the three
conditions the Form FDA 483 records, read the response commitments against their own words, established a
periodic review and closed the repository's oldest open issue. **Every one of those phases handed something
forward. This one has nowhere to hand it.**

**Phase 09 does five things.** **It scores the eight charter objectives, each from the artefact its measure
names.** It reports the envelope against the ten budget lines the charter set. **It records what the
programme built, what makes each of those things go out of date, and who holds it afterwards.** It hands the
open issues to whoever will take them, **and records the ones nobody will.** **And it answers `IS-58`, which
asked whether Helix can make this programme independent retrospectively.**

**`IS-03`, `IS-52` and `IS-58` close here. Nothing else does.**

**The vantage is 2027-04-30, a Friday, `MS-13`, twenty-one days after the Phase 08 vantage and four hundred
and twenty-four days after kickoff.** **`MS-13`'s owner is Lydia Marchetti-Nwosu, Programme Manager**, who
owns the registers, the calendar and the budget report, and one of the objectives this phase scores. **That
is stated once, here, and nothing is made of it anywhere else in the phase.**

## 2. The boundary, stated hard

**Phase 09 closes no observation, makes no representation to FDA, states no compliance conclusion and makes
no readiness claim.**

**"Helix is ready" and "Helix is compliant" are both forbidden, and no document in this phase asserts
either.**
**Inspection readiness is the ability to answer, not the quality of the answer** —
`09.05-readiness-is-the-ability-to-answer.md` argues it and **`ADR-0059`** records it. **A readiness position
is not a compliance position**, and the phase says so in terms.

**Close-out is not a communication to FDA.** **No artefact in this phase is addressed to, drafted for,
intended for transmission to, or written to be read by any agency** — **`ADR-0064`**. **The written response
of 2026-03-06 remains the only thing Helix has said to the agency about any of this**, and this phase neither
repeats, extends, amends nor supplements it.

**A score is not a verdict.** An objective is scored from the artefact its measure names and from nothing
else — **`ADR-0058`** — and the score records whether an artefact is in a described state at a date. **It is
not a finding, not a deviation, not an inspectional observation, not a non-compliance and not a legal
conclusion.**

**An arrangement is not an assessment.** `IS-58` closes on the answer that **Helix cannot make this
programme independent retrospectively**. What is established instead is the arrangement under which the
**next** assessment would be independent — **and nobody has scoped, commissioned or scheduled one**;
`IS-63` carries it. **No access is described as performed** — the grant is recorded as decided and dated,
**`ADR-0061`**.

**The programme closes with its open entries open** — **`ADR-0063`**. **Nothing is closed at close-out for
the sake of closing it**, because **a close-out that tidies is a close-out that lies to whoever inherits it.**

**Phase 09 re-determines nothing.** No Phase 05 control position is re-determined, no Phase 03 pair
re-scored, no Phase 04 band re-taken and no Phase 07 period re-derived. **Every earlier register is an input
and is never edited.**

**And `IS-01` is not this phase's.** **It closed at Phase 08, on what the programme can establish and
expressly not on why, and it stays closed.** Its refusal is permanent, it was expressly not handed to this
phase, and **no candidate explanation is named here, by number, by content, by paraphrase or by allusion.**

## 3. Read this first

Seven method decisions, and everything else in the phase follows from them. **None of them is a result.**

**An objective is scored from the artefact its measure names, and from nothing else** — not from effort, not
from progress, not from intent. Effort, progress and intent are reports about work rather than the work's
product. `09.01-how-an-objective-is-scored.md`, **`ADR-0058`**.

**Inspection readiness is the ability to answer, and is never reported as a compliance position.** In
February 2026 Helix could not say which of its records were Part 11 records, which systems held them, or what
its control position was; it can now answer all three.
`09.05-readiness-is-the-ability-to-answer.md`, **`ADR-0059`**.

**An open entry is handed to a named individual or it is recorded as having no destination.** **A function is
not a destination**: it cannot accept, it cannot be asked a question, and it cannot be held to a date.
`09.07-the-handover.md`, **`ADR-0060`**.

**An access grant is recorded as decided and dated, never as performed**, until an artefact evidences the
performance. **A decision to grant access is an act of governance; an account somebody can log into is an act
of administration.** `09.09-independence-cannot-be-retrofitted.md`, **`ADR-0061`**.

**Every artefact carries what makes it go out of date and who holds it**, and one with no holder is published
as having none. **An artefact does not decay because time passes; it decays because something happens.**
`09.06-what-goes-out-of-date-and-how-fast.md`, **`ADR-0062`**.

**The programme closes with its open entries open.** `09.01-how-an-objective-is-scored.md §6`,
**`ADR-0063`**.

**Close-out is not a communication to FDA**, and no artefact in this phase is written to be read by one.
`09.12-what-this-does-not-establish.md`, **`ADR-0064`**.

**The two that fix how the phase would judge itself were taken before it judged anything.** The boundary was
fixed at `DEC-901` on 2027-04-12, **before any work was done**; `ADR-0058` was taken on 2027-04-14 and the
empty score record form authorised at the same sitting, **six days before the first objective was scored at
`DEC-906` on 2027-04-20**; and `ADR-0060` was taken on 2027-04-21, **before the destination enquiry was put
on 2027-04-26.** **A method settled after the results are known is a method chosen for the results it
produces**, and the dates are on the record so the claim can be checked rather than believed.

between the dates**; **a duration of an activity is counted inclusive of both endpoints.** The Phase 05
vantage to this one is an interval — **the difference, and it is ninety-one** — and the Phase 08 vantage to
this one is an interval of **twenty-one days**. `09.11-the-position-is-ninety-one-days-old.md §1` argues the

not an ordering, **there is no third value and no aggregate is computed from them**, and nothing in the
quality system says what either of them obliges anybody to do.

## 12. Standing framing

These positions hold across all nine phases and are restated here so that a reader arriving at Phase 09 has
them. **`09.12-what-this-does-not-establish.md §8` carries them in full.**

- **There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate and no FDA-approved or FDA-validated
  software.** A product has **features that support** compliance; compliance is a property of Helix's
  validated system in its intended use, under its procedures, operated by its trained people. **Outsourcing
  to a service provider transfers no responsibility.**
- **Part 11 applies to records, not to systems**, and this repository never says "a Part 11 system" without
  saying which records make it one — `ADR-0001`, `01.03 §2`.
- **Enforcement discretion is FDA's stated intention, and the set is stated with all its limbs.** FDA has
  stated an intention to exercise it over **`21 CFR 11.10(a)`, `(b)`, `(c)` and `(e)` in full**, over
  **`21 CFR 11.10(k)` in part** — the guidance names `(k)(2)` — over **the corresponding requirements in
  `21 CFR 11.30`**, and over **all Part 11 requirements for legacy systems operational before 20 August 1997
  that meet the guidance's conditions, against which no Helix system has been assessed.** **It is not a legal
  exemption, it is not a safe harbour, and it confers no rights**; the guidance carries FDA's own disclaimer
  that *"It does not create or confer any rights for or on any person and does not operate to bind FDA or the
  public"*. **`21 CFR 211.68`, `21 CFR 211.100(b)` and `21 CFR 211.180` survive it entirely.**
- **FDA's Computer Software Assurance guidance is device guidance under `21 CFR Part 820`, as amended by the
  Quality Management System Regulation, and does not apply to Helix.** Adopted voluntarily as good practice,
  **labelled as adopted every time** — `ADR-0005`, `ADR-0026`.
- **GAMP 5 Second Edition, ISPE, July 2022, is good-practice guidance and never a requirement**, and **no
  software-category count and no enumeration of the categories is published in this repository.**
- **FDA uses plain ALCOA, and ALCOA does not map onto `21 CFR 11.10`** — FDA's own footnote 5 maps the
  attributes to the CGMP predicate rules and cites Part 11 nowhere, `ADR-0019`. **The four additional
  attributes are MHRA's**, MHRA itself refers to the shorter acronym, states there is no difference in
  expectations regardless of which is used and defines all four, **and it does not reject them.** **No
  originator is named for either set.**
- **Part 11 requires no reason-for-change field, no read-event logging, no cryptographic hashing under
  `21 CFR 11.70`, no multi-factor authentication under `21 CFR 11.200` and no password interval under
  `21 CFR 11.300(b)`** — and **no review frequency of any kind.** `21 CFR 11.10(e)` requires audit trails to
  be generated, not to obscure previously recorded information, to be retained and to be available for agency
  review and copying, **and imposes no internal review obligation at all** — `ADR-0036`.
- **`21 CFR 11.30`'s additional measures are additive to the `21 CFR 11.10` controls it requires be included
  as appropriate, and never a substitute for them.** **That *as appropriate* is `21 CFR 11.30`'s own and
  reaches open systems only; on a closed system the eleven paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10` are owed in full, and
  all sixty-one systems in scope are closed** — `ADR-0011`.
- **Nothing in `21 CFR Part 11` or Parts 210 and 211 requires a periodic review of a computerised system**,
  and **`21 CFR 211.180(e)` is a product review and is never the basis for one** — `ADR-0053`.
- **EU GMP Annex 11 is EU-derived and only its January 2011 text is in force.** **FDA does not enforce
  EudraLex; an FDA inspection is not an Annex 11 inspection and an Annex 11 expectation is not an FDA
  requirement.** **No Annex 11 obligation binds Helix's US-facing route**, and **the Qualified Person is not
  a US regulatory role.**
- **`21 CFR Part 11` sets no retention period of its own.** `21 CFR 11.10(e)` sets a **relation** whose
  second term comes from the predicate rule.
- **`21 CFR 211.68` imposes no general computerised-system validation requirement**, and the word
  *validation* appears in the section once, inside `(b)`'s backup carve-out. `(b)` requires appropriate
  controls so that changes are instituted only by authorised personnel, and requires input and output to be
  checked for accuracy, with *"[t]he degree and frequency of input/output verification shall be based on the
  complexity and reliability of the computer or related system."* `(a)` requires equipment so used to be
  routinely calibrated, inspected or checked according to a written program designed to assure proper
  performance.
- **A closed system is defined by who controls access** — `21 CFR 11.3(b)(4)`. Hosting is not the test.
- **`21 CFR 11.3(b)` contains nine definitions and Part 11 contains ten sections.** There is no §11.60, no
  §11.400 and no Subpart D. Citations in this repository are exact and can be looked up.
- **A Form FDA 483 is not a legal finding** — *"The FDA Form 483 does not constitute a final Agency
  determination of whether any condition is in violation of the FD&C Act or any of its relevant
  regulations."* **A Warning Letter is informal and advisory and only a court order binds.** **No Warning
  Letter, Untitled Letter or judicial action of any kind exists**, **no document in this repository reaches a
  legal conclusion of any kind**, and **never forecast what a future inspection will find.**
- **An open gap is Helix's own assessment of its own position** — not a violation, not a breach, not a
  non-compliance, not a finding and not an inspectional observation.
- **Applicability is not integrity, a rating is not a validation status, a requirement is not a control, a
  control position is not validation evidence, a depth finding is not an estate finding, a derived period is
  not a decision Helix has taken, a disposition is not a closure, an arrangement is not a review, a grant
  decided is not access performed, a score is not a verdict, an arrangement is not an assessment, and
  readiness is not compliance.**
- **All content about Helix is fictional and illustrative.**

## 13. How to read this phase

Read **`09.03`** if you read only one chapter. It is the one in which the pattern in the scores turns out to
be the finding of the entire programme, **and it does not soften the result.**

Read **`09.01`** first if you intend to argue with a score, because it fixes what a score is taken from and
what it is not, and **the argument about a particular score is almost always an argument about the method.**

Read **`09.05`** before assuming you know what the title of this phase means. **Inspection readiness is the
ability to answer, and the answers are at `09.02`.**

Read **`09.09`** with attention to what it declines to do, and **`09.12`** with particular attention: it
answers the reading a hostile reviewer will produce first, **and the answer is not that the reading is
unreasonable.** `09.12 §7` is addressed to the reader of the published portfolio rather than to anybody at
Helix.

Read for the arguments rather than the citations. Where a chapter cites `21 CFR 11.10(e)` or
`21 CFR 211.194(a)(8)`, it is locating a requirement in the text — **not** asserting that Helix meets it.

**And read `09.13` last.** It is where the programme stops.
