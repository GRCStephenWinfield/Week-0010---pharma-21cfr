## 1. The principle this form rests on

**The answer that produced it** — `IS-58`, closed 2027-04-29:

> **Helix cannot make this programme independent retrospectively. Every determination in this repository was
> made by the people who own the things determined, and no later act reaches backwards into a determination
> already made.**

**What was established instead is an arrangement, and this form is the instrument the *next* assessment
would use.** **An arrangement is not an assessment**, and this form does not make one out of one.

**And the recording rule that governs the access section** — `ADR-0061`, `DEC-911`:

> **An access grant is recorded as decided and dated, never as performed, until an artefact evidences the
> performance.**

**Three things follow, and they govern every field below.**

**The assessment's object is an artefact, not a person and not a judgement.** What an assessment of this kind
examines is whether a determination is supported by what it says it rests on. **It examines no individual's
work and characterises nobody** — `01.09 §2`: *"This document names no individual auditor and makes no
statement about any individual's work."*

**Independence is a property of the assessor's relation to the object, and it is decided before the scope
is.** **An assurance opinion from the party that designed the thing being assured is not an opinion, it is a
restatement** — `ADR-0004`. The exclusions at `§7` are therefore completed before `§3`.

**And the access the assessment needs is stated as a requirement, never as a fact.** A grant decided is not
access performed. **A scope that assumes access nobody has evidenced is a scope that will fail on its start
date.**

its face and the entry that carries the absence is named.

| **What the assessment would not be able to reach** | `[each item, with the reason]` |

**The fourth and fifth rows exist because an assessment of a determination is an assessment of something
taken at a date.** **A determination is evaluated against the method in force when it was made** —
`ADR-0049` — and an assessment that treats an old determination as a current claim is assessing the wrong
proposition. **Where the gap between the two vantages is material, the row says so and the assessment states
it rather than closing it.**

**And the sixth row cannot be left blank.** **Where the answer is *nothing*, it says so.**

## 4. The objects of examination

**This section cannot be left blank. Without it the scope names a subject and not a thing to look at.**

| Field | Enter |
|---|---|
| **The artefacts to be examined** | `[each register, determination, record or configuration, named exactly, with the version and vantage]` |
| **What would be examined about each** | `[per artefact: whether it exists, whether it is supported, whether it is current, or whether it is complete — stated, not assumed]` |
| **The sampling frame, per artefact** | `[the population the sample would be drawn from, and where that population is enumerated]` |
| **Whether any object can be examined only inside a system** | `[each named, with the system and the access it would need]` |
| **What evidence would support a conclusion about each** | `[stated in advance of looking]` |
| **Assumptions the scope rests on** | `AS-[nn]`, … — **each stated, never buried** |

**The second row is the row that decides what kind of assessment this is.** **Whether an artefact exists,
whether it is supported by what it cites, whether it is current and whether it is complete are four
different questions with four different methods**, and a scope that does not choose among them produces a
report that answers whichever one the evidence made easiest.

**And the fifth row is completed before the first object is opened.** **Evidence that would support a
conclusion, named after the looking, is evidence chosen for the conclusion it supports.**

**The third and fourth rows are separate questions and are answered separately.** **A decision record
evidences a decision. It evidences no account, no entitlement, no session and no capability on any system.**
Where the fourth row reads *no*, the seventh row states what that costs the scope, **and the register
entries that carry the position stay open.**

## 7. Who may not perform it — express exclusions

**Completed on every record, before `§3`, and no row may be removed.**

| Exclusion | Confirm |
|---|---|
| **Anybody who owns a system in scope may not perform the assessment.** **The owner of a thing is not an assessor of it**, and the register of system owners is where the question is answered — not the assessor's own statement about itself | `[yes]` |
| **Anybody who made a determination in scope may not assess it**, whatever their present role | `[yes]` |
| **Anybody who designed, drafted, specified or implemented an artefact in scope may not assess that artefact** — `ADR-0004`. **An assurance opinion from the party that designed the thing being assured is not an opinion, it is a restatement** | `[yes]` |
| **The advisory firm engaged to design and draft may not audit or assess its own work**, and its engagement excludes it expressly | `[yes]` |
| **Anybody who operates or administers a system in scope may not assess the controls on it** | `[yes]` |
| **The function that judges whether a system's validated state holds does not sit in the line that builds and administers it**, and an assessor drawn from that line is in the same position | `[yes]` |
| **The programme that produced the artefacts may not assess them** — and **this programme has ended, which does not make it eligible** | `[yes]` |
| **Where a candidate party has any excluded relation to any object, the object is removed from scope or the party is** | `[stated — which of the two, and the decision that chose]` |

**Why the exclusions are completed before the scope.** **A scope drawn first and then matched to an
available assessor is a scope shaped by who was available.** Fixing the exclusions first means the scope is
drawn from what needs examining, **and where no eligible party exists for an object, that is a finding about
the arrangement rather than a reason to narrow the object.**

the party.** **The form's whole use is to make those three facts visible before anybody commissions
anything**, and none of them is improved by being written down.

## 10. Completion rules

| Rule | Statement |
|---|---|
| **`§7` is completed before `§3`** | **A scope matched to an available assessor is a scope shaped by availability** |
| **`§3`, `§4`, `§5`, `§7` and `§8` cannot be blank** | Without `§4` the scope names a subject and not a thing to look at; **without `§5` the scope assumes access nobody has evidenced** |
| **Access is a requirement, never a fact** | **No grant is recorded as performed, and no status qualifier is available** — `ADR-0061` |
| **Two vantages, always** | The assessment's and the object's. **A determination is evaluated against the method in force when it was made** |
| **Evidence named before looking** | `§4`'s fifth row is completed in advance of the first object being opened |
| **No result, anywhere** | **This form reports nothing and anticipates nothing.** No finding, no opinion, no conclusion and no forecast |
| **No exclusion is waived** | **Where no eligible party exists for an object, that is recorded as a finding about the arrangement** and the object is not narrowed to fit a party |
| **No individual is characterised** | **No document in this repository assesses any individual's work** |
| **Named individuals** | Commissioning at `§6`, compilation and approval — never a team, `ADR-0008` |
| **Nothing is graded** | **No object, artefact, party or arrangement is described as adequate, sufficient, robust, effective or mature** |
| **Change** | A completed record is amended under change control with a `CR-nnn` record naming the trigger, **and the prior state is retained** |
