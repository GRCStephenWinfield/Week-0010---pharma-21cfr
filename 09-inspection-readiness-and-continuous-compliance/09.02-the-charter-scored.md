## 1. What this chapter does

**It scores the eight charter objectives, one at a time, each against its own measure and each from the
artefact that measure names.** The method is `09.01-how-an-objective-is-scored.md`'s and is not re-argued
here: **an objective is scored from the artefact its measure names and from nothing else** — `ADR-0058`,
`DEC-903`, taken by Lydia Marchetti-Nwosu on 2027-04-14 and authorised at `GOV-41`. **Two states and no
third.**

**The register is `logs/objective-score-register.md` and it is authoritative.** This chapter argues each
row; the register carries it. **Neither restates the other in full.**

**Three things this chapter is not, and each needs saying before the first score.**

**It is not a verdict.** A score of *Not met* records that the artefact the measure names does not exist in
the state the measure describes at this vantage. **It is not a finding, not a deviation, not an
inspectional observation, not a non-compliance and not a legal conclusion**, and **an open gap is Helix's
own assessment of its own position.**

**It is not a report on anybody.** Each of the eight objectives has a named individual owner — `ADR-0008` —
and **this chapter assesses no individual's work.** `01.09 §2` holds without variation across all nine
phases.

**It is not a compliance position and it is not a readiness claim.** `09.05-readiness-is-the-ability-to-answer.md`
owns the distinction and this chapter does not restate it. **Nothing below states that Helix is ready for
anything, and nothing below forecasts what any inspection would find.**

**The charter's own words on this are worth repeating, because the eight measures were written to be scored
this way from the start.** `01.06 §3` records: *"No objective in this set is measured by an opinion. There
is no objective whose measure is a regulator's view, an auditor's view, a consultant's assessment of
maturity, or a score on a scale somebody sells. Each of the eight is scored from an artefact that either
exists or does not."*

**The measures were fixed on 2026-03-06 and the owners were assigned at `DEC-106` on 2026-03-20.** **The
charter did not merely make its measures hard to vary; it made them unvariable.** `01.06 §9` answers the
question of who may vary an objective's measure with one word and a reason: *"Nobody, during the programme.
A measure that changes mid-flight is a measure that was chosen to be met"*. **The eight measures scored
below are therefore the eight measures the charter published on 2026-05-29, word for word** — transcribed
and checked at `EV-906`. **`AS-59` records that the assumption underneath that statement could not be
tested from the papers**: what was read is the charter, not the question of whether anybody varied it
outside the document.

## 2. `OS-01` — `OBJ-01`, the inventory. **Met**

**The objective**, as `01.06 §3` words it: *"A controlled inventory of computerised systems exists, and
every entry carries a determination of which of its records, if any, are Part 11 records"*. **Owner:
Priyanka Venkataraman.**

**The measure**, as `01.06 §3` states it: *"The inventory exists under document control; every entry
carries a determination record with a named determiner and a date; entries with no determination are
countable and counted"*.

**The artefact scored:** the systems register and the record-type register beside it, both published at
`MS-06` on 2026-07-31 and both under document control, with the six review triggers and the annual floor
`ADR-0015` set.

**Three limbs, and all three are satisfied.** The inventory exists and is under document control. Every
entry carries a determination record with a named determiner and a date. **And the third limb is the one
that decides the score, because it is the limb a programme is most tempted to omit**: entries with no
determination are countable and counted, and the register counts them rather than leaving them off. **A
register that quietly omits what it could not determine reports a completeness it does not have.**

**Met.**

**And the score is narrower than it looks, which is why the register's last column says so.**
**`OS-01` is a statement about the inventory as published; it is not a statement that the inventory is
still true.** `ADR-0015`'s six trigger events and their annual floor are the mechanism that was supposed to
keep it true, **and `SOP-DI-05` has fired on nothing, across nine phases and four hundred and twenty-five
days** — `IS-10`, `AS-19`, `DK-01`. **A determination nobody maintains is a determination that was true
once**, and the measure does not reach that. **The measure asks what it asks.**

**`OS-01` says nothing whatever about whether any record in the inventory is complete, consistent or
accurate.** **Applicability is not integrity** — `PR-12`.

## 3. `OS-02` — `OBJ-02`, the validation packages. **Not met**

**The objective:** *"Every GxP-critical platform holds an approved validation package covering its intended
use"*. **Owner: Colm Ó Braonáin.**

**The measure:** *"One approved package per platform, each carrying an intended-use statement and traceable
to the obligation at `21 CFR 211.68(b)`"*.

**The artefact scored:** the system rigour register and `04.07-the-packages-helix-already-holds.md`, both
published at `MS-08` on 2026-11-30.

**The measure fails on both of its limbs, and the second is the graver.**

**On coverage.** **Forty-four validation packages are held and seventeen systems hold none. 44 + 17 = 61**
— `04.07 §6`, `IS-23`. **Thirteen of the seventeen carry the validation status report EU GMP Annex 11
clause 11 names — EU-derived, a *should* in a guideline that is not FDA's, and binding nothing on the
US-facing route — as an item a periodic review would examine and would not find; the other four carry it
under the retention-estate item. 13 + 4 = 17.**

**On content.** **Not one of the forty-four evidences `21 CFR 11.10(a)`'s fourth clause** —
*"…the ability to discern invalid or altered records"* — `IS-22`. **A package that covers installation,
operation and performance and is silent on whether the system can discern an invalid or altered record has
not covered the clause**, and forty-four of forty-four are silent on it.

**And `IS-34` states what the two limbs come to together: a control position is not validation evidence,
and the evidence the Phase 04 framework requires is owed in full.** **Not one of the thirty-eight
obligations is discharged.** **No validation plan has been written** — `DP-21`, `AS-32` — which is also why
the validation execution line comes in under its committed figure, `09.04-the-envelope.md`, `IS-59`.

**Not met.**

**Nothing in this section withdraws, invalidates or supersedes any of the forty-four packages**, and
**nothing in it is a statement about whether any Helix system works.** `PR-27` carries the reading of a
coverage finding as a control position. **There is no Part 11 certification and no FDA-approved or
FDA-validated software**; **`21 CFR 211.68` is the surviving predicate-rule hook for computerised systems and it
survives enforcement discretion entirely** — `ADR-0003`. `21 CFR 211.68(a)` requires equipment used,
**including computers**, to be *"routinely calibrated, inspected, or checked according to a written
program designed to assure proper performance"*, and `21 CFR 211.68(b)` requires *"appropriate
validation data"* where laboratory calculations are eliminated by computerization. **The word
*validation* appears in `§ 211.68` once, inside that carve-out; it is not a general system-validation
clause and is not cited as one here.**

## 4. `OS-03` — `OBJ-03`, the named individual owner. **Met**

**The objective:** *"Every record type determined to be a Part 11 record has a named individual owner"*.
**Owner: Terrence Abbatiello.**

**The measure:** *"The record register contains zero entries owned by a team, a function or a job title
without a name"*.

**The artefact scored:** the record-type register's Owner column.

**The measure is a count of a column and the count is zero.** Not one entry is owned by a team, a function,
a department or a job title without a name; not one has been at any of the eight vantages since 2026-07-31;
**and the rule that produced that was fixed in Phase 01, before there was a control set to attach anybody
to.** `ADR-0008`'s own reason for the timing is worth quoting because it is the reason the score is what it
is: *"Fixing the ownership rule now costs nothing, because there is no specific control whose owner anybody
has an interest in leaving vague. Proposing the same rule in Phase 05, with a control set on the table and
named individuals about to be attached to inconvenient obligations, would be a negotiation."*

**Met.**

**And this is the objective on which the gap between the measure and the world is widest, and the register
says so.** **The measure counts names in a column. It does not ask whether any named individual has done
anything.** **Ownership does not mean performance**: the owner is accountable that the control is defined,
resourced, performed as defined and evidenced — and **`OS-02`, `OS-04`, `OS-05`, `OS-06` and `OS-08` are
the five places where a named owner holds an objective that is not met.** **The eight objectives have seven
named owners between them, and exactly one individual appears in both columns: the owner of `OS-03`, which
is Met, also owns `OS-06`, which is not.** That is not an inconsistency in the scoring; **it is what happens
when one measure asks for a name and another asks for a change.**

**`OS-03` is met exactly as its measure defines met, and by no wider definition.**

## 5. `OS-04` — `OBJ-04`, the audit trail. **Not met**

**The objective:** *"Audit trail functionality is configured, enabled and demonstrated on every system
holding records determined to be Part 11 records, with the audit trail retained at least as long as the
record"*. **Owner: Joachim Reuter.**

**The measure:** *"A per-system configuration record, plus a dated retrieval demonstration witnessed by a
named person not in the operating team"*.

**The artefacts scored:** the control gap register, the auditable event register and the retention
derivation register.

**Three grounds, and each would defeat the measure alone.**

**First, configuration.** **`CG-14` to `CG-18` are five gaps against `21 CFR 11.10(e)` across the estate.**
`CG-16` names **eight systems on which the product has no audit trail function at all and none can be
configured.** `CG-14` names eight on which the trail's scope is configurable at administrator level and a
change to that configuration is not itself recorded. `CG-17` names four on which the trail is written to a
local circular store overwritten while the record it describes is still live. **A configuration record
cannot record a configuration that does not exist.**

**Second, the two classes that cannot be enabled.** **`AE-08` and `AE-16` cannot be enabled by any setting
available to Helix on the product version Helix runs** — `IS-36`. `AE-08` is the class that records an
integration parameter change applied to a single injection at review time, **the act that sits closest to
the number that is reported.** **The whole of the ground for that statement is `AS-43`, a vendor statement
Helix has not tested**, and the assumption is stated rather than buried. **A product limitation is a fact
about a product; it is not a discharge of an obligation** — `PR-43`.

**Third, retention, and it is the ground that has no remedy in configuration at all.** **`21 CFR 11.10(e)`
does not set a period. Its third sentence sets a relation**, requiring audit trail documentation to be
retained *"…for a period at least as long as that required for the subject electronic records…"*. **On
sixteen systems the relation has no second term at all**, because those systems hold only record types for
which no predicate rule sets a period — `07.09`, `IS-43`, `IS-44`. **A relation with one term is not a
comparison that has failed; it is a comparison that cannot be made.**

**And the demonstration limb is separately unsatisfied.** The measure asks for **a dated retrieval
demonstration witnessed by a named person not in the operating team**, per system. **No such demonstration
exists for any of the sixty-one.** `SUC-2` puts the same point in one word: an audit trail that has been
**demonstrated, not merely asserted**.

**Not met.**

**Nothing in this section re-determines any Phase 05 cell, moves any `CG` row, or re-reads any
configuration.** **Nothing in Phase 09 opened a system.**

## 6. `OS-05` — `OBJ-05`, the shared accounts. **Not met**

**The objective:** *"Shared and generic accounts are removed from systems holding GxP records — or, where a
technical constraint prevents removal, the constraint is documented and a named individual owns the
compensating control"*. **Owner: Amara Sissoko.**

**The measure:** *"The account register shows every shared account either closed with a date, or open with
a documented constraint and a named compensating-control owner. No third state"*.

**The artefact scored:** the control gap register — `CG-11` and `CG-13`.

**This is the only one of the eight measures that names the failure mode in advance, and the estate is in
it.** The measure allows two states: **closed with a date**, or **open with a documented constraint and a
named compensating-control owner.** It then adds three words — **"No third state"** — which exist because
whoever drafted the measure knew what the third state would look like.

**Nine systems carry a shared account.** **`CG-11` names seven — `SYS-003`, `SYS-004`, `SYS-005`,
`SYS-006`, `SYS-007`, `SYS-009`, `SYS-010` — a shared local operator account. `CG-13` names two —
`SYS-031`, `SYS-032` — a shared workstation logon. 7 + 2 = 9.** **The packaging line system FDA observed is
among the seven.**

**Not one of the nine is closed with a date.** **And on not one of the nine does a compensating control
have a named individual owner.** **The nine sit in the third state the measure says does not exist**: open,
with the constraint described, and with nobody named against anything.

**Not met.**

**Two things this section states carefully.**

**The distinction Phase 02 drew is carried and not lost.** **A shared account does not fail
`21 CFR 11.10(d)` on its face**, because access is still limited to a group of authorised operators. **What
it defeats is attribution**, which sits on the predicate-rule side at `21 CFR 211.188(b)(11)` and
`21 CFR 211.194(a)(7)`. FDA's December 2018 drug CGMP data integrity guidance puts the second half of it
in its own words: *"Shared, read-only user accounts that do not allow the user to modify data or settings
are acceptable for viewing data, but they do not conform with the part 211 and 212 requirements for
actions, such as second person review, to be attributable to a specific individual."* **On none of the nine
is the shared credential a read-only account: each is an operating account through which entries are
made** — `EV-819`, and that is Phase 08's finding, not this chapter's.

**And no observation is closed by anything in this section, and none is described as closed.** **`OBS-4`'s
position is `08.06-obs-4.md`'s and stands exactly as published**: the condition confirmed and not
corrected. **A position is not a closure; closure is a representation to FDA, and this programme makes
none.**

## 7. `OS-06` — `OBJ-06`, the record review procedures. **Not met**

**The objective:** *"The record review procedures state whether and how the associated audit trail forms
part of the record reviewed"*. **Owner: Terrence Abbatiello.**

**The measure:** *"Approved revisions of the production and control record review procedure
(`21 CFR 211.192`) and the laboratory record review procedure (`21 CFR 211.194(a)(8)`), each stating the
position expressly rather than by omission"*.

**The artefacts scored:** the two procedures the measure names, as read at `EV-807`, and their status as
reported at `RC-05` and `RC-19`.

**The measure names two documents and asks for an approved revision of each. Neither has been revised.**

**`RC-05` is the commitment the written response of 2026-03-06 made to revise the laboratory record review
procedure. Its named owner is Terrence Abbatiello, the date it gave was 2026-09-30, and it is Not met.**
**`RC-19` is the commitment to require the quality unit's review of an electronic batch record to include
the record of changes made after approval. It is Not met.** **Both are past the date the response stated.**

**The measure's last clause is the one that closes the argument.** It asks that each procedure state the
position **expressly rather than by omission** — and **a procedure that is silent on a question is not
neutral about it.** **Neither procedure says it.**

**Not met.**

**And this is where the tempting defence has to be refused in terms.** **Phase 04 specified nine SOPs.**
The framework was built, the procedure set was designed, the obligations were mapped into it, and **none of
the nine has been applied to anything** — `IS-24`, `PR-32`, `DP-22`. **Specification is not the issue. The
measure does not ask whether a procedure was designed; it asks for an approved revision of two named
documents, and there is none.** **A programme that scored this objective from the specification would be
scoring from progress**, which `ADR-0058` forbids.

**What `21 CFR 211.194(a)(8)` requires is established and is not re-argued here** — `08.05-obs-2.md §2`
owns that argument. **What the procedures say is also established: they still do not say it.**

## 8. `OS-07` — `OBJ-07`, the envelope. **Met**

**The objective:** *"The programme is delivered within its approved envelope"*. **Owner: Lydia
Marchetti-Nwosu.**

**The measure:** *"Committed and actual spend reported monthly against the ten budget lines at `CAL-01`;
variance stated as a figure, not as a narrative"*.

**The artefact scored:** the monthly budget reports made at `CAL-01` against the ten lines at `01.06 §5`,
for fourteen months — `EV-914`, `EV-915`.

**Two limbs, and both are satisfied.** **Committed and actual were reported monthly against all ten lines
for the whole of the programme's life**, at every sitting of the Data Integrity Steering Committee from
2026-03-13 to 2027-04-29. **The variance is stated as a figure on every line and on every report**, and not
as a narrative. **The programme closes under its envelope.**

**Met.**

**And `09.04-the-envelope.md` owns what the shape of that variance means, and this chapter states only the
score.** **Two lines over, three under, five on plan. 2 + 3 + 5 = 10.** **Two of the three under-lines are
under because work named in them was not done** — the validation execution line and the account remediation
line — and **`IS-59` is published as an open issue rather than as a footnote to a Met.**

**The measure asks whether the reporting happened and whether the envelope held. It does not ask what the
money bought.** **Scoring it any other way would be scoring an objective against a measure it does not
have**, and `ADR-0058` refuses that in both directions: **an objective is not failed for a reason its
measure does not name, any more than it is passed for one.**

## 9. `OS-08` — `OBJ-08`, the response commitments. **Not met**

**The objective:** *"Every commitment in Helix's written response of 2026-03-06 has a named owner, a due
date and an evidence reference, and its status is reported quarterly"*. **Owner: Rebekah Sandquist.**

**The measure:** *"The response commitment register shows, per commitment, an owner, a due date, an
evidence reference and a status; reported at `CAL-04`"*.

**The artefact scored:** the commitment register — `RC-01` to `RC-22`.

**The artefact exists. It has existed since the day the charter was approved. And the measure still fails,
on both limbs.**

**On content.** **Of the fourteen commitments assessed, three carry no completion criterion** — `RC-07`,
`RC-14` and `RC-20`, `IS-51` — **and one carries no date at all.** The three are not recorded as met and
not as unmet: **a commitment written so that nobody can say whether it was met is a commitment nobody can
be held to**, and recording it either way would credit Helix with a change it cannot demonstrate or
manufacture a failure out of a drafting defect — `ADR-0052`. **The measure asks for a status per
commitment. Three of the fourteen have no status any register could carry**, because **the element that is
absent is not the owner and it is not the date, it is the test.**

**On reporting, and this is the limb that fails outright.** The measure asks that status be **reported at
`CAL-04`**, the Board audit committee's quarterly data integrity report. **The register was not opened
between the day it was filed on 2026-03-06 and 2027-03-30** — thirteen months, and five occurrences of
`CAL-04` in between. **No occurrence of `CAL-04` reported a status read from it, because nobody read it.**

**Not met.**

**And this is the score the rest of the phase has to be honest about, so it is stated here rather than
smoothed later.** **`OS-08` is an artefact objective. The artefact was not missing. Nobody read it.**
`09.03-what-the-score-is-shaped-like.md` takes that up and does not fold it into a pattern it does not fit.

**Nothing in this section rewrites, varies, narrows, replaces or adds to any commitment the response made,
and nothing in it supplies a completion criterion to `RC-07`, `RC-14` or `RC-20`** — `ADR-0052`, `PR-59`.
**Writing one now would be writing a commitment Helix did not file.** **The response of 2026-03-06 remains
the only thing Helix has said to the agency about any of this**, and **nothing in this chapter is a
communication to FDA** — `ADR-0064`.

## 11. What this chapter does not establish

- **No compliance conclusion and no legal conclusion.** **Five *Not met* scores are Helix's own assessment
  of its own position against its own charter**, and are not a violation, a breach, a non-compliance, a
  finding or an inspectional observation.
- **No readiness claim.** Neither *Helix is ready* nor *Helix is compliant* appears in this phase.
- **No forecast of what any inspection would find**, from three Met or from five Not met.
- **No closure of any observation**, and no representation to FDA — `ADR-0064`.
- **No CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan and no cost.**
- **No re-determination, re-scoring, re-banding or re-derivation.** Every figure above is cited from a
  published register and no cell in any of them moves.
- **No retention period** for any of the fourteen record types nothing sets one for.
- **No result of any periodic review and of any independent assessment.**
- **No candidate explanation of `IS-01`**, which closed at Phase 08 and stays closed.
- **No assessment of any individual's work**, and **no aggregate, index, percentage or maturity level.**
