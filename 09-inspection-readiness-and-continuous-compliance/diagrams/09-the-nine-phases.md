**What this diagram shows.** It shows the nine phases of the programme in order, from kickoff on 2026-03-02
to close-out on 2027-04-30 — **four hundred and twenty-five days, counted inclusive of both endpoints as a
duration; four hundred and twenty-four as the interval from kickoff to the close-out vantage** — with one
line for what each phase
established and the milestone that closed it. `01.10 §2` named the nine at the outset and described each in
the future tense on purpose; **this draws the same nine in the past tense, from what each phase actually
published.** The sequence ends where it ends: **there is no tenth phase, no successor programme is
chartered, and no envelope exists after the last vantage.** The chapter that owns this is
`../09.13-close-out.md`.

**What this diagram does not show.** **It shows no tenth phase and no successor arrangement**, and the
empty terminus is the position at the vantage and not a prediction about what follows. **It shows no result
of any phase as a compliance position**: each line records what a phase established, and **establishing
that something is not in place is not a finding, a deviation, a non-compliance or an inspectional
observation.** **It shows no closure of any observation** — Phase 08's line says positions and means
positions, and **closure is a representation to FDA that this programme does not make.** **It shows no
figures for any phase**, deliberately: every count in this repository has exactly one owning register, and
a number restated in a diagram is a number that will eventually disagree with itself. **It shows no
milestone as delayed or early** — thirteen milestones, all complete, and `PR-04`'s compression did not
materialise. **It forecasts nothing, and it makes no claim that Helix is ready for anything.** **And the
Phase 01 line does not name what the programme opened on**: `IS-01` closed at Phase 08, the refusal
recorded in its closure is permanent, and **no candidate explanation appears here or anywhere in this
phase.**
