## 3. What closes, and what each never asked

**Three close, and `ADR-0063` records why there are not more: the programme closes with its open entries
open, and nothing is closed at close-out for the sake of closing it.** **A close-out sweep was available —
closing entries whose subject matter has been superseded, absorbed or made academic — and it was argued and
refused.** **Believed complete is not a closure condition.**

**`IS-03`, closed 2027-04-23 by Amara Sissoko, who raised it on 2026-04-24.** **What closed it:** the
predicate-rule attribute the entry asked the systems register to carry was never that register's to carry.
**The unit of determination is the record type**, the record-type register carries the predicate rule on
all fifty-eight rows, and each of those rows names the systems that hold the record type. **An inventory is
a population, not a determination.** **The answer had been available since 2026-07-31 and nobody wrote it
down for three hundred and sixty-four days.** **What it never asked:** whether anything joins a record type
or a system **to a product**. **Nothing does** — `IS-54`, `DP-44`, both open, and it is why Helix cannot
say which systems the EU-facing route reaches.

**`IS-52`, closed 2027-04-30 by Lydia Marchetti-Nwosu, who raised it on 2027-03-31.** **What closed it:**
`DP-43` discharged, by five statements — one per commitment, from the three owners the response named
across `RC-03`, `RC-04`, `RC-10`, `RC-11` and `RC-18` — **each a new act with its own record, and no
tracker edited to agree with any register.** **Both original records stand unaltered**, because two records
made to agree by editing one of them are a tidier finding about nothing. **What it never asked:** whether
anything will keep the two records in step in future. **Nothing will.** No procedure requires the
commitment register to be read.

**`IS-58`, closed 2027-04-29 by Gideon Halvorsen, who raised it on 2027-04-09.** **What closed it: the
answer, and the answer is no.** **Helix cannot make this programme independent retrospectively.** Every
determination in the repository was made by the people who own the things determined, and **no later act
reaches backwards into a determination already made.** **What Phase 09 established instead is the
arrangement under which the next assessment is independent** — `09.09-independence-cannot-be-retrofitted.md`
owns it. **What it never asked:** whether an arrangement produces an assessment. **It does not.** **`IS-63`
carries the first independent assessment under the arrangement and nobody has scheduled one** — no date, no
scope, no commissioning decision and no party.

**`IS-01` closed at Phase 08 and stays closed.** **The refusal recorded in its closure is permanent and was
expressly not handed to this phase.** **No document in Phase 09 names, numbers, quotes, reproduces,
summarises, characterises, paraphrases or alludes to any candidate explanation, and no document in this
repository may be cited in support of one.**

## 5. What is still not true

Stated as absences, because an absence written down is an absence somebody can act on.

- **Seventeen of the sixty-one systems hold no validation package**, and **not one of the forty-four that
  exist evidences `21 CFR 11.10(a)`'s fourth clause.** **Not one of the thirty-eight obligations is
  discharged. No validation plan has been written.**
- **Nine systems carry a shared account**, none closed with a date and none with a named
  compensating-control owner.
- **Neither of the two record review procedures has been revised**, and **none of the nine SOPs Phase 04
  specified has been applied to anything.**
- **On sixteen systems the retention relation has no second term**, and **fourteen record types have a
  period set by no predicate rule and determined by nobody.**
- **Two auditable event classes cannot be enabled on the product version Helix runs**, and the whole of the
  ground for that is a vendor statement Helix has not tested.
- **Sixty-one periodic reviews are established and none has been performed.** The earliest first due date
  is 2027-05-31.
- **Read access for the audit function has been requested on none of the sixty-one systems and granted on
  none.** **A grant is decided and dated; no access is described as performed.**
- **The estate-wide control position is ninety-one days old and nothing has re-taken it.**
- **`ADR-0015`'s review trigger has never fired.**
- **Seven calendar entries outlive the programme with no named owner beyond today** — `IS-07`, raised
  2026-05-27, **the only entry in this repository carried as deliberately unresolved, through nine phases.**
- **Twelve of the forty-nine open entries have a function and no name. Four have nothing.** **Two of the
  twelve artefacts the programme built have a function and no name. One has nobody, and it is the evidence
  index.**
- **No observation is closed and none is described as closed. No representation about any of the three has
  been made to FDA.** **The written response of 2026-03-06 remains the only thing Helix has said to the
  agency about any of this.**

against ten budget lines. Eighteen decisions in this phase, seven of them argued at length as architecture decisions, and two
