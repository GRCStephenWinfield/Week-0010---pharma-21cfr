## 4. Item two — the closures, and why there are three. `ADR-0063`, `DEC-915`

**Taken by Dr Marguerite Oyelaran on 2027-04-29.**

> **The programme closes with its open entries open. Nothing is closed at close-out for the sake of closing
> it.**

**The alternative was on the table and the chair asked for the refusal to be minuted in full.** **A
close-out sweep — closing entries whose subject matter has been superseded, absorbed or made academic by a
later phase — would have reduced the open count materially and would have been defensible in each
individual case.** **It was refused because an entry closed without evidence has been tidied and not
resolved**, and because **believed complete is not a closure condition** — `01.12 §9`, which has held since
2026-05-29.

**Three closed in this phase and each names what closed it, its evidence, and what it never asked.**

**`IS-03`**, 2027-04-23, Amara Sissoko. **The predicate-rule attribute was never the systems register's to
carry.** **What it never asked: whether anything joins a record type or a system to a product. Nothing
does.**

**`IS-58`**, 2027-04-29, Gideon Halvorsen, ratified at this sitting. **Helix cannot make this programme
independent retrospectively, and the answer is no.** **What it never asked: whether an arrangement produces
an assessment. It does not** — `IS-63`.

**`IS-52`**, expected at publication on 2027-04-30 under `DEC-918`, on five statements discharging
`DP-43`, **with no tracker edited to agree with any register.** **What it never asks: whether anything will
keep the two records in step in future. Nothing will.**

**`IS-01` closed at Phase 08 and stays closed.** **The refusal recorded in its closure is permanent, was
expressly not handed to this phase, and no candidate explanation is named anywhere in it.** **`PR-63`
remains open.**

## 7. Item five — close-out is not a communication to FDA. `ADR-0064`, `DEC-917`

**Taken by Rebekah Sandquist on 2027-04-29.**

> **Close-out is not a communication to FDA, and no artefact in this phase is written to be read by one.**

**The alternative was available and was refused: writing the close-out pack so that it would read well to
an inspector.** **A document written for a second reader acquires a second criterion — how it will look —
and the second criterion wins arguments the first should have won.**

**The committee recorded four consequences.**

**Every document in this phase is an internal document and carries the classification line saying so.**

**No observation is closed and none is described as closed.** **The three positions Phase 08 recorded stand
exactly as published**: `OBS-2` confirmed and incomplete, `OBS-4` confirmed and not corrected, `OBS-6`
corrected and unevidenced by the programme's registers. **A position is not a closure, and closure is a
representation to FDA that this programme does not make** — `ADR-0051`.

**The written response of 2026-03-06 remains the only thing Helix has said to the agency about any of
this.** **Nothing in this phase amends, supplements, supersedes or adds to it**, and **no completion
criterion is written for `RC-07`, `RC-14` or `RC-20`.**

**And no document in this phase forecasts what any inspection will find, or states that Helix is ready for
one.** **`01.10 §6` has carried that refusal since 2026-05-29 and it holds at the last sitting.**

**`PR-70` was opened.**

## 9. Item seven — the dissolution of this committee

**The Data Integrity Steering Committee first sat on 2026-03-13, and it stands in the programme calendar as
`CAL-01` — monthly, with additional sittings called by the chair, owned by the Vice President, Quality —
adopted at `DEC-120` on 2026-05-29.** **It has sat every month of the programme's life, and `AS-01` — that
it would continue monthly to close-out — is held and consumed.**

**It is dissolved at the close of this sitting.**

**The committee recorded four things about the dissolution.**

**It dissolves because the programme it governed ends.** **A steering committee for a programme that has
closed is a body with no business**, and **a committee that continues in order to have somewhere to put
unresolved items is a place where items go to be discussed rather than to be taken.**

**It owns nothing and therefore transfers nothing.** **A committee may approve. It may not own** — `ADR-0008`.
**No control, procedure, record or obligation in this programme was owned by this committee at any
vantage**, and **none of the forty-nine open entries or the twelve artefacts is handed to it, from it, or
through it.**

**Its dissolution changes nothing about `CAL-02` to `CAL-12`, and it resolves `IS-07` in no respect.**
**`CAL-01` ends with the programme. Seven of the twelve calendar entries do not**, and **their ownership
after today is exactly what it was before this sitting: unstated.** **`CAL-03`, `CAL-04` and `CAL-11` are
standing bodies of Helix's quality system and of its Board's governance, exist independently of this
programme, and are untouched by anything in this minute.**

**And it does not close, discharge, transfer or extinguish anything.** **The register is what it is at
`../logs/raid-log.md`, and it is the same register after this sitting as before it.**

**The chair thanked nobody, at her own request, and asked that the minute record why: the programme
delivered three of eight objectives, and a vote of thanks at the end of a minute that says so would be
read as a comment on the other five.** **The final item was taken without further business.**
