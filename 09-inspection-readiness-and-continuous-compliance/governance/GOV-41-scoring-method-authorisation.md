## 4. Item two — the scoring rule. `ADR-0058`, `DEC-903`

**Taken by Lydia Marchetti-Nwosu on 2027-04-14.**

> **An objective is scored from the artefact its measure names, and from nothing else.**

**Not from effort. Not from progress. Not from intent. Not from an opinion, a regulator's view, an
auditor's view or a consultant's assessment of maturity.** The charter said so about itself at `01.06 §3`:
*"No objective in this set is measured by an opinion. There is no objective whose measure is a regulator's
view, an auditor's view, a consultant's assessment of maturity, or a score on a scale somebody sells. Each
of the eight is scored from an artefact that either exists or does not."*

**Two states and no third.** **Met and Not met.** **The committee considered a third state — a partial —
and refused it**, and `ADR-0058` argues the refusal at length. The short form the chair asked to be
minuted: **a partial would be used first on the objective where the artefact exists and does not do what
the measure asks, and once used there it would be available everywhere.**

**The measures are the charter's and are not the committee's to vary.** `01.06 §9` answers the question of
who may vary an objective's measure: *"Nobody, during the programme. A measure that changes mid-flight is a
measure that was chosen to be met"*. **The eight measures to be scored against are the eight the charter
published on 2026-05-29, word for word.**

**`ADR-0058` is the record and `../09.01-how-an-objective-is-scored.md` is the argument.** **Neither
restates the other in full.**

## 5. Item three — the objective score record form. `DEC-904`

**Taken by Dr Marguerite Oyelaran on 2027-04-14.** **Authorised empty, and authorised before any objective
was scored.**

**The form requires, per objective:** the objective as the charter words it · the named individual owner ·
**the measure verbatim** · **the artefact scored** · the position, Met or Not met · **whether the measure is
satisfied by one of the programme's own artefacts or requires the estate to change** · what would change the
score.

**Two of those columns were argued and both are minuted.**

**The measure verbatim.** **A measure summarised is a measure that can drift towards the evidence
available**, and the form therefore carries the charter's own words and requires them to be checked against
the published document. **`EV-906` is that check.**

**The artefact-or-estate column.** The committee recorded that it is **a classification of what each
measure requires to exist**, taken at the same moment the artefact is identified and **before any score is
known.** **It is not a second score, it produces no aggregate, and it is not a judgement of importance.**

**The first objective was scored on 2027-04-20, six days after this form was authorised.**

---

## 6. Item four — what the score will not be

**The committee recorded four things the score would not be, and opened `PR-64` against the first.**

**It will not be a general-purpose grading scheme.** **The instrument reads eight measures.** It is not
applied to a system, a control, a record type, a commitment or a function, and **`01.12 §4`'s prohibition on
scoring the programme registers is unchanged: `PR-01` to `PR-70` carry no likelihood, no impact and no
score, and none ever will.**

**It will not aggregate.** **No index, no percentage, no maturity level and no single number.** **The
result is eight rows.**

**It will not be a verdict on anybody.** **Each objective has a named individual owner and the register is
not a report on those people** — `01.09 §2` holds without variation.

**And it will not be a compliance position.** **`ADR-0059` was scheduled for 2027-04-19 and is not taken
here**; the committee noted only that **a score against a charter measure is a statement about an artefact
and about nothing else.**
