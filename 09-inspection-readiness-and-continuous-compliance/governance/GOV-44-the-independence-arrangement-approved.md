## 3. Item one — the question

**`IS-58` was raised on 2027-04-09, at the Phase 08 vantage, by the individual who chairs internal audit:**

> **Every determination in this repository was made by the people who also own the things determined, and
> the one function that could have tested them independently has never been given the access to do it. The
> programme has assessed itself.**

**The committee recorded the ground, from the entry's own words.** The record-level determination was made
by the Data Integrity Lead and approved through Quality. The complexity-and-reliability band was determined
by the validation function. **The six hundred and seventy-one control positions were determined by the
function that operates the systems.** The backup and restore position was taken from enquiries answered by
the people who operate the arrangements — **`AS-22`, `AS-40` and `AS-49` are the three places that was
written down before anybody added them up.** **The retention derivation, the auditable event
characterisation, the commitment assessment and the three dispositions were all made inside the programme
that had an interest in what they said.**

**And read access for the audit function has been requested on none of the sixty-one systems and granted on
none** — `IS-55`, `AC-05`, `DP-46`.

## 4. Item two — the answer, and `IS-58` closed. `DEC-916`

**Taken by Gideon Halvorsen on 2027-04-29.**

> **Helix cannot make this programme independent retrospectively, and no act available to Helix at this
> vantage or at any later one would change that.**

**A later grant of access does not reach backwards into a determination already made.** **A later
assessment would be an assessment of the artefacts, not of the making of them.** **What was closed is the
question whether independence can be retrofitted. What was not closed, and cannot be, is the condition the
question describes.**

**The committee recorded three things about the closure at the chair's request.**

**It is not an allegation of bias and it names no individual.** It records the structure the work was
produced under.

**It does not make the determinations independent, and no reader may take it as having done so.** **The
determinations stand exactly as published, made by the people who made them** — **and every one of them was
made on a method fixed before the work and published with its assumptions**, which is the whole of what the
programme could do about it.

**It is not `IS-01` in another form and it does not touch `IS-01`.** **`IS-01` closed at Phase 08, its
refusal is permanent, and it was expressly not handed to this phase.** **No candidate explanation is named
by this closure, reachable through it, or supported by any document in this repository.** **`PR-63` remains
open and the boundary holds.**

**`ADR-0063` was taken separately at `GOV-45` and the chair noted its bearing here: the programme closes
with its open entries open, and `IS-58` closed because it was answered and not because the phase was
ending.**

## 5. Item three — the arrangement, and its two limbs

**What is approved is the arrangement under which the *next* assessment is independent.** **Both limbs are
arrangements.** **`../09.09-independence-cannot-be-retrofitted.md` owns the argument and this minute records
what was approved.**

**Limb one — read access for the audit function.** **Decided at `DEC-911` on 2027-04-26 by Amara Sissoko,
and recorded as decided and dated.** **It is not recorded as performed, and `ADR-0061` is the reason:** an
access grant is recorded as decided and dated, never as performed, until an artefact evidences the
performance. **`EV-925` and `EV-931` evidence a decision.** **No artefact in this phase evidences access on
any system.** **`AC-05` is not described as capable of being met.** **`audit-condition-register.md`'s
published position — requested on none of the sixty-one and granted on none — is unmoved.** **`IS-55` stays
open and `DP-46` stays open, and both say so.**

**Limb two — the periodic review owned by a function that owns no system.** **The reason is `ADR-0002`'s,
applied one step further:** *"…the function that judges whether a system's validated state holds should not
sit in the line that builds and administers it"*. **`DP-45` asks for a named individual reviewer for each
review who is not the owner of the system reviewed, and it is open: no reviewer is named on any of the
sixty-one rows.** **Reviews performed: none.** **The earliest first due date is 2027-05-31.** **Nothing at
this sitting reported the result of a review, because none has been performed** — `IS-57`.

**`PR-68` and `PR-69` were opened against the two ways the arrangement will be misread.**

## 6. Item four — an arrangement is not an assessment. `IS-63`

**Raised here, at the same decision, by the same individual, on the same day `IS-58` closed.**

> **No independent assessment has been scheduled. The arrangement exists; the assessment does not, and the
> arrangement is not one.**

**No date. No scope. No commissioning decision. No party.** **`DP-50` was opened against that absence and
its source is the Board's audit committee**, which established the internal audit function and to which it
reports.

**`CAL-09` and `CAL-10` are not it.** They are the programme's own assurance activities — an internal audit
of the remediated programme and an independent third-party audit by a party that designed and implemented
none of it. **No result of either is stated, summarised, characterised or anticipated anywhere in this
phase**, and `AS-12` and `DP-06` are carried open unchanged. **The programme does not forecast the outcome
of an audit any more than it forecasts the outcome of an inspection.**

**The committee recorded the pattern the entry belongs to, because it has now recurred five times.** **An
arrangement to review a system is not a review of it. A frequency adopted is not a review performed. A
completion criterion written now is not a commitment filed. A grant decided is not access performed. And an
arrangement for independence is not an assessment.** **`IS-20` is where the shape was first written down,
on 2026-09-29, and it is still open.**
