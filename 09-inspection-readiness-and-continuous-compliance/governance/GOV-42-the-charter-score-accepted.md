## 4. Item two — the register accepted. `DEC-907`

**Taken by Dr Marguerite Oyelaran on 2027-04-21.**

**`../logs/objective-score-register.md` accepted as the authoritative record of the score** — `OS-01` to
`OS-08`, with the measure verbatim, the artefact scored, the position, the artefact-or-estate
classification and what would change the score, on every row.

**The committee recorded three limits on what it was accepting.**

**The register records eight positions against eight measures at one vantage.** **It produces no aggregate,
no index, no percentage and no maturity level**, and **it does not score `SUC-1` to `SUC-5`.**

**A score of Not met is Helix's own assessment of its own position against its own charter.** **It is not a
violation, not a breach, not a non-compliance, not a finding and not an inspectional observation.**

**And acceptance of the register is not a compliance position.** `ADR-0059` had been taken two days earlier
and was ratified at this sitting: **inspection readiness is the ability to answer, and this repository never
reports it as a compliance position.** **The chair recorded that the phrase *Helix is ready* would not
appear in any document of this phase, and neither would *Helix is compliant*.**

## 5. Item three — the pattern, and `IS-60`

**The committee recorded the cut in the register's own column.**

**Four objectives are satisfied by an artefact the programme itself produces — `OBJ-01`, `OBJ-03`, `OBJ-07`
and `OBJ-08`. Three of the four are Met.**

**Four are satisfied only if something outside the programme's own artefacts changes — `OBJ-02`, `OBJ-04`,
`OBJ-05` and `OBJ-06`. None of the four is Met.**

**3 + 1 = 4, 0 + 4 = 4, and 4 + 4 = 8.**

**`IS-60` was raised at this sitting**, owned by the chair. **The committee recorded four things about it
and asked that all four be minuted.**

**It is not an accusation of bad faith and it names no individual.**

**It is not a claim that the programme should have done the estate work.** The scope at `01.06 §2` puts the
determination, the framework, the registers and the evidence inside the programme and puts a validation
execution resource, an account administration function, a vendor product change and the quality unit's
approval capacity outside it. **The determiner does not operate.**

**It is not a claim that the artefacts are worth less than the changes.** **A determination that has not
been made cannot be acted on by anybody.**

**And the mechanism is stated rather than implied: the estate changes when somebody with the authority to
change it is funded to change it.** **The committee recorded that at this vantage nobody has been, and that
the charter ends on 2027-04-30.** **`DP-51` was opened later, at publication, against that absence.**

**`OS-08` was minuted separately at the chair's request, because it does not fit the pattern and is not to
be folded into it.** **It is an artefact objective and it fails — not because the artefact is missing, but
because three of the fourteen commitments assessed carry no test, and because nobody opened the register
between 2026-03-06 and 2027-03-30.**

## 6. Item four — the handover rule. `ADR-0060`, `DEC-908`

**Taken by Gideon Halvorsen on 2027-04-21.**

> **An open entry is handed to a named individual or it is recorded as having no destination. A function is
> not a destination.**

**The reason, minuted in three clauses: a function cannot accept, because acceptance is an act by somebody
on a date; a function cannot be asked a question and answer it; and a function cannot be held to a date,
because a commitment with no individual behind it has no failure mode.**

**It is `ADR-0008` applied to an entry rather than to a control.** **A team owner is a plausible-looking
absence of an owner.**

**The committee recorded that the alternative was available and was refused**: recording a function as the
destination and treating receipt as acceptance. **It would have produced a register in which almost every
open entry had a destination, and it would have been false.**

**No entry was handed over at this sitting.** **The register was drawn on 2027-04-26 and accepted at
`GOV-43` on 2027-04-27.**
