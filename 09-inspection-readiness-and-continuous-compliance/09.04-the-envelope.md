# 09.04 — The Envelope

*The programme closes under its envelope, and two of the three lines that came in under did so because work named in them was not done. All content is fictional and illustrative.*

---

## 1. What the measure asks, and what this chapter reports

**`OBJ-07`'s measure**, as `01.06 §3` states it: *"Committed and actual spend reported monthly against the
ten budget lines at `CAL-01`; variance stated as a figure, not as a narrative"*.

**That is a measure about reporting.** It asks two things: **was committed and actual reported monthly
against the ten lines**, and **is the variance stated as a figure.** **It does not ask what the money
bought.** `09.02-the-charter-scored.md §8` scores it **Met** on that ground and
`logs/objective-score-register.md` carries the row.

**This chapter reports the variance itself, and it reports it as the measure asks: as a figure, and not as
a narrative.** **The figure the measure asks for is per line, and there are ten lines**, so **the report is
a count of lines and a direction on each.** **No currency amount appears in this chapter**, and none is
needed: **the ten committed figures are published at `01.06 §5` and at FACTS §9.2 and are not restated
here**, because a number restated in three places is a number that will eventually disagree with itself.

**The artefact is `EV-914`** — the fourteen monthly budget reports made at `CAL-01`, committed and actual
against the ten lines, from the first sitting on 2026-03-13 to the last on 2027-04-29 — **and `EV-915`, the
reconciliation of directions across the ten.** **`AS-60` states what the report rests on**: that the
fourteen reports are the whole of what was reported, and that the actual column describes money spent
rather than money committed or accrued. **The assumption was not tested.**

---

## 1.1 What the regulation says about resourcing, and what this chapter does not say about it

**No predicate rule requires a budget, an envelope or a variance report**, and `OBJ-07` exists because
Helix's Board asked for one. **`21 CFR 211.25(c)` does require that there be an adequate number of
qualified personnel to perform and supervise the manufacture, processing, packing or holding of each drug
product**, and `21 CFR 211.22(a)` gives the quality control unit its responsibility and authority.

**This chapter reports that work named in two budget lines was not done. It draws no conclusion under
either provision, and a reader must not draw one from it** — `ADR-0057`, carried forward, and `09.12 §2`.
**What is recorded is a fact about spend and scope. What is not recorded is an adequacy judgement, which
this programme has no standing to make about itself.**

---

## 2. The reporting limb

**Fourteen monthly reports, one per sitting of the Data Integrity Steering Committee, across the whole of
the programme's life.** **`CAL-01`'s cadence is monthly, with additional sittings called by the chair**,
and the calendar records what an omitted occurrence would mean: *"Budget variance, scope change and
escalation have no forum for a month; `OBJ-07` reporting lapses"*.

**No occurrence was omitted.** **`AS-01` — that the Steering Committee continues monthly to close-out — is
held and consumed**, and the final sitting of 2027-04-29 is minuted at `GOV-45`.

**Every report carried all ten lines, committed and actual, and stated the variance as a figure.** **No
report stated a variance as a narrative, and none omitted a line because the line had not moved.** **A
budget report that drops the lines with nothing on them is a report that stops anybody noticing when
nothing happens on a line for fourteen months** — which is precisely what two of the ten did.

**The reporting limb is satisfied. That is the whole of what makes `OS-07` Met.**

---

## 3. The variance, as line counts and directions

| Direction at close-out | Lines | Count |
|---|---|---|
| **Over the committed figure** | **2** | Two lines closed above what was committed to them |
| **Under the committed figure** | **3** | Three lines closed below what was committed to them |
| **On plan** | **5** | Five lines closed at the committed figure, within the reporting tolerance the monthly report has used since 2026-03-13 |
| **Total** | **10** | **2 + 3 + 5 = 10** |

**The programme closes under its envelope.** **The three under-lines are larger in aggregate than the two
over-lines**, and the committed total closes below the approved envelope.

**Neither the contingency line nor the unallocated headroom was exhausted.** **The two figures are held by
different people on purpose: the programme manager may draw on contingency, and only the sponsor may
release headroom** — `01.06 §5`, `PR-01`. **This chapter does not state how much of either was used**,
because the measure does not ask and because **`01.06 §5` is the owning chapter for those two figures and
they are not restated here.**

**`PR-01` — that committed scope exceeds the envelope because remediation reveals work the estimate did not
carry — did not materialise in the direction it was written for**, and it is retained rather than deleted,
**because a risk deleted the moment it fails to occur teaches nothing about why it did not.** **Why it did
not is the subject of the next section.**

---

## 4. The two over-lines

**Two lines closed above their committed figures**, and **both are lines on which work was performed.**

**Neither over-line is a finding about anybody's estimating**, and this chapter draws no conclusion about
the quality of the March 2026 costing. **No re-costing has been performed at this vantage and none is
stated** — the same refusal `02.12 §3` recorded about the vendor-hosted qualification line, which the
charter carries **in the singular against an estate containing three vendor-hosted systems.**

**What is worth recording about the two over-lines is only this:** **a line goes over when work is done and
costs more than the estimate, and a line goes under when work is done and costs less — or when it is not
done at all.** **The report's direction column cannot tell those last two apart, and that is why this
chapter has a next section.**

---

## 5. The three under-lines, and two of them are not savings

**A budget line that closes under its committed figure can do so for two entirely different reasons, and
the currency column does not distinguish them.**

**One of the three under-lines is under because the work named in it was performed for less than the
estimate.** That is an underspend in the ordinary sense.

**Two of the three are under because the work named in them was not done.**

**The validation execution resource line.** **It funds execution resource against the Phase 04 framework's
requirement — the validation plans, the qualification activity and the evidence every system must
produce.** **No validation plan has been written.** `DP-21` asks for validation execution capacity sized
against sixty-one system validation plans and is open; `DP-05` asks for the resource contracted and
available and is open, **and has never been drawn on**; `AS-32` assumes the resource is available at the
scale sixty-one plans require and **has never been tested, because nothing has drawn on it.** **`IS-34`
records what the position comes to: a control position is not validation evidence, and the evidence Phase
04 requires is owed in full.** **Not one of the thirty-eight obligations is discharged.** **The line is
under because nothing was executed against it.**

**The account remediation line.** **It funds the withdrawal of shared and generic credentials and the issue
of named individual accounts.** **Nine systems carry a shared account** — `CG-11`'s seven and `CG-13`'s two
— **and at the latest established position not one of the nine is closed with a date.** **`RC-12`, the
commitment the response of 2026-03-06 made to withdraw the shared local operator account and issue a named
individual account to every operator, was owned by Amara Sissoko, carried the date 2026-08-31, and is Not
met.** **`DP-20` — account administration records sufficient to say which named individual held the
credential in use — has not moved since 2026-09.** **The line is under because the credentials were not
withdrawn.**

**And the two lines are the funding side of two of the four estate objectives.** **`OS-02` is Not met and
the validation execution line is under. `OS-05` is Not met and the account remediation line is under.**
**The same fact appears in two registers in two vocabularies**, and the currency column is the one that
looks like good news.

> **A programme that comes in under its envelope because it did not do the work has not managed its budget;
> it has reported its scope failure in the currency column.**

**`IS-59` carries it**, and it is raised as an open issue rather than recorded as a footnote to a Met.

---

## 6. What follows, and what does not

**`OS-07` is Met and stays Met.** **The measure asks whether committed and actual were reported monthly
against the ten lines and whether variance was stated as a figure. Both were.** **An objective is not
failed for a reason its measure does not name, any more than it is passed for one** — `ADR-0058`. **A
programme that marked `OS-07` down because of what the under-lines mean would be scoring from a measure it
invented at close-out**, which is the same defect as scoring from effort, and in the opposite direction.

**`IS-59` is not a criticism of the budget report.** **The report did its job**: it carried all ten lines,
monthly, for fourteen months, with a figure against each. **The report is the reason anybody can see this
at all.** **What `IS-59` records is that a budget report is not built to distinguish a line that is under
because work was cheap from a line that is under because work did not happen**, and that **at close-out two
of the three under-lines are the second kind.**

**And it is not a proposal.** **Nothing in this chapter re-costs anything, states the cost of work not
done, proposes a successor envelope, or asks for money.** `DP-51` records that a funded authority for work
outside the programme's own artefacts does not exist at this vantage — **and `DP-51` states no amount
either.**

**No figure in this chapter is a fine, a penalty, a sanction or a forfeiture.** **None of those exists
anywhere in this programme, and professional fees are not one.**

---

## 7. What this chapter does not establish

- **No currency amount, no re-costing, no forecast of spend and no successor budget.**
- **No cost of any work not done**, and no estimate of one.
- **No CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan and no remediation cost.**
- **No re-scoring of `OS-07` or of any other objective.**
- **No statement about anybody's estimating, forecasting or financial management**, and **no assessment of
  any individual's work.**
- **No compliance conclusion, no legal conclusion, no readiness claim and no forecast of what any
  inspection would find.**
- **No closure of any observation and no representation to FDA.**
- **No candidate explanation of `IS-01`.**
