## 0. The rules this log keeps

- **Every decision has a named individual decider** — `ADR-0008`. **No decision below was taken by
  a committee acting as a person**; where a body met, the decision is attributed to the individual who
  took it there.
- **The cast is the twelve individuals `01.07 §1` names and there is no thirteenth.**
- **Seven of the eighteen decisions are ADR-paired**, and each pairing is **exact on date and decider**:
  `DEC-903`, `DEC-905`, `DEC-908`, `DEC-911`, `DEC-913`, `DEC-915`, `DEC-917`. **An ADR whose date or
  decider differed from its `DEC` row would be a defect to be fixed rather than a difference to be
  explained** — `../adr/README.md`. **Eleven decisions have no ADR, which is the ordinary case.**
- **No decision below closes an inspectional observation, describes one as closed, or makes any
  representation to FDA** — `ADR-0051`, `ADR-0064`.
- **No decision below re-determines, re-scores, re-bands or re-derives anything published in Phases 02 to
  08**, sets a retention period, reports the result of a periodic review or of an independent assessment,
  or describes any access as performed.
- **No decision below names a candidate explanation of `IS-01`.**
