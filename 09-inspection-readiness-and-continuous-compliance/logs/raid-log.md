## 0. What this log is not, and the reconciliation

**This is not the objective score register, the decay register or the handover register.** Those exist from
this vantage, at `objective-score-register.md`, `decay-register.md` and `handover-register.md`, and this
log is kept apart from them deliberately. **An `OS` identifier never appears as an `IS` identifier, a `DK`
identifier never becomes one, an `HO` identifier never becomes one, and none of the three converts into any
other.** **`handover-register.md` has one row per open issue in this log and the two are reconciled at
`§1.9` below.**

**`PR-01` to `PR-70` carry no likelihood, no impact and no score, and none ever will.**

**No entry in this log closes an inspectional observation or describes one as closed.** The three positions
Phase 08 recorded stand exactly as it published them; **a position is not a closure, closure is a
representation to FDA, and this programme makes none** — `ADR-0051`, `ADR-0064`. **No entry reaches a
compliance conclusion or a legal conclusion. No entry states that Helix is ready for anything. No entry
forecasts what any inspection will find. No entry states a CAPA, a deviation, a remediation plan or a
remediation cost. No entry reports the result of a periodic review or of an independent assessment**,
because none of either has been performed — `IS-57`, `IS-63`. **No entry describes any access as
performed** — `ADR-0061`.

> by allusion. `IS-01` closed at Phase 08, the refusal recorded there is permanent, and it was expressly
> not handed to this phase.**

record type.** **The join `IS-03` asked for exists, and it exists on the other side of it.** **The systems
register will not carry a predicate-rule attribute**, and the decision to that effect is `DEC-910`'s rather
than an omission left standing.

record-type register was published, and nobody wrote it down for three hundred and sixty-four days.**

answer it**, and it closes at close-out because close-out is the first occasion on which somebody read the
register of open entries end to end.
