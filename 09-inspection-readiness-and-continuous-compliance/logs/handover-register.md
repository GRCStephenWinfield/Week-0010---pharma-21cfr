## 0. What this register is, and what it is not

**Forty-nine rows, one per open issue, in `IS` order.** The population is `raid-log.md`'s: **sixty-six
issues, seventeen closed and forty-nine open. 66 − 17 = 49.** **No entry appears here that is closed there,
and no entry is open there that is missing here** — the two files are reconciled at `§2` below and at
`raid-log.md §0`.

**The rule is `ADR-0060`'s: an open entry is handed to a named individual or it is recorded as having no
destination.** **A function is not a destination.** **The argument is `../09.07-the-handover.md`'s and is
not restated here** — the register's business is to say, for each of the forty-nine, where it went.

**Why the *accepted* column reads No on every row whose destination is a function.** **A function cannot
accept.** It has no signature, it cannot be asked a question and answer it, it cannot be held to a date,
and it does not turn up to a meeting. **Where the destination column carries a function, the acceptance
column carries No as a matter of arithmetic and not as a judgement about anybody in that function** —
`ADR-0060`, and the register prints it rather than leaving the cell blank, because a blank invites
completion.

**Acceptance means one thing and it is narrow.** It means the named individual has stated, on a date, that
the entry is theirs after 2027-04-30. **It is not agreement that the entry is soluble, a commitment to a
date, a statement that resources exist, or an undertaking to do anything by any time.** **No row below
carries a target date, and none ever will**, because the programme that could have set one ends at this
vantage.

**Nothing here closes an issue, and nothing here re-opens one.** **No row states a CAPA, a deviation, a
remediation plan or a cost.** **No row closes an inspectional observation or describes one as closed.**
**No row is a representation to FDA.** **No row names a candidate explanation of `IS-01`**, which closed
at Phase 08 and stays closed.
