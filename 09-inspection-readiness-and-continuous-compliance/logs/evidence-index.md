## 0. What an earlier phase said about this phase's subject matter, and what is superseded or narrowed

**Eight paragraphs follow. Six earlier statements published in Phases 01, 02, 07 and 08 are narrowed or
spent here, and two are recorded as correct and unsuperseded. All are written down rather than smoothed**,
on the discipline Phase 02's evidence index `§0` set for `EV-129` and every phase since has kept.

**First, and it is the one this index has to say about itself.** **This file is `DK-12` in
`decay-register.md`, and `DK-12` is the one artefact of the twelve whose holder after 2027-04-30 is
recorded as *None*.** **It is the artefact in which every `EV` identifier the other eleven cite resolves**,
and **no named individual and no function has accepted it.** That is recorded here because it is true and
because an index that omitted its own position would be the first document in this repository to make an
exception for itself. **It is not remarked on further**: `../09.06-what-goes-out-of-date-and-how-fast.md`
states what follows from it once, `IS-61` carries it, `DP-49` is the route, and **`DP-49` is the only
dependency in the log with no source at all.**

**Second, the `EV` series' own correction, carried to the last block.** Phase 01's evidence index `§3`
stated: *"The series continues into Phase 02 at `EV-129` and is never restarted."* **Phase 02's evidence
index `§0` recorded that that statement is superseded, and as published it is wrong.** The series carries
its phase block in the number, the change was taken at `DEC-201`, and **no identifier already issued
changes meaning: `EV-122` is the same artefact it was.** **`EV-129` to `EV-200` have never been issued, and
`EV-839` to `EV-900` are now permanently unused on the same principle** — a phase's block closes when the
phase closes, and **there is no further block, because there is no further phase.**

**Third.** `01.09 §7` records what the programme committed to instead of choosing a cause, and Phase 08's
evidence index `§0` narrowed its closing clause. **Both records stand and this index adds nothing to
either.** **`IS-01` closed on 2027-04-07, the refusal recorded in its closure is permanent, and it was
expressly not handed to this phase.** **No document in Phase 09 names, numbers, quotes, reproduces,
summarises, characterises, paraphrases or alludes to any candidate explanation**, and **no document in this
repository may be cited in support of one.**

**Fourth.** `01.06 §9` answers the question of who may vary an objective's measure: *"Nobody, during the
programme. A measure that changes mid-flight is a measure that was chosen to be met"*. **That is correct,
is not superseded, and it is what `AS-59` rests on.** **The eight objectives, their owners and their
measures were read at `EV-906` exactly as `01.06 §3` published them on 2026-05-29**, and the score at
`objective-score-register.md` is against those measures and no others — which is what `ADR-0058` requires.
**What could not be tested from the papers is whether anybody varied a measure outside the document**, and
`AS-59` states that rather than burying it.

**Fifth.** `01.06 §5` records the position on the money at the Phase 01 vantage: **committed against the
envelope, with** *"no variance to report at the vantage."* **That was true on 2026-05-29 and is not
superseded as a statement about that date.** **What is recorded here is that at this vantage the variance
is reported, line by line, across all ten lines and fourteen months of monthly reporting** — `EV-914`,
`EV-915` — **and that `IS-59` records what two of the three under-lines are under for.** **A variance
reported is not a saving**, and this index states neither as the other.

**Sixth.** `01.12 §9` fixed the closure rule for the four programme registers: **Closure names the
evidence. "Believed complete" is not a closure condition.** **It is correct, it is unsuperseded, and it is the rule three closures in this
phase were tested against.** `IS-03`, `IS-52` and `IS-58` each name what closed it, the evidence, and what
it never asked; **and `ADR-0063` records that nothing else was closed at close-out for the sake of closing
it.** **Forty-nine issues stand open at the last vantage.**

**Seventh.** Phase 08's `08.13 §4` states that `CAL-09` and `CAL-10` may be reported **once their reports
exist**, and that neither's result may be anticipated. **That is correct and is not superseded.** **No
result of `CAL-09` and no result of `CAL-10` is stated, summarised, characterised or anticipated anywhere
in Phase 09**, and no item in this index is a report of either. **`AS-12` and `DP-06` are carried open
unchanged.**

**Eighth, and it narrows something this phase itself decided.** **`DEC-911` records the read-access grant
as decided and dated on 2027-04-26**, and `EV-925` and `EV-931` are the records of that decision. **Neither
item evidences access on any system**, and **no item in this index does.** **`audit-condition-register.md`'s
published position — requested on none of the sixty-one and granted on none — is unmoved by anything in
this block**, and `AC-05` is not described as capable of being met. **An access grant is recorded as
decided and dated, never as performed** — `ADR-0061`, `IS-55`, `DP-46`, `PR-68`.
