# 09.03 — What the Score Is Shaped Like

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document ID | `HLX-DI-2027-903` |
| Version | 1.0 |
| Date | 2027-04-30 |
| Owner | Dr Marguerite Oyelaran — Vice President, Quality |
| Approver | Gideon Halvorsen — Head of Internal Audit |
| Classification | Confidential — Helix Pharma, Inc. Data Integrity Programme // Illustrative Portfolio Sample |

*The eight scores fall into two groups of four, and which group an objective falls into predicts its score better than anything else about it. All content is fictional and illustrative.*

---

## 1. The cut, and why it is not arbitrary

**`09.02-the-charter-scored.md` scored the eight and `logs/objective-score-register.md` carries them.**
This chapter does neither. It asks one question of the eight: **what does each measure require to exist
before it can be satisfied?**

**There are two answers and only two.**

**Four measures are satisfied by a document the programme itself produces.** An inventory with a
determination on every entry. A record register whose Owner column contains no team. A monthly budget
report against ten lines. A commitment register showing an owner, a date, an evidence reference and a
status. **Each of those four is an artefact the programme can write, and having written it, the measure is
met.** **`OBJ-01`, `OBJ-03`, `OBJ-07`, `OBJ-08`.**

**Four measures are satisfied only if something outside the programme's own artefacts changes.** A
validation package that does not exist has to be produced by a validation function with execution resource.
An audit trail that cannot be enabled has to be enabled by a vendor, or by a change of product. A shared
account has to be withdrawn by the people who administer accounts and replaced by named individual
credentials for every operator on nine systems. A record review procedure has to be revised and approved
through the quality unit. **None of those four is a document the programme can write. Each is a change to
the estate, and the programme has no authority to make any of them.** **`OBJ-02`, `OBJ-04`, `OBJ-05`,
`OBJ-06`.**

**Four and four. 4 + 4 = 8.** The classification is recorded in the register's own column, row by row, and
is not a reading imposed afterwards: **`ADR-0058` required the artefact each measure names to be identified
before any objective was scored**, and **the objective score record form was authorised on 2027-04-14, six
days before the first score was taken.** **The cut fell out of that identification.** It was not drawn to
produce a pattern.

---

## 1.1 And the cut falls where the regulation does

**Read the four estate objectives against the provisions their measures reach and the cut stops looking
like an accident.** `OBJ-02`'s measure is traceable to the validation obligation this repository has
carried since Phase 04 — **`21 CFR 11.10(a)`, whose fourth clause is the ability to discern invalid or
altered records.** `OBJ-04`'s is **`21 CFR 11.10(e)`**, and all three of its sentences are engaged. The
first requires *"[u]se of secure, computer-generated, time-stamped audit trails to independently record
the date and time of operator entries and actions that create, modify, or delete electronic records"*; the
second, that *"[r]ecord changes shall not obscure previously recorded information"*; and the third, that
*"[s]uch audit trail documentation shall be retained for a period at least as long as that required for
the subject electronic records and shall be available for agency review and copying."* **The
configuration ground reaches the first, the unrecorded-scope-change and circular-store grounds reach the
second, and the sixteen systems with no second term reach the third.**

`OBJ-05`'s is attribution — **`21 CFR 211.188(b)(11)`**, which requires *"[i]dentification of the persons
performing and directly supervising or checking each significant step in the operation, or if a
significant step in the operation is performed by automated equipment under § 211.68, the identification
of the person checking the significant step performed by the automated equipment."* **Both limbs are
engaged: a packaging line system performing a significant step is automated equipment under `§ 211.68`,
so the provision reaches the identification of the person checking it.** `OBJ-05`'s measure also reaches
`21 CFR 211.194(a)(7)`, which `09.02 §6` owns. **`OBJ-06`'s reaches two** — **`21 CFR 211.192`**, the
quality unit's review and approval of production and control records before a batch is released, and
**`21 CFR 211.194(a)(8)`**, the second-person review — **and neither of the two procedures its measure
names has been revised.**

**The four objectives whose measures reach a regulation are the four estate objectives, and not one of
them is met. The four whose measures reach a document the programme itself was writing are the four
artefact objectives, and three of those four are met.** **The score is three met and five not met**, and
the fifth failure — `OS-08` — is an artefact objective that fails for a reason of its own, which `§5`
sets out and this section does not fold into the pattern.

## 2. The pattern

| | Met | Not met | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Artefact objectives** | **3** | **1** | **4** |
| **Estate objectives** | **0** | **4** | **4** |
| **Total** | **3** | **5** | **8** |

**Of the four objectives satisfied by the programme's own artefacts, three are met. Of the four requiring
the estate to change, none is met.**

> **The programme met every objective that was in its own gift and missed every objective that required
> anything outside it to change.**

That sentence is written once, here, and it is not repeated anywhere in this repository. **`IS-60` carries
it.**

---

## 3. What that sentence does not mean

**It is not an accusation of bad faith, and nothing in this chapter names, characterises or assesses any
individual.** `01.09 §2` holds without variation: *"This document names no individual auditor and makes no
statement about any individual's work"*, and the same rule has governed every document in nine phases.
**The eight objectives have seven named owners between them, five of whom own an objective that is not met,
and this chapter is not a report on those five.** `PR-65` carries the risk that it is read as one.

**It is not a claim that the programme should have done the estate work.** **It could not.** The charter's
scope at `01.06 §2` puts the determination, the framework, the registers and the evidence inside the
programme; **it does not put a validation execution resource, an account administration function, a vendor
product change or the quality unit's approval capacity inside it.** **The Data Integrity Lead does not
administer accounts, configure systems or approve changes** — `01.07 §6` — **and the determiner does not
operate.** Every one of the four estate objectives requires exactly one of those things.

**It is not a claim that the artefacts are worth less than the changes.** **A determination that has not
been made cannot be acted on by anybody**, and in February 2026 Helix could not say which of its records
were Part 11 records. **A programme that determines, registers and evidences is doing what it was funded to
do**, and the four hundred and twenty-five days produced a record-level determination across fifty-eight
record types, one hundred and twenty-one attribute assessments, six hundred and seventy-one control
positions, thirty-four auditable event classes, a retention derivation across the in-scope record types,
fourteen commitments read against their own words out of the twenty-two the response made, and sixty-one
periodic review rows. **None of that
existed before, and none of it is undone by the shape of the score.**

**It is not a statement that the estate objectives are unachievable.** **The estate changes when somebody
with the authority to change it is funded to change it.** That is the whole of the mechanism. **What this
chapter records is that at close-out nobody has been.** `DP-51` states the dependency: funding, and an
authority to commit it, for work outside the programme's own artefacts — **from the sponsor and the Board,
and no successor programme is chartered and no envelope exists after 2027-04-30.**

**And it is not a compliance conclusion, a legal conclusion, a readiness claim or a forecast.** **Five
objectives not met is Helix's own assessment of its own position against its own charter.** **An open gap
is not a violation, not a breach, not a non-compliance, not a finding and not an inspectional
observation.** **Nothing in this chapter states that Helix is ready or compliant, and nothing in it
forecasts what any inspection would find.**

---

## 4. What it does mean, stated plainly

**A programme's authority ends where its artefacts end.** That is not a defect of this programme. It is
what a programme is: **a funded, time-boxed instrument with a scope, a sponsor and an end date**, and its
scope was written down in March 2026 and never varied.

**What the shape of the score records is that the two halves of the charter were funded differently.**
**The four artefact objectives were funded in full**, because the resource they needed was the internal
labour line and the advisory line and both were released. **The four estate objectives were funded in part
or not at all**: the validation execution line was committed and never drawn on, because no plan was
written to draw on it; the system remediation and configuration line came in over; **and no line in the
envelope buys quality unit approval capacity, which is what `OBJ-06` needs, because approval capacity is a
person's time and the person already has a day job.** **The charter said that about itself on
2026-05-29**, at `01.06 §5`: *"Internal labour is the largest line and it is not the largest risk. It funds
the 9.2 FTE-equivalents, and those are people who already work at Helix and already have day jobs. The line
buys their time; it does not create capacity."* **`PR-02` — the 9.2 FTE-equivalents funded but not
released — carries it and is open at the last vantage.**

**And the charter said this about itself, at the start, in the section that records its own defect.**
`01.06 §9`: *"The charter creates obligations that outlive it and does not say who holds them
afterwards."* **`IS-07` was raised on 2026-05-27 against that sentence and is still open at the last
vantage.** **The shape of the score is the same defect at a larger scale**: a programme scoped to produce
artefacts, holding eight objectives of which four required somebody else to act, **and no instrument in the
charter that says who that somebody is or how they are funded.**

---

## 5. `OS-08`, which does not fit and is not made to

**Four artefact objectives, three met. The fourth is `OS-08` and it fails.**

**It would be easy to fold `OS-08` into the pattern by calling it half an estate objective** — the
commitments are Helix's obligations to FDA, their owners are line managers, their discharge requires acts
outside the programme. **That reading is available and it is wrong**, and the chapter refuses it in terms.

**`OBJ-08`'s measure asks for four things per commitment — an owner, a due date, an evidence reference and
a status — and for the status to be reported quarterly at `CAL-04`.** **Every one of those five things is
an act on a document.** **No system has to change. No account has to be withdrawn. No vendor has to release
anything. No procedure has to be approved.** **`OBJ-08` is an artefact objective on the same test that puts
`OBJ-01`, `OBJ-03` and `OBJ-07` in the same column, and it fails.**

**It fails for two reasons and neither is that the artefact is missing.**

**Three of the fourteen commitments assessed carry no completion criterion**, and one carries no date.
**The register cannot show a status for a commitment that has no test** — `IS-51` — **and the element that
is absent is not the owner and it is not the date, it is the test.** That is a defect in the drafting of
the response of 2026-03-06, and **this programme has not repaired it and may not**: writing a completion
criterion now would be writing a commitment Helix did not file — `ADR-0052`, `PR-59`.

**And the quarterly-reporting limb failed outright, for the plainest reason in the whole score.** **The
commitment register was not opened between the day it was filed on 2026-03-06 and 2027-03-30.** Thirteen
months. **Five occurrences of `CAL-04` fell inside that period and not one of them reported a status read
from it.** **The document existed the whole time, in a place everybody knew, with a named owner. Nobody
read it.**

**That is the honest complication in the score and it is stated rather than smoothed.** **Three of the four
artefact objectives were met because the programme produced an artefact and then used it. The fourth failed
because an artefact was produced and then nobody went back to it** — **and an artefact nobody reads is not
a weaker version of an artefact somebody reads; it is a different thing, and it produces the same result as
having no artefact at all.**

**`OS-08` is the one score in the eight that is not about authority.** **Nobody needed a budget, a vendor
or an approval to open a document.**

## 6. Where the pattern recurs, and it is not only in the score

**The same shape appears in three other registers this phase built, and the recurrence is recorded because
a pattern that shows up four times is not an artefact of one table.**

| Register | The programme's own | Outside it | The result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The charter score** — `logs/objective-score-register.md` | Four artefact objectives | Four estate objectives | **3 of 4 met · 0 of 4 met** |
| **The decay register** — `logs/decay-register.md` | Twelve artefacts the programme built | The holders they need after 2027-04-30 | **Nine have a named holder, two have a function, one has nobody** |
| **The handover register** — `logs/handover-register.md` | Forty-nine entries the programme raised | The destinations they need | **Thirty-three accepted, twelve to a function, four nowhere** |
| **The dependencies** — `logs/raid-log.md §3` | What the programme could do | What it needed from elsewhere | **Fifty open, two closed in nine phases, one discharged in this one** |

**In every one of the four, what the programme could produce it produced, and what it needed from somewhere
it did not control it mostly did not get.** **`09.06-what-goes-out-of-date-and-how-fast.md` and
`09.07-the-handover.md` own the second and third rows and this chapter does not restate their figures'
arguments.**

**One observation about the fourth row, and it is the most uncomfortable of the four.** **`DP-46` asks for
read access for the audit function — requested, then granted, and recorded system by system in the working
papers.** It is **the only dependency in the log whose subject matter could be discharged in an
afternoon**, it draws on nobody's capacity, it needs no budget and no vendor — **and it has been discharged
on none of sixty-one systems: requested on none and granted on none, with the grant at `DEC-911` recorded
as decided and dated and never as performed** — `ADR-0061`. **So the pattern is not
simply that estate change is expensive.** Some of it is not expensive at all. **What the four rows have in
common is not cost; it is that each needed somebody outside the programme to do something, and the
programme had no instrument that made anybody do anything.**

---

## 7. What this chapter does not establish

- **No compliance conclusion, no legal conclusion, no readiness claim and no forecast.** **Neither *Helix
  is ready* nor *Helix is compliant* appears in this phase.**
- **No statement about any individual's conduct, performance or work.** **The pattern is about where a
  programme's authority ends and not about who held it.**
- **No recommendation, no plan, no proposal and no business case.** **`DP-51` records that a funded
  authority does not exist. It does not ask for one, cost one or design one.**
- **No CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan and no cost.**
- **No re-scoring.** The eight scores are `09.02`'s and `logs/objective-score-register.md`'s and none moves
  here. **The four-and-four cut is a classification of measures, not a second score**, and it produces no
  aggregate, no index and no percentage.
- **No closure of any observation and no representation to FDA** — `ADR-0064`.
- **No candidate explanation of `IS-01`.**
