# 09.10 — What Happens When the Programme Stops

*The post the programme created continues. The programme does not. All content is fictional and illustrative.*

---

## 1. One idea

**The Data Integrity Lead post outlives the programme that created it, and almost nothing else about the
programme does.**

**`IS-65` records the consequence**: every artefact whose holder is the programme rather than a line function
is an artefact with twenty-one days of ownership left. **The interval from `MS-12` on 2027-04-09 to `MS-13`
on 2027-04-30 is twenty-one days, and at this vantage it is spent.**

**This chapter states which things stop and what that leaves.** It states no count from any register. Which
artefacts have which holders is `09.06-what-goes-out-of-date-and-how-fast.md`'s and
`logs/decay-register.md`'s, and it is not restated here.

## 2. The post the programme created, and why it was put where it was

**The Data Integrity Lead post did not exist at Helix before 2026-03-06.** It was created by the charter, at
`DEC-102`, in the same decision that approved the scope, the objectives, the envelope and the close-out date,
and it was created with a reporting line rather than only with a name: **it reports to Quality and not to
IT.**

**`ADR-0002` is why**, and its reasoning is the reasoning `IS-58` is a later instance of: **"[a] data integrity
function inside the organisation that operates the systems is assessing its own work."** The same argument
put the Computerised System Validation Lead under the Head of Quality Assurance rather than under IT —
*"…the function that judges whether a system's validated state holds should not sit in the line that builds
and administers it"*.

**The post is a line post and not a programme role, and that distinction is the whole of this chapter.** A
programme role is funded by the programme, is described by the programme's charter, and ends when the
charter's close-out date arrives. **A line post is part of the organisation's establishment and does not.**
The Data Integrity Lead holds the record determination method, the data integrity procedure set and the
training content, **and holds them tomorrow on the same footing as today.**

**One thing about the post is recorded because it has been recorded at every vantage and is unchanged at this
one.** **`PR-10` — key-person concentration on the Data Integrity Lead and the CSV Lead, each a single holder
on the critical path, one of the two posts created by this programme.** **No response was agreed at any of
the nine vantages and the cell has said so every time.** The post continuing is not the same as the post
being resourced, and the programme does not claim it is.

## 3. What "the programme stops" means, concretely

**Four things end at this vantage, and each of them is a thing somebody was doing.**

**The Data Integrity Steering Committee dissolves.** It first sat on 2026-03-13, it stands in the calendar as
`CAL-01`, and it has sat every month of the programme's life. **`GOV-45` is its last minute and it dissolves
at the close of that sitting** — because a steering committee for a programme that has closed is a body with
no business, and **a committee that continues in order to have somewhere to put unresolved items is a place
where items go to be discussed rather than to be taken.** It owned nothing and therefore transfers nothing:
**a committee may approve; it may not own.**

**The programme manager's appointment to this programme ends.** It was made at `DEC-101` on 2026-03-02, in
the decision that established the programme, and it is an appointment to a programme with a close-out date.
**The registers, the calendar and the budget report are the programme manager's**, and the role in which they
are held ends today.

**The calendar and the budget lines close.** The envelope was approved for a programme with a defined life;
**there is no envelope after this vantage and no line in one.** The monthly reporting that `OBJ-07`'s measure
names was reporting against those lines at `CAL-01`, and both the reporting and the forum for it end
together. `CAL-01` ends with the programme. **What happens to the calendar entries that outlive it is
`IS-07`'s question and it is not answered here** — it has stood unresolved since 2026-05-27, through nine
phases and nine vantages, **and this chapter is not going to answer it on the quality unit's behalf either.**

**And the registers stop being maintained by anybody whose job it is.** They were reviewed monthly at
`CAL-01` and reported quarterly at `CAL-04`. **After today neither happens**, and no entry's status will
change because nothing will cause one to change. **That is not the same as the registers being wrong**; it is
the ordinary condition of a document that describes a moving object and has stopped being updated, **and
`ADR-0062` exists so that each of them carries what would falsify it and who would notice.**

**Two things do not end, and saying so is not a comfort.** **The obligations do not end.** `21 CFR 211.22`
puts the quality unit's responsibility and authority where it has always been, and the review obligations the
programme worked to are the quality unit's own — **they existed before this programme and will outlast it.**
**And the conditions the registers describe do not end.** Nothing recorded as absent becomes present because
a programme has closed.

## 4. Twenty-one days of ownership, and what happens at the end of them

**An artefact whose holder is the programme is an artefact held by something with an end date.**

**The end date has been on the record since 2026-03-06.** The charter fixed close-out at 2027-04-30, and
`MS-13` has carried it at every vantage. **What `IS-65` records is not that the date arrived unexpectedly**;
it is that the artefacts whose holder is the programme reached `MS-12` on 2027-04-09 with twenty-one days of
programme ownership remaining, **and that at this vantage those twenty-one days are spent.**

**What follows from an artefact having no holder is not that it is destroyed or lost.** It stays where it is,
in the state it is in, under whatever document control it was published under. **What it loses is the person
whose job it is to notice when it stops being true.** `ADR-0062` names, for each artefact, the event that
would falsify it and who holds it afterwards — **and `09.06-what-goes-out-of-date-and-how-fast.md` publishes
the answers, including the ones that are a function with no named individual and the one that is nobody.**
**None of those answers is restated here and none of them is improved by this chapter.**

**The rule that produced them is the rule that has held for nine phases.** **"Every control, every procedure,
every record and every obligation in this programme has a named individual owner. No control is owned by a
team, a function, a department or a committee."** **A vacancy is recorded as a vacancy, not covered by a
function** — *"It is never converted back to a department name."* **At close-out the rule costs something for
the first time**, because the alternative to a name is no longer another name; it is a blank that somebody
would like to fill.

**And the programme is not available as a holder for anything.** It cannot accept, it cannot be asked a
question, and after today it cannot be held to a date — **which are the three reasons a function is not a
destination**, applying with more force to a body that has dissolved than to one that still exists.
`09.07-the-handover.md` owns that argument.

## 5. What this chapter is careful not to be

**It is not a transition plan.** No artefact is assigned, no holder is proposed, no sequence is set and no
date is given. **`IS-65` states no successor arrangement and proposes none**, and neither does this chapter.

**It is not a request.** The programme has no standing at this vantage to ask anybody to take anything, and a
document that read as a request would be a programme allocating somebody else's work by writing it down —
**which is exactly what `IS-07` has declined to do for nine phases.**

**It is not a criticism of the functions that continue.** The quality unit's responsibilities are statutory
and are unaffected by anything here. **What is recorded is that certain artefacts have no named individual
holder**, which is a fact about a register column and not a judgement about anybody.

**And it is not an argument that the programme should have been longer.** No document in this repository
states what a further programme would achieve, what it would cost, or that one should exist. **There is no
successor programme, no successor envelope and no chartered continuation**, and recording the absence is not
a proposal to remedy it.

## 6. What this chapter does not establish

- **No count from any register.** Which artefacts have which holders is
  `09.06-what-goes-out-of-date-and-how-fast.md`'s and `logs/decay-register.md`'s; where the open entries go
  is `09.07-the-handover.md`'s and `logs/handover-register.md`'s.
- **No successor arrangement, no transition plan, no assignment, no proposal and no recommendation.**
- **No holder for any artefact recorded as having none**, and no function written into a cell to complete a
  column — `ADR-0062`.
- **No statement that the quality unit holds the calendar entries that outlive the programme.** **`IS-07`
  records that the obvious answer is probably right and is still not written down**, and this chapter does
  not write it down on anybody's behalf.
- **No closure of any register entry.** **The programme closes with its open entries open** — `ADR-0063`.
- **No CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan, no remediation cost, no sequence, no priority and no date.**
- **No cost, no forecast of cost and no statement about funding.** **No figure anywhere in this repository is
  a fine, a penalty, a sanction or a forfeiture, and none exists.**
- **No compliance conclusion, no legal conclusion and no readiness position** — `ADR-0059`.
- **No closure of any inspectional observation and no representation to FDA** — `ADR-0051`, `ADR-0064`.
- **No re-determination, re-scoring, re-banding or re-derivation of anything**, and no retention period for
  any record type.
- **No forecast** of what any inspection, audit, review or assessment would find.
- **No statement about any individual's work**, and no characterisation of anybody. **`PR-10` is a statement
  about a structure and about a post, and about nobody.**
- **No candidate explanation of `IS-01`**, which closed at Phase 08 and stays closed.

would give the unheld artefacts a holder has not been taken by anybody.**
