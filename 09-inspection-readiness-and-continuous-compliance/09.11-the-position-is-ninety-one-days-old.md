# 09.11 — The Position Is Ninety-One Days Old

| Document ID | `HLX-DI-2027-911` |

| Owner | Joachim Reuter — Head of Automation and Manufacturing IT |

*Six hundred and seventy-one control positions were taken on 2027-01-29. Nothing has re-taken them, and this phase does not either. All content is fictional and illustrative.*

---

## 1. The date, the count and the interval

**The estate-wide control position was taken at the Phase 05 vantage of 2027-01-29** — `MS-09`. **It is six
hundred and seventy-one positions: the eleven paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10` against each of the sixty-one
systems Phase 02 determined hold at least one Part 11 record.** **The register is
`../05-access-audit-trails-and-electronic-signatures/logs/control-position-register.md` and it is
authoritative; no figure in it is restated here.**

**2027-01-29 to 2027-04-30 is ninety-one days.**

**The convention is the repository's and is applied here to the right object.** **An interval between two
events is the difference between the dates.** **A duration of an activity is counted inclusive of both
endpoints.** **This is an interval** — the gap between the vantage at which the position was taken and the
vantage at which it is handed over — **so it is the difference, and it is ninety-one and not ninety-two.**
The same rule gives the seventeen days from the Form FDA 483 of 2026-02-13 to kickoff on 2026-03-02, the
twenty-one calendar days to the written response of 2026-03-06, and **the three hundred and fifty days from
the 483 to the Phase 05 vantage** that `IS-56` carries.

**Nothing has re-taken any of the six hundred and seventy-one.**

---

## 2. What each later phase did instead, and why none of it is a re-taking

**Phase 06** assessed two of the sixty-one systems at a depth unavailable for the other fifty-nine. **It
characterised auditable events on two chromatography instances. It did not re-take a control position on
either**, and `IS-41` records the asymmetry as open by design.

**Phase 07** derived retention positions from the record-type register and the predicate rules. **It read
backup and restore facts from enquiries answered by the people who operate the arrangements** — `AS-49`.
**It re-derived no Phase 05 cell**, and where its findings bear on `21 CFR 11.10(c)` and `(e)` it published
them as separate entries — `IS-43`, `IS-44` — **rather than moving a determination.**

**Phase 08 read documents.** A commitment register, four audit records, three published registers and a set
of provisions. **It opened no system, executed no test and examined no record.**

**Phase 09 read artefacts.** **It scored eight objectives from documents, read fourteen budget reports, and
drew three registers.** **It opened no system, executed no test and examined no record.**

**So the position handed over is the position of 2027-01-29, unchanged in every cell, and ninety-one days
old.**

---

## 3. Why the age matters more than an age usually would

**A control position register is a photograph.** **It records a state and not a transition** — and that is
not a defect in its design, because a point-in-time instrument is what Phase 05 was asked to build and what
it built. **`IS-53` and the correction recorded at `08.07 §4` establish the derived form of the point, and
it is not restated here.**

**What follows from it is this.** **A cell that has moved since 2027-01-29 looks exactly like a cell that
has not.** **In both directions.** A control that was Met and has since been lost reads Met. A control that
was Not met and has since been put right reads Not met. **`DP-47` asks for the route by which a change to a
published position is recorded as a change — the previous position, the act, the date and the authority —
and it is open.**

**The estate is not still, and the register has no way of saying so.** **Nine entries were removed from the
inventory during the programme. The walk of June 2026 found seventeen systems nobody had entered.**
**`ADR-0015`'s six review triggers and their annual floor are the mechanism that would have caught a change
to the determination, and `SOP-DI-05` has fired on nothing across nine phases** — `AS-19`, `IS-10`. **A
trigger set with no floor is a control that reports success by being silent**, and this one has a floor and
has still never fired.

**And one position in the register is load-bearing on a disposition Phase 08 recorded, which is where the
age bites hardest.** **At the Phase 05 vantage `SYS-001` is *Met and required* on all eleven paragraphs of
`21 CFR 11.10` — the only one of the sixty-one that is.** **That fact is ninety-one days old.** **Nothing
in this phase re-reads it, and nothing in this phase states that it still holds.**

---

## 4. The assumption, stated rather than buried

**`AS-63`** — that the Phase 05 vantage remains the latest established estate-wide control position, and
that nothing on any of the sixty-one systems changed between 2027-01-29 and 2027-04-30 that would move any
of the six hundred and seventy-one.

**It has not been tested and could not be tested from the papers.** **Nothing in Phase 09 opened a system,
read an account list, looked at a line or put an enquiry to anybody who operates anything.**

**It is `AS-55`'s Phase 09 form, stretched.** `AS-55` made the same assumption over seventy days about the
nine systems at `CG-11` and `CG-13`; **`AS-63` makes it over ninety-one days about all sixty-one.**
**`AS-34` is where the chain starts** — the configuration and behaviour observed between 2027-01-04 and
2027-01-20 taken as the configuration at the Phase 05 vantage — **so the oldest observation behind the
position is itself older than the vantage that carries it.**

**If the assumption fails, the ninety-one days is a floor rather than a figure**, and what is handed over is
not merely old but wrong in a direction nobody can name.

---

## 5. Phase 09 re-determines nothing, and says so

**No cell of the control position register moves in this phase.** **No paragraph is re-assessed on any
system. No gap is re-opened or re-closed. No band is moved. No pair is re-scored. No period is re-derived.
No auditable event class is re-characterised. No commitment is re-read.**

**That is not an omission and it is not modesty.** **A re-determination made at close-out, from documents,
by the programme that made the original determination, would be worth less than the determination it
replaced** — and it would take a register whose vantage is stated and clear and give it a second vantage
nobody could separate from the first. **`ADR-0049` fixed the rule in Phase 06 and every phase since has
kept it.**

**What Phase 09 does instead is state the age.**

> **The estate-wide control position Helix hands over at close-out is ninety-one days old, nothing has
> re-taken it, and this phase does not claim it is current.**

**`IS-64` carries it.** **`DK-04` records it as the fastest-decaying of the six that move with the estate,
and records that it is already ninety-one days behind.** **`HO-47` hands it to Joachim Reuter, who
has accepted it.**

**And the arrangement that would refresh it exists and has not been run.** **`RV-01` to `RV-61` carry a
frequency and a first review due date for every one of the sixty-one systems.** **The earliest of those
dates is 2027-05-31 — thirty-one days after this vantage — and every one of the sixty-one falls on or after
it, and therefore every one of them falls after this vantage.**
**Reviews performed: none** — `IS-57`. **A periodic review is not a re-taking of a control position in any
case**, and no row of `periodic-review-register.md` sets, proposes or bounds one.

---

## 6. What this chapter does not establish

- **No re-determination, re-scoring, re-banding or re-derivation of anything.** Every figure cited is
  Phase 05's as published.
- **No statement that any control position has changed since 2027-01-29**, in either direction. **The
  chapter states that a change would be invisible, not that one occurred.**
- **No statement that the position is wrong.** **It was true at the vantage it was taken.**
- **No result of any periodic review**, because none has been performed.
- **No CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan and no cost.**
- **No compliance conclusion, no legal conclusion, no readiness claim and no forecast of what any
  inspection would find.**
- **No closure of any observation and no representation to FDA.**
- **No candidate explanation of `IS-01`.**
