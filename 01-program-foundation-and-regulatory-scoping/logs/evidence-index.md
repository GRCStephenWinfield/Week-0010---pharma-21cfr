## 0. What is in this index and what is not

**Every item below exists at the vantage.** The index records what a foundation phase produces: governance
records, engagement records, the response as filed, the objectives and registers, and the internal audit
reports the phase publishes.

**It contains no system evidence, because none exists.** There is no system inventory, no record-level Part
11 determination, no risk assessment, no ALCOA assessment, no validation plan or package, no configuration
record, no audit trail export, no access review, no periodic review, no internal audit of the remediated
programme, no third-party audit report and no inspection record after 2026-02-13. Those are produced in
Phases 02 to 09 and indexed there.

**The index is extended by later phases, never replaced.** `EV-122` means the same artefact in Phase 08 as
it does here.

**Retention.** Each item is retained for at least the life of the obligation or commitment it supports.
For the internal audit reports at `EV-122` to `EV-126` and the response at `EV-104`, that is beyond
programme close-out.

## 2. What the index shows about the phase

| Class | Items | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| Governance and decision records | `EV-101` to `EV-103`, `EV-107` to `EV-117`, `EV-127`, `EV-128` | Sixteen items. A foundation phase is mostly minutes, and this one says so |
| Inspection-related | `EV-104` to `EV-106` | The 483 as received, the response as filed, and the commitment register the response created |
| External engagements | `EV-118` to `EV-120` | Three, and the exclusions in each are the evidence `ADR-0004` is real |
| Regulatory scoping | `EV-121` | One record. The phase's substantive output |
| **Internal audit history** | **`EV-122` to `EV-126`** | **Five items, and they are the only Helix-generated artefacts in this index that predate the inspection.** `EV-105`, the Form FDA 483 of 2026-02-13, also predates the programme, and it is not Helix's |

**The minutes in this index record eight Data Integrity Steering Committee sittings between 2026-03-13 and
2026-05-29** — 2026-03-13 (`EV-109`), 2026-04-10 (`EV-111`), 2026-04-24 (`EV-121`), 2026-05-01 (`EV-112`),
2026-05-08 (`EV-113`), 2026-05-15 (`EV-115`), 2026-05-27 (`EV-116`) and 2026-05-29 (`EV-117`), five of them
in May. **The cadence at `CAL-01` is monthly with additional sittings called by the chair**, and eleven
weeks carrying eight sittings is what a programme looks like before it is in a rhythm. The index is where
that is checkable.

**`EV-122` to `EV-126` are the reason `ADR-0007` exists.** An organisation that publishes the observations
and withholds the audits that preceded them has published half a record. Indexing the four reports and
their closure register makes the history retrievable rather than described.

**No item in this index is an inspection outcome after 2026-02-13**, and none is a certification, an
approval, an attestation of compliance or a determination by any agency. **There is no Part 11
certification** and no artefact in this index could be one.

## 3. Rules the index keeps

| Rule | Statement |
|---|---|
| **It records what exists** | An artefact that has not been produced has no `EV` number. The index is not a plan |
| **One artefact, one identifier** | An artefact is indexed once. Later phases cite the same number rather than re-issuing it |
| **Named owners** | Every item has a named individual owner — `ADR-0008` |
| **No result in a template** | The four templates in `templates/` are empty forms and carry no `EV` number, because a blank form is not evidence |
| **No forecast** | No item supports a statement about what a future inspection will find |
| **Continuity** | The series continues into Phase 02 at `EV-129` and is never restarted |
