## 6. What Phase 02 must do, and what it must not

Phase 02's slug is `02-the-system-inventory-and-part-11-applicability`.

| Phase 02 **must** | Phase 02 **must not** |
|---|---|
| Produce the controlled inventory of computerised systems, with a named owner per entry | Implement, configure or change any control. That is Phase 05 |
| Determine, **record by record**, which records are Part 11 records — `ADR-0001` — and record the determiner and the date | Say "a Part 11 system" without naming the records that make it one |
| Answer the first half of the open question: **which records actually are Part 11 records, and did anybody ever write that down?** — `IS-02` | Score anything. The data integrity risk assessment and the ALCOA attributes are **Phase 03** |
| Apply the paper-printout test as it actually reads, with its three conjunctive conditions, where a printout is relied on — `01.03-what-part-11-actually-is.md` | Present the printout route as a general escape hatch, or apply it to a dynamic record without meeting the first condition |
| Classify each system as closed or open on the test at `21 CFR 11.3(b)(4)` — **who controls access** | Classify the vendor-hosted system as open because it is hosted. Hosting is not the test |
| Record the estate's missing predicate-rule attribute as closed or still open — `IS-03` | Answer `IS-01`. The second half of the question is **Phase 08's** |
| Extend `EV`, `DEC`, `IS`, `AS`, `DP` and `PR` rather than restarting them | Publish anything as a Phase 01 outcome. Phase 01 determined no record's status and says so |

**The sequence is not the programme's invention and is not negotiable for convenience.** Determine which
records are in scope, then assess the risk to them, then set the validation rigour against that risk, then
implement. A programme that implements first has chosen its controls before it asked which records needed
them, and `ADR-0001` exists to stop exactly that.

## 7. The question Phase 01 leaves open

**Which records actually are Part 11 records — and did anybody ever write that down?**

Phase 01 established that **four internal audits between 2023-06 and 2025-09 covered all three systems
between them — each system falling inside the scope of at least two — and none raised a finding on the
subject matter of `OBS-2`, `OBS-4` or `OBS-6`.** `01.09-the-four-audits-that-found-nothing.md`
publishes the history in full, sets out four candidate explanations, and **chooses none of them.**

It chooses none because the choice cannot yet be made on evidence. Whether an audit trail on any Helix
system records what `21 CFR 11.10(e)` describes is not established. Whether any given record was a Part 11
record was never determined, by anybody, at any point in the period the four audits cover. An explanation
selected now would be selected for comfort, and it would set the shape of the remediation on a guess.

So the question is split. **Phase 02 asks the first half** — the record determination, `IS-02`. **Phase 08
returns to the second half**, when the periodic review, the internal audit of the remediated programme and
the 483 response commitments come together and the evidence to answer it exists. `IS-01` stays open across
six phases, and it stays open with an owner, a date and a reason.

Phase 01 could not have answered it. What Phase 01 did was refuse to write around it.

## 9. What is still not true

Stated as absences, because an absence written down is an absence somebody can act on.

- **There is no system inventory.** Phase 02.
- **No record at Helix has been determined to be a Part 11 record.** Not one. Phase 02.
- **There is no data integrity risk assessment and no ALCOA assessment.** Phase 03.
- **There is no validation framework and nothing has been validated.** Phase 04.
- **No control has been implemented, no audit trail has been configured and no electronic signature has
  been deployed.** Phase 05.
- **No periodic review has been performed.** Phase 08.
- **No internal audit of the remediated programme has been performed**, and no third-party audit has been
  performed. Phases 08 and 09.
- **There has been no further inspection and there is no inspection outcome of any kind.** The programme
  does not forecast one, at this vantage or any other.
- **There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate and no FDA approval or validation of any
  software Helix runs.** None is available to be obtained.
- **There is no FIPS 199 categorization, no baseline and no authorization.** Helix chose NIST SP 800-53
  Revision 5 as a catalogue; Helix is not a federal system.
- **The reason four internal audits raised nothing on three observations is not established**, and Phase 01
  declines to state one.
