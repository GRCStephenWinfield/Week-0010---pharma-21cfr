## 3. What was tabled

The Head of Internal Audit tabled the internal audit history of the three systems named in the inspectional
observations that concern electronic records.

**Four internal audits between 2023-06 and 2025-09 covered all three systems between them — each system
falling inside the scope of at least two — and none raised a finding on the subject matter of `OBS-2`,
`OBS-4` or `OBS-6`.**

Tabled with it: **all fourteen findings the four audits did raise**, the scope statement of each audit, the
basis of each audit programme, and the closure record showing that all fourteen were closed before
2026-02-02. `../01.09-the-four-audits-that-found-nothing.md` publishes the whole of it.

**The committee did not receive a proposed cause and did not ask for one to be supplied.**

## 4. `DEC-117` and `ADR-0007` — publish it in full

The committee decided that **the internal audit history is published in full, including the four audits
that raised nothing.**

| Considered | Position |
|---|---|
| Publish the observations and not the audit history | **Refused.** It reads as though nobody had looked, which is not what happened |
| Publish the history once its cause is established | **Refused.** A history withheld until its cause is known is a history withheld, and the cause may take six phases to establish |
| Publish the history in full now, with the findings that were raised, and state that no cause has been established | **Adopted** — `ADR-0007` |

**The committee recorded the cost of the decision in the minute.** An organisation that publishes the
audits that raised nothing invites the reading that its assurance function does not work. The committee's
recorded position was that only one of the two available readings is a decision it could take about its own
function's records, and that a committee unwilling to publish an uncomfortable history about internal audit
is not providing independent oversight of it.

**The committee also recorded what the decision is not.** It is not a finding against the audit function,
not an assessment of any individual, and not an admission of anything. **It is a decision about what the
record shows and what gets published.**

## 5. `DEC-118` and `IS-01` — no cause is stated

The committee decided that **the programme states no cause**, and directed that the candidate explanations
be published without one being chosen.

Four candidates were tabled, in no order of likelihood:

1. an audit programme scoped to procedures rather than to records;
2. a checklist derived from a prior inspection;
3. auditors without the technical access to read an audit trail;
4. a company that had never written down which records were Part 11 records.

**The committee declined to choose between them, and recorded three reasons.**

**The programme does not yet know what there was to find.** Whether an audit trail on any Helix system
records what `21 CFR 11.10(e)` describes is not established. Phase 02 determines which records are Part 11
records; Phase 05 examines the audit trails.

**The determination that would have anchored the audits did not exist.** No record-level Part 11
determination has ever been made at Helix — `IS-02`, raised at `GOV-02` on 2026-03-13. An audit programme
cannot test against a determination nobody made.

**A cause chosen now would be chosen to be comfortable.** Each candidate places the problem somewhere
convenient. Selecting one at this vantage would set the shape of the remediation on a guess.

**`IS-01` was raised**, owner Gideon Halvorsen, and directed to remain open until **Phase 08**, when the
periodic review, the internal audit of the remediated programme and the 483 response commitments come
together. The first half of the underlying question — which records actually are Part 11 records, and did
anybody ever write that down? — is **Phase 02's**.

## 7. Directions recorded

**First, the audits are described by what they covered and what they raised.** No programme document
characterises any auditor, any Helix employee or any FDA investigator, and none assesses any individual's
work. `../01.09-the-four-audits-that-found-nothing.md §2` and `§10` carry the constraint.

**Second, no legal conclusion.** Nothing in the audit history or the observations is characterised as a
violation of anything. **A Form FDA 483 is not a legal finding** and this committee reached no finding
either.

**Third, the three vocabularies stay separate.** An **inspectional observation** is `OBS-n`, in the 483's
own numbering. A **finding** is what internal audit or a third-party auditor raises, and the fourteen in
the history are findings. A **deviation** is `DEV-nnn`, a Helix quality record. `ADR-0006`.

**Fourth, no forecast.** Nothing in this minute or in any document arising from it states what any future
inspection will find.
