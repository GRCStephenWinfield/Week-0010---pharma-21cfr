## 2. What was being responded to

A **Form FDA 483** issued at the close of a routine CGMP surveillance inspection of the Ridgemont site
conducted from **2026-02-02 to 2026-02-13**, carrying **seven inspectional observations**, of which
**three concern electronic records**.

`../01.02-the-inspection-and-the-form-483.md` is the authoritative account of the inspection, of the seven
observations in the 483's own numbering, and of what a Form FDA 483 is and is not. **This record does not
restate any of it**, and in particular does not restate the observations.

Two things were recorded expressly before the response was drafted, and they governed how it was written.

**A Form FDA 483 is not a legal finding.** It records conditions that in the investigator's judgment may
constitute violations, and it is not a final agency determination. The response therefore concedes nothing,
contests nothing and adjudicates nothing. It states what Helix will do.

**The observations keep the 483's numbering.** Helix does not renumber an investigator's observations into
an internal scheme. The decision was formalised later at `DEC-114` / `ADR-0006`; the response of 2026-03-06
already followed the convention.

## 4. Rules the response was drafted to

| Rule | Applied how |
|---|---|
| **No legal conclusion** | The response does not state that any regulation was or was not contravened |
| **No characterisation of the inspection** | The response does not describe the inspection, the investigator or the observations as anything other than what they are |
| **Every commitment has a named individual owner** | No commitment is owned by a function — `ADR-0008` |
| **Every commitment has a due date and an evidence reference** | Recorded in the commitment register at `EV-106` |
| **No commitment the programme cannot evidence** | A commitment whose completion could not be shown from an artefact was not made |
| **No forecast** | The response does not state what a future inspection will find, and does not describe any commitment as guaranteeing an outcome |
| **Vocabulary** | An observation stays `OBS-n`. Where a deviation is raised in response to one, it carries its own `DEV-nnn` and the link is recorded — `DEC-115` |

## 5. The fifteen-business-day undertaking

Helix committed, as part of the response, to **provide a written response within fifteen business days of
any future inspectional observation.**

**This is Helix's own procedural undertaking. It is not a rule FDA imposes on Helix**, and no programme
document may describe it as a regulatory requirement or as a deadline set by the agency. It is recorded
here in those terms so that the distinction survives into later phases, where a shorter internal
commitment is easily mistaken for an external one.

The undertaking is carried in the commitment register at `EV-106` and reported quarterly at `CAL-04`.

## 6. What the response did not do

| Not done | Why |
|---|---|
| It did not accept or reject any observation as a matter of law | A Form FDA 483 is not an adjudication and a response is not a plea |
| It did not renumber the observations | `ADR-0006` |
| It did not convert any observation into a deviation | An observation is not a deviation and never becomes one. Where Helix raised a deviation in response, the two identifiers are kept and linked — `DEC-115` |
| It did not report on the four observations outside this programme's scope | They are handled inside the quality system. `../01.06-the-programme-charter.md §2` records them as out of scope |
| It did not commit to a date the programme could not own | Two proposed commitments were removed in review for that reason |
| It did not describe any system as Part 11 compliant or certified | **There is no Part 11 certification** |

## 7. How the commitments are tracked

`OBJ-08` is the objective, owned by Rebekah Sandquist, and its measure is readable from `EV-106`: per
commitment, a named owner, a due date, an evidence reference and a status.

| Reporting point | Cadence | To |
|---|---|---|
| Commitment register review | Monthly | Data Integrity Steering Committee, `CAL-01` |
| Commitment status report | Quarterly | Board audit committee, `CAL-04` |

**No commitment is reported as *on track*.** A commitment is open with a date, or closed with an evidence
reference. `AS-08` records the assumption that Helix does not vary the commitments during the programme,
because an objective whose measure moves mid-flight is an objective chosen to be met.

## 8. Position at the vantage

At 2026-05-29 the response has been filed for **84 days**. The commitment register exists, every commitment
has an owner and a date, and the programme's nine phases are the delivery route for the ones that require
technical work.

**Nothing further has occurred.** There has been no further inspection, no further correspondence recorded
in this phase, and **no inspection outcome of any kind**. The programme does not forecast one.
