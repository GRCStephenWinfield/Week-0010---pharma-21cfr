## What this phase deliberately does not establish

**No system inventory and no Part 11 applicability determination for any system** — which records are Part 11 records is Phase 02's. **No data integrity risk assessment and no ALCOA assessment** · **no validation framework and no validation of anything** · **no control implementation, no audit trail configuration and no electronic signature deployment** · **no periodic review** · **no internal audit of the remediated programme and no third-party audit** · **no further inspection and no inspection outcome of any kind.** **A Form FDA 483 is not a legal finding and nothing here reaches a legal conclusion.**
