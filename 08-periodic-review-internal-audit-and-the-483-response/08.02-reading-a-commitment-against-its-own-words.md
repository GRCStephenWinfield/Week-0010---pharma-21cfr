---

## 1. One idea

**The commitments Helix filed with FDA on 2026-03-06 are read against their own words, and a commitment
that names no completion criterion is recorded as having none rather than judged met or unmet.**

That is `ADR-0052`, taken by the Head of Regulatory Affairs on 2027-03-29 at `DEC-805`. The method it rests
on was authorised four days earlier at `DEC-804` and recorded at `GOV-36`, **and both were settled before
`EV-106` was opened.** The register of commitments had been in the quality system since the day it was
filed; the order in which the method and the reading happened is recorded because a method settled after
the answers are known is a method chosen for its answers.

This chapter argues that decision and its companion, `ADR-0056` — **a remediation the programme performed
is credited to the programme and not to the commitment's named owner, and the difference is recorded** —
taken by the Programme Manager on 2027-04-08 at `DEC-815`.

> **This chapter states no count and no position on any commitment.** What the reading found is at
> `08.03-the-commitments.md`; who performed the acts that satisfied what was satisfied is at
> `08.04-who-actually-did-it.md`; and the register is `logs/commitment-register.md`, which is authoritative.

## 1.1 What requires any of this — and the answer is nothing

**No provision of `21 CFR Part 11`, and no provision of Parts 210 and 211, requires a firm to respond to a
Form FDA 483 at all, or prescribes what a response must contain, or attaches any consequence to a
commitment made in one.** A 483 records an investigator's observations; a response is a firm's own
document, written in its own words, and the words are therefore the only thing there is to read it
against. **That is the whole ground of `ADR-0052`.**

What the predicate rules do require is what happens *after* a firm adopts something. `21 CFR 211.100(b)`
requires that *"[w]ritten production and process control procedures shall be followed in the execution of
the various production and process control functions and shall be documented at the time of performance.
Any deviation from the written procedures shall be recorded and justified."* **The
contemporaneous-documentation limb is quoted because it is the predicate-rule requirement that most
directly reaches how a record is made, and it survives enforcement discretion entirely.** **`21 CFR 211.22(d)` requires that the
responsibilities and procedures applicable to the quality control unit be in writing and that *"such
written procedures shall be followed."*** **A commitment
is not a procedure until Helix makes it one** — and where a commitment named an act that has since become
a procedure, the predicate rule reaches the procedure and not the promise.

**So the reading in this phase is a reading of Helix's own words against Helix's own words**, and the
chapter says so rather than borrowing authority the regulation does not lend it. `08.01-what-a-disposition-is.md §2`
holds the same line about the disposition of an observation.

---

## 2. What a completion criterion is

**A completion criterion is a state of the world, named in the commitment's own words, that would satisfy
it.**

The definition is deliberately mechanical, because a definition with any give in it will be given. Three
tests, all of which must hold.

**It names a state of affairs rather than an activity.** *An account list on which no credential is shared*
is a state of affairs. *Working towards the removal of shared credentials* is an activity, and an activity
is satisfied by its own performance, whatever it produces.

**The state of affairs is observable by somebody who was not party to the commitment.** Not necessarily
easy to observe — a determination may take a fortnight to check — but observable in principle by a person
holding the systems, the records and the commitment's own text, without needing to be told what the drafter
had in mind.

**And there is a state of the world in which it is false.** This is the one that does the work, and it is
the one that fails. **A commitment satisfied by every state of the world is satisfied by the state of the
world that obtained on the day the Form FDA 483 was issued** — which is to say it was already met before it
was made, and nothing that happened afterwards could have failed it.

**Note what is not in the definition.** A completion criterion is not a date; a date says when, not what. It
is not an owner; an owner says who, not what. It is not a metric, a threshold or a target. **A commitment
can carry an owner and a date and still name no state of the world at all**, and the drafting habit that
produces that combination is the one this chapter exists to make visible: **the fields that are easy to
fill get filled, and the field that is hard to fill is the commitment itself.**

## 3. Where the criterion has to be, and when it is looked for

**In the commitment's own words, as the response worded them, and nowhere else.**

Not in the covering text of the response. Not in the programme charter, which did not exist when the
response was drafted. Not in the deviation, change or CAPA records that later attached to the same subject
matter. Not in what the drafters would have said if asked. **The commitment is the sentence Helix gave to a
regulator, and it is assessed as that sentence.**

**And the sequence of the reading is part of the method rather than an accident of how the work went.**
The authorised method — `EV-805`, `DEC-804` — requires, for each commitment: read the words the response
used; ask whether those words name a state of the world that could be observed to obtain; record the answer
or its absence. **Only then is the practice described.**

**The reason is that a commitment read backwards from what happened is a description of what happened.**
Given a sentence with any elasticity in it and a known outcome, a competent professional will find the
reading under which the outcome satisfies the sentence, and will do it honestly, because that reading is
genuinely available. **The elasticity is the finding**, and it is invisible from the far end. Reading the
words first, in ignorance of the outcome, is the only arrangement in which the elasticity is visible at all.

## 4. Why a commitment with no criterion is recorded rather than judged

**A commitment written so that nobody can say whether it was met is a commitment nobody can be held to.**

The obvious alternative is to judge it anyway, on the balance of what is known, and to record *met* or *not
met* with a note. **Both halves of that are refused, and for different reasons.**

**Recording it *met* would credit the company with a change it cannot demonstrate.** The evidence that
would support *met* is the same evidence that supports anything else, because a commitment with no
falsifying state of the world is satisfied by whatever occurred. **A position that could not have come out
the other way is not a position.**

**Recording it *not met* would manufacture a failure out of a drafting defect.** Nothing in the record
suggests that the activity a loosely worded commitment describes did not happen. Reinforcement, awareness,
emphasis and improvement are real things that organisations really do, and it is entirely possible that
Helix did them. **What is absent is not the work. It is the test** — and converting an absent test into an
adverse finding would be the same error as converting it into a favourable one, pointed the other way.
**Unassessed is not adverse**, and the repository has held that since Phase 02.

**So the position recorded is the third one: no completion criterion.** It is a complete cell, not an empty
one. It says something true, specific and checkable about the commitment — that its own words name no state
of the world that would satisfy it — and it says nothing whatever about whether Helix did the work.

## 5. And no criterion is written for one now

**The temptation at this point is precise and it is strong.** The programme knows what a good version of
each such commitment would have said. Writing it down would take an afternoon, would make the register
tidy, and would let the phase report a position on every commitment instead of a position on some and a
statement about the drafting of the rest.

**Refused, and the reason is the one that gives this chapter its title.**

**Supplying the test now would mean assessing Helix against a commitment Helix never filed.** The sentence
in the register would be the programme's sentence, not the response's, and the position recorded against it
would be a position about a document that does not exist outside this repository. **What Helix owes is what
Helix said.**

**And the firm would be marking its own homework twice.** It set the question — the commitment, drafted
inside the company, reviewed inside the company, released inside the company. If it now also writes the
mark scheme, after the work, knowing the answer, **the entire exercise closes on itself: the assessment can
only report that the company met a standard the company wrote for itself once it knew what it had done.**
An assurance opinion from the party that designed the thing being assured is not an opinion, it is a
restatement, and a criterion supplied after the fact is that restatement with an extra step.

**There is a second, quieter cost.** A criterion written now would convert a finding about how the response
was drafted into a finding about how the remediation was performed, **and those are different findings
owned by different people.** The response's drafting is the regulatory function's; the remediation is the
accountable owners'. **Smoothing the first into the second would take a defect in a document Helix filed
with an agency and re-describe it as a delivery shortfall**, which is both wrong and convenient.

**What the programme does instead is record the absence, name what would have to change, and stop.** No
commitment is rewritten, extended, narrowed, replaced or added to; **no new commitment is made**; and no
date any commitment carried is extended, replaced or proposed. `08.03-the-commitments.md` reports what the
reading found and `logs/commitment-register.md` carries the rows.

## 6. The owner of a commitment and the party that discharged it

**These are two different questions and the register asks both.**

**The owner of a commitment is the individual the response named to FDA.** That is a fact about the
response, fixed on 2026-03-06, and it does not move. **The party that discharged it is whoever performed
the act that brought about the state of affairs the commitment named.** That is a fact about what happened
afterwards, and there is no reason in logic why the two must be the same person.

**In practice they diverge in a very specific way, and the way matters.** A programme that runs for
thirteen months across an estate will, in the course of doing its own work, bring about states of affairs
that somebody else undertook to bring about. It does not do so by taking the work over. It does so because
the artefact the commitment described — a determination, an enumeration, a characterisation, a register —
is the same artefact the programme needed for its own purposes, and once it exists the commitment's
condition is satisfied by whoever caused it to exist.

**`ADR-0056` fixes what the register does with that.** **Where the act that satisfied a commitment was
performed by this programme, the register says so.** It does not report the commitment as discharged by the
individual the response named. **And it does not silently correct that individual's own tracking to agree
with it.**

## 7. Why the difference is recorded rather than smoothed

**The smoothing is not a lie and that is exactly why it needs a rule.**

Every step of it is defensible on its own. The commitment's condition genuinely obtains. The named owner
genuinely holds accountability for the area. The programme genuinely operates under Helix's governance,
with the owner's cooperation, on the owner's estate. **A register that recorded the commitment as met by
its owner would be saying something that, at the level of the organisation, is true.** The company
undertook to do a thing; the thing is done; the company did it.

**Three reasons it is refused anyway.**

**Accountability that is discharged by somebody else has not been exercised.** `ADR-0008`'s rule is that
ownership means the control is defined, resourced, performed as defined and evidenced — and the owner is
accountable for all four. **Where another party performed the act, what the owner did was not object.**
That may be entirely reasonable. It is not the same as having discharged the commitment, and a register
that cannot tell the difference cannot tell anybody anything about how the organisation actually runs.

**The programme ends and the accountability does not.** Close-out is 2027-04-30. Every commitment
discharged by the programme rather than by its owner is a commitment whose recurrence, maintenance and
evidencing sit with somebody who has not yet performed it once. **A register that recorded the owner as
having discharged it would have concealed exactly the thing the organisation needs to know before the
programme's people leave.**

**And two records that disagree are evidence; one record that has been reconciled is not.** Where the
owner's own tracking says one thing and the programme's register says another, **the disagreement is itself
the finding**, and it survives only for as long as nobody tidies it. **The programme therefore edited no
owner's tracker, corrected none and reconciled none**, and the enquiry that established what each tracker
showed is retained rather than only its answer. Any later act by an owner that brings the two into
agreement is a new act with its own record and its own date; **it is not a correction of history.**

> **A commitment tracked in one place and discharged in another is a commitment nobody is tracking.**

`08.04-who-actually-did-it.md` reports where this obtains and what it means; **this chapter states no
instance of it.**

## 8. What this chapter does not establish

- **It states no count, no position and no name.** Which commitments were assessed, what position each
  carries and who discharged what are `08.03-the-commitments.md`'s,
  `08.04-who-actually-did-it.md`'s and `logs/commitment-register.md`'s.
- **It rewrites no commitment, supplies no completion criterion, extends no date and makes no new
  commitment** — `ADR-0052`.
- **It states no CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan and no remediation cost.** The commitments are
  read; they are not re-planned.
- **It closes no observation, describes none as closed and makes no representation to FDA** — `ADR-0051`,
  `ADR-0057`, `08.01-what-a-disposition-is.md`.
- **It reaches no compliance conclusion and no legal conclusion**, and it assesses no individual's work. **A
  commitment discharged by another party is a fact about an act, not a characterisation of a person.**
- **It re-determines no Phase 05 cell, re-scores no Phase 03 pair, re-bands no Phase 04 obligation and
  re-derives no Phase 07 period.**
- **It bears on `IS-01` not at all**, and it names no candidate explanation of it, by number, by content, by
  paraphrase or by allusion — `08.11-is-01.md`, `ADR-0055`.
