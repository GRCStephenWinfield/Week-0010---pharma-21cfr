## 0. What this log is not

**This is not the commitment register, the periodic review register or the audit condition register.**
Those exist from this vantage, at `commitment-register.md`, `periodic-review-register.md` and
`audit-condition-register.md`, and this log is kept apart from them deliberately. **An `RC` identifier
never appears as an `IS` identifier, an `RV` identifier never becomes one, an `AC` identifier never becomes
one, and none of the three converts into any other.**

**`PR-01` to `PR-63` carry no likelihood, no impact and no score, and none ever will.**

**No entry in this log closes an inspectional observation or describes one as closed.** `OBS-2`, `OBS-4`
and `OBS-6` each have a position stated at `../08.05-obs-2.md`, `../08.06-obs-4.md` and
`../08.07-obs-6-and-the-register-that-cannot-show-it.md`; **a position is not a closure and closure is a
representation to FDA that this programme does not make** — `ADR-0051`. **No entry reaches a compliance
conclusion or a legal conclusion.** **No entry states a CAPA, a deviation, a remediation plan or a
remediation cost.** **No entry rewrites, varies or replaces a commitment the response of 2026-03-06 made,
and no entry makes a new one** — `ADR-0052`. **No entry reports the result of a periodic review**, because
no review has been performed against any of the sixty-one rows — `IS-57`.
