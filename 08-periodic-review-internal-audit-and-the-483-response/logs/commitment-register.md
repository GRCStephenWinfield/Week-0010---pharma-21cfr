## 0. What this register is, and the five things it is not

**This register reads `EV-106` — the response commitment register — and records what each of its
twenty-two commitments says, whom it named, what date it gave, whether it can be tested, and where each
one stands.** `EV-106` has existed since 2026-03-06. **This is the first document in the programme that
opens it.**

**The method is `../08.02-reading-a-commitment-against-its-own-words.md`'s and is not re-argued here.**
A commitment is read against the words the response used, not against what the programme now wishes it had
said; a commitment with no completion criterion is recorded as having none rather than judged; and who owns
a commitment and who discharged it are two columns because they are two facts — `ADR-0052`, `ADR-0056`.

**`EV-106` also carries one standing procedural undertaking that attaches to no observation** — the
undertaking to file a written response within fifteen business days of any future inspectional
observation. **It carries no `RC` identifier, it is not one of the twenty-two, and it is not assessed
here.** It is Helix's own procedural undertaking, it matches FDA's own recommended window rather than
beating it, **it is not a rule FDA imposes**, and it is measured by `OBJ-08` and reported quarterly at
`CAL-04`.

### The five things this register is not

| It is not | Why |
|---|---|
| **A closure of any observation** | **Nothing here closes anything.** A commitment position is a statement about a commitment. **The condition an observation records is disposed of by stating its present position, never by stating that it is closed** — `ADR-0051`, `../08.01-what-a-disposition-is.md`, and the three positions are at `../08.05-obs-2.md`, `../08.06-obs-4.md` and `../08.07-obs-6-and-the-register-that-cannot-show-it.md` |
| **A representation to FDA** | **This programme makes none.** The response is Helix's own document and this register reads it internally. **Nothing here has been filed, communicated or offered to any agency** |
| **A revision of any commitment** | **No commitment is rewritten, restated, narrowed, widened or replaced, and no new one is made.** The three with no completion criterion are recorded as having none — **giving them one now would be writing a commitment Helix did not file** |
| **A CAPA, a deviation or a remediation plan** | None is stated by this phase in any document. **A commitment recorded as Not met is a reading of a commitment, not the opening of a quality record** |
| **A statement about any individual's performance** | **The Discharged-by and Owner's-own-tracking columns are statements about two records that do not speak to each other.** `IS-52` is a governance finding about tracking, and `../08.04-who-actually-did-it.md` states in terms that it is not an allegation that anybody acted improperly |

### The scope of the assessment, and why eight commitments are not in it

**Fourteen of the twenty-two attach to `OBS-2`, `OBS-4` or `OBS-6` and are assessed here. Eight attach to
`OBS-1`, `OBS-3`, `OBS-5` or `OBS-7` and are not.** The four out-of-scope observations are named once at
`../../01-program-foundation-and-regulatory-scoping/01.02-the-inspection-and-the-form-483.md §3` and this
repository does not report on them again — `ADR-0006`. **They are being addressed under Helix's ordinary
quality system by their own owners, on their own timelines**, and a data integrity programme that annexed
them at the last phase would be claiming work it has not done. **The eight rows carry position *Not
assessed* and a Discharged-by cell of `—`, and they are published rather than omitted**, because a
commitment left out of a register cannot be seen to have been left out.

## 3. The three with no completion criterion

**`RC-07`, `RC-14` and `RC-20`.** Read them beside a commitment that has one. `RC-12` says the shared
local operator account will be withdrawn and a named individual account issued to every operator; a person
holding the account list and the system can say whether that is so. **`RC-20` says that Helix "*will
improve the visibility of changes made to electronic batch records after approval*"** — and no state of the
world satisfies it, because no state of the world fails it.

**A commitment written so that nobody can say whether it was met is a commitment nobody can be held to.**
`IS-51`, and `../08.03-the-commitments.md §4` argues it.

**These three are recorded as having no completion criterion and are not judged.** They are not recorded as
met, because nothing establishes that they are; they are not recorded as not met, because nothing
establishes that either. **A register that graded them either way would be inventing a test the response
did not contain**, and inventing one now would be writing a commitment Helix never filed — `ADR-0052`.

**All three were owned, all three carried a date, and all three passed their date without anything
happening that could be recorded.** That is the shape of the finding: **the absent element is not the
owner and it is not the date. It is the test.**
