## 0. What an earlier phase said about this phase's subject matter, and what is superseded or narrowed

**Nine paragraphs follow. Seven earlier statements published in Phases 01, 05, 06 and 07 are narrowed or
spent here, seven are recorded as correct and unsuperseded, and one addition is made. All are written down
rather than smoothed**, on the
discipline Phase 02's evidence index `§0` set for `EV-129` and every phase since has kept.

**First, and it is the one that matters most.** `01.09 §7` records what the programme committed to instead
of choosing a cause: *"publish the history, name the candidates, and revisit the question in **Phase 08**,
when the periodic review, the internal audit of the remediated programme and the 483 response all come
together and **the evidence to answer it exists**."* **As published, the closing clause reads as an
undertaking that Phase 08 would answer the question, and to that extent it is narrowed.** **Phase 08
establishes six conditions and that not one of them obtained in any of the four audits. It does not
establish why, and `ADR-0055` records that it never will.** **The evidence that now exists is evidence
about what the audit records show; it is not evidence that discriminates between explanations**, and a
phase that treated the arrival of one as the arrival of the other would have chosen a cause on material
that cannot choose. **`01.09` is not edited**; the original stands with this narrowing recorded against it,
and **no identifier already issued changes meaning.**

**Second.** `01.09 §9` records `IS-01`'s *Next examined* as **Phase 08**, and `01.09 §1`'s statement that
**"A company that missed something has a detection problem; a company whose own audits looked at it and
passed it has a different one."** **Both are correct and neither is superseded.** `IS-01` was examined in
Phase 08 and closed in it, on 2027-04-07 — `EV-835`.

**Third.** `CAL-07` — *periodic review of computerised systems* — has stood in the programme calendar since
2026-05-29 with a cadence of **annual, per system**, an owner in Colm Ó Braonáin and a first occurrence of
**2027-02-01**. **It is narrowed in two respects and neither is a correction.** **No review has been
performed under it, and its first occurrence has passed** — `IS-57`. And **the frequency established in
this phase is not uniformly annual**: it is derived from the Phase 04 complexity-and-reliability band and
departs from that default only upward, so **the single cadence in the calendar is now a per-system
frequency at `periodic-review-register.md`** — `ADR-0054`, `DEC-811`. **The calendar entry is not edited**,
and `IS-07` continues to record that `CAL-07` is one of the seven entries that outlive the programme with
no named owner beyond close-out.

**Fourth.** `01.02 §5` records, at the Phase 01 vantage, under the heading of what corrective actions have
been completed: **"None has been completed at this vantage."** **That was true on 2026-05-29 and is not
superseded as a statement about that date.** **What is recorded here is that at this vantage six of the
fourteen commitments assessed are Met** — `commitment-register.md §2` — **and that five of the six were
discharged by this programme rather than by the individual the response named** — `IS-52`. **A commitment
met is not a corrective action completed**, and this index states neither as the other.

**Fifth.** Phase 05's `control-gap-register.md §5` records that whether any account falls within the
category the December 2018 guidance describes as acceptable for viewing — a shared, read-only account
for viewing data, where the user cannot modify data or settings — *"…is Phase 08's and is not answered here or implied here."* **It is answered here.** **On none
of the nine systems named at `CG-11` and `CG-13` is the shared credential a read-only account**: each is an
operating account through which entries are made — `EV-819`. **No Phase 05 cell moves, and the answer is a fact about the accounts and not a re-determination
of `(d)`** — `ADR-0049`.

**Sixth.** Phase 06's `auditable-event-register.md §5` records that **"No primary source names a
stand-alone periodic audit trail review frequency, this register names none, and `IS-04` stays deliberately
unresolved."** **The first two limbs are correct and unsuperseded. The third is spent: `IS-04` closes here**
— `EV-837` — **and it closes without naming a frequency, without setting one, and without attributing one
to any source.** **No FDA source names a stand-alone periodic frequency**; EU GMP Annex 11 clause 9's
expectation is that audit trails *"…be available and convertible to a generally intelligible form and
regularly reviewed"*, **which is a *should* in a guideline that is not FDA's, is labelled EU-derived, and names no
interval either.** **The two are never merged.**

**Seventh.** `07.13 §5` directs Phase 08 to **"Set an audit trail review frequency *if a primary source is
found that names one*"**. **None was found and none is set.** The direction is discharged by its own
condition failing.

**Eighth.** `07.13 §5` also directs Phase 08 to **"Report the third-party audit and the internal audit
*once they have occurred*"**. **No report from `CAL-09` or from `CAL-10` is indexed below, and no result of
either is stated anywhere in Phase 08.** **No outcome of either is anticipated, described, forecast or
budgeted for, in this index or in any other document in the phase** — the standing refusal at `01.10` and
`ADR-0007`'s condition hold without variation.

**Ninth, and it is an addition rather than a correction.** **`EV-737` to `EV-800` are permanently unused
and are never issued**, on the same rule that left `EV-129`–`EV-200`, `EV-233`–`EV-300`, `EV-337`–`EV-400`,
`EV-441`–`EV-500`, `EV-543`–`EV-600` and `EV-635`–`EV-700` unused. **`07.13 §4` states the block and Phase
08 carries it unchanged; no identifier within it is ever issued.** **Phase 07 correctly recorded that Phase
08 would open at `EV-801` and at document IDs `HLX-DI-2027-8NN`; both statements hold and neither is
superseded.**

**Every item below exists at the vantage.** Nothing in this index is a plan, and no item is dated after
2027-04-09.

**The ordering discipline.** **The method items pre-date the result items they govern, and the counting
items post-date the records they count.** The assessment method at `EV-805` is dated 2027-03-25 and the
read of `EV-106` at `EV-806` 2027-03-30 — **because a commitment read backwards from what happened is a
description of what happened.** The register at `EV-811` is dated 2027-03-31 and post-dates the fourteen
assessment records at `EV-808`. The six conditions at `EV-831` are dated 2027-04-06 and the register at
`EV-830` 2027-04-07. The frequency derivation at `EV-827` is dated 2027-04-05 and the register at `EV-828`
2027-04-08. **Where two items carry the same date the weaker relation is the one stated**, because a
strict *post-dates* asserted of a same-day pair is a claim the Date column does not support.

**What is not here.** **No closure of any observation and no document describing one as closed.** **No
communication to FDA of any kind, and no draft of one** — the response of 2026-03-06 remains the only thing
Helix has said to the agency about any of this. **No compliance conclusion and no legal conclusion.** **No
CAPA record, no deviation record, no remediation plan and no remediation cost.** **No revised, extended or
new commitment.** **No periodic review record** — none exists, and the template is an empty form. **No
report from `CAL-09` or `CAL-10` and no result of either.** **No re-determined Phase 05 cell, no re-scored
Phase 03 pair, no re-banded Phase 04 obligation and no re-derived Phase 07 period.** **No retention period
for any Group C record type.** **No executed validation package, no protocol, no test result and no system
validation plan.** **No document naming, ranking or weighting any candidate explanation of `IS-01`.**

**Nothing in this index is a certification, an approval or an attestation of compliance by any agency.**
**There is no Part 11 certification**, and no artefact here could be one.

**Retention.** Each item is retained for at least the life of the determination, decision or register it
supports. **For the three registers at `EV-811`, `EV-828` and `EV-830` that is for as long as the positions
they record are relied on** — and for `EV-830` that is indefinite, because the twenty-four cells it records
are historical facts about four audits and cannot be superseded by anything that happens next.

## 3. The ordering rule, stated

**Where an item counts, reconciles or concludes from other items, its date is on or after the date of the
last item it counts.** `EV-811` is dated 2027-03-31 and the fourteen assessment records at `EV-808`
2027-03-30. `EV-828` is dated 2027-04-08 and the derivation at `EV-827` 2027-04-05; `EV-829` shares
`EV-828`'s date, so the relation stated is **no earlier than**. `EV-830` is dated 2027-04-07 and the
working papers at `EV-831` and `EV-832` 2027-04-06. `EV-835` is dated 2027-04-07 and counts items dated
2027-04-06 and earlier.

**Where an item authorises, enumerates or specifies, its date is before the date of the first item it
governs, and strictly before.** `EV-804` 2027-03-24 and `EV-805` 2027-03-25 before `EV-806` 2027-03-30.
`EV-802` and `EV-803` 2027-03-23 before every result item in the phase. `EV-825` 2027-03-31 and `EV-827`
2027-04-05 before `EV-828` 2027-04-08. **A rule settled after the results are in is not a rule**, and the
index is built so that the claim can be checked against the Date column rather than believed.

**One item is dated earlier than its block and the reason is stated.** `EV-807`, the two procedure reads,
is dated 2027-03-26 — **before** the commitments were read on 2027-03-30 — because what a procedure says
is a fact about the procedure and had to be established without knowing which commitment it would bear on.
