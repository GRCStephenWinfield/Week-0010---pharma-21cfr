## 1. What this phase is

**This is the phase the whole programme has been walking towards.**

Phase 01 published the inspection, the seven observations and the four internal audits that raised nothing
on the three the programme took, and left an open question it could not answer. Phase 02 determined which
records are Part 11 records. Phase 03 assessed the ALCOA attributes. Phase 04 built the validation
framework and banded every system on complexity and reliability. Phase 05 determined a control position for
every paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` on every system in scope. Phase 06 characterised the chromatography
estate at depth. Phase 07 derived a retention position for every in-scope record type and read backup and
restore across the estate. **Every one of those phases deferred something to this one.**

**Phase 08 does four things.** It states the present position of the three conditions the Form FDA 483
records, on a method authorised before any of them was looked at. **It reads the commitments Helix filed
with FDA on 2026-03-06 against their own words, for the first time since they were filed.** It establishes
a periodic review of the estate where no source binding Helix requires one, adopted voluntarily and
labelled so. **And it closes `IS-01`, after eight phases open and unweighted — on what the programme can
establish, and without naming a candidate explanation.**

**`IS-01`, `IS-04`, `IS-06` and `IS-50` close here.**

**The vantage is 2027-04-09, a Friday, `MS-12`, twenty-one days after the Phase 07 vantage.** **`MS-12`'s
owner is Gideon Halvorsen, Head of Internal Audit, and the phase in which `IS-01` closes is owned by the
individual who raised it.** Nothing more is made of that here or anywhere else in the phase.

## 2. The boundary, stated hard

**Phase 08 closes no observation, makes no representation to FDA and states no compliance conclusion.**

**An observation is disposed of by stating the condition's present position, never by stating that it is
closed.** Closure is a representation about the state of a matter between the firm and an agency, and Helix
is one of two parties to it. `08.01-what-a-disposition-is.md` argues it and **`ADR-0051`** records it;
**`ADR-0057`** carries the phase-level boundary, and `08.12-what-this-does-not-establish.md` publishes the
whole of it as a numbered chapter at the same version as the findings.

**A disposition is not a closure, and an arrangement is not a review.** The periodic review is established
and has not been run — **`IS-57`** — and **no result of any review is reported anywhere in this phase.**

**Phase 08 re-determines nothing.** No Phase 05 control position is re-determined, no Phase 03 pair
re-scored, no Phase 04 band re-taken and no Phase 07 period re-derived. Every earlier register is an input
and is never edited.

**And no commitment is rewritten.** The commitments are read against their own words; **none is extended,
narrowed, replaced or added to, no new one is made, and no completion criterion is supplied to one that
carries none** — `ADR-0052`.

**`IS-01` closes and names no candidate explanation.** Not by number, not by content, not by paraphrase,
not by allusion. **The refusal is permanent and is not handed to Phase 09** — `ADR-0055`.

## 3. Read this first

Seven method decisions, and everything else in the phase follows from them. **None of them is a result.**

**An observation is disposed of by stating the condition's present position, never by stating that it is
closed.** Three verbs — *confirmed*, *corrected*, *not corrected* — and no qualifier attaches to any of
them. `08.01-what-a-disposition-is.md`, **`ADR-0051`**.

**The commitments are assessed against their own words, and one whose words name no completion criterion is
recorded as having none rather than judged.** **A commitment written so that nobody can say whether it was
met is a commitment nobody can be held to**, and supplying the test now would be assessing Helix against a
commitment Helix never filed. `08.02-reading-a-commitment-against-its-own-words.md`, **`ADR-0052`**.

**Periodic review is adopted voluntarily where FDA requires none, and its EU-derived limb is labelled
EU-derived at every citation.** Nothing in `21 CFR Part 11` or Parts 210 and 211 requires a periodic review
of a computerised system, and **`21 CFR 211.180(e)` is a product review and is never the basis for one.**
`08.08-nothing-requires-a-periodic-review.md`, **`ADR-0053`**.

**The review frequency defaults to the Phase 04 complexity-and-reliability band and departs from it only
upward, with a reason in the row.** **A band that can be argued away in the same document that publishes it
is not a band.** `08.09-the-review-established.md`, **`ADR-0054`**.

**The four candidate explanations are never chosen among, and `IS-01` closes on what the programme can
establish rather than on what it cannot.** **A condition is not a cause.** `08.11-is-01.md`, **`ADR-0055`**.

**A remediation the programme performed is credited to the programme and not to the commitment's owner, and
the difference is recorded.** **A commitment tracked in one place and discharged in another is a commitment
nobody is tracking.** `08.02-reading-a-commitment-against-its-own-words.md §6`–`§7`, **`ADR-0056`**.

**Phase 08 states no compliance conclusion, closes no observation and makes no representation to FDA.**
`08.01-what-a-disposition-is.md §3`–`§5` and `08.12-what-this-does-not-establish.md`, **`ADR-0057`**.

**The two that fix how the commitments would be read were taken before `EV-106` was opened, and the
boundary was fixed before either.** The boundary was fixed at `DEC-801`
on 2027-03-22; `ADR-0051` was taken on 2027-03-24, the method behind `ADR-0052` at `DEC-804` on 2027-03-25
and `ADR-0052` itself on 2027-03-29; **and
`EV-106` was opened on 2027-03-30.** **A method settled after the results are known is a method chosen for
the results it produces**, and the dates are on the record so the claim can be checked rather than believed.

## 12. Standing framing

These positions hold across all nine phases and are restated here so that a reader arriving at Phase 08 has
them.

- **There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate and no FDA-approved or FDA-validated
  software.** A product has **features that support** compliance; compliance is a property of Helix's
  validated system in its intended use, under its procedures, operated by its trained people. **Outsourcing
  to a service provider transfers no responsibility.**
- **Part 11 applies to records, not to systems**, and this repository never says "a Part 11 system" without
  saying which records make it one — `ADR-0001`, `01.03 §2`.
- **Enforcement discretion is FDA's stated intention, and the set is stated with all its limbs.** FDA has
  stated an intention to exercise it over **`21 CFR 11.10(a)`, `(b)`, `(c)` and `(e)` in full**, over
  **`21 CFR 11.10(k)` in part** — the guidance names `(k)(2)` — over **the corresponding requirements in
  `21 CFR 11.30`**, and over **all Part 11 requirements for legacy systems operational before 20 August
  1997 that meet the guidance's conditions, against which no Helix system has been assessed.** **It is not
  a legal exemption, it is not a safe harbour, and it confers no rights**; the guidance carries FDA's own
  disclaimer that *"It does not create or confer any rights for or on any person and does not operate to
  bind FDA or the public"*. **`21 CFR 211.68`, `21 CFR 211.100(b)` and `21 CFR 211.180` survive it
  entirely.** `ADR-0003`, `ADR-0035`.
- **FDA's Computer Software Assurance guidance is device guidance under `21 CFR Part 820`, as amended by
  the Quality Management System Regulation, and does not apply to Helix.** Adopted voluntarily as good
  practice, **labelled as adopted every time** — `ADR-0005`, `ADR-0026`.
- **GAMP 5 Second Edition, ISPE, July 2022, is good-practice guidance and never a requirement**, and **no
  software-category count and no enumeration of the categories is published in this repository.**
- **FDA uses plain ALCOA, and ALCOA does not map onto `21 CFR 11.10`** — FDA's own footnote 5 maps the
  attributes to the CGMP predicate rules and cites Part 11 nowhere, `ADR-0019`. **The four additional
  attributes are MHRA's**, MHRA itself refers to the shorter acronym, states there is no difference in
  expectations regardless of which is used and defines all four, and **it does not reject them.** **No
  originator is named for either set.**
- **Part 11 requires no reason-for-change field, no read-event logging, no cryptographic hashing under
  `21 CFR 11.70`, no multi-factor authentication under `21 CFR 11.200` and no password interval under
  `21 CFR 11.300(b)`** — and **no review frequency of any kind.** `21 CFR 11.10(e)` requires audit trails
  to be generated, not to obscure previously recorded information, to be retained and to be available for
  agency review and copying, **and imposes no internal review obligation at all.** Where Helix implements
  any of them, it is Helix's decision and this repository says so — `ADR-0036`.
- **`21 CFR 11.30`'s additional measures are additive to the `21 CFR 11.10` controls it requires be
  included as appropriate, and never a substitute for them.** **That *as appropriate* is `21 CFR 11.30`'s
  own and reaches open systems only; on a closed system the eleven paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10` are owed in
  full, and all sixty-one systems in scope are closed** — `ADR-0011`.
- **Nothing in `21 CFR Part 11` or Parts 210 and 211 requires a periodic review of a computerised system**,
  and **`21 CFR 211.180(e)` is a product review and is never the basis for one** — `ADR-0053`.
- **EU GMP Annex 11 is EU-derived and only its January 2011 text is in force.** **FDA does not enforce
  EudraLex; an FDA inspection is not an Annex 11 inspection and an Annex 11 expectation is not an FDA
  requirement.** **No Annex 11 obligation binds Helix's US-facing route**, and **the Qualified Person is
  not a US regulatory role.**
- **`21 CFR Part 11` sets no retention period of its own.** `21 CFR 11.10(e)` sets a **relation** whose
  second term comes from the predicate rule.
- **`21 CFR 211.68(b)` requires appropriate controls over computer or related systems to assure that
  changes in master production and control records or other records are instituted only by authorised
  personnel; it further requires that input to and output from the system be checked for accuracy and that
  *"[t]he degree and frequency of input/output verification shall be based on the complexity and
  reliability of the computer or related system."* — an obligation whose object is input/output
  verification and nothing else under that provision. `21 CFR 211.68(a)` requires equipment so used to be
  routinely calibrated, inspected or checked according to a written program designed to assure proper
  performance.** **`21 CFR 211.68` imposes no general computerised-system validation requirement**, and the
  word *validation* appears in the section once, inside `(b)`'s backup carve-out.
- **A closed system is defined by who controls access** — `21 CFR 11.3(b)(4)`. Hosting is not the test, and
  **all sixty-one systems in scope are closed** — `ADR-0011`.
- **`21 CFR 11.3(b)` contains nine definitions and Part 11 contains ten sections.** There is no §11.60, no
  §11.400 and no Subpart D. **Part 11 defines neither "audit trail" nor "metadata" nor "data integrity" nor
  "static record" nor "dynamic record" nor "archive"** — FDA defines the first five in the December 2018
  drug CGMP data integrity guidance. Citations in this repository are exact and can be looked up.
- **A Form FDA 483 is not a legal finding** — *"The FDA Form 483 does not constitute a final Agency
  determination of whether any condition is in violation of the FD&C Act or any of its relevant
  regulations."* **A Warning Letter is informal and advisory and only a court order binds.** **No Warning
  Letter, Untitled Letter or judicial action of any kind exists**, **no document in this repository reaches
  a legal conclusion of any kind**, and **never forecast what a future inspection will find.**
- **An open gap is Helix's own assessment of its own position** — not a violation, not a breach, not a
  non-compliance, not a finding and not an inspectional observation.
- **Applicability is not integrity, a rating is not a validation status, a requirement is not a control, a
  control position is not validation evidence, a depth finding is not an estate finding, a derived period
  is not a decision Helix has taken, a disposition is not a closure, and an arrangement is not a review.**
- **All content about Helix is fictional and illustrative.**

## 13. How to read this phase

Read **`08.07`** if you read only one chapter. It is the one in which the programme applies to its own
registers the standard it has applied to the estate for seven phases, **and it does not soften the result.**

Read **`08.01`** and **`08.02`** for the two method decisions everything else rests on — the first for what
a position on an observation is and is not, the second for how a commitment is read. Read **`08.08`** before
**`08.09`**, because the arrangement is only intelligible once it is clear that nothing required it.

Read **`08.11`** with attention to what it declines to do. It is the chapter this repository has been
carrying since Phase 01, **and the thing it does not say is the point of it.**

Read **`08.12`** with particular attention. It answers the reading a hostile reviewer will produce first,
and the answer is not that the reading is unreasonable.

Read for the arguments rather than the citations. Where a chapter cites `21 CFR 211.194(a)(8)` or
`21 CFR 11.10(e)`, it is locating a requirement in the text — **not** asserting that Helix meets it.
