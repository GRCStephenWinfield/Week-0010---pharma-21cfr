## 3. Item one — the commitments are read against their own words

**`DEC-805` and `ADR-0052`, taken by Rebekah Sandquist at the sitting of 2027-03-29.**

**Decision.** **The commitments are assessed against their own words, and a commitment with no completion
criterion is recorded as having none rather than judged.**

| Considered | Position |
|---|---|
| *Judge the criterion-less commitments as met, on the ground that the activity plainly occurred* | **Refused.** Nothing would have been tested. **A commitment satisfied by any state of the world is satisfied by the state of the world that obtained on the day the 483 was issued**, and recording it as met would credit the company with a change it cannot demonstrate |
| *Judge them as not met* | **Refused.** It is the same error with the sign reversed and it is worse: **it manufactures a failure out of a drafting defect**, and nothing suggests that the activity did not occur |
| *Write completion criteria now and assess against them* | **Refused.** It would be **assessing Helix against a commitment Helix never filed**, and it would quietly convert a finding about how the response was drafted into a finding about how the remediation was performed |
| **Record them as having no completion criterion, and do not judge them** | **Adopted** — `ADR-0052`, `EV-809` |

**`PR-59` opened** at the sitting of 2027-03-31, against the drift by which a criterion is written later and
the commitment reported against it as though the response had contained one.

## 4. Item two — the read of `EV-106`

**`DEC-806`, taken by Rebekah Sandquist on 2027-03-30 and ratified at the sitting of 2027-03-31.**

**`EV-106` carries twenty-two commitments, `RC-01` to `RC-22`, and one standing procedural undertaking that
attaches to no observation and carries no `RC` identifier.**

**Fourteen attach to `OBS-2`, `OBS-4` or `OBS-6` and are assessed; eight attach to `OBS-1`, `OBS-3`,
`OBS-5` or `OBS-7` and are not. 14 + 8 = 22.** **The eight are not assessed because this repository has
never annexed the four out-of-scope observations and does not start at its eighth phase** — `ADR-0006`.
**They are published with a position of *Not assessed*, so that the exclusion can be seen rather than
inferred.**

**Of the fourteen: Met 6 · Not met 5 · No completion criterion 3. 6 + 5 + 3 = 14.**

**The committee minuted one fact about the document itself.** **`EV-106` was filed on 2026-03-06, and there
is no record of anybody at Helix having opened it between then and 2027-03-30.** **`AS-08` — that the
commitments are not varied by Helix — was raised on 2026-05-29 and carried through six phases with nothing
resting on it; `AS-53` is its Phase 08 form and states what fails if it is wrong.**

## 5. Item three — the three with no completion criterion

**`RC-07`, `RC-14` and `RC-20`.** **`IS-51` raised**, owned by Rebekah Sandquist.

**The committee tested each against the same question: is there a state of the world in which this is true
and a state of the world in which it is false, and are the two distinguishable by looking?** **On all three
the answer is no.** `EV-809` retains the test and the three answers.

> **A commitment written so that nobody can say whether it was met is a commitment nobody can be held to.**

**The committee recorded three further facts about the three, because each of them removes an excuse.**
**All three were owned by a named individual. All three carried a date the response stated. All three
passed that date.** **The element that is absent is not the owner and it is not the date. It is the test.**

**And it recorded that the response's own review did not catch it.** `GOV-03 §3` names six reviewers, and
`GOV-03 §6` records that **two proposed commitments were removed in review because they named dates the
programme could not own.** **The review was looking at dates. Nobody was looking at tests.**

**4 + 1 = 5.**

**The committee did not extend, replace or propose a date for any of the five, and recorded that it had
been asked to.** **A date rewritten inside the company thirteen months later is not a date; it is a
description of the present tidied to look like a plan.** **The dates are the response's, they were stated
to the agency, and the register records what they were and whether they have passed.**

**Two of the five are the same failure in two places** — `RC-05` and `RC-19` are both commitments to put a
sentence into a procedure, and neither sentence exists; `DP-07` has been open since Phase 01 and has not
moved. **And `RC-06` cannot be met while `RC-05` is unmet**, because it is training in a procedure that has
not been revised — **a dependency invisible on the face of `EV-106`, which lists them as two rows with two
owners and two dates.**

## 7. Item five — who discharged the six that are met

**`DEC-807`, taken by Terrence Abbatiello at the sitting of 2027-03-31.** **`IS-52` raised**, owned by
Lydia Marchetti-Nwosu.

**Of the six commitments met, five were discharged by this programme and not by the commitment's named
owner — `RC-03`, `RC-04`, `RC-10`, `RC-11` and `RC-18` — and in each of the five the owner's own tracking
still shows the commitment open.** **The sixth, `RC-17`, was discharged by the individual the response
named, under change control, and is recorded complete in that individual's own tracking.**

> **A commitment tracked in one place and discharged in another is a commitment nobody is tracking.**

**The committee minuted, before it minuted anything else about this item:**

> **This is not a finding that anybody failed to do anything they were asked to do. The work described in
> five of the six commitments was done, and this programme's registers rest on it. It is a finding about
> two records that do not speak to each other.**

**The committee refused to correct the five trackers.** **Two records that disagree are a finding; two
records made to agree by editing one of them are a tidier finding about nothing**, and the editing would
have been done by the programme that benefited from it. **`DP-43` asks for something different and harder:
five statements, from five owners, of what their own tracking should now record — and a reconciliation that
edits neither record retrospectively.**

**`PR-60` opened**, against the reading that `IS-52` is an allegation against five named individuals.
**`ADR-0056` was not recorded at these sittings**; the rule it states was taken to the sitting of
2027-04-08 and recorded there at `DEC-815`, so that the finding was made before the rule that credits it
was written.

- **No date was extended, replaced or proposed for any of the five not met.**
- **No tracker was edited, corrected or reconciled**, and no instruction was issued to any owner to close a
  commitment.
- **The eight commitments attaching to the four out-of-scope observations were not assessed, and nothing

- **No CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan and no remediation cost.**
- **No representation to FDA, no compliance conclusion and no legal conclusion.**
