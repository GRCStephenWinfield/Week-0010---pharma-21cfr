# ADR-0057 — Phase 08 states no compliance conclusion, closes no observation and makes no representation to FDA

*The phase that looks most like an answer states the fewest conclusions of any phase in the programme, and says so at the same version as its findings.*

## Context

**This is the phase everybody has been waiting for, and that is the whole of the risk.**

Seven phases produced material that was visibly partial. **Phase 08's material looks finished**: three
conditions with a stated present position, a set of commitments read against their own words, a review
arrangement covering the estate, and the repository's oldest open issue closed. **Out of context each of
those reads as a conclusion, and one of them reads as the conclusion** — that the matters which produced a
Form FDA 483 have been dealt with.

**The boundary is therefore under a pressure it has not been under before, and from three directions at
once.**

**From the vocabulary.** A disposition sits where a closure would sit, an arrangement names frequencies and
dates the way a completed control would, and a closed issue occupies the place an answered question
occupies. **Each is a resemblance and not an identity**, and resemblances survive summarising better than
distinctions do.

**From the audience.** The Board audit committee, the Steering Committee and the accountable owners all
want a status. **The absence of one is read internally as an absence of progress**, which is exactly what
the phase's material contradicts, and the cheapest way to contradict it is a word this decision forbids.

**And from the programme's own achievement.** Thirteen months of work has produced determinations,
registers, positions and an estate-wide arrangement that did not exist in February 2026. **A phase that
reported all of that and then declined to say what it means will be read as false modesty**, and the people
who did the work know how much of it there was.

**The decision is taken by the Vice President, Quality**, who holds the quality unit, sponsored the
programme and released the response of 2026-03-06 — **that is, by the person with both the authority to
make a compliance statement and the most to gain from one.**

## Decision

**Phase 08 states no compliance conclusion, closes no observation and makes no representation to FDA. It
states the present position of three conditions, the position of the commitments the response made, and the
arrangement it has established, and it stops there.**

Six limbs.

**No compliance conclusion.** No document in this phase states, implies or forecasts that Helix, or any
system, or any record, is compliant or non-compliant with `21 CFR Part 11`, with Parts 210 and 211, or with
anything else. **There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate and no FDA-approved or
FDA-validated software**, and compliance is a property of a validated system in its intended use, under its
procedures, operated by trained people.

**No closure.** **No observation is closed or described as closed**, in any word that means closed —
`ADR-0051`.

**No representation.** **The only representation Helix has made about the observations is the written
response of 2026-03-06**, and this phase neither repeats, extends, amends nor supplements it. No document
in the phase is addressed to, drafted for or intended for transmission to any agency.

**No legal conclusion**, of any kind, and **no characterisation of the Ridgemont inspection, or of any
condition observed at it, as a violation of anything.**

**No CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan, no remediation cost, no sequence, no priority and no date**;
**no result of any periodic review**, because none has been performed; **and no forecast of what any future
inspection, internal audit or third-party audit will find.**

**And the boundary is published as a numbered chapter at the same version as the findings**, owned by
internal audit — `../08.12-what-this-does-not-establish.md`.

## Alternatives considered

**State a bounded compliance conclusion — not about Helix, not about Part 11 as a whole, but about the
specific paragraphs the three observations touch, on the specific systems concerned, at the vantage, with
the evidence named.**

**This is the strongest alternative in the phase, and it is strong precisely because it is disciplined
rather than expansive. It has to be put at full strength.**

**The programme has the material for it and no phase before this one did.** A control position was
determined for every paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10` on every system in scope, on a method authorised before
the first system was looked at. The gaps are enumerated. The audit trail configuration is characterised.
**A statement of the form *at this vantage, on this system, the control this paragraph describes is present
and a named procedure obliges somebody to maintain it* is a statement Helix can evidence line by line.**

**Firms are expected to reach conclusions about themselves.** `21 CFR 211.22` places the quality unit in a
position of judgement, not of description. **A quality unit that will only ever describe has not exercised
the responsibility the regulation gives it**, and a programme that produces determinations and refuses to
say what they add up to has done the analysis and declined the job.

**And the bounded form answers the usual objection.** The danger in a compliance statement is
over-generalisation — the leap from *these controls are present* to *we are compliant*. **A statement
bounded by paragraph, by system and by date does not make that leap**, and the programme has demonstrated
repeatedly that it can hold a boundary of exactly that kind.

**Refused, on three grounds.**

**The bounded statement is not the statement anybody will carry away.** `ADR-0035` established the
mechanism in Phase 05 and `ADR-0050` in Phase 07: **a qualifier does not survive a summary, a slide or a
report.** *Compliant with `21 CFR 11.10(d)` on `SYS-nnn` at 2027-04-09* becomes *compliant* by the second
retelling, and **the retelling that matters is the one that happens outside the room.** The bounds are the
part that gets dropped, always, because they are the part that makes the sentence long.

**Compliance is not the sum of the control positions, and treating it as one is a category error the
registers themselves refuse.** **Applicability is not integrity; a rating is not a validation status; a
requirement is not a control; a control position is not validation evidence.** A control being present is
not the same as the system being validated, the procedure being followed, the people being trained or the
records being reliable — **and no phase of this programme has established a validation outcome at all**:
no package executed, no protocol run, no test result. **A compliance conclusion drawn from control
positions would be asserting a totality out of one of its parts.**

**And the decider is the wrong party to make it, which is the reason that decided it.** The Vice President,
Quality sponsored the programme, holds the quality unit and released the response. **An assurance opinion
from the party that designed the thing being assured is not an opinion, it is a restatement** —
`ADR-0004`. **The conclusion this alternative proposes is exactly the conclusion the three-way separation
exists to prevent the programme from reaching about itself**, and the fact that it would be well evidenced
makes it more persuasive, not more proper. **The parties who could reach it are the internal audit of the
remediated programme and the third-party audit, neither of which has occurred, and neither of whose result
is anticipated anywhere.**

---

**Publish the boundary as an appendix, a preamble or a standing caveat rather than as a numbered chapter,
so that the phase reads as a set of findings rather than as a set of disclaimers.**

**A presentational argument with real force.** A phase whose twelfth chapter is a list of things it does
not say invites the reading that the programme is defensive, or that the findings are weaker than they
look. **A caveat at the front, or a standard paragraph at the foot of each register, would do the same work
with less noise** — and readers who need the boundary would find it exactly where they need it.

**Refused.** **A caveat is read once and a chapter is cited.** The boundary in this phase is not a
disclaimer about the quality of the work; **it is a finding about the limits of what the work can support**,
and findings go in chapters. A statement at the foot of a register travels nowhere; **a numbered chapter at
the same version as the findings, owned by a function that took no position in the phase, is an object
somebody can quote back.**

**And the precedent matters more than the presentation.** Every phase since Phase 02 has published this
chapter, and **the one phase that demoted it to a caveat would be the phase whose boundary was most under
pressure.** The pattern is the control.

---

**Say nothing at all about compliance and also nothing about the boundary — publish the findings and let
them speak, on the ground that a reader competent enough to matter will not over-read them.**

**The most confident option**, and it has the merit of not insulting the reader. Professional readers know
that a control position is not a compliance conclusion. **A phase that explains at length what it is not
saying can look as though it expects to be misread by people who would not misread it.**

**Refused, on two grounds.** **The reader who over-reads is not incompetent; they are downstream.** The
board paper, the supplier questionnaire, the customer answer and the induction slide are all produced by
people acting reasonably on a summary of a summary. **The material does not reach them with its context
attached, and the boundary exists for that journey rather than for the first reader.**

**And silence is not neutral in a document that looks finished.** **A phase that closes an eight-phase issue,
disposes of three conditions and establishes an estate-wide arrangement, and then says nothing about what
it has not established, has left the inference to the reader in a context that supplies one.**

## Consequences

**Accepted — the organisation ends the programme's penultimate phase without a statement of where it
stands.** The people who need one are no closer to holding one than they were, and **the phase does not
apologise for that and does not soften it.**

**Accepted — the phase will be read as more negative than the underlying position warrants**, because a
document that lists what it does not establish alongside what it does invites a reader to weigh the two
lists. `../08.12-what-this-does-not-establish.md §7` states positively what a reader may legitimately take,
once, for that reason.

**Accepted — the boundary will be breached outside this repository before it is breached inside it**, and
this decision cannot prevent that. **What it can do is make the breach checkable**, by ensuring that no
document here contains the sentence somebody will attribute to it.

**Accepted — the decider is the party with the most to gain from the refused conclusion**, and the record
says so on its face.

**Gained — every sentence in the phase is one Helix can defend from its own records**, at a stated vantage,
with named evidence, and none of it depends on a proposition about anybody else's judgement.

**Gained — the parties who could reach an assurance conclusion still can.** Nothing in this phase
pre-empts, anticipates or shapes the internal audit of the remediated programme or the third-party audit.
**A conclusion reached against an analysis its author could have argued with is a conclusion somebody has
actually thought about.**

## Refused

**This decision forbids the following in Phase 08 and binds every later phase that cites its registers.**

- **Stating, implying or forecasting that Helix, or any system, or any record, is compliant or
  non-compliant with anything** — including in a bounded, qualified or paragraph-specific form.
- **Describing any system or product as Part 11 certified, FDA-approved, FDA-validated or FDA-certified**,
  or asserting that outsourcing to a service provider transfers any responsibility.
- **Closing any observation or describing one as closed** — `ADR-0051`.
- **Making any representation to FDA**, or drafting any document in this phase for transmission to any
  agency.
- **Reaching a legal conclusion of any kind**, or characterising the Ridgemont inspection or any condition
  observed at it as a violation.
- **Stating a CAPA, a deviation, a remediation plan, a remediation cost, a sequence, a priority or a
  date.**
- **Reporting the result of any periodic review** — none has been performed, `IS-57`.
- **Forecasting what any future FDA inspection will find**, or anticipating the result of the internal
  audit of the remediated programme or of the third-party audit. **Neither has occurred.**
- **Re-determining any Phase 05 cell, re-scoring any Phase 03 pair, re-banding any Phase 04 obligation or
  re-deriving any Phase 07 period**, and **setting a retention period for any record type no predicate rule
  reaches** — `ADR-0044`, `ADR-0049`, `ADR-0050`.
- **Naming any candidate explanation of `IS-01`** — `ADR-0055`.
- **Demoting the boundary from a numbered chapter** in this phase or any later one.
