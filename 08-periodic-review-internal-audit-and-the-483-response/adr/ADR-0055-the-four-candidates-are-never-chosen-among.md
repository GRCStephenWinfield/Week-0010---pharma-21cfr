# ADR-0055 — The four candidate explanations are never chosen among, and `IS-01` closes on what the programme can establish rather than on what it cannot

*A condition is not a cause, and adding a column to a record adds nothing to an explanation.*

## Context

**`IS-01` has been open since 2026-05-19 and this is the phase Phase 01 named as the one that would return
to it.**

It asks why four internal audits between 2023-06 and 2025-09, which covered all three of the systems
between them with each falling inside the scope of at least two, raised no finding on the subject matter of
`OBS-2`, `OBS-4` or `OBS-6`. **Why is not established.** `01.09 §6` recorded four candidate explanations,
in no order of likelihood, and chose none. **`ADR-0007` published the audit history in full anyway, on the
express condition that it would not become a judgement on anybody**, and `ADR-0012` established in Phase 02
that *"[e]stablishing a candidate explanation's premise does not promote the candidate to the answer."*
Phases 03 to 07 each recorded that nothing in them discriminated between the four.

**What is different at this vantage is that the examination Phase 01 said would have to happen has
happened.** The six observable features `01.09 §5` recorded have been restated as conditions and tested,
audit by audit, against each audit's own scope statement, checklist and its provenance, sampling record,
test design and working papers. The result is at `../08.10-what-would-have-had-to-be-true.md` and
`../logs/audit-condition-register.md`.

**And that is exactly the circumstance in which the refusal becomes hardest to hold.** For seven phases the
answer to *why have you not chosen?* was that the evidence did not exist. **The evidence Phase 01 named now
exists**, the phase has been performed by the function whose own records they are, and the material is rich
enough that a choice could be dressed in it convincingly. **What has changed is the quantity of material
available to make a convenient choice look evidenced. What has not changed is that none of it is evidence
about any of the four.**

**There is a second pressure and it is organisational.** This is the last but one phase. **A question
carried open across eight phases and then closed without an answer looks, to a reader who has followed the
repository, like a failure** — and the person who has to publish it is the head of the function whose
records the issue is about, who raised it in the first place.

## Decision

**The four candidate explanations are never chosen among, and `IS-01` closes on what the programme can
establish rather than on what it cannot.**

Five limbs.

**`IS-01` closes**, at this decision, taken by the individual who raised it, ratified at the Steering
Committee sitting of 2027-04-08. **What it establishes: the six conditions, and that none of them obtained
in any of the four audits. What it does not establish, and never will: why.**

**The closing note records what closed it, its evidence, and what it never asked** — the discipline every
closure in this repository follows. **It names no candidate explanation.**

**No candidate is named, ranked, weighted, preferred, eliminated, reproduced, summarised, paraphrased or
alluded to** — by number or by content — **in this record, in any chapter of this phase, in any register,
diagram, template, governance record, decision entry or workbook.** They stand at `01.09 §6`, in the order
that chapter set.

**No document in this repository may be cited in support of any of the four**, and **the audit condition
register in particular may not be.**

**And the refusal is permanent. It is not handed to Phase 09.** Phase 09 does not inherit the question, is
not asked to answer it, and may not treat the closure as having selected among them, ranked them, or found
any of them more consistent with the evidence than another.

## Alternatives considered

**Choose the candidate the material now best supports, record it with its basis, and let the organisation
act on it.**

**This is the alternative the whole phase has been walking towards and it must be put at its strongest,
because the case for it is the best case against this decision and it is not a weak one.**

**A programme that identifies a failure of assurance and declines to say why has left the remediation
without a target.** That is the operational heart of it. Something did not work; the organisation would
like to fix it; **fixing a thing requires knowing what was wrong with it**, and a phase that says only
*here are six conditions, none of which obtained* has described a state and prescribed nothing. **The
organisation will fix something anyway** — it always does — and if the programme declines to say what,
somebody less well informed will decide.

**The material is no longer thin.** Four audits' working papers, read against a record determination, a
control position for every paragraph on every system, an auditable event characterisation, a retention
derivation and two access enquiries. **Phase 01 refused because it did not know what there was to find and
because the determination that would have anchored the audits did not exist. Both of those reasons have
expired.** It is not obvious why a refusal justified by an absence should survive the absence.

**And the recurrence argument is the sharpest form.** **An unexplained silence recurs.** If nobody knows
why four audits looked at three systems and raised nothing, there is nothing to stop a fifth doing the
same — and Helix has just committed to a periodic review programme across its estate, which is another
looking activity that could be scoped, checklisted and sampled in whatever way produced the silence the
first time. **Refusing to name a cause is, on this argument, refusing to protect the thing the phase just
built.**

**Refused, on four grounds, and the first is dispositive on its own.**

**A condition is not a cause, and the material establishes conditions.** `01.09 §5` recorded six features
of the audit records and said in terms that *"Nothing in this section is a cause."* This phase restated
those features as conditions and recorded that they did not obtain. **That is the same fact in a second
grammar. It adds a column to the record; it adds nothing to the explanation.** To move from *this condition
did not obtain* to *this is why the audits raised nothing* requires an account of how the absence operated
on those audits — **and `ADR-0012` set out what such an account needs and this phase establishes none of
it**: that the absence operated on these audits rather than being a condition that held everywhere and
therefore explains no difference between one case and another; that its presence would have changed what
the audits did, **which is a counterfactual and is the load-bearing one, and this phase examined no
counterfactual at all**; and that no other account explains the same thing at least as well.

**The four are not alternatives, so choosing among them is not a finding.** They can all be true at once,
in different proportions for different audits. **A selection among things that are not mutually exclusive
is an emphasis, not a conclusion** — and an emphasis chosen at the end of a programme, by the programme,
about the function that would have audited the programme, is the least defensible emphasis available.

**A cause chosen now would be chosen to be comfortable, and the material makes that easier rather than
harder.** `01.09 §7`'s third reason has not weakened; it has sharpened. **Every one of the four assigns the
problem somewhere convenient**, and there is now thirteen months of register material with which any of
them could be made to look evidenced. **The temptation is stronger and the inference is no sounder.**

**And the recurrence argument does not need a cause.** This is the concession, and it is the reason the
alternative loses without its best point being ignored. **What protects the next looking activity is not a
name for what went wrong last time; it is the six conditions themselves, stated as conditions — and where
a condition cannot yet be met, recorded as an open issue with a named owner.**
`../08.10-what-would-have-had-to-be-true.md` states which are which; this record states none of them. **They are
instruments, and an instrument works whichever of the four explanations is true, and works if all four are
true at once.** **The remediation does not require the diagnosis**, and this is the rare case where that is
so, precisely because the conditions were read off the record rather than derived from a theory about it.

---

**Do not choose one, but record which is most consistent with the evidence, or rank them, or say which the
material has made less likely.**

**A genuine middle position and the one most likely to be proposed by somebody who accepts the argument
above.** It concedes that a cause cannot be asserted while declining to waste the material. **A ranking with
its basis stated is more honest than a silence**, on this view, and it leaves the reader better informed
than a flat refusal does.

**Refused, and this is the alternative `ADR-0012` was written to defeat.** ***Unweighted*** **is an express
commitment and it was made because it is the half that erodes**: it means **no ranking, no probability, no
confidence level, no leading candidate, no most likely and no ordering**, in a chapter, a register, a
diagram, a decision record or any later phase. **A ranking is a choice with deniability**, and it behaves
in the organisation exactly as a choice does: *"A weighted candidate becomes a working assumption within
about a quarter"*, a working assumption becomes the premise of the remediation design, **and by the time
anybody re-opens the question the answer is load-bearing and nobody wants to.**

**The elimination form is refused for the same reason and one more.** **Narrowing the set is weighting the
set** — it promotes what remains — **and nothing in the material is inconsistent with any of them.** A
statement that the evidence has made one less likely would be a statement about a likelihood, in a
repository in which no entry carries a likelihood and none ever will.

---

**Leave `IS-01` open and hand the question to Phase 09, which is inspection readiness and close-out and
will have the third-party audit and the internal pass over the remediated programme to draw on.**

**The most cautious option and superficially the one most faithful to the refusal.** It changes nothing, it
asserts nothing, and it keeps the question visible in the register where somebody might yet answer it. **An
issue is supposed to be carried open with a reason, and *the evidence is not yet complete* has been the
reason for seven phases.**

**Refused, on three grounds.**

**The reason has expired and carrying it forward would misrepresent the register.** `IS-01` was carried
because the examination Phase 01 named did not exist. **It exists.** An issue carried open on a reason that
is no longer true is an issue nobody will look at, because its stated basis is checkable and false.

**Handing it forward is handing forward an instruction.** A question passed to a later phase with all the
material assembled and none of the answer reached **is a question that reads as an assignment.** Phase 09
would inherit it in a phase about inspection readiness, at close-out, with a programme ending — **which is
the worst possible circumstance in which to be under pressure to produce a cause**, and the pressure would
be inherited along with the question.

**And neither assurance activity is a route to it.** The internal pass over the remediated programme and
the third-party audit both examine the remediation; **neither examines audits performed between 2023 and
2025, and this repository anticipates the result of neither.** There is no evidence route in Phase 09 that
does not exist here.

**What is conceded is that closing an issue can look like answering a question**, and the remedy is in the
closing note: **it records what closed it, its evidence and what it never asked**, and the chapter states in
terms that the closure is on what can be established and expressly not on why.

## Consequences

**Accepted — the repository's oldest question closes without an answer, and says so.** A reader who has
followed it from Phase 01 will reach the end of the chapter and find that nobody ever found out. **That is
the honest position and the phase does not dress it.**

**Accepted — the closure will be read by somebody as a selection.** Any statement about audits invites the
inference that the person making it has a view. **The chapter, the closing note, the register and this
record each say in terms that no candidate is chosen, and each says it because a single unqualified
sentence somewhere else would be enough to undo all four.**

**Accepted — the refusal now has no register entry keeping it honest.** For seven phases `IS-01` sat open
in the RAID log and every phase had to state its position on it. **After closure there is no such
mechanism**, which is why the permanence is written into the decision, the closing note and the transition
chapter rather than left to habit.

**Accepted — the audit function has published an examination of its own records that establishes nothing
flattering and reaches no conclusion.** `ADR-0007`'s bargain, honoured a second time: **an organisation
that publishes the audits that raised nothing invites the reading that its assurance function does not
work, and the alternative invites the reading that nobody looked.**

**Gained — the remediation does not depend on a guess.** The six conditions are instruments and they work
whichever explanation is true. **Nothing Helix does next rests on an attribution of cause it could not
defend.**

**Gained — a closure somebody can check.** The closing note names its evidence, and the register the
closure rests on is published in full and may be disagreed with row by row.

## Refused

**This decision forbids the following in Phase 08 and binds every later phase, every close-out document and
every third party working from this repository.**

- **Naming, numbering, quoting, reproducing, summarising, paraphrasing or alluding to any of the four
  candidate explanations**, in any artefact of this phase or any later one.
- **Ranking, weighting, ordering, preferring, eliminating or attaching a likelihood, probability or
  confidence to any of them.**
- **Describing any of them as the leading candidate, the most likely, the most consistent with the
  evidence, or as having been made more or less likely by anything.**
- **Citing any document in this repository in support of any of them** — **including
  `../logs/audit-condition-register.md`, `../08.10-what-would-have-had-to-be-true.md`, any diagram, any
  governance record and any working paper of this phase.**
- **Stating a cause for what any of the four audits raised or did not raise.**
- **Treating `IS-01`'s closure as an answer**, as a selection, or as reopenable.
- **Handing the question to Phase 09**, or to any later reader, as work.
- **Characterising any of the four audits as adequate or inadequate**, or assessing any individual's work.
  **No individual auditor is named in this repository.**
- **Re-opening, re-closing, re-characterising or re-scoping any `IA-YYYY-MM/Fn`.**
- **Reading `IS-58` as `IS-01` in another form**, or reaching any candidate explanation through it.
