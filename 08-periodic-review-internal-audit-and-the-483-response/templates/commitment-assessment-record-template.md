## 1. The principle this form rests on

**`ADR-0052`, `DEC-805`:**

> **The commitments are assessed against their own words, and a commitment with no completion criterion is
> recorded as having none rather than judged met or unmet. A commitment written so that nobody can say
> whether it was met is a commitment nobody can be held to.**

**And `ADR-0056`, `DEC-815`:**

> **A remediation the programme performed is credited to the programme and not to the commitment's named
> owner, and the difference is recorded. A commitment tracked in one place and discharged in another is a
> commitment nobody is tracking.**

**Four things follow, and they govern the order of the sections below.**

**A completion criterion is a state of the world, named in the commitment's own words, that would satisfy
it** — observable in principle by somebody who was not party to the commitment, **and with a state of the
world in which it is false.** A date is not a criterion. An owner is not a criterion.

**The words assessed are the words the response used** — not the covering text, not the programme's
records, not what the drafters would say if asked now.

**`§3` is completed before `§5`.** **A commitment read backwards from what happened is a description of
what happened**, and the elasticity that is the whole finding is invisible from that direction.

**And no criterion is supplied now.** Supplying one would assess Helix against a commitment Helix never
filed, **and the firm would be marking its own homework twice** — setting the question, then writing the
mark scheme after the work, knowing the answer.

**Four values and no fifth.** ***No completion criterion*** **is a complete position, not an empty one**:
it says something true and checkable about the commitment — that its own words name no state of the world
that would satisfy it — **and it says nothing whatever about whether the work was done.** It is not
recorded as *Met*, which would credit the company with a change it cannot demonstrate; **and it is not
recorded as *Not met*, which would manufacture a failure out of a drafting defect.** **Unassessed is not
adverse.**

whose owner believes it is open and which the register reports as met is a commitment about which the
organisation holds two incompatible beliefs** — and the informal record is the one the owner actually works
from.

## 11. Completion rules

| Rule | Statement |
|---|---|
| **One commitment, one record** | Two commitments are two records, whatever their subject matter has in common |
| **`§3` before `§5`, and both dated** | **A commitment read backwards from what happened is a description of what happened** |
| **`§2`'s second row is a transcription** | Never a summary, never a paraphrase, never a tidied version |
| **No criterion is ever supplied** | **None may be written, proposed, inferred or implied**, in any field, note or example — `ADR-0052` |
| **Four positions and no fifth** | **No qualifier, no hedge.** *Substantially met* and *largely complete* are not available |
| **Three separate fields at `§7`** | Named owner, discharging party, owner's tracking. **None may be merged** — `ADR-0056` |
| **No tracker is edited** | **Not on this form, not anywhere.** The route is the owner's own act, with its own record — `DP-43` |
| **A date is recorded as past and never replaced** | **No date is extended, replaced or proposed** |
| **Out-of-scope commitments are recorded, not omitted** | **An unexplained exclusion is indistinguishable from an oversight** — `ADR-0014` |
| **Two named individuals** | Statement and check at `§6`, compilation and approval at `§9` — never a team, `ADR-0008` |
| **Nothing is graded** | **No commitment, owner or arrangement is described as adequate, sufficient, robust, effective or mature** |
| **A blank is a blank** | **A response column completed for the sake of completion is worse than an empty one** |
| **Change** | A completed record is amended under change control with a `CR-nnn` record naming the trigger, **and the prior state is retained** |
