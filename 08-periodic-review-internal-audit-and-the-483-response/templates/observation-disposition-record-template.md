## 1. What this form rests on

**FDA's own statement of what a Form FDA 483 is, verbatim:**

> *"The FDA Form 483 does not constitute a final Agency determination of whether any condition is in
> violation of the FD&C Act or any of its relevant regulations."*

**And the principle this form exists to carry** — `ADR-0051`, `DEC-803`:

> **An observation is disposed of by stating the condition's present position, never by stating that it is
> closed. Closure is a representation about the state of a matter between the firm and the agency, and this
> programme makes none.**

**Three things follow, and they govern every field below.**

**The subject of this record is a condition, not an observation.** The observation is the investigator's
record of what was seen, in the 483's own numbering, and **this form does not amend it, renumber it, retire
it or give it a status** — `ADR-0006`. What it records is what the state of affairs described in it
presently looks like on Helix's own estate.

**It speaks at a vantage and names its evidence.** A condition obtains or does not obtain on a particular
day. **A position with no date has no expiry and therefore no way of being wrong**, and a position that
names no evidence is an opinion.

**And it uses three verbs and no others.** ***Confirmed*** — the condition is presently established to
obtain. ***Corrected*** — it no longer obtains. ***Not corrected*** — it presently obtains and the position
is stated as it is. **No qualifier attaches to any of them**, because a qualifier on one of these verbs is
a score with the scoring prohibition evaded.

## 11. Completion rules

| Rule | Statement |
|---|---|
| **One observation, one record** | Two observations are two records, whatever their subject matter has in common |
| **`§4`, `§5`, `§7` and `§8` cannot be blank** | Without `§4` the verb has nothing behind it; without `§7` the record implies that nothing is owed; **without `§8` the record is a closure in everything but name** |
| **Three verbs and no others** | **No qualifier, no adverb, no hedge.** *Substantially corrected* and *largely complete* are not available |
| **No closure field, ever** | **None appears and none may be added**, under any label — `ADR-0051` |
| **A vantage on every position** | **A position with no date has no way of being wrong** |
| **Evidence named, always** | **"Believed complete" is not a closure condition**, and it is not a position either |
| **Two named individuals** | Statement and check at `§5`, compilation and approval at `§9` — never a team, `ADR-0008` |
| **The observation is untouched** | Not renumbered, not regrouped, not amended, not retired, not given a status — `ADR-0006` |
| **Nothing is graded** | **No condition, system, remediation or arrangement is described as adequate, sufficient, robust, effective or mature** anywhere on this form |
| **A blank is a blank** | **A response column completed for the sake of completion is worse than an empty one, because it stops anybody looking** |
| **No inference across records** | **A position on one observation is no evidence about another.** Three is not a population |
| **Change** | A completed record is amended under change control with a `CR-nnn` record naming the trigger, **and the prior state is retained** |
