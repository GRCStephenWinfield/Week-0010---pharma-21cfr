## 1. The position

**`OBS-2` — audit trails on the chromatography data system were not reviewed as part of laboratory record
review.**

> **The condition the observation records is confirmed. The remediation is defined. It is not complete.**

**The observation is not closed, and nothing in this chapter, this phase or this repository describes it as
closed.** `08.01-what-a-disposition-is.md` owns the argument and it is not restated here: **an observation
is disposed of by stating the condition's present position, never by stating that it is closed, because
closure is a representation to FDA and this programme makes none** — `ADR-0051`, `DEC-803`.

`ADR-0006` holds. **The observation keeps the 483's own numbering, is not renumbered, is not regrouped and
is not converted into a deviation or a finding.** `OBS-2` is the 483's identifier, `IA-YYYY-MM/Fn` is an
internal audit finding and `DEV-nnn` is a Helix quality record, and the three are never swapped.

## 2. What the condition was, and what confirms it

**The observation records that laboratory records generated on the chromatography data system were reviewed
and approved without the associated audit trail forming part of that review.** The reviewing analyst
examined the result, the chromatogram as reported and the calculation; **the record of who changed what,
and when, was not opened.**

**The regulatory anchor is the predicate rule and not Part 11.** `21 CFR 11.10(e)` requires audit trails to
be generated, requires that record changes not obscure previously recorded information, and requires the
documentation to be retained and available for agency review and copying; **it imposes no internal review
obligation at all.** The review obligation for laboratory records is `21 CFR 211.194(a)(8)`, which requires *"[t]he
initials or signature of a second person showing that the original records have been reviewed for accuracy,
completeness, and compliance with established standards"* — **three tests and not one** — and **FDA's
December 2018 drug CGMP data integrity guidance treats the audit trail as part of the record being
reviewed.** That guidance is nonbinding, it represents FDA's current thinking, and it is never described
here as a requirement.

**Two published facts confirm the condition, and neither of them is new work.**

**First, the procedure.** The laboratory record review procedure was read on 2027-03-26 against the sentence
`RC-05` committed Helix to inserting — `EV-807`. **It does not say whether the audit trail associated with
a result forms part of the original record reviewed, and it does not say what the reviewer must examine.**
**`IS-06` was raised on 2026-05-15 on exactly that absence and it closes in this phase** — not because the
sentence now exists, but because the question is answered in both directions and the answer is recorded
with what remains owed. `IS-24` carries the wider class: **twelve obligations land in a procedure that
exists and does not require the act.**

**Second, the subject matter.** Phase 06 characterised the two chromatography instances at a depth
available for no other system in the estate. **`SYS-023` at Ridgemont and `SYS-024` at Cary, thirty-four
auditable event classes, of which eleven record an act capable of changing a reportable result.** The
register at `../06-the-chromatography-data-system/logs/auditable-event-register.md` is authoritative and
this chapter restates none of its counts beyond the two it needs. **What Phase 06 established, and what
this chapter takes from it, is that a reviewer who did open the audit trail would not see everything that
can change a number** — `IS-36`, `IS-38`.

## 3. What "remediation defined" means, and what it does not

**Defined is a low bar and the chapter says so.** It means that the acts required are identified, that each
has a named individual against it, and that each is carried by an entry somebody owns. **It does not mean
scheduled, resourced, started or evidenced**, and this chapter states no plan, no sequence, no priority, no
date and no cost.

**What remains owed under `OBS-2`, and against which existing entry.**

| What is owed | Whom it is owed by | Where it is carried | Position at this vantage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A sentence in the laboratory record review procedure** stating whether the audit trail associated with a result forms part of the original record reviewed, and what the reviewer must examine | **Terrence Abbatiello** | **`RC-05`** — the commitment the response made, dated 2026-09-30 · `IS-06`, `IS-24`, `DP-07` | **Not met, and past the date the response gave** |
| **Training of every analyst and every second-person reviewer in the revised procedure** | **Dr Sunita Raghunathan** | **`RC-06`** — dated 2026-10-30 | **Not met, and past the date the response gave.** It cannot be met while `RC-05` is unmet: **it is training in a procedure that has not been revised** |
| **A configuration decision, and a sentence requiring it**, for the event classes recorded on neither instance and for those on which the two instances differ | **Terrence Abbatiello** | `DP-35` · `IS-36`, `IS-37` | **Open. Nothing is agreed**, and no configuration was changed in this phase |
| **A vendor product route to record two event classes at all**, and what a compensating control would have to do until there is one | **Joachim Reuter** | **`DP-33`** · `IS-36` | **Open, and nothing is agreed** — and it is the one that cannot be discharged by Helix at all. See `§4` |

**Two of the response's own commitments on this observation are met and both were discharged by the
programme rather than by the individuals the response named** — `RC-03`, the record-level determination,
and `RC-04`, the audit trail characterisation. **In each, the named owner's own tracking still shows the
commitment open** — `IS-52`, and `08.04-who-actually-did-it.md` owns it.

**And one commitment on this observation is neither met nor unmet.** `RC-07` — *"Helix will continue to
reinforce the importance of data integrity in the quality control laboratories."* — **carries no completion
criterion and is recorded as having none rather than judged** — `IS-51`, `ADR-0052`.

## 4. The part that cannot be completed on this product version

**Of the thirty-four auditable event classes, six are recorded on neither instance. Two of the six cannot
be enabled by any setting available to Helix on the current product version, and both of those two record
an act capable of changing a reportable result.**

| Class | What it records | Configurable on this product version | Can change a reportable result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`AE-08`** | An **integration parameter change applied to a single injection at review time**, after the sequence has been processed — which parameter, on which injection, from what value to what value, under which account and at what time | **No** | **Yes** |
| **`AE-16`** | A **system suitability evaluation overridden and the sequence carried forward notwithstanding** — that the suitability outcome was set aside, under which account, and against which criterion | **No** | **Yes** |

**`AE-08` is the class that sits closest to the number that is reported.** It records a change to
integration parameters applied to one injection at the moment of review — **which is the act a
second-person review of a chromatographic result exists to see.** It is not recorded on either instance,
and no setting available to Helix would record it.

> **A reviewer who opened the audit trail on either chromatography instance today, having read a procedure
> that told them exactly what to look for, would still not see either of these two acts — because neither
> is written.**

**That is why the remediation of `OBS-2` cannot be completed on the current product version, and why the
sentence has to be put that way round.** **It is a fact about a product. It is not a discharge of an
obligation, it is not a mitigating circumstance, and it does not reduce what is owed.** The order of this
chapter is deliberate: **the condition is confirmed and the remediation incomplete, and only then is the
product limitation stated.** `PR-43` carries the risk that it is read the other way — that the three
result-changing classes recorded on neither instance become an excuse and `OBS-2` is reported as reduced —
and it has been open since Phase 06. **Three of the eleven classes that can change a reportable result are
recorded on neither instance; six classes in all are recorded on neither; and two of the three are the two
that cannot be enabled. The three populations are nested and are never reported as one** —
`../06-the-chromatography-data-system/logs/auditable-event-register.md §4.3`–`§4.4`.

**Two further cautions belong on the same page.**

**The limitation rests on a vendor statement Helix has not tested.** **`AS-43` — that the vendor's
statement that two classes cannot be enabled describes the product version — is open, untested, and this
is the phase in which it carries more weight than it has ever carried.** `AS-17`, the assumption that
vendors supply documentation sufficient to describe the seven newly in-scope record types, **was tested in
Phase 03 and failed.** That is the precedent `AS-43` works against.

**And a product route is not the whole of what `DP-33` asks for.** It asks for the route **and** for what a
compensating control would have to do until there is one. **Nothing is agreed on either limb**, and
**neither the route nor the compensating control is designed, proposed or described anywhere in this
phase.**

## 5. What this chapter does not establish

- **It does not close `OBS-2` and does not describe it as closed.** **No observation is closed by this
  programme and no representation is made to FDA.**
- **It states no CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan, no remediation sequence, no date and no cost.**
  The table at `§3` records what is owed and against which existing entry; **it is not a plan and nothing
  in it was created by this phase.**
- **It re-determines no Phase 05 cell and re-reads no Phase 06 configuration.** Nothing in this phase
  touched either instance; **`AE-08` and `AE-16` are recorded exactly as
  `../06-the-chromatography-data-system/logs/auditable-event-register.md §4.4` published them** on
  2027-02-26.
- **It sets no audit trail review frequency and attributes none to any source.** **No primary source names
  a stand-alone periodic one**; `21 CFR Part 11` names none; **EU GMP Annex 11 clause 9's expectation that
  audit trails be *"…regularly reviewed"* is a *should* in a guideline that is not FDA's, is labelled
  EU-derived, and names no interval either.** `08.08-nothing-requires-a-periodic-review.md` owns the
  distinction. **`IS-04` closes in this phase without a frequency being set.**
- **It characterises no reintegration**, in either direction — `ADR-0043`, `PR-45`.
- **It reaches no compliance conclusion and no legal conclusion, and it forecasts nothing about any future
  inspection.**
