## 1. The position

**`OBS-4` — a shared user account was in use on a packaging line system.**

> **The condition the observation records is confirmed. It is not corrected.**

**The observation is not closed and nothing here describes it as closed** — `ADR-0051`, `DEC-803`, and
`08.01-what-a-disposition-is.md` owns the argument. `ADR-0006` holds: the observation keeps the 483's own
numbering and is not renumbered, regrouped or converted into a deviation or a finding.

**This is the hardest sentence in the phase and it was derivable from a register this programme published
itself, more than two months before anybody wrote it down.**

## 2. What the condition was

**The observation records that a user account shared by more than one operator was in use on a packaging
line system, with the consequence that entries made through it could not be attributed to a named
individual.**

**Attribution is a CGMP requirement before it is a Part 11 requirement.** **`21 CFR 211.188(b)(11)`**
requires *"[i]dentification of the persons performing and directly supervising or checking each significant
step in the operation, or if a significant step in the operation is performed by automated equipment under
§ 211.68, the identification of the person checking the significant step performed by the automated
equipment."* `21 CFR 11.10(d)` requires limiting system access to authorised individuals;
`21 CFR 11.10(g)` requires authority checks over five acts, including using the system, altering a record
and performing the operation at hand; and
**`21 CFR 211.194(a)(7)` and `(a)(8)` carry the laboratory-side equivalents.** FDA's position on shared
credentials in the December 2018 drug CGMP data integrity guidance is grounded in Parts 211 and 212, and
that guidance is nonbinding and is never described here as a requirement.

## 3. The nine

**At the Phase 05 vantage of 2027-01-29, a shared credential through which operating entries are made was
in use on nine of the sixty-one systems.**

| Gap | Paragraph | Systems | The cause, as `CG` records it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`CG-11`** | `21 CFR 11.10(d)` | **7** — `SYS-003`, `SYS-004`, `SYS-005`, `SYS-006`, `SYS-007`, `SYS-009`, `SYS-010` | **A shared local operator account is in use** on the blister, bottling and cartoning line systems and on the two vision inspection systems |
| **`CG-13`** | `21 CFR 11.10(d)` | **2** — `SYS-031`, `SYS-032` | **The workstation operating-system logon is the only access control on the instrument application, and it is shared within the laboratory** |

**7 + 2 = 9, and the packaging line system FDA observed is among the seven.**

**Two other shared-credential gaps exist and are deliberately not counted here, and the reason is stated so
that the nine can be checked rather than taken.** **`CG-12`** — `21 CFR 11.10(d)` on `SYS-051`, `SYS-052`
and `SYS-055` — records **one administrative account held in common by the facilities and utilities
function and by its contractor**. **That is an administrative credential and not the account through which
a record of a regulated operation is made**, and folding it into `OBS-4`'s class would widen the
observation beyond its own subject matter. **`CG-21`** reaches `21 CFR 11.10(g)`, which is authority
checks and a different paragraph. **Both remain open gaps in their own right at
`../05-access-audit-trails-and-electronic-signatures/logs/control-gap-register.md`, and neither is
disposed of, reduced or reported on by this chapter.**

## 4. Three hundred and fifty days

**The Form FDA 483 was issued on 2026-02-13. The Phase 05 vantage is 2027-01-29. The interval between the
two dates is three hundred and fifty days.** This repository states an interval from the issue of the 483
as the difference between the dates — the convention `01.02 §3` uses for the seventeen days to kickoff and
the twenty-one to the response, and `EV-818` records it on the face of the working paper. **A duration of
an activity is counted inclusive of both endpoints, and this is not one.**

**On the last of those days, the shared local operator account was still in use on the packaging line
system FDA observed.**

**Phase 05 published that and drew no disposition from it, because disposition is this phase's.** The
control gap register recorded, in its own `§5`, that **`CG-11`'s subject matter is the subject matter of
`OBS-4`**, that the link was recorded and that no disposition was drawn from it. **The fact has therefore
been in the repository, in a controlled register, since 2027-01-29.** **What this chapter adds is the
sentence.**

**And Helix's own commitment names the same act and the same date.** **`RC-12`** — *"Helix will withdraw
the shared local operator account from the packaging line systems and will issue a named individual account
to every operator who uses them."* — **was owned by Amara Sissoko and carried the date 2026-08-31. It is
Not met, and 2026-08-31 is one hundred and fifty-one days before the Phase 05 vantage.** **`RC-13`** — the
commitment to record the identity of the person performing and the person checking each significant step on
a packaging line — **states no date at all, and is Not met.**

**Two commitments on this observation are met, and both were discharged by the programme rather than by the
individual the response named.** `RC-10`, the identification of every system on which an account is shared,
was satisfied by Phase 05's control gap register; `RC-11`, the record of whether access is limited and an
authority check operates on each packaging line system, was satisfied by Phase 05's control position
register. **In each, the named owner's own tracking still shows the commitment open** — `IS-52`.
**Identifying nine systems is not correcting nine systems**, and the two commitments that were satisfied
are both commitments to find out.

**`RC-14`** — *"Helix will reinforce with packaging operators the importance of individual accountability
for entries made on line systems."* — **carries no completion criterion and is recorded as having none
rather than judged** — `IS-51`.

## 5. The distinction Phase 02 drew, carried and not lost

**A shared account does not fail `21 CFR 11.10(d)` on its face.** `02.07 §6` states the distinction it
rests on — *"[a] shared account does not make a system open"*, and what it defeats is attribution — and
Phase 05's register repeats it on every row: **access to these systems is still limited to a group of authorised
operators**, and a shared account does not make a system open — **the open-and-closed test at
`21 CFR 11.3(b)(4)` turns on who controls access, and Helix controls it.** All sixty-one systems are closed
systems and none of them stops being one because a credential is shared.

**What a shared account defeats is attribution** — the ability to say which individual did what — **and
attribution arises from different text.** **`21 CFR 211.188(b)(11)`** requires *"[i]dentification of the
persons performing and directly supervising or checking each significant step in the operation, or if a
significant step in the operation is performed by automated equipment under § 211.68, the identification of
the person checking the significant step performed by the automated equipment."* **The second limb reaches
the packaging line systems directly: they are automated equipment under `21 CFR 211.68`, and the
identification the rule requires of them is of the person checking the step the equipment performed — which
is precisely what a shared operator account defeats.** `RC-13`, which the response worded as an undertaking
that the identity of the person performing and the person checking each significant step on a packaging
line is recorded, is a commitment to that limb, and `logs/commitment-register.md` records it Not met with
no date stated. **`21 CFR 211.194(a)(7)`** carries the laboratory-side requirement on the analyst who
performed the test. **Those are the provisions the nine systems reach, and they are predicate rules:
enforcement discretion has never had anything to say about either of them.**

**Phase 05's own `21 CFR 11.10(d)` determination on the nine rests on something narrower again**, and it is
worth stating exactly because it is easy to overstate. **The determination is Not met because the
credential is held by persons whose authorisation is not individually recorded, so the limiting that `(d)`
requires cannot be demonstrated for any individual.** **That is a determination about what can be
demonstrated, not a finding that an unauthorised person had access**, and nothing in this chapter says or
implies otherwise. **No cell of the control position register is re-determined here** — `ADR-0049`.

**And `21 CFR 11.10(d)` is not one of the paragraphs FDA said it would exercise enforcement discretion
over.** `IS-33` records that eighty-nine of the estate's failures sit inside the discretion set; **none of
the nine positions discussed here is among them, and this chapter states the position without reference to
discretion at all.** **Enforcement discretion is FDA's stated intention about what it will enforce. It is
not an exemption, not a safe harbour, and it confers no rights.**

## 6. The category the guidance describes as acceptable, and whether any of the nine is in it

**FDA's December 2018 drug CGMP data integrity guidance states that** *"[s]hared, read-only user accounts
that do not allow the user to modify data or settings are acceptable for viewing data, but they do not
conform with the part 211 and 212 requirements for actions, such as second person review, to be
attributable to a specific individual."* **Both limbs are carried: the account is acceptable for viewing
and is not acceptable wherever an action must be attributable.** **That is a nonbinding recommendation and
is not described here as a requirement.** **The second limb is FDA's own published support for the position
this chapter takes**: a shared credential defeats attribution, and FDA says in terms that such an account
does not conform with the Part 211 attribution requirement for actions such as second person review. It is
easy to overstate the rule in the other direction, and Phase 01 recorded the nuance for that reason.
Phase 05's control gap register recorded that whether any account at Helix falls into that category
*"…is Phase 08's and is not answered here or implied here."*

**It is answered here. On none of the nine.**

**Each of the nine credentials is an operating account through which entries are made** — a line operator
starting, stopping, adjusting and confirming on the packaging line systems and the vision inspection
systems; an analyst acquiring and processing on the FTIR spectrometer and the Karl Fischer titrator data
systems. **None of the nine is read-only, and on none of them is the user unable to modify data or
settings.** `EV-819` records the enquiry and retains it, so that the answer can be tested as a search
rather than taken as a conclusion.

**That is the whole of the finding on this point, and it is stated as a negative because that is what it
is.** **No conclusion follows from it about any other account in the estate**, and no account outside the
nine was examined.

## 7. What this chapter does not establish

- **It does not close `OBS-4`, does not describe it as closed, and makes no representation to FDA.**
- **It states no CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan, no remediation cost, no date and no sequence.**
  **`RC-12` is not rewritten and no new date is given to it**, and `DP-20` — account administration records
  sufficient to say which named individual held the credential in use — **is carried open exactly as it has
  been since 2026-09.**
- **It re-determines no Phase 05 cell.** The nine `21 CFR 11.10(d)` positions stand exactly as published on
  2027-01-29 — `ADR-0049` — and **nothing in this phase read a system, opened an account list or looked at
  a line.** **`AS-55` records what that means: the position is Phase 05's as published, and if anything on
  any of the nine has changed since, three hundred and fifty days is a floor and not a figure.**
- **It says nothing about any individual's conduct or performance**, and no document in this repository
  does. **The nine rows are rows about accounts.**
- **It does not report the condition as an outcome of anything.** **There has been no further inspection,
  there is no inspection outcome of any kind, and no forecast of what any future inspection will find
  appears in this repository.**
- **It reaches no compliance conclusion and no legal conclusion** — `ADR-0057`.
