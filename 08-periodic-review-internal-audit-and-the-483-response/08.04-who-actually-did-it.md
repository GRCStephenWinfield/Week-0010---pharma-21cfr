## 1. What this chapter is not, said before it is said

**This chapter is not an allegation that anybody acted improperly, failed to act, or was slow.** Nobody
named in it failed to do anything they were asked to do. **The work described in five of the six
commitments was done, and it was done well enough that this programme's registers rest on it.**

**It is a claim about two records that do not speak to each other**, and the reason it is published is that
nothing else in the repository would show it. **`ADR-0056`, `DEC-815`, taken by the Programme Manager on
2027-04-08**, records the rule the register applies: a remediation the programme performed is credited to
the programme and not to the commitment's owner, and the difference is recorded rather than smoothed.
`08.02-reading-a-commitment-against-its-own-words.md` owns the argument for treating ownership and
discharge as two facts and this chapter does not re-argue it.

**No observation is closed here, none is described as closed, and no representation is made to FDA.**

## 3. Why this is a finding and not an administrative untidiness

**It would be easy to read the five rows as a filing problem.** Somebody should update a spreadsheet; the
work is done either way; the company is in the same position whether the tracker says so or not.

**That reading is wrong, and it is wrong in a way that is worth setting out, because the tracker is not a
record of the past. It is the instrument by which the future of the commitment is managed.**

**A commitment shown as open in its owner's tracking is a commitment that will appear on that owner's next
report, will be chased, and may be worked on again.** A commitment shown as discharged in a programme
register is a commitment nobody will chase, because the register is not a tracker and does not chase
anything. **Between the two records there is no reader.** Nobody at Helix holds both, because the trackers
sit inside five functions and the registers sit inside the programme, and the programme has never reported
against `EV-106`.

> **A commitment tracked in one place and discharged in another is a commitment nobody is tracking.**

**And the failure mode runs in the direction that is hardest to see.** The risk here is not that the work
will be repeated; repetition is wasteful and visible. **The risk is that the programme ends on 2027-04-30,
its registers become documents rather than an activity, and five commitments to FDA remain open in five
trackers whose owners believe the work is still to be done — with the evidence that it was done sitting in
a register nobody in those five functions maintains.** **`IS-52`**, raised on 2027-03-31, owned by Lydia
Marchetti-Nwosu.

**The one row that behaves properly is `RC-17`, and it is worth saying why.** It was performed by the
individual the response named, under a change control record in the quality system, and it is recorded as
complete in that individual's own tracking. **The two records agree because the same person made both of
them.** The other five were satisfied by an act performed somewhere else entirely, and **an act performed
somewhere else does not write itself into a tracker.**

## 4. What the programme refused to do about it

**Three formulations were available and two were refused** — `ADR-0056`, `EV-812`.

| Considered | Position |
|---|---|
| *Record the five as discharged by their named owners, on the ground that the work is Helix's work and the internal division of labour is nobody else's business* | **Refused.** The five owners' own trackers show the five open. **A register reporting them discharged by their owners would be the only document at Helix asserting something those owners' own records deny**, and it would have made a real disagreement invisible by describing it away |
| *Correct the five trackers so that the two records agree* | **Refused, and it is the one that would have taken an afternoon.** **Two records that disagree are a finding; two records made to agree by editing one of them are a tidier finding about nothing.** Editing the trackers would have destroyed the only evidence that the disagreement existed, and would have done so at the hand of the programme that benefited from it |
| **Record both, in two columns, credit the act to whoever performed it, and edit neither record** | **Adopted** — `ADR-0056`, `DEC-815`, and `logs/commitment-register.md` carries a Discharged-by column and an Owner's-own-tracking column so that neither can stand for the other |

**What the programme did not do instead is also recorded.** **It did not write to the five owners
instructing them to close the five commitments.** `DP-43` asks for something different and harder: **a
statement, from each of the five, of what their own tracking should now record — five statements, not one
correction** — and a reconciliation that edits neither record retrospectively. **`DP-43` is open and
nothing is reconciled.**

## 5. The shape this has appeared in before

**This is the fourth time in the repository that a thing existed in one place and was managed in another,
and the pattern is worth naming because it is now clearly a pattern rather than four accidents.**

`IS-03` records that the record-type register carries the predicate rule and the systems register does not.
`IS-29` records that two hundred and eighteen controls are present and no procedure requires any of them to
stay there. `IS-24` records that twelve obligations land in a procedure that exists and does not require
the act. **`IS-52` records that five commitments are discharged in a register and open in a tracker.**

**In every one of the four, both records are individually correct.** Nobody wrote anything false. **What is
missing in each case is the join** — the person, or the procedure, or the report, that would put the two
side by side and notice. **`IS-58` is the same shape at the largest scale the repository contains**, and
this chapter is one of the four places it was visible before anybody added them up.

## 6. What this chapter does not establish

- **It does not establish that any of the five commitments was performed late, badly, or at all by the
  owner named.** It establishes who performed the act that satisfied it and what each owner's own record
  says.
- **It does not assess any individual's work**, and no document in this repository does — `01.07`, and the
  standing refusal since Phase 01.
- **It does not close any commitment, any observation or any issue.** `RC-17` is Met and `RC-03`, `RC-04`,
  `RC-10`, `RC-11` and `RC-18` are Met; **none of the six is closed, because a commitment position is not a
  closure and this programme closes nothing.**
- **It states no CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan and no remediation cost.**
- **It makes no representation to FDA**, and nothing here has been communicated to any agency.
