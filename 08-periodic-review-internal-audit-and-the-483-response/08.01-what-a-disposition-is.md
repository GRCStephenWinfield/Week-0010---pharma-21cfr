*A firm may state what it has found to be so about its own estate; it may not state that a matter between itself and an agency is finished.*

---

## 1. One idea

**An observation is disposed of by stating the condition's present position, never by stating that it is
closed.**

That is `ADR-0051`, taken by the Head of Quality Assurance on 2027-03-24 at `DEC-803`, authorised at
`GOV-36` **before `EV-106` was opened and before any of the three observations was looked at.** The order
matters and is recorded because it matters: a method settled after the results are known is a method
selected for the results it produces.

This chapter argues the decision and the boundary that goes with it — `ADR-0057`, `DEC-817`, taken by the
Vice President, Quality on 2027-04-08. **It states no position on any observation.** The three positions
are at `08.05-obs-2.md`, `08.06-obs-4.md` and `08.07-obs-6-and-the-register-that-cannot-show-it.md`, each
owned by the individual accountable for the estate it concerns, and none of them is restated here.

## 2. What a disposition is

**A disposition is a statement of a condition's present position, made by the firm about its own estate, in
its own records, at a stated vantage, with the evidence named.**

Four things are load-bearing in that sentence and each of them is doing work.

**It is about a condition, not about an observation.** The Form FDA 483 records conditions that in the
investigator's judgement may constitute violations. **The observation is the investigator's record of what
was seen; the condition is the state of affairs at Ridgemont.** Helix cannot alter the observation — it is
somebody else's document, made at a date that has passed — and it does not try to. What Helix can do is
establish what the condition described in it presently looks like, which is a question about Helix's own
systems and Helix's own records. **`ADR-0006` fixed the numbering so that the two never merge: `OBS-2`,
`OBS-4` and `OBS-6` are the 483's own identifiers and are never renumbered, regrouped or converted into
anything.** A disposition does not touch the observation. It says what the condition beneath it is now.

**It is made about the firm's own estate.** Every fact a disposition rests on is a fact Helix is competent
to establish because Helix owns the thing: an account list, a system configuration, a control position
determined on a published method, a register row. **A firm is the authority on the state of its own
systems.** It is not the authority on how somebody else will characterise that state.

**It speaks at a vantage.** A condition is a thing that obtains or does not obtain on a particular day, and
a statement about it is true on the day it is made or it is not true at all. Every position in this phase
speaks as at 2027-04-09 and several rest on determinations that speak as at the Phase 05 vantage of
2027-01-29, which is stated wherever it is so. **A disposition without a date is a claim with no expiry and
therefore no way of being wrong**, which is the property the dating rule at `01.12 §9` exists to prevent —
*"[a]n entry with no date has no age, and age is what gets an entry looked at."*

**And it names its evidence.** *"Closure names the evidence. 'Believed complete' is not a closure
condition"* — `01.12 §9`. The same discipline is applied here to a weaker object than a closure: **a
position that names no evidence is an opinion, and an opinion about one's own conduct offered to a
regulator is the least useful sentence a quality system can produce.**

## 3. What a closure is, and to whom it is made

**A closure is a representation. Its subject is not the condition; its subject is the state of the matter
between the firm and the agency.**

To say *this observation is closed* is to assert something about a relationship that has two parties, and
**Helix is one of them.** The sentence has a form that conceals this. It looks like a status field — a
value in a column, of the kind Helix sets in its own quality system a hundred times a week when a deviation
is closed or a change request completed. Those are Helix's own records about Helix's own processes, and
Helix sets their status because Helix owns the process the status describes. **An inspectional observation
is not one of those.** Nobody at Helix owns the process by which it came into existence, and nobody at
Helix owns whatever process determines what happens to it next.

**So the same word, applied to the same-looking field, changes what is being claimed.** A closed deviation
is a statement about Helix's investigation. **A closed observation is a statement about FDA's position**,
and Helix cannot make a statement about FDA's position, because it does not hold it.

**This programme makes no representation to FDA.** Not in this phase, not in a register, not in a
governance minute and not in a diagram label. What Helix filed on 2026-03-06 was a written response, and
the response was written to a discipline that this phase inherits: *"A response concedes nothing, contests
nothing and adjudicates nothing. It states what the organisation will do."* **A disposition is the same
sentence in the past tense — it states what the organisation has found, and it adjudicates nothing
either.**

**And the practical consequence is stated plainly, because the boundary is otherwise abstract.** No
document in this phase says *closed*, *cleared*, *resolved*, *satisfied*, *discharged in full* or *no
further action* about any of `OBS-2`, `OBS-4` or `OBS-6`; no register carries a status column in which such
a value could be entered; and no governance record ratifies one. **A field that permits the word will be
filled with it**, which is why the instrument at
`templates/observation-disposition-record-template.md` carries no such field and an express statement in
its place.

## 4. Why a firm may state its own condition and may not certify a conclusion about it

The two halves of this look symmetrical and are not, and the asymmetry is the whole architecture of the
chapter.

**Helix may say what it found when it looked at a named system on a named day, and name what it looked
at.** A statement of that form is a fact about a configuration, an account list or a register row. Somebody
can check it, somebody can disagree with it, and if it is wrong it is wrong in a way that can be
demonstrated. It is the kind of statement a firm makes about itself all day, and the regulatory system
depends on firms being able to make it. **What each of the three conditions presently looks like is stated
at `08.05`, `08.06` and `08.07` and is not stated here.**

**Helix may not say: the condition FDA observed was not a violation, or was one, or has been brought into
compliance.** Three different sentences, all of them refused, and for three different reasons.

**The first two are legal conclusions and belong to nobody in this building.** FDA's own words on the
instrument are the frame: *"The FDA Form 483 does not constitute a final Agency determination of whether
any condition is in violation of the FD&C Act or any of its relevant regulations."* **If the agency's own
document is not a determination of whether a condition violates anything, the firm's document is certainly
not one either** — in either direction. A firm that wrote *this was not a violation* would be adjudicating
a question no party has yet put; a firm that wrote *this was a violation* would be adjudicating it against
itself on the same non-existent authority. **No document in this repository reaches a legal conclusion of
any kind**, and this one does not start.

**The third is a compliance conclusion, and it is the one that is actually tempting.** *Brought into
compliance* sounds like a statement about a system rather than about law, and it is not. Compliance is not
a property of a configuration; **it is a property of a validated system in its intended use, under its
procedures, operated by its trained people** — which is why there is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11
certificate and no FDA-approved or FDA-validated software, and why a vendor cannot discharge on Helix's
behalf a duty that `21 CFR 11.10` places on **persons** who use closed systems. **A firm asserting its own
compliance is asserting a conclusion about a totality it cannot observe from inside**, and it is doing so
at the one moment when it has the strongest possible motive to be generous with itself.

**The programme has been here before under a different name.** **An open gap is Helix's own assessment of
its own position — not a violation, not a breach, not a non-compliance, not a finding and not an
inspectional observation.** The standing rule cuts both ways: if a gap is not a violation, **a closed gap
is not a clearance.** `08.12-what-this-does-not-establish.md` carries the full inventory of what this phase
declines to state and why.

## 5. What FDA does with a response, and what this programme says about it

**Stated narrowly, from FDA's own published material, and no further.**

A Form FDA 483 is issued to a firm's management at the close of an inspection when an investigator has
observed conditions that, in their judgement, may constitute violations, **and FDA is explicit that it is
not an all-inclusive list.** The agency then considers the 483 alongside the Establishment Inspection
Report, the evidence collected and the firm's own response **in deciding what further action, if any, is
appropriate.** FDA's *Review of Post-Inspection Responses* notice, Docket No. FDA-2009-N-0335, published in
the Federal Register on 2009-08-11, states that *"Under the program, before issuing a warning letter, FDA
will generally allow firms 15 business days to provide a response to FDA 483 observations."* and that
*"[i]f we receive a response to FDA 483 observations within 15 business days
after the FDA 483 was issued, we plan to conduct a detailed review of the response
before determining whether to issue a warning letter."* **The notice speaks to that one decision and this
chapter claims nothing beyond it.**

**Three things follow, and this chapter states them and stops.**

**Filing a response is an act of the firm. Everything that happens to the response afterwards is an act of
the agency.** Helix controls the first completely and the second not at all. **Nothing Helix writes in its
own records — however carefully, however well evidenced, and however plainly true — can constitute a
determination by FDA about anything**, because the record is Helix's and the determination would be
somebody else's.

**The programme therefore describes no agency mechanism, timetable or outcome for any of the three
observations.** Whether, when and on what basis the agency treats a matter as concluded is the agency's
question and not the firm's, and **a firm document that described it would be characterising somebody
else's process from the outside.** That refusal is the same one the repository has held since Phase 01
against forecasting inspection outcomes, applied one step earlier: **it is not knowable, it is a claim
about somebody else's judgement, and holding a position on it corrupts the work** — every remediation
decision acquires a second criterion, *how it will look*, and the second criterion wins arguments the first
should have won.

**And the absence of a legal finding is not a reason to do less.** This is the trap on the other side and
it is the more dangerous one. *"A programme that reads 'not a final agency determination' as 'not
established, therefore not urgent' has drawn precisely the wrong inference."* **The reason Helix does not
close observations is not that they matter less than they appear. It is that closing them is not a thing
Helix can do**, and the work that remains is unaffected by the vocabulary.

## 6. The three verbs, and what each licenses

**The phase uses three words about a condition and no others: *confirmed*, *corrected*, *not corrected*.**
Each is a statement about a state of affairs at a vantage. **None of the three is a statement about an
observation, about a violation, about compliance, or about FDA.**

| Verb | What it asserts | What it licenses | What it does not license |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | **The condition the observation records is presently established to obtain, on evidence named.** Helix has looked, at a stated vantage, at the thing the observation describes, and it is there | A statement that work is owed, and the identification of what and to whom | **No admission of any kind.** A condition confirmed is a fact about a system, not a concession about a regulation. **Confirming is not conceding**, and `ADR-0006`'s separation of `OBS-n` from `DEV-nnn` and from *finding* keeps the three vocabularies apart |
| **Corrected** | **The condition no longer obtains, on evidence named.** A state of affairs that was there is not there now | A statement that the condition has ceased, at the vantage, on the systems examined | **Not a closure. Not a clearance. Not a statement that the remediation was adequate, timely, well governed or complete**, and **not a statement that the correction is evidenced by any particular record** — which is a separate question with its own answer at `08.07-obs-6-and-the-register-that-cannot-show-it.md` |
| **Not corrected** | **The condition presently obtains, on evidence named, and the position is stated as it is** | A statement of what is owed and by whom, and nothing more | **Not a finding, not a deviation, not a violation and not a self-report of one.** It is Helix's own assessment of its own position, in Helix's own register |

**Three properties hold across all three verbs.**

**Each is falsifiable.** Somebody holding the same evidence can disagree, and the register names the
evidence so that they can. A word that nobody could refute is not a position.

**None is a grade.** There is no *substantially corrected*, no *largely complete*, no *materially
addressed*. **A qualifier attached to one of these three verbs is a score with the scoring prohibition
evaded**, and the register admits none.

**And *corrected* carries no implication whatever about the other two.** Each condition is disposed of on
its own evidence, on its own systems, by its own accountable owner. **A phase that reported three positions
as a set would be reporting a trend, and three is not a population.**

## 7. The adjacent objects, and why none of them is a disposition

`ADR-0006` established that this repository keeps one set of books, and seven phases have added vocabulary
to it. **A disposition sits beside several objects it resembles and is none of them.**

| Object | Whose it is | Why it is not a disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **An inspectional observation — `OBS-n`** | **FDA's investigator's** | A professional judgement recorded at a point in time by the person who made it. **Helix does not write it, amend it, renumber it or retire it**, and a disposition leaves it exactly as it stands |
| **A deviation — `DEV-nnn`** | **Helix's** | A quality record raised under Helix's own procedure. **A 483 observation never becomes a deviation**; a deviation may be raised in response to one, and both identifiers survive with the link recorded. **This phase raises none** |
| **A CAPA — `CAPA-nnn`** | Helix's | A corrective and preventive action, with a plan, an owner, a date and an effectiveness check. **A disposition is a statement of position and contains none of those things**, and **this phase states no CAPA, no remediation plan and no remediation cost** |
| **A commitment position — `RC-nn`** | The programme's, reading Helix's own filed words | A statement about **a commitment**, read against its own words — `08.02-reading-a-commitment-against-its-own-words.md`. **A commitment is not a condition.** A commitment can be met while the condition it addressed persists, and a condition can cease without any commitment having been met |
| **A control position — `CG-nn`, and the Phase 05 register** | The programme's | A determination against a paragraph of `21 CFR 11.10`, made on a method authorised before the first system was looked at. **A disposition reads those determinations and re-determines none of them** |
| **A closure** | **Nobody's, here** | **A representation about the state of a matter between Helix and FDA. This programme makes none** — `ADR-0051`, `ADR-0057` |

**The row that will be tested is the fourth.** A commitment and a condition are close enough that a reader
in a hurry will treat the position of one as the position of the other, and the two chapters are kept
separate for that reason: **the commitments are read at `08.03-the-commitments.md` and the conditions at
`08.05`, `08.06` and `08.07`, and neither set of statements is offered as evidence of the other.**

Three of the seven observations on the 483 concern electronic records and are in this programme's scope.
**Four do not, they were named once at
`../01-program-foundation-and-regulatory-scoping/01.02-the-inspection-and-the-form-483.md §3`, and this
repository has not reported on them since and does not start now** — `ADR-0006`. They are being addressed
under Helix's ordinary quality system by their own owners, on their own timelines.

**Each position is owned by the individual accountable for the estate it concerns, and none is owned by the
method.** That is `ADR-0008` applied to a disposition: **a position stated by the party that designed the
method for stating positions is a restatement, not an assessment.** The method is authorised here; the
statements are made there; and the acceptance of all three is a governance act recorded at `GOV-38` on
2027-04-06.

## 9. What this chapter does not establish

- **It states no position on any observation**, and no count of anything.
- **It closes nothing, describes nothing as closed, and makes no representation to FDA** — `ADR-0051`,
  `ADR-0057`.
- **It reaches no compliance conclusion and no legal conclusion**, and it does not characterise the
  Ridgemont inspection, or any condition observed at it, as a violation of anything.
- **It states no CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan, no remediation cost, no sequence, no priority and
  no date.**
- **It forecasts nothing about any future inspection**, and it describes no agency process, timetable or
  outcome.
- **It re-determines no Phase 05 cell, re-scores no Phase 03 pair, re-bands no Phase 04 obligation and
  re-derives no Phase 07 period.**
- **It bears on `IS-01` not at all**, and it names no candidate explanation of it, by number, by content,
  by paraphrase or by allusion — `08.11-is-01.md`, `ADR-0055`.
