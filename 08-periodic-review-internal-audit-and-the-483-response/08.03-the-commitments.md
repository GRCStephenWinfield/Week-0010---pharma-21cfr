## 1. The document nobody had opened

`EV-106` is the response commitment register. It was created on 2026-03-06 as part of the written response
to the Form FDA 483, it was reviewed by six named individuals before release, it was countersigned by the
Vice President, Quality, and it has sat in the quality system ever since. It is the only document in which
Helix has told a regulator what it intends to do about the three observations this programme took.

**`EV-106` has existed since 2026-03-06, and there is no record of anybody at Helix having opened it
between then and 2027-03-30.**

That is not a claim that its contents were forgotten. Several of the commitments describe work that
happened, and the programme itself performed a good deal of it. It is a narrower and more uncomfortable
claim: that thirteen months of a funded programme, seven published phases, one hundred and thirty-six
recorded decisions and thirty-five governance records passed without anybody reading the register of
undertakings the company had given, side by side, against what the company had done. **The commitments were the reason the programme existed and
they were not the instrument anybody managed it with.** `AS-08` — that the commitments in the written
response are not varied by Helix — was raised on 2026-05-29, carried open through six phases, and had
nothing resting on it until this one.

**This chapter reports what the reading found.** The method is
`08.02-reading-a-commitment-against-its-own-words.md`'s and is not restated here: a commitment is read
against the words the response used, and a commitment with no completion criterion is recorded as having
none rather than judged — `ADR-0052`, `DEC-805`. **The register is `logs/commitment-register.md` and it is
authoritative; the figures below are counts of its rows.**

**Nothing in this chapter closes an observation, describes one as closed, or makes any representation to
FDA.** A commitment position is a statement about a commitment. What the three conditions the 483 records
presently look like is at `08.05-obs-2.md`, `08.06-obs-4.md` and
`08.07-obs-6-and-the-register-that-cannot-show-it.md`, and **a disposition is not a closure** —
`08.01-what-a-disposition-is.md`, `ADR-0051`.

## 2. Twenty-two, and fourteen

**`EV-106` carries twenty-two commitments, `RC-01` to `RC-22`, each attaching to one of the seven
observations on the 483.** It carries one further thing that attaches to none of them — the standing
undertaking to file a written response within fifteen business days of any future inspectional observation
— **which carries no `RC` identifier, is Helix's own procedural undertaking rather than a rule FDA
imposes, is measured by `OBJ-08` and reported quarterly at `CAL-04`, and is not assessed here.**

**Fourteen of the twenty-two attach to `OBS-2`, `OBS-4` or `OBS-6` and are assessed. Eight attach to
`OBS-1`, `OBS-3`, `OBS-5` or `OBS-7` and are not. 14 + 8 = 22.**

| Observation | Subject | Commitments | Assessed here |
|---|---|---|---|
| `OBS-1` | Cleaning validation | `RC-01`, `RC-02` | **No** |
| **`OBS-2`** | **Audit trail review in the QC laboratory** | `RC-03` to `RC-07` | **Yes — 5** |
| `OBS-3` | Environmental monitoring trending | `RC-08`, `RC-09` | **No** |
| **`OBS-4`** | **Attribution of entries on a packaging line system** | `RC-10` to `RC-14` | **Yes — 5** |
| `OBS-5` | Supplier qualification | `RC-15`, `RC-16` | **No** |
| **`OBS-6`** | **Change to an approved value in the electronic batch record** | `RC-17` to `RC-20` | **Yes — 4** |
| `OBS-7` | Training records | `RC-21`, `RC-22` | **No** |

**5 + 5 + 4 = 14** and **2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 8.**

**The eight are published rather than omitted, and they are not assessed.** The four observations they
attach to were named once at
`../01-program-foundation-and-regulatory-scoping/01.02-the-inspection-and-the-form-483.md §3` and this
repository has not reported on them since — `ADR-0006`. They are being addressed under Helix's ordinary
quality system by their own owners, on their own timelines. **A data integrity programme that annexed them
in its eighth phase would be claiming work it has not done**, and it would be doing so at the one moment
in the programme when a reader has least reason to check. **So the eight carry a position of *Not
assessed* and a discharged-by cell of `—`, and they are on the register so that the exclusion can be seen
rather than inferred.**

**The response named five individuals as commitment owners and no team, no function and no distribution
list** — Bernard Kwiatkowski, Dr Sunita Raghunathan, Terrence Abbatiello, Amara Sissoko and Joachim Reuter.
**The Data Integrity Lead is named on none of the twenty-two**, because the post was created on the day the
response was filed and there was nobody in it to name.

## 3. Six, five and three

**Of the fourteen assessed: six are met, five are not met, and three carry no completion criterion.
6 + 5 + 3 = 14.**

| Position | Count | The commitments |
|---|---|---|
| **Met** | **6** | `RC-03`, `RC-04`, `RC-10`, `RC-11`, `RC-17`, `RC-18` |
| **Not met** | **5** | `RC-05`, `RC-06`, `RC-12`, `RC-13`, `RC-19` |
| **No completion criterion** | **3** | `RC-07`, `RC-14`, `RC-20` |

**Eleven of the fourteen carry a completion criterion and three do not, and the three are exactly the three
whose position is *No completion criterion*. 11 + 3 = 14.** That correspondence is not a coincidence and it
is not a convention: **a commitment is judged if and only if its own words name a state of the world that
could be observed to obtain**, and the three that do not name one are the three that cannot be judged.

**The six met are not a report of progress and this chapter does not offer them as one.** Four of the six
are commitments to *establish*, *determine*, *identify* or *record something* — that is, commitments to
find out. **They were satisfied by the programme finding out.** One is a commitment to remove a capability
from a system, and it was satisfied by the capability being removed. **What none of the six is, is a
commitment to change what a reviewer does**, and the commitments that were is where the five not met sit.

## 4. The three nobody can be held to

**`RC-07`, `RC-14` and `RC-20`.**

> *"Helix will continue to reinforce the importance of data integrity in the quality control
> laboratories."*
>
> *"Helix will reinforce with packaging operators the importance of individual accountability for entries
> made on line systems."*
>
> *"Helix will improve the visibility of changes made to electronic batch records after approval."*

Read the first of them beside `RC-12`, which says that Helix *"will withdraw the shared local operator
account from the packaging line systems and will issue a named individual account to every operator who
uses them."* A person holding the account list and the systems can say whether `RC-12` is so. There is a
state of the world in which it is true and a state of the world in which it is false, and the two are
distinguishable by looking.

**`RC-07`, `RC-14` and `RC-20` have no such pair of states.** No sequence of events would fail them.
Reinforcement of an importance is satisfied by a poster, by a meeting, by a sentence in a shift handover,
by nothing at all; improvement of a visibility is satisfied by any change in any direction that anybody
chooses to describe that way. **A commitment satisfied by any state of the world is satisfied by the state
of the world that obtained on the day the 483 was issued.**

> **A commitment written so that nobody can say whether it was met is a commitment nobody can be held to.**

**`IS-51`**, raised on 2027-03-30, owned by Rebekah Sandquist.

**The three are recorded as having no completion criterion and are not judged, and both halves of that
matter.** They are not recorded as met, because nothing establishes that they are and recording them met
would credit the company with a change it cannot demonstrate. They are not recorded as not met, because
nothing establishes that either and recording them not met would manufacture a failure out of a drafting
defect — **nothing suggests that the reinforcement described did not occur.** **And no completion criterion
is written for them now.** Writing one would mean assessing Helix against a commitment Helix never filed,
and it would quietly convert a finding about how the response was drafted into a finding about how the
remediation was performed. `ADR-0052`, `DEC-805`, `EV-809`.

**All three were owned by a named individual. All three carried a date the response stated. All three
passed that date without anything happening that could be recorded either way.** **The element that is
absent is not the owner and it is not the date. It is the test** — and the response's own review, which
ran through six named individuals and removed two proposed commitments for naming dates the programme could
not own, did not catch it.

## 5. Four past their date, and one with no date at all

**Of the five not met, four carry a date the response stated and that date has passed. One states no date
at all. 4 + 1 = 5.**

| Commitment | Named owner | Date the response gave | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RC-05` — revise the laboratory record review procedure to state whether the audit trail forms part of the record reviewed | Terrence Abbatiello | **2026-09-30** | **Not met**, and past |
| `RC-06` — train every analyst and second-person reviewer in the revised procedure | Dr Sunita Raghunathan | **2026-10-30** | **Not met**, and past |
| `RC-12` — withdraw the shared local operator account and issue named individual accounts | Amara Sissoko | **2026-08-31** | **Not met**, and past |
| `RC-19` — extend the quality unit's review of the electronic batch record to include the record of changes made after approval | Terrence Abbatiello | **2026-11-30** | **Not met**, and past |
| `RC-13` — record the identity of the person performing and the person checking each significant step on a packaging line | Bernard Kwiatkowski | **None stated** | **Not met** |

**No date on this register is extended, replaced or proposed by this chapter or by anything in this
phase.** The dates are the response's, they were stated to the agency, and the register records what they
were and whether they have passed. **A date rewritten inside the company thirteen months later is not a
date; it is a description of the present tidied to look like a plan.**

**Two of the five are the same failure in two places.** `RC-05` and `RC-19` are both commitments to put a
sentence into a procedure — one in the laboratory, one on the shop floor — and neither sentence exists.
That is `IS-24`'s subject matter seen from the response's side: **twelve obligations land in a procedure
that exists and does not require the act**, and two of Helix's own commitments to FDA were to fix exactly
that on exactly these two procedures. `DP-07` — quality unit capacity for the two record review procedure
revisions — **has been open since Phase 01 and has not moved.**

**`RC-06` cannot be met while `RC-05` is unmet.** It is a commitment to train people in a revised procedure
that has not been revised. **A dependency between two commitments is not visible on the face of `EV-106`**,
which lists them as two rows with two owners and two dates; **it becomes visible only when somebody reads
them together, which is what did not happen for thirteen months.**

**`RC-13` is the one to read last.** It states no date. It attaches to `OBS-4`, whose subject matter is
attribution. `21 CFR 211.188(b)(11)` requires *"[i]dentification of the persons performing and directly
supervising or checking each significant step in the operation, or if a significant step in the operation
is performed by automated equipment under § 211.68, the identification of the person checking the
significant step performed by the automated equipment."* **And a packaging line system performing a
significant step is automated equipment under `21 CFR 211.68`, so what the provision requires of it is the
identification of the person checking that step.** The commitment to record that identity is the only one
of the twenty-two that carries no date at all. **`GOV-03 §6` records that two proposed commitments were removed in review because
they named dates the programme could not own.** This one survived review with no date to remove.

## 6. What the reading did not do

**It did not vary, extend, narrow, widen or replace any commitment**, and it made no new one. `EV-106`
stands exactly as it was filed on 2026-03-06.

**It did not close any observation and it describes none as closed.** Six commitments met is not an
observation disposed of, and the fourteen do not add up to a position on anything: **`OBS-2`'s position
depends on a product limitation no commitment mentions, `OBS-4`'s depends on nine systems no commitment
enumerates, and `OBS-6`'s depends on a determination made in Phase 05 against a paragraph no commitment
cites.** The three positions are drawn in the three chapters that own them.

**It made no representation to FDA.** Nothing in this chapter, in `logs/commitment-register.md`, or in any
document in this phase has been filed, sent or offered to any agency. **The response of 2026-03-06 remains
the only thing Helix has said to FDA about any of this.**

**It stated no CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan and no remediation cost.** A commitment recorded as
Not met is a reading of a commitment; **it is not the opening of a quality record, and `OBS-n`, `IA-YYYY-MM/Fn`
and `DEV-nnn` are three vocabularies that are never swapped** — `ADR-0006`.

**And it reached no compliance conclusion and no legal conclusion** — `ADR-0057`, `DEC-817`.
