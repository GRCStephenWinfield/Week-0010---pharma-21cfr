*A disposition is not a closure and an arrangement is not a review, and this is the chapter where both are said.*

---

## 1. One idea

**This is the phase that will be read as the answer, and it answers nothing that the earlier phases left
open about whether Helix is compliant with anything.**

Seven phases produced determinations, registers and positions, and every one of them was visibly partial.
**Phase 08 produces something with a different shape: three conditions with a stated position, a set of
commitments read for the first time since they were filed, a review arrangement covering the estate, and an
issue that has been open since the first phase, closed.** Out of context that reads as completion. **It is
not completion, and several of the sentences a reader will produce from it are sentences this phase does
not make.**

**The hazard has a specific mechanism here and it is worth naming.** In the earlier phases the boundary was
protected by the material's obvious incompleteness — nobody reads a register with an empty column as a
clearance. **This phase's material looks finished.** A disposition looks like a closure because it occupies
the place in a document where a closure would sit; an arrangement looks like a review because it names
frequencies and dates; a closed issue looks like an answered question. **Each of the three is a resemblance
and not an identity, and the resemblance is the whole reason this chapter is a numbered chapter at the same
version as the findings rather than a caveat at the foot of a register.**

It is owned by internal audit, which disposed of no observation, assessed no commitment, established no
review arrangement and took no position in this phase — `ADR-0004`. **`08.10-what-would-have-had-to-be-true.md`
is internal audit's own work about its own records and is the exception; nothing else in the phase is.**

## 3. The distinctions this phase must not blur

`ADR-0006` established that this repository keeps one set of books, and seven phases have added vocabulary
to it. **Phase 08 adds four objects and none of them is any of the others.**

| Object | What it is | What it is not |
|---|---|---|
| **A disposition** | **A statement of a condition's present position, made by the firm about its own estate, in its own records, at a stated vantage, with the evidence named** | **Not a closure, not a clearance, not an admission, not a concession, not a self-reported violation and not a representation to anybody.** **Closure is a representation about the state of a matter between Helix and FDA, and this programme makes none** — `ADR-0051` |
| **`RC-nn`** — a commitment position | **A statement about one commitment, read against the words the response used**: whether those words name a state of the world that could be observed to obtain, the position, who performed the act that satisfied it, and what the owner's own tracking shows | **Not a statement about a condition, not a progress report, not a re-issued commitment and not a plan.** **A commitment with no completion criterion is recorded as having none and is not judged**, and no criterion is supplied now — `ADR-0052` |
| **`RV-nn`** — a periodic review row | **A statement about one system: its Phase 04 band, the default frequency that band gives, the frequency adopted, whether it moved, the reason if it did, the first review due and what the review examines** | **Not a review, not a review result, not a validation status, not a control determination and not an audit trail review frequency.** **The arrangement has not been run** — `IS-57`. **No row's frequency may be lengthened** — `ADR-0054` |
| **`AC-nn`** — an audit condition | **A statement of a condition that would have had to obtain for an audit record to show something other than what it shows, and whether it obtained in each of the four audits** | **Not a cause, not an explanation, not a criticism of any audit and not a judgement on anybody's work.** **It may not be cited in support of any candidate explanation of `IS-01`** — `ADR-0055` |

**And three verbs, which carry the same discipline.** ***Confirmed*** asserts that a condition presently
obtains, on named evidence, and admits nothing. ***Corrected*** asserts that it no longer obtains, on named
evidence, and clears nothing. ***Not corrected*** asserts that it presently obtains, and is Helix's own
assessment of its own position rather than a finding, a deviation or a violation. **No qualifier attaches
to any of the three** — there is no *substantially corrected* and no *largely complete*, because **a
qualifier on one of these verbs is a score with the scoring prohibition evaded.**

argued at `08.11-is-01.md` and recorded at `ADR-0055`, with its closing note and its named evidence at

> **It closes on what the programme can establish and expressly not on why. What it establishes: the six
> conditions, and that none of them obtained in any of the four audits. What it does not establish, and
> never will: why.**

**The standing form since Phase 06 is unchanged and survives the closure: there is no promotion of any of
the four candidate explanations by statement, by ordering or by tone.** No ranking, no probability, no
confidence level, no leading candidate and no ordering — **in this chapter, in `08.11`, in the registers,
in any diagram, in any governance record, in any decision entry, or in any later phase that cites this
one.** **The four are not reproduced anywhere in this phase**, because naming one and not the others is a
weighting and reproducing all of them in a document about something else is an invitation to read one of
them across. **They are at `01.09 §6`, in the order that chapter set.**

**The refusal is permanent and it is not handed to Phase 09.** Phase 09 does not inherit the question, is
not asked to answer it, and may not cite any document in this repository in support of any of the four. **No
later phase, no close-out document and no third party working from this repository may treat the closure as
having selected among them, as having ranked them, or as having found any of them more consistent with the
evidence than another.** **If a document does, it is wrong and this paragraph is why.**

**And the pressure in this phase is the strongest it has ever been, which is why the statement is longest
here.** Phase 06 supplied a technical fact that felt like an explanation and Phase 07 supplied something
that felt like a pattern. **Phase 08 supplies an examination of the audits themselves, performed against
everything the programme has built** — which is precisely the material a reader will expect to yield a
cause, and which does not, because **a condition is not a cause and adding a column to a record adds
nothing to an explanation.** `ADR-0012`'s mechanism is unchanged: **a weighted candidate becomes a working
assumption within about a quarter**, and after that nobody re-opens it because the answer has become
load-bearing.

## 6. Two things the phase must never be read as saying

**First: that a condition which no longer obtains is a matter that is finished.**

**A correction is a statement about a state of affairs, and it is one of the three verbs this phase uses.**
It is not a statement that the remediation was adequate, timely or well governed; not a statement that the
correction is durable; not a statement that it is evidenced by any particular record — **which is a
separate question, with its own answer, and the answer is not flattering to this programme**
(`08.07-obs-6-and-the-register-that-cannot-show-it.md`, `IS-53`). And it is emphatically **not a statement
about the observation**, which is somebody else's document and is untouched.

**The word that will do the damage is *closed*, and it will not arrive through this phase.** It will
arrive when a figure travels out of its document into a slide, a management report, a board paper or a
supplier discussion, **and the context that made its status obvious does not travel with it.** `ADR-0035`
established the mechanism in Phase 05 for a landscape fact and `ADR-0050` for a number; **the status of a
disposition is more fundamental than either and survives no better.** That is why the register carries no
status column in which the word could be entered, why the disposition record form carries an express field
stating that the record is not a closure, and why this chapter exists at the same version as the findings.

**Second: that an arrangement is a review, and that a register of frequencies is assurance.**

**Every system in scope now has a review frequency, a first due date and a stated content. Not one of them
has been reviewed.** The most quotable output of this phase is that the estate has a periodic review where
it had none, **and out of context that reads as assurance.** It is not assurance. It is a schedule.

**`IS-57` says so in the register and it is not softened anywhere.** **An arrangement with a first due date
is not a review, and a review that has not been performed has told nobody anything about whether any system
remains in a valid state.** The clause the content comes from asks a question — whether systems remain in a
valid state and are compliant with GMP — and **a review that produces a signed form and answers no question
has not performed the clause.** The first pass has not begun.

**And the standing refusals that go with it.** **There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate
and no FDA-approved, FDA-validated or FDA-certified software.** **A product has *features
that support* compliance, and compliance is a property of Helix's validated system in its intended use,
under its procedures, operated by its trained people. Outsourcing to a service provider transfers no
responsibility.** **FDA's Computer Software Assurance guidance is device guidance under `21 CFR Part 820`,
as amended by the Quality Management System Regulation, and does not apply to Helix**; it is adopted
voluntarily and labelled at every citation — `ADR-0005`, `ADR-0026`. **GAMP 5 Second Edition is ISPE
good-practice guidance and never a requirement, and no software-category count and no enumeration of the
categories appears anywhere in this repository.** **NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 is a control catalogue Helix
chose, not an obligation it is under: no FIPS 199 categorization, no baseline, no ATO and no authorizing
official.**

**And enforcement discretion, stated with all its limbs, because this phase touches paragraphs it
reaches.** **FDA has stated an intention to exercise enforcement discretion over `21 CFR 11.10(a)`, `(b)`,
`(c)` and `(e)` in full and over `(k)` in part — the guidance names `(k)(2)` — together with the
corresponding requirements in `21 CFR 11.30`, and over all Part 11 requirements for legacy systems
operational before 20 August 1997 that meet the guidance's conditions, against which no Helix system has
been assessed.** **It is FDA's stated intention. It is not a legal exemption, it is not a safe harbour, and
it confers no rights** — the guidance carries FDA's own disclaimer that *"It does not create or confer any
rights for or on any person and does not operate to bind FDA or the public"*. **`21 CFR 211.68`,
`21 CFR 211.100(b)` and `21 CFR 211.180` survive it entirely.** **A programme that reads enforcement
discretion as permission has read the wrong document.**

## 8. The standing framing this phase keeps

- **There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate and no FDA-approved or FDA-validated
  software.** **"Part 11 certified" is not a thing.**
- **Part 11 applies to records, not to systems**, and no document here says "a Part 11 system" without
  saying which records make it one — `ADR-0001`, `01.03 §2`.
- **A closed system is defined by who controls access** — `21 CFR 11.3(b)(4)`. **Hosting is not the test**,
  and all sixty-one systems in scope are closed — `ADR-0011`.
- **`21 CFR 11.3(b)` contains nine definitions and Part 11 contains ten sections.** There is no §11.60, no
  §11.400 and no Subpart D. **Part 11 defines neither "audit trail" nor "metadata" nor "data integrity" nor
  "static record" nor "dynamic record" nor "archive"** — FDA defines the first five in the December 2018
  drug CGMP data integrity guidance. Citations in this repository are exact and can be looked up.
- **Part 11 requires no reason-for-change field, no read-event logging, no cryptographic hashing under
  `21 CFR 11.70`, no multi-factor authentication under `21 CFR 11.200` and no password interval under
  `21 CFR 11.300(b)`** — and **no review frequency of any kind.** Where Helix implements any of them, it is
  Helix's decision and this repository says so — `ADR-0036`.
- **`21 CFR 11.30`'s additional measures are additive to the `21 CFR 11.10` controls it requires be
  included as appropriate, and never a substitute for them.** **That *as appropriate* is `21 CFR 11.30`'s
  own and reaches open systems only; on a closed system the eleven paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10` are owed in
  full, and all sixty-one systems in scope are closed** — `ADR-0011`.
- **`21 CFR 211.68` requires appropriate controls over Helix's computerised systems, at `(b)` the checking
  of input and output for accuracy with the degree and frequency of that verification based on the
  complexity and reliability of the system, and at `(a)` a written program designed to assure their proper
  performance.** **It imposes no general computerised-system validation requirement**, and the word
  *validation* appears in the section once, inside `(b)`'s backup carve-out.
- **`21 CFR Part 11` sets no retention period of its own**, and **`21 CFR 211.180(e)` is a product review
  and is never the basis for a periodic review of a computerised system.**
- **EU GMP Annex 11 is EU-derived, only its January 2011 text is in force, and no Annex 11 obligation binds
  Helix's US-facing route.** **FDA does not enforce EudraLex.** **The Qualified Person is not a US
  regulatory role.**
- **FDA uses plain ALCOA**, and its own footnote 5 maps the attributes to the CGMP predicate rules and
  cites Part 11 nowhere — `ADR-0019`. **The four additional attributes are MHRA's; MHRA itself refers to
  the shorter acronym, states there is no difference in expectations regardless of which is used, and
  defines all four. It does not reject them.** **No originator is named for either set.**
- **A Form FDA 483 is not a final agency determination**, a Warning Letter is informal and advisory —
  *"A Warning Letter is informal and advisory. It communicates the agency's position on a matter, but it
  does not commit FDA to taking enforcement action."* — and **only a court order binds.** **No Warning
  Letter, Untitled Letter or judicial action of any kind exists**, and **no document in this repository
  reaches a legal conclusion of any kind.**
- **This programme does not forecast the outcome of any future FDA inspection**, and the same refusal
  applies to the internal audit of the remediated programme and to the third-party audit.
- **An open gap is Helix's own assessment of its own position.** It is not a violation, not a breach, not a
  non-compliance, not a finding and not an inspectional observation.
- **Applicability is not integrity, a rating is not a validation status, a requirement is not a control, a
  control position is not validation evidence, a depth finding is not an estate finding, a derived period
  is not a decision Helix has taken, a disposition is not a closure, and an arrangement is not a review.**
- **No entry in the four programme registers carries a likelihood, an impact or a score. None ever will.**
- **No document characterises any Helix employee, any auditor or any FDA investigator, and no document
  assesses any individual's work.**
- **All content about Helix is fictional and illustrative.**
