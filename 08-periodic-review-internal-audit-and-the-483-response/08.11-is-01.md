## 1. The chapter this repository has been carrying since Phase 01

**`IS-01` closes.**

It was raised on 2026-05-19 at the Board audit committee sitting minuted at `GOV-05`, by Gideon Halvorsen,
Head of Internal Audit — **about internal audit's own records** — and it has been carried open and
unweighted at every vantage since. It is the only entry in this repository that was raised in the first
phase and carried, unresolved and deliberately unranked, to the last but one. **The decision that closes it
is `ADR-0055`, taken at `DEC-813` on 2027-04-07 by the individual who raised it**, and ratified at the
Steering Committee sitting of 2027-04-08 minuted at `GOV-40`.

**This chapter states what closes, what does not, and why no candidate explanation is named. It names
none.**

Not by number. Not by content. Not by paraphrase. Not by allusion. **Not by quoting `01.09 §6`, and not by
reproducing, summarising, characterising or gesturing at any of the four.** They are at `01.09 §6`, in the
order that chapter set, and a reader who wants them can read them there and will find them exactly as
Phase 01 left them.

## 1.1 And nothing required the four audits to exist

**Parts 210 and 211 contain no provision requiring a self-inspection, an internal audit or a quality audit
programme.** `21 CFR 211.22(a)` requires that *"[t]here shall be a quality control unit that shall have the
responsibility and authority to approve or reject all components, drug product containers, closures,
in-process materials, packaging material, labeling, and drug products, and the authority to review
production records to assure that no errors have occurred or, if errors have occurred, that they have been
fully investigated. The quality control unit shall be responsible for approving or rejecting drug products
manufactured, processed, packed, or held under contract by another company."* **That is an approval,
rejection and record-review authority over batches and materials. It is not a programme of periodic
independent examination of the quality system, and it establishes no audit function.**

EU GMP has a self-inspection chapter — **EudraLex Volume 4, Part I, Chapter 9, *Self Inspection*.
EU-DERIVED.** Its Principle is that *"Self inspections should be conducted in order to monitor the
implementation and compliance with Good Manufacturing Practice principles and to propose necessary
corrective measures."*, and its clause 9.3 that *"All self inspections should be recorded."* Both are
*shoulds* in a guideline that is not FDA's, and neither binds anything Helix does through FDA. **US drug
CGMP has no counterpart: Parts 210 and 211 contain no self-inspection, internal audit or quality audit
section anywhere in Subparts A to K, and this repository does not pretend otherwise.**

**Helix's internal audit function exists because the Board's audit committee established it**, and it
operates to an annual plan that same committee approves. **The four audits are therefore measured against
Helix's own audit programme and against nothing else** — the same discipline `01.09 §3.3` applied to
`IA-2025-01/F5`, where a password parameter was measured against Helix's own IT standard because Part 11
specifies none.

**This matters to what follows in two directions and they pull against each other.** A firm cannot be
faulted under a predicate rule for the design of a programme no predicate rule requires. **And a firm that
runs one anyway, reports it to its Board, and closes its findings through the quality system has made it
part of how it assures itself** — which is why `IS-01` was raised at all, and why it was raised by the
individual whose function it concerns rather than about him.

## 2. What `IS-01` asked

**Its own words, from the register, with the remainder elided because the remainder is the part this
chapter does not reproduce:**

> *"Four internal audits between 2023-06 and 2025-09 covered all three systems between them — the
> chromatography data system, the packaging line systems and the electronic batch record, each falling
> inside the scope of at least two — and none raised a finding on the subject matter of `OBS-2`, `OBS-4` or
> `OBS-6`. Why is not established…"*

**And the sentence the issue exists to answer, from `01.09 §1`:**

> *"A company that missed something has a detection problem; a company whose own audits looked at it and
> passed it has a different one."*

**Notice what the question is not.** It is not whether the audits were adequate; `01.09 §10` says in terms
that the chapter does not say they were inadequate, and neither does this one. It is not what any auditor
should have done; **no individual auditor is named anywhere in this repository, and none is assessed.** It
is not whether the observations were correct. **It is a question about why a particular silence occurred,
and a question about why is a question about cause.**

**Phase 01 refused to answer it and said so.** *"It does not state a cause. That is the point."* The
refusal was priced: *"A cause asserted at this vantage would be a guess wearing the clothes of a
conclusion."* And the history was published anyway, in full, including the audits that raised nothing,
because *"…a history withheld until its cause is known is a history withheld."*

**Phase 02 established the premise of one of the four and refused to promote it** — `ADR-0012`, and the
sentence that has governed six phases since: ***"Establishing a candidate explanation's premise does not
promote the candidate to the answer."*** Phases 03 to 07 each recorded, in their own boundary chapter, that
nothing in them discriminated between the four.

**Phase 01 also said where the answer would be looked for, and split the question to do it.** `01.13 §7`
states it whole — *"Which records actually are Part 11 records — and did anybody ever write that down?"* —
records that Phase 02 asks the first half, the record determination at `IS-02`, and states that **Phase 08
returns to the second half**, *"…when the periodic review, the internal audit of the remediated programme
and the 483 response commitments come together and the evidence to answer it exists."* **This is Phase 08.**

## 3. What eight phases established that bears on it

**`01.09 §5` recorded six observable features of the four audits.** They were recorded as facts about
documents, before the candidate explanations, so that the two would not be confused, and the section says
so on its own face: ***"Nothing in this section is a cause."***

**Phase 08 turns each of the six round.** Where `01.09 §5` recorded what the record shows, the six are
restated as **conditions that would have had to obtain for the record to show something else** — `AC-01` to
`AC-06`, in the order `01.09 §5` set its rows — and each is tested against each of the four audits, from
that audit's own scope statement, checklist and its provenance, sampling record, test design and working
papers.

> **Not one of the six conditions obtained in any of the four audits.**

**That is the finding, and it is the whole of the finding.** The tabulation, the working papers, the
capability column and every figure attaching to any of it are at
`08.10-what-would-have-had-to-be-true.md` and `logs/audit-condition-register.md`, which is authoritative.
**None of it is restated here.**

**And it rests on an assumption that is stated rather than buried** — that the working papers as retained
are the whole of what those audits produced. **A *No* read from an incomplete record is a statement about
the record and not about the audit**, and the whole force of the six is that they are read off documents.

## 4. What that is, and what it is not

**It is a statement about the audit records.** Six properties, four sets of working papers, read at a
vantage more than a year after the inspection and between a year and a half and nearly four years after the
audits themselves. Each cell is a reading of a document that exists, made by a person who can be
contradicted by another person reading the same document.

> **It is not a cause. It does not become one by being restated as a condition, by being tabulated, or by
> being read beside a silence it happens to be consistent with.**

**This is the load-bearing paragraph of the chapter and it is worth being slow about.** `01.09 §5` recorded
that a record shows a certain thing. This phase records that a condition did not obtain. **Those are the
same fact in two grammars.** Turning a feature of a document into a condition and recording that the
condition did not obtain **adds a column to the record. It adds nothing to the explanation.**

**What would have to be added, to get from here to a cause, is what has been missing since 2026-05-19: an
account of how the absence of a given condition operated on a given audit to produce a given silence.**
`ADR-0012` set out what that requires and Phase 02 established none of it: that the absence operated on
these audits rather than being a condition that held everywhere and therefore explains no difference
between one place and another; that its presence would have changed what the audits did, **which is a
counterfactual and is the load-bearing one**; and that no other account explains the same thing at least as
well. **Nothing in this phase establishes any of the three, for any candidate, and this phase examined no
counterfactual at all.**

**A weaker and more tempting form of the same error is available here and is refused too.** Six conditions,
none obtaining, across four audits, is a pattern — and a pattern invites the inference that whatever
explains one cell explains every cell. **It does not follow.** The conditions are not independent of
one another and were never claimed to be; some of them plainly bear on others; and **a set of readings that
covary tells you about the documents, not about what produced them.**

## 5. Why it closes now

**Because the thing it can be closed on now exists, and the thing it cannot be closed on never will.**

**An issue closes when the condition that made it an issue no longer holds, with the evidence named — and
this closure does not meet that test on `IS-01`'s own words, which is why the ground is stated rather than
assumed.** **`IS-01`'s condition is that why is not established, and that condition still obtains and always
will.** What has changed is the **reason the issue was carried**: it was carried because the evidence that
would bear on the question did not yet exist. **That evidence now exists, it has been examined, and it does
not answer the question.** The examination has been performed. Its evidence is named
in the closing note at `logs/raid-log.md §1.9`: the register at the vantage, the working paper restating
`01.09 §5`'s six features as conditions and joining each to each audit's own artefacts, the record of what
was examined audit by audit, the two access enquiries, the closure record, and the four audits and their
closure record unchanged since Phase 01.

**And the second thing is stated as plainly as the first.**

> **`IS-01` closes on what the programme can establish and expressly not on why. What it establishes: the
> six conditions, and that none of them obtained in any of the four audits. What it does not establish, and
> never will: why.**

**Closing an issue is not the same as answering a question, and the register is written so that the
difference survives.** The closing note records what closed it, its evidence **and what it never asked**,
on the discipline every closure in this repository follows. **A closure that quietly implied an answer
would be worse than leaving the issue open**, because an open issue is visibly unanswered and a closed one
is not.

**Why not leave it open, then.** Because leaving it open would be a pretence. **An issue is a thing that is
true now and is a problem, carried open with a reason — and the reason `IS-01` was carried was that the
evidence to answer it did not yet exist.** That reason has expired. The evidence that was named as needed
now exists, and it turns out not to answer the question. **Carrying the issue forward on a reason that is
no longer true would misrepresent the register**, and it would hand Phase 09 a question with an implied
instruction to answer it. **The honest act is to close it on what it established and to record that the
remainder is not a matter of insufficient work.**

## 6. Why no candidate is chosen

**Choosing one would be an attribution of cause, and the programme cannot evidence a cause.**

That is the whole reason and the rest of this section is its consequences.

**The four were recorded in no order of likelihood.** `01.09 §6` set them out in full and chose none, and
the order it printed them in is the order of a list and not a ranking. **`ADR-0012` made *unweighted* an
express commitment**, and stated why it is the harder half: **it means no ranking, no probability, no
confidence level, no leading candidate, no most likely and no ordering — in a chapter, in a register, in a
diagram, in a decision record or in any later phase.** *"A weighted candidate becomes a working assumption
within about a quarter"*, and a working assumption becomes the premise of the remediation design.

**They are not mutually exclusive.** They can all be true at once, in different proportions for different
audits. **A selection among things that are not alternatives is not a finding; it is an emphasis** — and an
emphasis chosen at the end of a programme, by the programme, about the function that would have audited the
programme, is the least defensible emphasis available.

**And the third of `01.09 §7`'s reasons is the one that still holds and has hardened.** *"…a cause chosen
now would be chosen to be comfortable."* **Every one of the four assigns the problem somewhere convenient**
— that was true in month three and it is true now, and what has changed is only that the programme now has
thirteen months of material with which to make a convenient choice look evidenced. **The material makes the
temptation stronger, not the inference sounder.**

**So the refusal is not a residue of the closure. It is part of it.**

> **The refusal is permanent. It is not handed to Phase 09.**

**Phase 09 does not inherit the question, is not asked to answer it, and may not cite any document in this
repository in support of any of the four.** No later phase, no close-out document and no third party
working from this repository may treat the closure as having selected among them, as having ranked them, or
as having found any of them more consistent with the evidence than another. **The audit condition register
may not be cited in support of any candidate explanation**, and nor may any diagram, minute or working
paper of this phase.

**The cheapest moment to refuse to weight the four was 2026-05-29, before anything was at stake in the
answer. The second cheapest is the moment the issue closes**, because after that there is no register entry
left whose status anybody has to keep honest.

## 7. What makes this a closure and not a surrender

**Stated positively, once, because a chapter that is only refusals invites the reading that nothing was
achieved.**

**The examination happened.** The four audit programmes, scope statements, checklists and their provenance,
sampling records, test designs and working papers were read against what Helix now knows — and Phase 01
named that examination as the thing that did not exist and that Phase 08 would have to perform. **It was
performed, by the function whose own records they are, and it is published in full.** That is `ADR-0007`'s
bargain honoured a second time: **an organisation that publishes the audits that raised nothing invites the
reading that its assurance function does not work, and the alternative invites the reading that nobody
looked.**

**A periodic review now exists.** The estate had none, at any point, ever. It has one now — voluntarily
adopted where FDA requires none, with the EU-derived limb labelled EU-derived at every citation, with a
frequency derived from an already-published band and a first due date on every row —
`08.08-nothing-requires-a-periodic-review.md`, `08.09-the-review-established.md`, `ADR-0053`, `ADR-0054`.
**It has not been run, and `IS-57` says so in terms**; an arrangement with a first due date is not a
review. **But the thing that was not there is there.**

**The conditions that can now be met became capable because this programme built the artefact each turns
on** — the record-level determination, the obligations, the control positions, the auditable event
characterisation, the retention derivation. **None of those existed when any of the four audits was
performed.** Which artefact answers which condition, and how many conditions the capability column reaches,
are at `08.10-what-would-have-had-to-be-true.md §4` and `logs/audit-condition-register.md`, and are not
restated here.

**And the condition that cannot be met is named as an open issue with an owner**, not smoothed, not
deferred into a general recommendation and not described as capable of being met on an intention. It is at
`08.10-what-would-have-had-to-be-true.md §5` with its identifier, its owner and its route. It is the
quietest finding in the phase and it is the one the phase is least comfortable with, and it is in the
register with a name against it.

> **`IS-01` closes having established what could be established, having named what could not, and having
> left the one thing that is still missing in the open with somebody's name on it. That is not a surrender.
> A surrender would have been an answer.**

## 8. What this closure does not say

- **It does not name, rank, weight, promote, prefer or allude to any candidate explanation.** **None is
  reproduced here.** They are at `01.09 §6`, in the order that chapter set — `ADR-0012`, `ADR-0055`.
- **It states no cause**, and no document in this phase may be cited in support of one.
- **It does not say the four audits were inadequate**, and it says nothing about what any auditor should
  have done. **No individual auditor is named in this repository and none is assessed**; all four record
  the auditor as *"Internal Audit, Helix Pharma, Inc."*
- **It is not a finding against internal audit as a function.** The four were planned audits under a plan
  approved by the Board's audit committee, executed by a function independent of Quality and of IT; **they
  raised fourteen findings between them and every one was accepted, assigned an owner and closed before
  2026-02-02**; and every one of the three systems was inside the scope of at least two of them. **The areas
  were visited.**
- **It converts nothing.** An inspectional observation does not become an audit finding and an audit
  finding does not become a deviation — `ADR-0006`. **No `IA-YYYY-MM/Fn` is re-opened, re-closed,
  re-characterised or re-scoped**, and the closure record of the fourteen stands unchanged since Phase 01.
- **It closes no observation and makes no representation to FDA** — `ADR-0051`, `ADR-0057`.
- **It reaches no legal conclusion and no compliance conclusion**, and it forecasts nothing about any
  future inspection, about the internal audit of the remediated programme, or about the third-party audit.
- **It re-determines no Phase 05 cell, re-scores no Phase 03 pair, re-bands no Phase 04 obligation and
  re-derives no Phase 07 period.**
- **And `IS-58` is not `IS-01` in another form.** `IS-01` asked why four audits raised nothing on three
  subjects between 2023 and 2025. **`IS-58` is a statement about this programme at this vantage** —
  `08.13-phase-summary-and-transition.md §7`. **Nothing in it re-opens `IS-01`, and no candidate
  explanation is named by it or reachable through it.**
