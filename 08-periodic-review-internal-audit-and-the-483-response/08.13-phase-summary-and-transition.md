## 1. What Phase 08 established, in order

**One. That an observation is disposed of by stating the condition's present position, never by stating
that it is closed.** `ADR-0051`, `DEC-803`, Terrence Abbatiello, 2027-03-24 — **fixed before `EV-106` was
opened and before any of the three observations was looked at.** **Closure is a representation to FDA about
a matter between the firm and the agency, and this programme makes none.** `08.01-what-a-disposition-is.md`
owns the argument.

**Two. That the commitments are read against their own words.** `ADR-0052`, `DEC-805`, Rebekah Sandquist,
2027-03-29. **A commitment with no completion criterion is recorded as having none rather than judged**, and
supplying the test now would be writing a commitment Helix did not file.

**Three. The commitments, read for the first time since they were filed.** **`EV-106` was filed on
2026-03-06 and there is no record of anybody at Helix having opened it before 2027-03-30.** **It carries
twenty-two commitments, `RC-01` to `RC-22`. Fourteen attach to `OBS-2`, `OBS-4` or `OBS-6` and are
assessed; eight attach to the other four observations and are not. 14 + 8 = 22.** **Of the fourteen: Met 6
· Not met 5 · No completion criterion 3. 6 + 5 + 3 = 14.** **Of the five not met, four are past a date the
response stated and one states no date at all. 4 + 1 = 5.** `logs/commitment-register.md`,
`08.03-the-commitments.md`, **`IS-51`**.

**Four. The inversion, and it is a governance finding.** **Of the six commitments met, five were discharged
by this programme and not by the commitment's named owner — and in each of the five the owner's own
tracking still shows the commitment open.** **A commitment tracked in one place and discharged in another
is a commitment nobody is tracking.** **It is a finding about two records that do not speak to each other
and it is not an allegation that anybody acted improperly.** `ADR-0056`, `08.04-who-actually-did-it.md`,
**`IS-52`**.

**Five. `OBS-2` — the condition is confirmed, the remediation is defined, and it is not complete.** The
laboratory record review procedure still does not state whether the audit trail associated with a result
forms part of the original record reviewed; **`RC-05` and `RC-06` are both Not met and both past the date
the response gave.** **And part of what remains owed cannot be completed on the current product version:
`AE-08` and `AE-16` cannot be enabled by any setting available to Helix, and both record an act capable of
changing a reportable result.** **That is a fact about a product. It is not a discharge of an obligation.**
`08.05-obs-2.md`.

**Six. `OBS-4` — the condition is confirmed and it is not corrected.** **At the Phase 05 vantage of
2027-01-29 the shared local operator account was still in use on the packaging line system FDA observed,
and on eight further systems: `CG-11` names seven and `CG-13` names two, and 7 + 2 = 9.** **The 483 was
issued on 2026-02-13; that is three hundred and fifty days.** **`RC-12`, the
commitment to withdraw the shared account and issue named individual accounts, carried the date 2026-08-31
and is Not met.** **A shared account does not fail `21 CFR 11.10(d)` on its face — access is still limited
to a group of authorised operators — and what it defeats is attribution, which is `21 CFR 211.188(b)(11)`
and `21 CFR 211.194(a)(7)`.** **`21 CFR 211.188(b)(11)`'s second limb reaches a packaging line system
directly — it is automated equipment under `21 CFR 211.68`, and what the provision requires of it is the
identification of the person checking the significant step the equipment performed — which is what `RC-13`
undertook, and `logs/commitment-register.md` records `RC-13` Not met with no date stated.** **FDA's December 2018 drug CGMP data integrity guidance states that**
*"[s]hared, read-only user accounts that do not allow the user to modify data or settings are acceptable
for viewing data, but they do not conform with the part 211 and 212 requirements for actions, such as
second person review, to be attributable to a specific individual."* **Both limbs are carried; it is a
nonbinding recommendation and is never called a requirement here; and on none of the nine is the credential
such an account.** `08.06-obs-4.md`, **`IS-56`**.

### 2.2 `IS-04` — CLOSED 2027-04-08

**Its text was that `CAL-06`'s cadence is undefined where CGMP does not specify a review frequency for the
underlying data.** Raised by Terrence Abbatiello on 2026-05-29. **`DEC-817`, taken by Dr Marguerite
Oyelaran on the paper put by its owner, minuted at `GOV-40`.**

**Closure evidence, named:** `EV-802` the source pack, with `21 CFR 211.194(a)(8)` read whole and
`21 CFR 211.192` beside it · `EV-803` the standing framing note · `EV-810` the record of what `RC-05` and
`RC-19` are and are not · `EV-825` the position paper on what requires a periodic review · `EV-837` the
closure record · `GOV-39`, `GOV-40`.

**What closed it: the first half of the question is answered and the second half is now correctly located.**
**The review of the audit trail associated with a record is part of the review of that record**, and
`21 CFR 211.192` and `21 CFR 211.194(a)(8)` require that on an event and not on a cycle. **Where CGMP
specifies the review frequency for the underlying data, the audit trail review inherits that frequency**,
and FDA's December 2018 drug CGMP data integrity guidance names two — *"…§ 211.188(b) requires review after
each significant step in manufacture, processing, packing, or holding, and § 211.22 requires data review
before batch release."* Where CGMP specifies none, the same guidance states that *"…you should determine the
review frequency for the audit trail using knowledge of your processes and risk assessment tools."* — **a
nonbinding recommendation, never described here as a requirement, and one this phase does not discharge.**
On the EU-facing route the EU-derived expectation is that audit trails be *"…regularly reviewed"* — **a
*should* in a guideline that is not FDA's, naming no interval.** **No stand-alone periodic audit trail
review frequency is named by any primary source, none is set here for any record, and none is attributed to
any source.** **`IS-04` closes on where the obligation sits and on what it inherits; the residual
determination the guidance recommends is `IS-24`'s subject and `DP-07`'s route, and both are open.** **The
periodic review established in this phase is a review of a system and is not `CAL-06`.**

**What `IS-04` never asked is whether any Helix procedure requires the reviewer to open the audit trail at
all.** **It does not. `RC-05` is Helix's own commitment to make it do so and is Not met**, and `IS-24` and
`DP-07` carry the remainder.

## 7. The question Phase 08 leaves open

> **Every determination in this repository was made by the people who also own the things determined, and
> the one function that could have tested them independently has never been given the access to do it. The
> programme has assessed itself.**

**`IS-58`**, raised at publication, owned by Gideon Halvorsen, open.

**Take it in order.** The record-level determination was made by the Data Integrity Lead and approved
through Quality. The complexity-and-reliability band was determined by the validation function. The six
hundred and seventy-one control positions were determined by the function that operates the systems. The
backup and restore position was taken from enquiries answered by the people who operate the arrangements —
**`AS-22`, `AS-40` and `AS-49` are the three places that was written down before anybody added them up.**
The retention derivation, the auditable event characterisation, the commitment assessment and the three
positions in this phase were all made inside the programme that had an interest in what they said.

**Every one of those determinations was made on a method fixed before the work and published with its
assumptions, and that is the whole of what the programme could do about it.** It is not nothing: a method
authorised before the first system was looked at is a method nobody could tune to the answer, and eight
phases of this repository are built that way on purpose.

**What the programme could not do is grant itself independence.** **Read access for the audit function has
been requested on none of the sixty-one systems and granted on none.** **The one function at Helix that
reports to the Board's audit committee rather than to Quality or to IT — the function `ADR-0004` exists to
keep separate, the function that could have tested any of this — has never been able to open a single
system this programme has written about.**

**`IS-58` is not `IS-01` in another form, it does not re-open `IS-01`, and no candidate explanation is
named by it or reachable through it.** `IS-01` asked about four audits between 2023 and 2025. **`IS-58` is
a statement about this programme, at this vantage, about work done in the last thirteen months.**

**Phase 08 is nineteen days long** — 2027-03-22 to 2027-04-09, counted inclusive of both endpoints
because it is a duration of an activity and not an interval between two events, on the convention
`01.02 §3` and `08.06 §4` state. **It closed nothing, certified nothing and represented nothing to anybody.
What it did was open a document filed thirteen months ago, read four audits against six conditions, dispose
of three inspectional observations by stating the present position of each condition, build an arrangement
for looking at sixty-one systems that nobody has yet looked at
— and close the question the repository has carried since its first phase, on what could be established and
on nothing else.**
