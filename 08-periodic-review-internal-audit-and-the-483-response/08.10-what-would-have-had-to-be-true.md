*Six conditions, four audits, twenty-four cells, and not one of them reads Yes.*

---

## 1. What this chapter does, and the sentence it will not write

`01.09 §5` recorded six observable features of the four internal audits conducted between 2023-06 and
2025-09. It recorded them as facts about documents and it said so in terms: *"The following are statements about
the audit records themselves… **Nothing in this section is a cause.**"*

**This chapter turns each of the six round.** Where `01.09 §5` recorded what the record shows, this chapter
states **the condition that would have had to obtain for the record to show something else**, asks whether
that condition obtained in each of the four audits, and asks whether Helix could meet it today. **That is
the whole of what it does.**

> **This chapter states conditions and whether they obtained, and it stops. It draws no inference about why
> any of the four audits raised what it raised, it explains nothing, and it names no candidate
> explanation.**

**The four candidate explanations recorded at `01.09 §6` stand exactly where that chapter left them, in the
order it set, none chosen, none weighted and none named here** — `ADR-0012`, `ADR-0055`. **Nothing in this
chapter, and nothing in `logs/audit-condition-register.md`, may be cited in support of any of them.**

**A condition is not a cause.** Recording that a scope statement did not name an object is a statement
about a scope statement. **Restating a feature as a condition and recording that it did not obtain adds a
column to the record; it adds nothing to the explanation**, and the moment it is read as adding something
the whole exercise has been converted into the thing `01.09` refused to do.

**Nothing here is a judgement on any audit or on anybody who performed them.** `01.09` does not say the
audits were inadequate and neither does this. **No individual auditor is named in this repository for any
of the four**; all four record the auditor as *"Internal Audit, Helix Pharma, Inc."*; and `01.09 §2` states
that *"[t]his document names no individual auditor and makes no statement about any
individual's work."* **That holds here without variation.**

## 2. The six

**`AC-01` to `AC-06`, in the order `01.09 §5` set its six rows.** The register is
`logs/audit-condition-register.md` and it is authoritative.

| ID | The condition that would have had to obtain |
|---|---|
| **`AC-01`** | **An audit scope statement names a system audit trail as an object of examination** |
| **`AC-02`** | **An audit checklist is derived from a source that states which records exist and what is required of them, rather than from the site's procedure set alone** |

## 3. None of them obtained, in any of the four

**Each of the six was tested against each of `IA-2023-06`, `IA-2024-03`, `IA-2025-01` and `IA-2025-09`,
from that audit's own scope statement, checklist and its provenance, sampling record, test design and
working papers.** `EV-831` is the working paper that makes the join and `EV-832` records what was examined,
audit by audit.

**Six conditions across four audits is twenty-four cells. 6 × 4 = 24.**

> **Not one of the twenty-four reads Yes.**

**Zero of twenty-four, and zero of six conditions obtained in even one of the four.**

**That figure has to be read carefully, and the chapter states the caution beside it rather than below
it.** **It is not a finding that nothing was examined.** The four audits were planned audits under a plan
approved by the Board's audit committee, executed by a function independent of Quality and of IT. **They
raised fourteen findings between them — 4 + 3 + 5 + 2 = 14 — and every one was accepted, assigned an owner
and closed before 2026-02-02.** **Every one of the three systems whose subject matter the 483 reached was
inside the scope of at least two of the four.** **The areas were visited.** What the twenty-four cells
record is what the visits consisted of, not whether they happened.

**And it rests on an assumption that is stated rather than buried.** **`AS-58` — that the working papers as
retained are the whole of what those audits produced.** A *No* read from an incomplete record is a
statement about the record and not about the audit, and **the whole force of `AC-01` to `AC-06` is that
they are read off documents.**

## 4. Five can now be met

**Five of the six conditions are capable of being met today, and every one of the five became capable
because this programme built the artefact it turns on.**

| Condition | What makes it capable now |
|---|---|
| **`AC-01`** | The objects a scope statement would name now exist: **the eleven `21 CFR 11.10` positions for all sixty-one systems**, the audit trail retention configuration read across the estate, and **thirty-four auditable event classes characterised on the two chromatography instances.** The condition is a sentence in a scope statement, and there is now something for the sentence to name |
| **`AC-02`** | A source of that kind now exists to derive a checklist from: **the record-level determination, the thirty-eight obligations, the thirty-four control gaps and the retention derivation.** **None of the four is a procedure, and none of them existed before this programme** |
| **`AC-03`** | The population is now enumerable without opening a system: **sixty-one systems, thirty-four in-scope record types and one hundred and twenty-one record-type-and-system pairs, each with a named holder.** What a sampling frame would be drawn from is stated. **Drawing it requires `AC-05`, which is a different condition and is its own row** |
| **`AC-04`** | The obligation a test would be written against has been read whole. **`21 CFR 211.194(a)(8)` states three tests and not one, and FDA's December 2018 drug CGMP data integrity guidance treats the audit trail as part of the record being reviewed.** The provision is read in its own words at `08.05-obs-2.md §2`, which owns that argument and is cited rather than restated. That guidance is nonbinding and is not described here as a requirement |
| **`AC-06`** | **The determination exists and has existed since 2026-07-24** — fifty-eight record types with thirty-four in scope, derived record type by record type, approved at `GOV-09`, and never entered as an opinion. **`IS-02` closed on 2026-07-28** |

**Those five are the programme's product, restated as an audit capability.** **Thirteen months of work
between the 483 and this vantage produced, among other things, exactly the artefacts an audit of these
systems would have needed and did not have.**

## 5. One cannot

**`AC-05`. System read access for the audit function.**

> **Read access for the audit function has been requested on none of the sixty-one systems, and granted on
> none.**

**`EV-833` is the request enquiry — whether any request for system read access by or on behalf of Internal
Audit exists, at any date, for any of the sixty-one. `EV-834` is the grant enquiry, put to the account
administration function.** **Both are retained rather than their answers alone**, so that a nil return can
be tested as a search rather than taken as a conclusion — `AS-23`. **They are two enquiries and not one,
because a request and a grant are different acts and a register that merged them could not say which was
missing.**

**The other five conditions became capable because the programme built something. `AC-05` turns on a grant,
and no artefact this programme could build supplies one.** A determination can be written; a register can
be compiled; a provision can be read. **An account cannot be granted by the function that needs it.**

> **The programme has spent thirteen months building the determination, the registers and the evidence the
> audits needed, and nobody has given the auditors the key.**

**`IS-55`**, raised on 2027-04-07, owned by Gideon Halvorsen. **`DP-46`** is the route: read access,
requested and then granted, recorded system by system. **It does not close by a policy statement that
access would be granted on request, which is not a grant, and it does not close by access being granted on
one system.**

**`DP-46` is the only dependency in the log whose subject matter could be discharged in an afternoon and
has been discharged on none of sixty-one systems.** It draws on no scarce capacity. It competes with
nothing. It is not waiting on a vendor, a validation resource or a quality unit queue. **Nobody has asked,
and nobody has offered.**

**Two things this section is careful not to say.** **It does not say that access was withheld, refused or
denied, at any date, by anybody** — the enquiries record that no request and no grant is identified, and
that is all they record. **And it does not say what any auditor could or could not have done at any
historical date**: **whether such access was available to the audit function at any of the four audit dates
is not established from the working papers**, `01.09 §5` recorded that limit, and it has not moved.

## 6. What follows from this chapter, and what does not

**What follows: `AC-01`, `AC-02`, `AC-03`, `AC-04` and `AC-06` are capable of being met, `AC-05` is not,
and an audit of these systems performed today would meet five of the six conditions and fail the sixth for
a reason that has nothing to do with how it was scoped.** `templates/audit-scope-statement-template.md` is
the form for the first of them and it is empty.

**What does not follow, and is not stated, implied, hinted at or reachable from anything here:**

- **Any explanation of why the four audits raised nothing on the subject matter of `OBS-2`, `OBS-4` or
  `OBS-6`.** **`IS-01` closes on what the programme can establish and expressly not on why** — `ADR-0055`,
  and `08.11-is-01.md` owns the closure.
- **Any selection, ranking, weighting or promotion of any of the four candidate explanations at
  `01.09 §6`**, by number, by content, by paraphrase or by allusion. **None is named here.**
- **Any judgement on the adequacy of any audit**, or on what any auditor should have done.
- **Any statement about any individual's work.**
- **Any finding, deviation or CAPA.** **A condition is not a finding**, and `OBS-n`, `IA-YYYY-MM/Fn` and
  `DEV-nnn` are three vocabularies that are never swapped — `ADR-0006`.
- **Any result of `CAL-09` or `CAL-10`.** **No report from either is indexed in this phase and no outcome
  of either is stated or anticipated anywhere in it.**
- **Any forecast of what any future inspection or audit will find.**

**The two are one act recorded as two decisions, and the split is deliberate.** **The determination that
none of the six conditions obtained is the Head of Internal Audit's**, taken on his own authority and
reported to the Board's audit committee; **the adoption of the result as a controlled register is the
quality unit's.** Collapsing them would have put the audit function's determination inside the quality
unit's approval, and `ADR-0004` exists to keep those apart. Both are minuted at
`governance/GOV-40-steering-committee-minute-2027-04-08.md`.
