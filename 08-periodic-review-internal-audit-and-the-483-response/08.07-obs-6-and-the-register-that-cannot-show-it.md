# 08.07 — `OBS-6`, and the Register That Cannot Show It

*The condition was corrected, and no register this programme built can show when, by what act, or on whose authority.*

---

## 1. The position

**`OBS-6` — a value could be changed after quality unit approval without the change appearing in the record
the reviewer saw.**

> **The condition the observation records is corrected. And the correction is evidenced by no register this
> programme built.**

**The observation is not closed and nothing here describes it as closed** — `ADR-0051`, `DEC-803`,
`08.01-what-a-disposition-is.md`. **Corrected and closed are not the same word.** A firm may state what it
has found to be so about its own condition at a vantage; **closure is a representation to FDA about a
matter between the firm and the agency, and this programme makes none.**

**This chapter contains the only finding in the repository that is about the repository.** It is set out
plainly and it is not softened.

## 2. `ADR-0006` holds, and the observation is disposed of whole

**`OBS-6` contains two propositions and only one of them is about audit trails.** `01.02 §4` says so and
says to read it carefully. **The first is that a post-approval change was possible. The second is that it
was not visible in the record presented for review.**

**`ADR-0006` refused to split the observation into the two, and that refusal stands here.** The decision
recorded that splitting was a genuine question: *"Splitting it would make the remediation easier to track
and would mean this programme's records no longer matched FDA's. The observation stays whole; the
**remediation** beneath it may have as many actions, owners and dates as it needs."*

**So the observation is disposed of whole, and the position records both propositions.**

| Proposition | Position at this vantage |
|---|---|
| **A value could be changed on the electronic batch record after quality unit approval** | **Corrected on `SYS-001`.** `§3` |
| **The change was not visible in the record presented for review** | **Corrected on `SYS-001`, and it is a class the estate still carries.** `CG-15` records six systems on which a change to a stored value replaces the previous value in the record the reviewer sees — `DP-29`, `IS-32` |

**Neither proposition is reported as the other, and the estate-wide class is not reported as reduced by the
correction on one system.** `PR-61` carries the risk that it is, and it was opened at the sitting that
accepted the three positions.

## 3. What corrects it

**At the Phase 05 vantage of 2027-01-29, `SYS-001` — the manufacturing execution system with electronic
batch record — is *Met and required* on all eleven paragraphs of `21 CFR 11.10`.**

**It is the only one of the sixty-one systems that is.** Six hundred and seventy-one control positions were
determined across the estate on a method authorised before the first system was looked at, and `SYS-001` is
the single row on which every one of its eleven cells reads **Met and required** — the control in place,
and a named sentence in a named procedure obliging somebody to maintain it. `EV-821` is the extract and it
is read off `../05-access-audit-trails-and-electronic-signatures/logs/control-position-register.md` and off
nothing else.

**Read against the observation, two of the eleven do the work.** `21 CFR 11.10(e)` requires that *"[r]ecord
changes shall not obscure previously recorded information"* — a no-overwrite requirement — and it is **Met
and required** on `SYS-001`. `21 CFR 11.10(g)` requires *"[u]se of authority checks to ensure that only
authorized individuals can use the system, electronically sign a record, access the operation or computer
system input or output device, alter a record, or perform the operation at hand."* — five acts, not three —
and it is **Met and required** on `SYS-001`. **The condition the 483 records — that a value could be changed after approval and
that the change did not appear in the record the reviewer saw — does not obtain on that system at that
vantage.**

**And Helix's own commitment says the same thing from the other side.** **`RC-17`** — *"Helix will remove
the ability to amend a value on an approved electronic batch record other than through a controlled change,
and will record any such change under change control."* — **is Met, and it is the only one of the six met
commitments that was discharged by the individual the response named.** Joachim Reuter performed it, under
change control, and **it is the only one of the six on which the owner's own tracking and the programme's
register agree** — `08.04-who-actually-did-it.md`.

**`RC-18`, the commitment to establish whether a change to a stored value obscures the value previously
recorded, is Met and was discharged by the programme.** **`RC-19`, the commitment to extend the quality
unit's review of the electronic batch record to include the record of any change made after approval, is
Not met and is past the date the response gave.** **`RC-20`, which says Helix will "*improve the visibility
of changes made to electronic batch records after approval*", carries no completion criterion and is
recorded as having none rather than judged** — `IS-51`.

## 4. And now the part that is about this programme

**Nothing in the paragraph above says when the condition stopped obtaining.**

`EV-821` says what the eleven cells read on 2027-01-29. It does not say what they read on 2026-02-13, on
2026-06-30, or on any other day. **The control position register was built to record a position at a
vantage, and it does exactly that: it records the present state.** The control gap register records what is
absent, and `SYS-001` appears on no row of it. **Neither register contains a column, a row or a cell in
which the previous position, the act that changed it, the date of that act or the authority for it could be
written down.**

**`EV-822` records the search.** Every register this programme has built was searched, one by one, for a
place in which the change could be recorded: the systems register and the record-type register from Phase
02; the risk register and the attribute determination register from Phase 03; the obligation register and
the system rigour register from Phase 04; the control position register and the control gap register from
Phase 05; the auditable event register, the instance comparison register and the reintegration register
from Phase 06; and the retention derivation register, the backup and restore register and the paper record
register from Phase 07. **Fourteen registers, and the answer is none, in any of them.** The search is retained rather than its result alone, so that *no register
records it* can be tested as a search rather than taken as an impression — `AS-23`.

> **The programme built its own registers on the property it recorded the estate's records having, and did
> not notice until it was asked to show that something had been put right.**

**`21 CFR 11.10(e)` is the paragraph about record changes not obscuring previously recorded information.**
It is the paragraph under which `CG-15` records six systems on which a change to a stored value replaces
the previous value in the record the reviewer sees, and it is the paragraph `IS-32` turns on. **The parallel
to this programme's registers is real and it is not an identity, and both halves are stated.** Nobody edited
a cell; nothing was overwritten; every earlier phase's register stands exactly as published, dated, with its
prior position recoverable from it — so no previously recorded information has been obscured, and the
estate's failure and the programme's are not the same failure. **What is the same is the property that
produces both: an instrument that records a state and not a transition cannot answer a question about a
transition, and the answer it gives instead looks like an answer.** The estate defends its overwriting on
the ground that the system was built to show the current value and it shows one; **this programme has spent
seven phases declining to accept that defence about a record, and it does not offer it about a register.**
The registers were built to hold a determination at a vantage, they hold one, and they were not built to
evidence a remediation — **and the programme reached its eighth phase before anybody asked them to.**

**Three things follow and each of them is stated because a reader would otherwise supply a softer one.**

**First, the finding is not that the correction did not happen.** It happened; `RC-17` is Met; a change
control record exists. **The finding is that this programme's registers cannot evidence it.**

**Second, the finding is not that any Phase 05 determination is wrong.** **Not one cell moves and none is
marked provisional, disputed or subject to review** — `ADR-0049`, and `PR-54` has carried that misreading
since Phase 07. **The eleven cells on `SYS-001` stand exactly as published, and so do the other six hundred
and sixty.**

**Third, the registers were not built to evidence remediation and nobody claimed they were.** **A register
that records a position is a different instrument from a register that records a change**, and the
programme built the first because the first was what every phase needed. **What is recorded here is that
the programme reached its eighth phase, was asked for the first time to show that something had been put
right, and discovered that it had built no instrument that could.** **`IS-53`**, raised on 2027-04-06,
owned by Joachim Reuter. **`DP-47`** is the route — a register carrying the previous position, the act, the
date and the authority — and it is open.

## 5. The record that does exist, and where it is

**A change control record for the electronic batch record system exists in Helix's quality system.**
`EV-824` records its existence, obtained from the Head of Quality Assurance, who owns change control.

**`EV-824` records that the record exists and records nothing about its contents, and that is deliberate.**
**This programme did not build that record, has not read it as evidence, has not tested it and does not
annex it.** Reading its contents into a programme document and reporting them would be the programme
adopting a quality-system record it has not assessed, in the one chapter where it is describing its own
inability to evidence something — which is the precise moment at which borrowing somebody else's evidence
is least defensible.

**So the position is stated in two sentences that must be read together.**

> **The change control record exists, outside every register this programme built. The registers this
> programme built cannot evidence remediation, and they were not built to.**

**Neither sentence is allowed to swallow the other.** A reader who takes only the first has read the
finding away; a reader who takes only the second has read the correction away.

## 6. What this chapter does not establish

- **It does not close `OBS-6`, does not describe it as closed, and makes no representation to FDA.**
  **Corrected is a statement about a condition at a vantage. It is not a closure and it is not offered to
  anybody as one.**
- **It re-determines no cell of the control position register, and marks none provisional or disputed** —
  `ADR-0049`.
- **It does not report the estate as having corrected the class.** **`CG-15`'s six systems stand, `DP-29`
  is open, and `IS-32` — twenty-three systems holding a reason-for-change field Part 11 does not require,
  fourteen of them failing the requirement it does — has not moved.**
- **It does not claim that Part 11 requires a reason-for-change field.** **It does not**, and where Helix
  operates one that is Helix's choice — `PR-39`, `PR-46`.
- **It states no CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan and no remediation cost**, and it designs no
  register. **`DP-47` states what would be needed; it does not specify it.**
- **It says nothing about any individual's conduct**, and no document in this repository does.
- **It reaches no compliance conclusion and no legal conclusion** — `ADR-0057`.
