## 1. What Phase 07 established, in order

**One. That a retention period is derived and never chosen, and that the difference decides everything
that follows.** `ADR-0044`, `DEC-703`, Terrence Abbatiello, 2027-03-02. The derivation method was
authorised on the same day, **before any record type was read** — `EV-703` — because a period read off a
storage configuration is a description of a configuration wearing the clothes of a requirement.

**Two. That the operative words in `21 CFR 211.180(a)` are *specifically associated with a batch*, and
that they do the whole job.** `ADR-0045`, `DEC-705`, Dr Marguerite Oyelaran, 2027-03-05. **A record that
spans batches is not reached by `(a)`'s period at all**, and the programme records that rather than
applying the period by analogy.

**Three. The derivation itself.** **The 34 in-scope record types partition 17 / 3 / 14. 17 + 3 + 14 = 34.**
Seventeen take their period from `21 CFR 211.180(a)`; two from `21 CFR 211.180(b)` and one from
`21 CFR 211.198(b)`; **fourteen from nothing at all.** Recomputed across Phase 02's systems column the
same partition gives **75 + 6 + 40 = 121 — Phase 03's own 121 pairs, partitioned and not recounted.**
`logs/retention-derivation-register.md`.

**Four. `RT-02`.** **The electronic master production and control record — the document every batch at
Helix is made against — falls in Group C.** `21 CFR 211.180(a)`'s period attaches to a record specifically
associated with a batch of a drug product and the master is associated with all of them; `21 CFR 211.186`
requires it to be prepared, dated, signed and independently checked and sets no period — **and neither
*maintained* nor *retained* appears anywhere in that section.** The counter-argument, `21 CFR 211.188(a)`'s
accurate reproduction of the master inside every batch record, is named and disposed of at
`07.03-the-derivation.md §4`: the period reaches the reproduction and not the record it was taken from.
**Nothing tells Helix how long to keep the document that defines how its products are made.** `IS-45`.

**Five. The retention estate.** **`SYS-036`, `SYS-059`, `SYS-060` and `SYS-061`.** Three of the four are
owned by the Chief Information Officer, none by the quality unit, and `SYS-061` entered the inventory by
walk rather than from the register. **`21 CFR 211.180(c)` requires records required under Part 211, or
copies of them, to be readily available for authorised inspection during the retention period, and no
individual is named as accountable for that availability on any of the four.** `IS-47`.

**Six. Where the records are and where the activity was.** **`SYS-060` sits in the Ridgemont data centre
and holds `RT-12` and `RT-13`, which are generated at both sites.** `21 CFR 211.180(c)`'s saving reaches
records that *"can be immediately retrieved from another location by computer or other electronic means"*,
**and no retrieval from Cary has been demonstrated, timed or recorded.** `IS-48`.

**Seven. Backup and restore.** **A backup file is maintained on 54 of the 61 and 7 have none. 54 + 7 = 61.
A restore has been performed on 4, every one of them incident recovery rather than a test. On none of the
4 was what came back compared with what went in.** And **`21 CFR 211.68(b)`'s backup requirement is
conditional** — it applies *"except where certain data, such as calculations performed in connection with
laboratory analysis, are eliminated by computerization or other automated processes"* — **and on 0 of the
61 has Helix determined whether the carve-out reaches any of its data.** `06.13 §7` recorded that
determination as owed; **Phase 07 records that it is still owed and names who owes it.** `ADR-0047`,
`ADR-0048`, `IS-46`.

**Eight. The endpoint that does not exist.** `21 CFR 11.10(c)` is *"Protection of records to enable their
accurate and ready retrieval throughout the records retention period."* **24 of the 61 systems hold at
least one Group C record type; Phase 05 recorded `(c)` as Met on 44 of the 61; and 13 of those 44 are among
the 24.** Also derivable: **16 systems hold only Group C record types, 37 hold only Group A or B, 8 hold
both. 16 + 37 + 8 = 61.** **Phase 07 re-determines nothing and the thirteen cells stand exactly as Phase 05
published them.** `ADR-0049`, `IS-43`.

**Nine. A relation with two terms.** `21 CFR 11.10(e)`'s third sentence requires audit trail documentation
to be *"retained for a period at least as long as that required for the subject electronic records"*.
**Part 11 sets no period of its own; it sets a relation, and the second term is the record's period.**
**A fixed window is configured on 26 systems; on 11 of those it is shorter than the longest derived period
of a record type the system holds; and on 16 systems the relation has no second term at all.**

**Ten. The paper question, and it inverts Phase 02.** **Phase 02's printout route holds for `RT-09`,
`RT-22`, `RT-23` and `RT-29`, was correctly applied, and Phase 07 does not disturb it.** **What the route
did was move the four out of `21 CFR Part 11` and into `21 CFR 211.180`, where the paper is the record
required under Part 211 — and nobody followed them there.** **Retention location stated: 0 of 4. Individual
accountable named: 0 of 4. Period derived: 0 of 4. Reproduction under `21 CFR 211.180(d)`: 0 of 4.** **The
medium is thermal on 3 of the 4 and Helix holds a statement of its archival life from no source.** **And
`RT-09` is an in-process control result specifically associated with a batch, so `21 CFR 211.180(a)`'s
period attaches to the paper.** `ADR-0046`, `IS-49`.

**Eleven. Three registers, and they are the phase.** `logs/retention-derivation-register.md`,
`logs/backup-and-restore-register.md` and `logs/paper-record-register.md`. **Every count above is computed
in one of the three and in no other place.**

## 2. Three issues close, and two of them close by splitting

### 2.1 `IS-14` — CLOSED 2027-03-09

**Its text was that no retention period is recorded against any of the 34 Part 11 record types**, raised by
Priyanka Venkataraman on 2026-07-24 and carried through five phases. **`DEC-707`, taken by Terrence
Abbatiello and ratified at `GOV-32`.**

**Closure evidence, named:** `EV-703` the derivation method as authorised, dated before the first record
type was read · `EV-706` the 34 derivation records · `EV-707` the register · `EV-708` the reconciliation ·
`EV-710` the record of the split · `GOV-31` and `GOV-32`.

**`IS-14` was one issue and it is two.** **For 20 of the 34 the period is set by regulation and Helix never
wrote it down — it did not need a policy, it needed to read the regulation.** For 14 no predicate rule
supplies a period at all, a decision is owed, and nobody has taken it. **Two failures, two remedies, and
neither is progress against the other.**

**What `IS-14` never asked is whether anything operates a period once it is known** — whether a record is
held for it, whether anybody is accountable for its availability, and whether what survives can be
produced. **None of those questions existed as an entry when `IS-14` was raised.** `IS-46`, `IS-47` and
`IS-48` carry them now. **The remainder is `IS-44`.**

### 2.2 `IS-05` — CLOSED 2027-03-18

**Its text was that Helix cannot state, for any system, how long audit trail data is retained relative to
the retention period of the underlying record**, raised by Joachim Reuter on 2026-05-01. **`DEC-716`,
taken by Joachim Reuter and ratified at `GOV-35`.**

**Closure evidence, named:** `EV-715` the audit trail retention configuration read across the 61 on
2027-03-10 · `EV-707` the retention derivation register, which supplies the second term where there is one ·
`EV-730` the working paper · `EV-731` the closure record · **`GOV-31` the method authorisation, `GOV-32`
the derivation accepted, and `GOV-35` the ratification.**

**Helix can now state the relation for every system.** **What `IS-05` never asked is what happens where
the relation has one term.** It was written on the assumption — reasonable in May 2026 and wrong in a way
nobody could have known then — that every record in scope had a period somewhere and Helix simply could not
state it. **On 16 systems the comparison is not unstatable because nobody looked; it is unstatable because
the second term does not exist.** **The remainder is `IS-44`.**

### 2.3 `IS-42` — CLOSED 2027-03-18

**Its text was that the record cannot show what happened, and the data it cannot show it about is retained
somewhere; nobody has established for how long, in what form, or whether the copy that survives is a true
one.** Raised by Dr Sunita Raghunathan on 2027-02-26. **`DEC-717`, taken by Dr Marguerite Oyelaran and
ratified at `GOV-35`.**

**Closure evidence, named:** `EV-707`, `EV-717` and `EV-725` the three registers · `EV-711` the retention
estate extract · `EV-713` and `EV-714` the location and retrieval records · `EV-732` the closure record ·
`GOV-32`, `GOV-33`, `GOV-34` and `GOV-35`.

**It asked three questions and Phase 07 answers two of them in the terms they were asked.** **For how
long:** `RT-12` and `RT-13` take their period from `21 CFR 211.180(a)` and Helix had never read it.
**In what form:** the data is on `SYS-036`, `SYS-060` and `SYS-061`; all three carry a backup file; no
restore has been performed on two of them and the one performed on the third was incident recovery whose
output nobody compared with its input. **Whether the copy that survives is a true one is answered in part
and no further** — the provision has been read at `07.11-original-or-a-true-copy-on-the-cgmp-side.md`, the
position is stated, **and no copy has been demonstrated to be true.** `IS-15` carries the attribute and has
not moved.

**And what `IS-42` never asked is whether any of the arrangements it asked about has ever been looked at by
anybody whose job it was.** **That limb becomes `IS-50` and it is Phase 08's.**

### 2.4 The pattern, now seven deep

`IS-02` recorded that no written determination existed. `IS-10` recorded that the determination existed and
nothing required it to be maintained. `IS-21` recorded that the assessment existed and nothing said what to
do about it. `IS-28` recorded that the requirement existed and nothing had been done under it. `IS-35`
recorded that the position existed and nothing held it in place. `IS-42` recorded that the record could not
show what happened and nobody knew where the material that could was kept. **`IS-50` records that
everything now has a period, an owner or an explicit gap — and that nobody whose job it was has ever looked
at any of it.**

**Each phase closed the previous question by producing the artefact and found the artefact upstream of what
anybody wanted.** **This is the first time the answer has been that the artefacts are all there.**

## 6. What is still not true

Stated as absences, because an absence written down is an absence somebody can act on.

- **No named individual has determined a retention period for any of the fourteen record types no
  predicate rule reaches** — `IS-44`, `DP-38`, and `RT-02` is one of them.
- **Nothing establishes that any of the twenty derived periods is operated on any system.** A period read
  is a fact about the regulation.
- **No individual is named as accountable, under `21 CFR 211.180(c)`, for the availability of the records
  the four retention systems hold** — `IS-47`.
- **No retrieval of a Cary-generated record from the Ridgemont data centre has been demonstrated, timed or
  recorded** — `IS-48`.
- **No restore has been performed as a test on any of the 61, and no restored content has been compared
  with what went in** — `IS-46`.
- **On no system has Helix determined whether `21 CFR 211.68(b)`'s carve-out reaches its data** — `IS-46`,
  `DP-41`, owed since `06.13 §7`.
- **No reproduction has been made under `21 CFR 211.180(d)` of any of the four paper record types**, and
  **Helix holds a statement of the thermal medium's archival life from no source** — `IS-49`.
- **Not one of the 38 obligations Phase 04 established is discharged**, no validation package has been
  executed and no system validation plan has been written — `IS-34`.
- **Not one of the 34 control gaps is closed.**
- **Not one of the ten `ND` pairs on the two chromatography systems has become determinable** — `IS-16`,
  `IS-27`.
- **None of the nine SOPs approved in Phase 04 has been applied to anything, and `SOP-DI-05` has still not
  fired on any change** — `PR-32`, `DP-32`.
- **`IS-01` is unanswered and unweighted**, and Phase 08 is where it is returned to.

## 7. The question Phase 07 leaves open

> **Everything now has a period, an owner or an explicit gap where one of those is missing — and not one of
> the arrangements has ever been examined by anybody at Helix whose job it was to look.**

**`IS-50`**, raised at publication, owned by Terrence Abbatiello, open.

**Take it in order.** Twenty record types have a period the regulation sets and fourteen have none; a
register now says which is which. Sixty-one systems have a backup position; four of them have ever been
restored and none of the four was checked. Four systems constitute the retention estate; three are owned by
the Chief Information Officer and none by the quality unit. Four record types carry their predicate rule on
paper and nobody is named against any of them. **Every one of those arrangements existed before this
programme opened.** Every one of them was in place through four internal audits between 2023-06 and
2025-09, through the inspection of 2026-02, and through six phases of this programme.

**And no periodic review exists at all.** Not for the retention position, not for the backup arrangement,
not for the paper, not for the audit trail configurations Phase 06 read, and not for the six hundred and
seventy-one control positions Phase 05 determined. **`IS-35` closed in Phase 06 with the observation that a
position is not a control that will still be there next year, and named the mechanism as Phase 08's.
`IS-42` closed here and named the same absence from a different direction.** **They are the same absence,
and Phase 08 is where it is looked at.**

**Phase 07 is the shortest phase in the programme — twenty-one days from the Phase 06 vantage, nineteen of
them working days from `DEC-701` — and this is what it bought: three registers, three closures — two by
splitting — eight issues, and a question about whether anybody has ever looked at any of it.**
