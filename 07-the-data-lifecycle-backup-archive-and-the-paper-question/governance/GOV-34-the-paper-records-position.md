## 3. Item one — the printout route holds, and is not disturbed

**The committee opened the item by recording the position and asked that it be minuted before anything
else.**

> **Phase 02's printout route holds for `RT-09`, `RT-22`, `RT-23` and `RT-29`. It was correctly applied,
> it was tested rather than asserted, and nothing at this sitting disturbs it.**

**Helix had treated eleven record types as outside Part 11 because they were printed.** Phase 02 refused
to carry any of the eleven forward on the existing position, **and refused equally to declare all eleven in
scope without testing**, which would have put four record types and their instruments into a control
programme they do not need. It tested each against **the printout route's three conditions — two FDA
states expressly and a third Helix derives from the narrow interpretation's second category.** Seven
failed the first condition. **Four survived, on facts, evidenced instrument by instrument at `EV-223`** —
the testers, balances, meters and calibrators produce a fixed output and retain nothing. `ADR-0010`,
`DEC-210`, Dr Sunita Raghunathan, 2026-07-07.

**`PR-55` was opened at this item**, before the finding was reported, so that the risk was on the record
ahead of the material that creates it: **that somebody reads `IS-49` as evidence that the route was wrongly
applied, or as a reason to re-open the determination.** **The committee refused that reading in advance**
and recorded that the remedy `DP-42` asks for is a location, an individual, a period and a medium
statement — **not a re-determination.**

## 4. Item two — the register approved, and `ADR-0046` ratified

**`DEC-714`, taken by Priyanka Venkataraman at this sitting.**

**Approved:** `../logs/paper-record-register.md` — `PP-01` to `PP-04`, one row per record type, carrying
Phase 02's determination on every row and re-arguing it on none.

**`DEC-708` and `ADR-0046`, taken by Priyanka Venkataraman on 2027-03-09, ratified at this sitting.**
**A determination that a record is not a Part 11 record is not a determination that it is unregulated.**

**The committee minuted the mechanism in the guidance's own terms.** The route's first condition is that
the paper record *"meet all the requirements of the applicable predicate rules"* — **so a record type that
reaches the route at all is a record type a predicate rule requires.** The predicate rules were the
premise of the route and not a casualty of it.

> **The route did not remove the four from regulation. It moved them out of `21 CFR Part 11` and into
> `21 CFR 211.180`, where the paper is the record required under Part 211 — and nobody followed them
> there.**

## 5. Item three — the four counts

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is a retention location stated for the paper? | **No, on 4 of 4** |
| Is an individual named as accountable for it? | **No, on 4 of 4** |
| Has a retention period been derived for it? | **No, on 4 of 4** |
| Has a reproduction been made under `21 CFR 211.180(d)`? | **No, on 4 of 4** |

**Four questions, four record types, and the same answer sixteen times.**

**On the location.** The paper is filed into the in-process log, the analytical worksheet or the
calibration record, and the filing is what the operator or analyst does rather than what a document says
must happen. **No procedure, register or record states where the paper is retained thereafter.**

**On the individual.** **`ADR-0008` has required a named individual owner for every control, procedure,
record and obligation in this programme since Phase 01, and for these four there is none.** There is an
instrument owner; **an instrument owner is not an owner of paper the instrument has already printed and
forgotten.**

**On the period and the reproduction.** No period was derived for any of the four by any earlier phase,
because no earlier phase was asking. **`21 CFR 211.180(d)` permits records required under Part 211 to be
retained either as original records or as true copies** such as photocopies, microfilm, microfiche or
other accurate reproductions — **and Helix has not used the permission on any of the four.**

## 6. Item four — the medium

**The medium is thermal on three of the four — `RT-09`, `RT-22` and `RT-23`. `RT-29`'s certificate is
printed on plain paper.**

**Helix holds a statement of the archival life of the thermal stock from no source.** The enquiry at
`EV-724` records the sources searched — the stock supplier, the four instrument vendors, Helix's own
qualification records, and the standards set the programme works to — **and records that none of them held
one.** The list of what was searched is retained so that *none found* can be tested as a search.

**The committee minuted the boundary in one sentence, at the chair's request:**

> **Helix cannot answer a question about a record it is required to keep, and this committee makes no
> claim about what the answer is.**

**No statement was made that thermal print fades, that any ticket has faded, or that any of the four is at
risk.** Every one of those would be a claim about a property nobody has established — **which is the
finding itself, made in the other direction.**

**And the committee recorded that `RT-29`'s position is different in kind rather than absent.** Plain paper
raises a different set of questions and Helix has not asked those either.

## 7. Item five — `RT-09`

**`EV-727` records it and Bernard Kwiatkowski attended for it.**

**`RT-09` is the tablet hardness and friability test printout, and it is an in-process control result
specifically associated with a batch.** The ticket carries the individual measurements, the mean, the
instrument identity, the date and time and **the sample identity** — a sample from a named batch, taken
during the manufacture of that batch. `21 CFR 211.188(b)(5)` requires in-process and laboratory control
results to be documented in the batch production and control record.

**`21 CFR 211.180(a)` reaches** *"[a]ny production, control, or distribution record that is required to be
maintained in compliance with this part **and is specifically associated with a batch of a drug
product**…"* — **and nothing in the paragraph is about electronic records.**

**So `21 CFR 211.180(a)`'s period attaches to the paper**, and it has attached to it throughout: before
this programme, before the inspection, and before Phase 02 determined that the record type is outside Part
11.

**The sentence the committee minuted:**

> **A period the regulation sets, on a record nobody owns, on a medium nobody has characterised.**

**And the committee recorded the difference between `RT-09` and the other three, so that the other three
are not read as the smaller case.** **`RT-09` lacks an arrangement for a period the regulation has already
set. `RT-22`, `RT-23` and `RT-29` lack the arrangement and the determination both.**

**`IS-49` was raised at this sitting**, owned by Dr Sunita Raghunathan, with `DP-42` as the route.

## 8. What this sitting expressly did not decide

- **It re-opened no Phase 02 determination.** **The printout route holds for all four record types**, and
  no record type moves into or out of Part 11 scope by anything decided here.
- **It stated no retention location, named no individual and derived no period for any of the four.**
  **Those are Helix's to decide and are recorded as owed** — `DP-42`.
- **It made no claim about the durability of any medium**, in either direction.
- **It examined no ticket, worksheet or certificate for condition**, and no paper record was inspected.
- **It brought none of the four inside the 34 in-scope record types, the 121 pairs or the 61 systems**, and
  it created, changed or implied no cell of any Part 11 register.
- **It stated no CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan and no remediation cost.**
- **It disposed of neither `OBS-2` nor `OBS-4`** — Phase 08.
- **It reached no legal conclusion and forecast no inspection outcome.**

**The sentence the committee closed the item on:**

> **A route correctly applied is not an obligation discharged.**
