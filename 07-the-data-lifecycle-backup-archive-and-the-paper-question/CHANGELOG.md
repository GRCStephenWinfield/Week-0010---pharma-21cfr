# CHANGELOG — Phase 07: The Data Lifecycle: Backup, Archive and the Paper Question

## 1.0 — 2027-03-19

First issue of the Phase 07 record set, speaking as at the vantage of **2027-03-19**.

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| **A retention period is derived from the predicate rule, never chosen.** Where the regulation sets it Helix reads it; where nothing does, a named individual decides and the decision is recorded as a decision — **and Helix had done neither** | `07.01`, ADR-0044, `DEC-703` |
| **THE OPERATIVE WORDS DO THE WHOLE JOB.** `21 CFR 211.180(a)` reaches *"Any production, control, or distribution record that is required to be maintained in compliance with this part and is specifically associated with a batch of a drug product"*. **A record that spans batches — a cleaning log, a trend, a specification, a training record — is not reached by `(a)`'s period at all** | `07.02`, ADR-0045, `DEC-705` |
| **The 34 in-scope record types partition 17 / 3 / 14, and the pairs partition 75 / 6 / 40 = 121 — Phase 03's own 121, recomputed rather than re-counted.** **`IS-14` was recorded as one issue and it is two: for 20 the period is set by regulation and Helix never wrote it down; for 14 nothing sets one and a decision is owed that nobody has taken** | `07.03`, `07.04`, retention derivation register |
| **THE MASTER PRODUCTION AND CONTROL RECORD HAS A RETENTION PERIOD SET BY NOTHING.** `21 CFR 211.180(a)`'s period attaches to a record specifically associated with a batch and the master is associated with all of them; **`21 CFR 211.186` contains neither the word *maintained* nor the word *retained*.** The strongest counter — that `21 CFR 211.188(a)` requires the batch record to include an accurate reproduction of the appropriate master — **is named and disposed of: a reproduction inside a batch record is retained on that batch's period and says nothing about the master, its versions or its approvals** | `07.03`, `IS-45` |
| **THE PAPER QUESTION, AND IT INVERTS PHASE 02 WITHOUT DISTURBING IT.** The printout route holds for all four record types and was correctly applied. **What it did was move them out of `21 CFR Part 11` and into `21 CFR 211.180`, where the paper is now the record required under Part 211 — and nobody followed them there.** FDA's own answer is that *"[a] paper printout or static record may satisfy retention requirements if it is the original record or a true copy of the original record"* | `07.10`, ADR-0046, `DEC-708` |
| **On none of the four is a retention location stated, an individual accountable named, a period derived, or a reproduction made under `21 CFR 211.180(d)`. The medium is thermal on three of the four and Helix holds a statement of its archival life from no source.** **And the tablet hardness and friability ticket is an in-process control result specifically associated with a batch, so `21 CFR 211.180(a)`'s period attaches to it** — a period the regulation sets, on a record nobody owns, on a medium nobody has characterised | `07.10`, paper record register, `IS-49` |
| **A BACKUP NOBODY HAS RESTORED IS A CLAIM ABOUT A FILE, NOT A CONTROL OVER A RECORD.** A backup file is maintained on **54 of 61**; a restore has been performed on **4**, every one as incident recovery rather than as a test; **on none of the four was what came back compared with what went in** | `07.07`, ADR-0047, `IS-46` |
| **`21 CFR 211.68(b)`'s backup requirement is conditional** — *"except where certain data, such as calculations performed in connection with laboratory analysis, are eliminated by computerization or other automated processes"* — **and on none of the 61 systems has Helix determined whether the carve-out reaches any of its data** | `07.07`, ADR-0048, `DEC-713` |
| **`21 CFR 11.10(c)` HAS AN ENDPOINT AND ON THIRTEEN SYSTEMS THE ENDPOINT DOES NOT EXIST.** The paragraph is *"Protection of records to enable their accurate and ready retrieval throughout the records retention period."* **24 systems hold at least one record type nothing sets a period for; Phase 05 recorded `(c)` as Met on 44 of 61; 13 of the 44 are among the 24.** **Nothing is re-determined and the thirteen cells stand exactly as published** | `07.08`, ADR-0049, `IS-43` |
| **FDA declined to enforce `21 CFR 11.10(c)` and pointed firms at the predicate rule instead** — *"such as 21 CFR 211.180(c) and (d)"* — **which are the two paragraphs that presuppose a retention period without supplying one** | `07.08` |
| **`21 CFR Part 11` sets no retention period of its own.** `21 CFR 11.10(e)` sets a **relation** — the audit trail retained *"for a period at least as long as that required for the subject electronic records"* — **and the second term comes from the predicate rule.** A fixed window is configured on 26 systems, is shorter than the longest derived period of a record type held on 11 of them, and **on 16 systems there is no second term at all** | `07.09`, `IS-05` closes |
| **Three of the four systems that constitute the retention estate are owned by the Chief Information Officer, none by the quality unit, and one of the four entered the inventory by walk rather than from the register.** **`21 CFR 211.180(c)` has three sentences, and its middle one — records *"shall be subject to photocopying or other means of reproduction as part of such inspection"* — is an obligation the retrieval saving does not reach** | `07.05`, `07.06`, `IS-47`, `IS-48` |
| The question left open: **everything now has a period, an owner, or an explicit gap where one of those is missing — and not one of the arrangements has ever been examined by anybody at Helix whose job it was to look** | `07.13`, `IS-50`, `DEC-718` |
