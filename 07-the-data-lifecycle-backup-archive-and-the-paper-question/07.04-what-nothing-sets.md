*Fourteen record types whose retention period is set by no regulation at all — and the difference between a rule nobody read and a decision nobody took.*

---

## 1. Two failures wearing one identifier

**`IS-14` has read since 2026-07-24 that no retention period is recorded against any of the 34 Part 11
record types.** It was raised in Phase 02, carried through Phases 03, 04, 05 and 06 unchanged, and cited
in every one of them as the reason `21 CFR 11.10(e)`'s retention limb could not be assessed. **It was
correct throughout. It was also two issues.**

**For twenty of the thirty-four, the period is set by regulation and Helix had never written it down.**
Seventeen take it from `21 CFR 211.180(a)`, two from `21 CFR 211.180(b)` and one from
`21 CFR 211.198(b)` — `logs/retention-derivation-register.md`. **Those periods were in force before this
programme opened, before the inspection, and before the four internal audits that found nothing.** They
did not need to be decided, agreed, negotiated or approved. **Helix did not need a retention policy for
them. It needed to read the regulation.**

**For fourteen, no predicate rule supplies a period at all.** No provision sets one, no guidance sets one,
and no amount of further reading will produce one. **Somebody at Helix has to decide, and the decision has
to be recorded as a decision with a named individual against it** — `ADR-0044`, `DEC-703`, Terrence
Abbatiello, 2027-03-02. **Nobody has decided.**

**`IS-14` closes and `IS-44` opens in its place**, carrying the fourteen. `DEC-707`, 2027-03-09,
Terrence Abbatiello, ratified at `GOV-32`; the closure evidence is named at `logs/raid-log.md §1.6` and at
`EV-710`.

**The reason the split is the finding, rather than a piece of bookkeeping, is that the two halves have
nothing in common except the sentence that used to describe them both.** One is a reading failure and is
closed by reading. The other is a decision failure and is closed by deciding. **A remediation plan built
against the old wording would have proposed a retention schedule, and a retention schedule would have
answered the first half by accident and the second half by writing something into a column.**

fourteen reasons are fourteen different reasons.** That matters more than it looks: **a register that
carried one sentence fourteen times would be a register in which nobody had asked the question fourteen
times.** The search behind each is retained at `EV-706`, and `AS-23`'s discipline applies — a *None*
records the absence of a provision that was searched for, not the absence of a search.

## 3. What it means that a decision is owed

**A rule broken and a decision owed are not the same object, and the programme has been careful in six
phases not to let one wear the other's clothes.**

**Nothing in this chapter says Helix is in breach of `21 CFR 211.180` in respect of the fourteen.** The
paragraph that sets periods sets them for records specifically associated with a batch and for component,
container, closure and labelling records; **it does not purport to set a period for a specification, a
training record or a master production and control record, and a firm cannot fail to meet a period that
does not exist.** **An open gap is Helix's own assessment of its own position. It is not a violation, not
a breach, not a non-compliance, not a finding and not an inspectional observation.**

**What is true is narrower and more uncomfortable.** `21 CFR 211.180(c)` requires all records required
under Part 211, or copies of them, to be readily available for authorised inspection **during the retention
period**, and to be subject to photocopying or other means of reproduction as part of that inspection.
`21 CFR 11.10(c)` requires protection enabling accurate and ready retrieval **throughout the
records retention period**. `21 CFR 11.10(e)`'s third sentence requires audit trail documentation to be
retained **for a period at least as long as that required for the subject electronic records**. **Three
provisions across two parts each presuppose a retention period and none of them supplies one**, and on
fourteen record types there is nothing for any of the three to attach to. `07.08-the-endpoint-that-does-not-exist.md`
and `07.09-a-relation-with-two-terms.md` are what follows.

**And a decision nobody takes is still a decision that gets made.** A storage platform has a default. A
licence tier has a cap. An administrator has a housekeeping setting, and a system that fills up gets a
lifecycle rule written for it by whoever is on duty. **Every one of the fourteen record types is on a
system that will eventually stop holding it, and if no named individual decides when, the decision will be
taken by a configuration.** `PR-51` carries that, and it is the reason `logs/retention-derivation-register.md`
prints *Not set by any predicate rule* rather than leaving the cell blank: **a blank invites completion.**

**The remedy is fourteen decisions, not one policy.** `DP-38` asks for exactly that — a determination for
each of the fourteen, by a named individual, recorded as a decision — and it is owned by Dr Marguerite
Oyelaran, because `21 CFR 211.22` gives the quality unit the responsibility and authority the decision
sits under and `21 CFR 211.22(d)` requires its responsibilities and procedures to be in writing and
followed. **A single schedule signed once would satisfy a filing requirement and would not be fourteen
decisions.**

## 4. What this chapter refuses

**It proposes no period for any of the fourteen.** Not a figure, not a range, not a floor, not a ceiling
and not a *pending* value. **The temptation here is considerable and it is worth naming.** For most of the
fourteen a defensible period is easy to reason towards — a specification could plausibly be kept for as
long as any batch made against it, a training record for as long as any record the person signed. **Every
one of those sentences is a decision, and writing it in this chapter would take a decision that belongs to
somebody else, in a document nobody approved for the purpose, at a vantage where the operational facts
that ought to inform it have not been assembled.** `ADR-0050`, `DEC-717`, Dr Marguerite Oyelaran,
2027-03-18.

**It applies no period by analogy.** `21 CFR 211.180(a)`'s period is not stretched to reach a record that
resembles a batch record, and `ADR-0045` is the decision that forbids it. **The analogy is comfortable and
it is not the text**, and a programme that stretches the words has quietly decided it knows better than the
provision what the provision means — and has then lost the ability to say which of its periods are the
regulation's and which are its own.

**It does not report the twenty as an achievement.** Twenty periods have been read. **Nothing has been
retained for a day longer or a day shorter because of it**, nobody is accountable for any of them, and
`07.05-the-retention-estate.md`, `07.06-where-the-records-are-and-where-the-activity-was.md` and
`07.07-backup-is-not-restore.md` are three separate accounts of what would have to be true before a period
read could become a period operated. **`PR-50` carries the risk that the twenty are reported inside Helix
as retention resolved**, and the sentence the programme holds to is that **a period is not an
arrangement.**

**It disposes of nothing.** No record is dispositioned, no record is destroyed, and this phase touches no
record in the estate. **Disposal is not this phase's subject and no document in it authorises one.**

## 5. What is still not true

- **No named individual has determined a retention period for any of the fourteen** — `IS-44`, `DP-38`.
- **No procedure in Helix's quality system requires a retention period to be derived, recorded or
  maintained for any record type** — `IS-24`, `AS-35`.
- **Nothing establishes that any of the twenty derived periods is operated on any system.** A period read
  is a fact about the regulation.
- **`21 CFR 211.180(a)`'s period runs from the expiration date of the batch, and nothing in this phase
  tested whether that date is retrievable for as long as the period lasts** — `AS-48`.
- **`RT-02`'s period is set by nothing, and `RT-02` is the electronic master production and control
  record** — `IS-45`.
