## 1. What this phase is

**This is the phase that answers what every earlier phase deferred: for how long, in what form, and whether
what survives is a true copy.**

Phase 02 determined which records are Part 11 records and recorded that no retention period was written
down against any of them. Phase 03 assessed five attributes against every record-type-and-system pair and
found that the attribute Helix could establish least often was the one about originals and copies. Phase 04
required evidence and set no period. Phase 05 determined a control position for every paragraph of
`21 CFR 11.10` on every system in scope, including the paragraph whose obligation runs *"throughout the
records retention period"*. Phase 06 established at depth what two chromatography instances record, and
closed with the observation that the data the record cannot speak about is retained somewhere and that
nobody had established for how long, in what form, or whether the copy that survives is a true one.

**Phase 07 does that work.** It reads `21 CFR 211.180` whole and derives a retention period for every
in-scope record type from the predicate rule or records that no predicate rule sets one; it establishes the
backup and restore position across the estate against `21 CFR 211.68(b)`'s conditional requirement; it
records what `21 CFR 11.10(c)` and `21 CFR 11.10(e)` require where the period they both depend on does not
exist; and it follows the four record types Phase 02's printout route correctly removed from
`21 CFR Part 11` into `21 CFR 211.180`, where the paper is now the record required under Part 211.
**`IS-05`, `IS-14` and `IS-42` close here, and two of the three close by splitting.**

**The vantage is 2027-03-19, a Friday, `MS-11`, twenty-one days after the Phase 06 vantage — shorter again
than Phase 06, which was itself the shortest phase in the programme.** It is short because its method was
fixed before the first derivation was made, because the population it works on was fixed by Phase 02 and
has not moved, and because reading a regulation against a register is faster than walking an estate;
**nothing about its length is apologised for here or anywhere else in it.**

## 2. The boundary, stated hard

**Phase 07 states no retention policy and no retention schedule, and it sets no period for any record type
no predicate rule reaches.**

A phase that has read every applicable retention provision and enumerated every system that holds a
regulated record is one short paragraph away from the document the organisation actually wants, and **that
paragraph is not written.** Reading a regulation is not an exercise of authority; deciding how long Helix
keeps its own records is, and it belongs to the accountable owners and to the quality unit rather than to a
programme. `07.01-a-period-is-derived-not-chosen.md` argues it and **`ADR-0050`** records it.

**Phase 07 re-determines nothing.** The `21 CFR 11.10(c)` determinations Phase 05 published stand exactly
as published; no Phase 03 pair is re-scored; no Phase 04 obligation is re-banded. What this phase records
is that a retrieval obligation was determined against a period that does not exist, **which is a fact about
the obligation's second term and not a criticism of any determination** — `ADR-0049`.

**And Phase 07 does not re-open Phase 02's applicability determination.** **The printout route holds for all
four record types Phase 02 published as out of scope, it was correctly applied, and this phase does not
disturb it for a moment.** What the phase adds is what the route left undischarged: **a determination that a
record is not a Part 11 record is not a determination that it is unregulated** — `ADR-0046`,
`07.10-the-paper-question.md`.

**`OBS-2` and `OBS-4` are disposed of here least of all.** Both are Phase 08's, and nothing in this phase
reaches either.

## 3. Read this first

Seven method decisions, and everything else in the phase follows from them. **None of them is a result.**

**A retention period is derived from the predicate rule and never chosen.** Where the regulation sets it,
Helix reads it and the reading can be checked; where the regulation sets none, a named individual must
decide and the decision is recorded as a decision. **An unchosen period is not a short period; it is no
period.** `07.01-a-period-is-derived-not-chosen.md`, **`ADR-0044`**.

**A record not specifically associated with a batch is not reached by `21 CFR 211.180(a)`'s period.** The
paragraph's operative words do the whole job, and the programme records the fact rather than applying the
period by analogy — because **a period applied by analogy has no basis to be tested against.**
`07.02-specifically-associated-with-a-batch.md`, **`ADR-0045`**.

**A determination that a record is not a Part 11 record is not a determination that it is unregulated.**
The four record types the printout route reached are followed into `21 CFR 211.180`.
`07.10-the-paper-question.md`, **`ADR-0046`**.

**A backup that has never been restored is recorded as a backup and never as a control.** A file whose
restorability nobody has demonstrated is a claim about a file, not a control over a record.
`07.07-backup-is-not-restore.md`, **`ADR-0047`**.

**`21 CFR 211.68(b)`'s backup requirement is conditional, and the condition is determined per record type
rather than assumed.** The carve-out names laboratory calculation, and whether it reaches any of Helix's
data is a determination and not an assumption. `07.07-backup-is-not-restore.md`, **`ADR-0048`**.

**Where a Phase 05 determination was made against an obligation with no endpoint, that is recorded and the
cell stands.** `07.08-the-endpoint-that-does-not-exist.md`, **`ADR-0049`**.

**Phase 07 states no retention policy and no archive design.** It establishes what the regulation requires
and what Helix holds, and stops there. `07.01-a-period-is-derived-not-chosen.md §11`, **`ADR-0050`**.

## 5. What Phase 07 deliberately does not establish

None of the following exists at the vantage, and nothing in this phase states, implies or forecasts any of
it. `07.12-what-this-does-not-establish.md` is the full inventory.

- **No retention policy, no retention schedule, no archive design, no migration plan, no storage
  specification and no vendor** — `ADR-0050`.
- **No period set, proposed, ranged or bounded for any Group C record type.** **The decision is owed, and a
  named individual owes it** — `07.04-what-nothing-sets.md`, `ADR-0044`.
- **No authority to destroy anything.** A derived period is a floor, not a deadline.
- **No re-opening of Phase 02's applicability determination.** **The printout route holds for all four, it
  was correctly applied, and this phase says so plainly** — `ADR-0046`.
- **No re-determination of any Phase 05 cell, no re-scoring of any Phase 03 pair and no re-banding of any
  Phase 04 obligation** — `ADR-0049`.
- **No disposition of `OBS-2` and no disposition of `OBS-4`** — Phase 08, `MS-12`.
- **No CAPA, no deviation, no remediation plan, no remediation cost, no sequence and no priority.**
- **No validation outcome.** No package executed, no protocol run, no test result. **The evidence Phase 04's
  framework requires is owed in full** — `ADR-0034`, `IS-34`. **A restore performed to recover from an
  incident is not a test.**
- **No periodic review, no internal audit result, no third-party audit result and no inspection outcome of
  any kind.** Phases 08 and 09. **Vashti Okonjo & Partners has audited nothing.**
- **No characterisation of any reintegration** as proper, improper, justified or unjustified — `ADR-0043`.
- **No claim that `21 CFR Part 11` sets a retention period.** `21 CFR 11.10(e)` sets a **relation** whose
  second term comes from the predicate rule — `07.02-specifically-associated-with-a-batch.md §11`.
- **No answer to `IS-01`**, and **no promotion of any of its four candidate explanations** by statement, by
  ordering or by tone — `ADR-0012`. **`IS-01` stays open and stays unweighted.**
- **No legal conclusion of any kind**, and no forecast of what any future inspection will find.

## 12. Standing framing

These positions hold across all nine phases and are restated here so that a reader arriving at Phase 07 has
them.

- **There is no Part 11 certification, no Part 11 certificate and no FDA-approved or FDA-validated
  software.** A product has **features that support** compliance; compliance is a property of Helix's
  validated system in its intended use, under its procedures, operated by its trained people. **Outsourcing
  to a service provider transfers no responsibility.**
- **Part 11 applies to records, not to systems**, and this repository never says "a Part 11 system" without
  saying which records make it one — `ADR-0001`, `01.03 §2`.
- **Enforcement discretion is FDA's stated intention, and the set is stated with all its limbs.** FDA has
  stated an intention to exercise it over **`21 CFR 11.10(a)`, `(b)`, `(c)` and `(e)` in full**, over
  **`21 CFR 11.10(k)` in part** — the guidance names `(k)(2)` — over **the corresponding requirements in
  `21 CFR 11.30`**, and over **all Part 11 requirements for legacy systems operational before 20 August
  1997 that meet the guidance's conditions, against which no Helix system has been assessed.** **It is not
  a legal exemption, it is not a safe harbour, and it confers no rights**; the guidance carries FDA's own
  disclaimer that *"It does not create or confer any rights for or on any person and does not operate to
  bind FDA or the public"*. **`21 CFR 211.68`, `21 CFR 211.100(b)` and `21 CFR 211.180` survive it
  entirely** — FDA's own words, whole: *"Persons must still comply with all applicable predicate rule
  requirements for record retention and availability (e.g., §§ 211.180(c),(d), 108.25(g), and
  108.35(h))."* **The parenthetical is FDA's and it names `21 CFR 211.180(c)` and `(d)` — the availability
  paragraph and the true-copy permission this phase is built on.** `ADR-0003`, `ADR-0035`.
- **FDA's Computer Software Assurance guidance is device guidance under `21 CFR Part 820`, as amended by
  the Quality Management System Regulation, and does not apply to Helix.** Adopted voluntarily as good
  practice, **labelled as adopted every time** — `ADR-0005`, `ADR-0026`.
- **GAMP 5 Second Edition, ISPE, July 2022, is good-practice guidance and never a requirement**, and **no
  software-category count and no enumeration of the categories is published in this repository.**
- **FDA uses plain ALCOA, and ALCOA does not map onto `21 CFR 11.10`** — FDA's own footnote 5 maps the
  attributes to the CGMP predicate rules and cites Part 11 nowhere, `ADR-0019`. **The four additional
  attributes are MHRA's**, MHRA itself refers to the shorter acronym, states there is no difference in
  expectations regardless of which is used and defines all four, and **it does not reject them.** **No
  originator is named for either set.**
- **Part 11 requires no reason-for-change field, no read-event logging, no cryptographic hashing under
  `21 CFR 11.70`, no multi-factor authentication under `21 CFR 11.200` and no password interval under
  `21 CFR 11.300(b)`.** Where Helix implements any of them, it is Helix's decision and this repository says
  so — `ADR-0036`.
- **`21 CFR Part 11` sets no retention period of its own.** `21 CFR 11.10(e)` sets a **relation** whose
  second term comes from the predicate rule, and `21 CFR 11.10(c)`'s obligation runs to an endpoint the
  predicate rule supplies.
- **`21 CFR 211.68(b)` requires appropriate controls to be exercised over computer or related systems to
  assure that changes in master production and control records or other records are instituted only by
  authorised personnel, and `21 CFR 211.68(a)` requires equipment so used to be routinely calibrated,
  inspected or checked according to a written program designed to assure proper performance.** **It
  imposes no general computerised-system validation requirement**, and the word *validation* appears in the
  section once, inside `(b)`'s backup carve-out.
- **A closed system is defined by who controls access** — `21 CFR 11.3(b)(4)`. Hosting is not the test, and
  **all sixty-one systems in scope are closed** — `ADR-0011`.
- **`21 CFR 11.3(b)` contains nine definitions and Part 11 contains ten sections.** There is no §11.60, no
  §11.400 and no Subpart D. **Part 11 defines neither "audit trail" nor "metadata" nor "data integrity" nor
  "static record" nor "dynamic record" nor "archive"** — FDA defines the first five in the December 2018
  drug CGMP data integrity guidance. Citations in this repository are exact and can be looked up.
- **A Form FDA 483 is not a legal finding.** No document in this repository reaches a legal conclusion of
  any kind, and **never forecast what a future inspection will find.** **An open gap is Helix's own
  assessment of its own position** — not a violation, not a breach, not a non-compliance, not a finding and
  not an inspectional observation.
- **Applicability is not integrity, a rating is not a validation status, a requirement is not a control, a
  control position is not validation evidence, a depth finding is not an estate finding, and a derived
  period is not a decision Helix has taken.**
- **All content about Helix is fictional and illustrative.**

## 13. How to read this phase

Read **`07.10`** if you read only one chapter. It is the one in which a determination the programme got
right turns out to have been the start of an obligation, and it takes nothing back from the determination.

Read **`07.01`** and **`07.02`** for the two method decisions everything else rests on — the first for how
a period is arrived at, the second for what `21 CFR 211.180` actually says. Read **`07.03`** before
**`07.08`** and **`07.09`**, because the partition is what those two chapters are read against.

Read **`07.11`** with attention to `§5`. It names two numerals that are equal and unrelated, and it names
them so that nobody later mistakes an arithmetical coincidence for a finding.

Read **`07.12`** with particular attention. It answers the reading a hostile reviewer will produce first,
and the answer is not that the reading is unreasonable.

Read for the arguments rather than the citations. Where a chapter cites `21 CFR 211.180(a)` or
`21 CFR 11.10(c)`, it is locating a requirement in the text — **not** asserting that Helix meets it.
