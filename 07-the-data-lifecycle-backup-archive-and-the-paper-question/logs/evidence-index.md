## 0. What an earlier phase said about this phase's subject matter, and what is superseded or narrowed

**Four statements published in Phases 02, 05 and 06 are narrowed here, one is recorded as correct and
unsuperseded, and one addition is made. All six are written down rather than smoothed.**

**First, and it is the one that matters most.** Phase 02's record-type register `§0` states that the
register carries **"No retention period"**, and **`IS-14`** has read since 2026-07-24 as *no retention
period is recorded against any of the 34 Part 11 record types*. Phase 05's control position register `§6`
and Phase 06's `06.13 §6` each repeat it in the form **"No retention period exists for any of the 34
in-scope record types."** **As published, that last formulation is wrong, and it is wrong in a way that
matters.** **For 20 of the 34 a retention period exists and has existed since long before this programme
opened: it is set by `21 CFR 211.180(a)` or, for three record types, by another predicate-rule provision,
and what did not exist was Helix's record of having read it.** For 14 no predicate rule supplies one at
all. **The accurate statement is that no retention period was *recorded by Helix* against any of the 34,
which is `IS-14`'s own wording and is exactly right.** **Phases 02, 05 and 06 are not edited**; the
originals stand with this narrowing recorded against them, on the discipline Phase 02's evidence index
`§0` set for `EV-129` and Phase 05's and Phase 06's repeated. **No identifier already issued changes
meaning.**

**Second.** Phase 06's `06.13 §5` directs Phase 07 to *"Establish a retention period for the in-scope
record types, which `IS-14` has recorded as absent since 2026-07-24."* **As published that reads as a
direction to establish a period for all 34, and to that extent it is narrowed.** **Phase 07 has read a
period for 20 and set none for any record type.** For the 14 that no predicate rule reaches, **a decision
is owed by a named individual and Phase 07 does not take it** — `ADR-0044`, `ADR-0050`, `DP-38`. The
boundary was fixed at `DEC-701` on 2027-03-01, before the first record type was read, and `EV-701` retains
it. **Phase 06 is not edited.**

**Third.** Phase 05's `AS-37` — carried forward unedited at `raid-log.md §2.3` — records that *"17 `(c)`
determinations and part of `(e)`'s 29 rest on a period nobody has set…"*, and continues *"…and Phase 07
changes the basis of a determination this phase published."* **Phase 07 neither tests nor
fails that assumption, and the cut it computes is a different one.** What Phase 07 establishes is that
**on thirteen systems `21 CFR 11.10(c)` was determined *Met* against a record type for which no predicate
rule sets a period** — `IS-43`. **The thirteen are Met cells, not Not met cells**, and the figure is not
`AS-37`'s and does not replace it. **`AS-37` is not edited and no Phase 05 cell is re-determined** —
`ADR-0049`.

**Fourth.** Phase 06's evidence index `§0`, second item, records that `IS-05` was handed to Phase 07 *"as
part of `IS-42`"*, because no retention period existed for any of the five chromatography record types and
the relation therefore had no second term. **That is narrowed rather than corrected.** For those five
record types the second term did exist in the regulation and had not been read; **`IS-05` closes here on
the limb where the relation has two terms, and it is on sixteen other systems that the relation has none**
— `DEC-716`, `EV-730`, `EV-731`. **`IS-42` closes with it, in part** — `EV-732`.

**Fifth.** Phase 02's record-type register `§0` states that a negative determination **"removes nothing
but Part 11"**, and Phase 02's `02.06 §7` records the four record types on which the printout route holds.
**Both statements are correct and neither is superseded.** What is recorded here is that **no document in
Phases 02 to 06 followed the four into `21 CFR 211.180`**, and that the Part 211 position on that paper had
never been stated anywhere. `EV-726` records the position at the `MS-11` gate, and **the printout route
holds for all four and is not disturbed** — `ADR-0010`, `ADR-0046`.

**Sixth, and it is an addition rather than a correction.** **`EV-635` to `EV-700` are permanently unused
and are never issued**, on the same rule that left `EV-129`–`EV-200`, `EV-233`–`EV-300`, `EV-337`–`EV-400`,
`EV-441`–`EV-500` and `EV-543`–`EV-600` unused. **`06.13 §4` states the block and Phase 07 carries it
unchanged; no identifier within it — `EV-637` to `EV-700` among them — is ever issued.** **Phase 06
correctly recorded that Phase 07 would open at `EV-701` and at document IDs `HLX-DI-2027-7NN`; both
statements hold and neither is superseded.**

**Every item below exists at the vantage.** Nothing in this index is a plan, and no item is dated after
2027-03-19.

**The ordering discipline.** **The method items pre-date the result items they govern, and the counting
items post-date the records they count.** The derivation method at `EV-703` is dated 2027-03-02 and the
34 derivation records at `EV-706` 2027-03-08 — **because a period read off a system before the provision
has been read is a configuration wearing the clothes of a requirement.** The register at `EV-707` is dated
2027-03-09 and post-dates `EV-706`. The 61 enquiry records at `EV-716` are dated 2027-03-11 and the
register at `EV-717` 2027-03-12. The four custody enquiries at `EV-723` are dated 2027-03-16 and the
register at `EV-725` 2027-03-17. **Where two items carry the same date the weaker relation is the one
stated**, because a strict *post-dates* asserted of a same-day pair is a claim the Date column does not
support.

**What is not here.** **No retention policy, no retention schedule, no archive design, no migration plan,
no storage specification and no vendor selection** — none exists and none is written by this phase. **No
period set, proposed or bounded for any of the fourteen record types no predicate rule reaches.** **No
restore test result** — none exists. **No demonstration that any backup file is a true copy of anything.**
**No deviation record, no CAPA record, no remediation plan and no remediation cost.** **No executed
validation package, no protocol, no test result and no system validation plan** under Phase 04's
framework — none exists. **No disposition of `OBS-2` and no disposition of `OBS-4`** — Phase 08. **No
periodic review, no internal audit result, no third-party audit report and no inspection record after
2026-02-13.**

**Nothing in this index is a certification, an approval or an attestation of compliance by any agency.**
**There is no Part 11 certification**, and no artefact here could be one.

**Retention.** Each item is retained for at least the life of the determination, decision or register it
supports. **For the three registers at `EV-707`, `EV-717` and `EV-725` that is for as long as the positions
they record are relied on and for the retention period of the records they describe** — which for twenty

nobody has determined** (`IS-44`, `DP-38`).

## 3. The ordering rule, stated

**Where an item counts, reconciles or concludes from other items, its date is on or after the date of the
last item it counts.** `EV-707` is dated 2027-03-09 and the derivation records at `EV-706` 2027-03-08;
`EV-708` shares `EV-707`'s date, so the relation stated is **no earlier than**. `EV-717` is dated
2027-03-12 and the enquiry records at `EV-716` 2027-03-11; `EV-718` shares `EV-717`'s date. `EV-725` is
dated 2027-03-17 and the custody enquiries at `EV-723` 2027-03-16. `EV-728` and `EV-730` are dated
2027-03-18 and both count items dated 2027-03-09 and 2027-03-10.

**Where an item authorises, enumerates or specifies, its date is before the date of the first item it
governs, and strictly before.** `EV-702` 2027-03-01 and `EV-703` 2027-03-02 before `EV-706` 2027-03-08.
`EV-704` and `EV-705` 2027-03-03 before every result item in the phase. `EV-715` 2027-03-10 before
`EV-730` 2027-03-18. **A rule settled after the results are in is not a rule**, and the index is built so
that the claim can be checked against the Date column rather than believed.
