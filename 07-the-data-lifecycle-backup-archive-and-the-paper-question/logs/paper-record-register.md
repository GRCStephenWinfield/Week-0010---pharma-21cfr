## 0. What this register is, and what it is not

**Phase 02's printout route holds for all four of these record types.** It was correctly applied, it was
tested rather than asserted, and **nothing in this register disturbs it** —
`../../02-the-system-inventory-and-part-11-applicability/02.06-the-paper-that-was-not-the-record.md §7`,
`ADR-0010`, `DEC-210`. **The four are not Part 11 records and this register does not say otherwise.**

**What this register adds is what the route left undischarged.** A determination that a record is not a
Part 11 record is not a determination that it is unregulated — `ADR-0046`. The route moved these four out
of `21 CFR Part 11` and left them where they had always been: **required records under Part 211, whose
retention is governed by `21 CFR 211.180`, now carried by the paper rather than by anything electronic.**
**This register records what happened to them after that, and the answer is nothing.**

**FDA states the condition these four stand or fall on, in guidance**, at Question 10 of the December 2018
drug CGMP data integrity questions and answers: *"A paper printout or static record may satisfy retention
requirements if it is the original record or a true copy of the original record (see §§ 211.68(b),
211.188, 211.194, and 212.60)."* **That is guidance and it is cited as guidance; the requirement is the
predicate rule's.** On each of the four the instrument keeps nothing and the printout is the original
record — `EV-223` — **so the condition is met and the obligations that attach to a retained original
attach here.** The columns below record whether any of them was discharged.

**The columns.** *What Phase 02 determined and why it holds* reproduces the substance of the published
determination and re-argues none of it. *The predicate rule the paper now carries* is the same predicate
rule Phase 02's record-type register publishes, restated as an obligation on paper because that is where
the record is. The four Yes/No columns are the four questions
`../07.10-the-paper-question.md` asks. *Medium* is a fact about the paper, established by looking at it.
*What would be needed* names the closure condition and **sets no date, no priority and no order.**

**This register states no CAPA, no deviation and no remediation.** It sets no retention location, names
no accountable individual and derives no period on Helix's behalf; **it records that none of the three
exists.**

## 2. The counts, taken from the rows above

| Cut | Value |

| **Medium — thermal** | **3 of 4** — `PP-01`, `PP-02`, `PP-03` |
| **Medium — plain paper** | **1 of 4** — `PP-04` |
| **Check** | **3 + 1 = 4** |
| **Statement of the medium's archival life held from any source** | **0 of 4** |

**The note on `PP-01`, and it is the row to read twice.** **`RT-09` is an in-process control result
specifically associated with a batch.** It is a hardness and friability result for a named sample from a
named batch, taken during the manufacture of that batch and read by the reviewer of that batch's record.
**`21 CFR 211.180(a)`'s period therefore attaches to it** — at least 1 year after the expiration date of
the batch; for an exempt OTC drug product lacking expiration dating because it meets the criteria for
exemption under `21 CFR 211.137`, 3 years after distribution of the batch — **and it
attaches to the paper, because the paper is the record.** **A period the regulation sets, on a record
nobody owns, on a medium nobody has characterised.**

**On the other three, the shape of the gap is different and is not smaller.** `PP-02`, `PP-03` and `PP-04`
are records whose predicate rules require the content and set no period of their own; the period, where
there is one, would come from the record they are filed into and nothing records what that is. **`PP-01`
lacks an arrangement for a period the regulation has already set. `PP-02` to `PP-04` lack the arrangement
and the determination both.**

**On the medium, and it is a fact rather than a prediction.** **Three of the four are thermal.** Helix
holds a statement of the archival life of that stock **from no source** — not from the paper's supplier,
not from the instrument vendors, not from a Helix qualification and not from any standard the programme
can cite. **This register makes no claim about how long the print on those tickets will remain legible.**
It records that nobody at Helix can answer the question, and that `21 CFR 211.180(d)`'s permission to
retain a true copy in place of the original has not been used on any of the four.

## 3. What this register does not do

| It does not | Because |
|---|---|
| **Re-open Phase 02's applicability determination** | **The route holds for all four.** It was tested against three conditions, evidenced instrument by instrument, and correctly applied — `ADR-0010`, `EV-223`. **Nothing in Phase 07 moves any of the four back into Part 11 scope**, and nothing here may be cited as though it did — `PR-55` |
| **State a retention location, name an individual or set a period** | **Those are Helix's to decide and they are recorded here as owed** — `ADR-0044`, `ADR-0050`, `DP-42`. **A register that supplied them would have taken a decision the programme has said belongs to a named individual** |
| **Assert that any of the four is at risk of loss** | **No claim is made about the durability of any medium**, because Helix holds no statement of it. **The absence of a statement is the finding**; a prediction in either direction would be an invention |
| **State a CAPA, a deviation or a remediation plan** | Phase 07 states none. The last column names closure conditions and **sets no date, no priority and no order** |
| **Say anything about `RT-13`, `RT-14`, `RT-15`, `RT-16`, `RT-26`, `RT-27` or `RT-33`** | Those seven failed the printout route's first condition in Phase 02 and are inside the 34. **They are in `retention-derivation-register.md` and not here** |
| **Reach `21 CFR Part 11` at all** | **These four are not Part 11 records.** `21 CFR 11.10(b)`, `(c)` and `(e)` do not attach to them, and no cell above is a control determination |
