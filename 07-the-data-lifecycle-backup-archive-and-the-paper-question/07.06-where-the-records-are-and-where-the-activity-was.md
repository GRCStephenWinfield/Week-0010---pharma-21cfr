# 07.06 — Where the Records Are and Where the Activity Was

|---|---|

*`21 CFR 211.180(c)` attaches an availability obligation to a place, and saves the position where retrieval from elsewhere is immediate. Records of Cary activities sit at Ridgemont and nobody has ever retrieved one from Cary.*

---

## 1. The provision, quoted whole

**`21 CFR 211.180(c)` has three sentences and this chapter turns on all three of them.**

**The first:**

> *"All records required under this part, or copies of such records, shall be readily available for
> authorized inspection during the retention period at the establishment where the activities described in
> such records occurred."*

**The second, which is an obligation and not a saving:**

> *"These records or copies thereof shall be subject to photocopying or other means of reproduction as part
> of such inspection."*

**The third, which is the saving:**

> *"Records that can be immediately retrieved from another location by computer or other electronic means
> shall be considered as meeting the requirements of this paragraph."*

`07.02-specifically-associated-with-a-batch.md` reads `21 CFR 211.180` whole, paragraph by paragraph, and
owns what each paragraph does; **this chapter states where `(c)` lands on Helix's estate and adds no
reading of its own.** The short form of the reading is that **the first sentence attaches availability to
the establishment where the activities occurred; the second requires the records or copies of them to be
capable of being photocopied or otherwise reproduced as part of the inspection itself; and the third
relieves a firm of holding the records there only where retrieval from another location is immediate.**
**The saving reaches the location requirement. It does not touch the reproduction obligation**, which a
firm relying on immediate retrieval must still be able to meet at the establishment. The saving is
conditional. **The condition is a property of an arrangement, and an arrangement's properties are
established by exercising it.**

## 2. Where the records are

**`SYS-060` is the analytical data backup and restore platform for the QC laboratories. It sits in the
Ridgemont data centre and it holds `RT-12` and `RT-13`** — the chromatographic raw data file with its
acquisition metadata, and the chromatographic integration and reprocessing record. Phase 02's systems
register carries all of that and it is not re-determined here.

**Both record types are generated at both sites.** `RT-12` and `RT-13` are held on `SYS-023`, the
chromatography data system in the QC laboratory at **Ridgemont**, and on `SYS-024`, the chromatography
data system in the QC laboratory at **Cary**. Phase 06 examined both instances at depth and established
that they are the same product at the same version in two laboratories — `ADR-0037`.

**So the position is this.** A chromatographic run performed at Cary produces `RT-12` and `RT-13` on
`SYS-024` at Cary. Those record types are also held on `SYS-060`, **which is at Ridgemont**. They are also
held on `SYS-061`, the laboratory data archive and retention store, **which is at Cary** — and on
`SYS-036`, the laboratory data archive and long-term retention store, **which is at Ridgemont**.

**For a run performed at Cary the record exists in four places — `SYS-024`, `SYS-036`, `SYS-060` and
`SYS-061` — and across both sites `RD-10` and `RD-11` put `RT-12` and `RT-13` on five systems. No document
in the repository says which instance Helix relies on as the original.** That is `DP-17` — a statement, per record type, of which instance Helix relies on as
the original — **open since Phase 03 and still the single largest closure route in the programme.** It is
not discharged here and this chapter does not attempt it.

## 3. Where the activity was

**`21 CFR 211.180(c)`'s first sentence attaches to the establishment where the activities described in the
records occurred.** For a chromatographic run performed in the Cary QC laboratory, the activities occurred
at Cary. **The record of them is held, among other places, on a platform in the Ridgemont data centre.**

**That is not, by itself, a problem, and the regulation says so in its own third sentence.** Records that
can be **immediately retrieved from another location by computer or other electronic means** are
considered to meet the paragraph. **Helix's arrangement is exactly the arrangement the saving was written
for**: a network, a storage platform at one site, and laboratories at two.

**What the saving requires is that the retrieval be immediate, and that is a question of fact about a
particular arrangement.** It is not answered by a network diagram, by a bandwidth figure, by a service
level in a contract or by an assurance from anybody that the link is fast. **It is answered by retrieving
a record — a real one, of a real Cary activity, from `SYS-060` — at Cary, and recording how long it took.**

**No such retrieval has been demonstrated, timed or recorded.** The enquiry is retained at `EV-714` and
its answer is *none identified*; the enquiry is retained rather than its answer alone so that the negative
can be tested as a search rather than taken as a conclusion — the discipline `EV-623` set in Phase 06.
**Nobody at Helix has ever produced `RT-12` or `RT-13` for a Cary activity from Cary and written down what
happened.**

**`IS-48`** carries it, owned by Joachim Reuter, raised 2027-03-11, open. `DP-39` is the route: **a
retrieval of `RT-12` and `RT-13` from `SYS-060` initiated at Cary, performed, timed and recorded.**

**And `DP-39` would not exhaust the paragraph even when it is discharged.** The saving relieves a firm of
holding the records at the establishment; it says nothing about the paragraph's second sentence, which
requires the records or copies of them to be *"subject to photocopying or other means of reproduction as
part of such inspection."* **That is an act performed during the inspection and at the establishment,
and it does not depend on the saving at all.** A retrieval that is timed and recorded answers the
immediacy question and leaves the reproduction question exactly where it was: **nobody at Helix has
established that a Cary-generated record held at Ridgemont can be reproduced at Cary in the course of an
inspection.** The enquiry retained at `EV-714` asked about retrieval and not about reproduction, and its
answer is silent on the second sentence for that reason.

## 4. What is being said, and what is not

**This chapter does not say that retrieval from Cary would fail.** It says nothing at all about whether it
would succeed, how long it would take, or whether the result would satisfy anybody. **A negative that
nobody can test is not a determination; it is a silence** — and so is a positive. **What is recorded is
that the condition on which the saving depends has never been exercised**, which is a different statement
from either.

**It states no CAPA.** No corrective action, no deviation, no preventive action, no remediation plan and
no remediation cost is stated by this chapter, implied by it, or attributable to it. `DP-39` names what
would answer the question and **sets no date, no priority and no order.**

**It states no legal conclusion.** Whether Helix's arrangement meets `21 CFR 211.180(c)` is not
adjudicated here and no document in this repository reaches a legal conclusion of any kind. **An open gap
is Helix's own assessment of its own position.**

**And it is not a finding about Cary.** `PR-47` and `PR-49` both carry the risk that a fact about an
estate decision is read as a criticism of one laboratory, and both are live here. **Cary did not decide
where the analytical data backup and restore platform sits.** The arrangement is an estate arrangement,
made for estate reasons, and the untested condition belongs to the arrangement rather than to either
laboratory. **No document in this repository assesses any individual's work.**

## 5. Why the question could not have been asked earlier

**Every provision this chapter cites was in force throughout the programme, and the arrangement it
describes has been visible in Phase 02's systems register since 2026-07-31.** Two columns — Location and
Record types held — carried everything above. **Nobody read across the row**, which is `IS-37`'s shape
exactly and is the third time this repository has recorded it.

**But there is a second reason, and it is this phase's own.** `21 CFR 211.180(c)`'s obligation runs
**during the retention period**. Until the retention period of `RT-12` and `RT-13` had been derived, the
paragraph had no term to run for on those record types, and an enquiry into immediate retrieval would have
been an enquiry into an obligation whose duration nobody could state. **`RD-10` and `RD-11` supply that
term — `21 CFR 211.180(a)`'s period, at least 1 year after the expiration date of the batch — and the
enquiry became askable in the same week the derivation was accepted.**

**Which is worth noticing on its own account.** `07.08-the-endpoint-that-does-not-exist.md` records the
same structure with a worse consequence: an obligation that presupposes a retention period, assessed on
systems where no retention period exists. **Here the term exists and the arrangement is untested. There
the arrangement was determined met and the term does not exist.**

## 6. What is still not true

- **No retrieval of a Cary-generated record from `SYS-060` has been demonstrated, timed or recorded** —
  `IS-48`, `DP-39`.
- **No reproduction of a Cary-generated record has been made or attempted at Cary**, and
  `21 CFR 211.180(c)`'s second sentence is not reached by the saving that `DP-39` would exercise.
- **No document states which instance of `RT-12` or `RT-13` Helix relies on as the original** — `DP-17`,
  open since Phase 03.
- **`21 CFR 11.10(c)` is Not met on `SYS-060`** in Phase 05's published register, and **nothing here
  re-determines it** — `ADR-0049`.
- **A restore has been performed on `SYS-060` once, as incident recovery, and what came back was not
  compared with what went in** — `BR-60`, `IS-46`.
- **`SYS-061`, the Cary archive, entered the inventory by walk and has never entered a validation
  lifecycle** — `IS-13`.
